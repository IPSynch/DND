# Homebrew Sorcerer Subclasses (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Five original Sorcerous Origins built on the 2024 *Player's Handbook* chassis.
Each grants features at **Sorcerer levels 3, 6, 14, and 18**, matching the
official 2024 subclass cadence, and each includes an always-prepared
**subclass spell list** that grows at sorcerer levels 3, 5, 7, and 9.

These are tuned to be **strong**, as requested. A few feel like they push past
official power budgets on purpose — they are meant for high-octane, anime-flavored
tables. Tune the dice and Sorcery Point costs down a notch if your group runs lean.

## Design conventions used throughout

- **Spell save DC** and **spell attack bonus** always mean your Sorcerer ones.
- **Sorcery Points (SP)** are the 2024 Font of Magic pool (equal to your sorcerer level).
- "Innate Sorcery" refers to the level 1 class feature (Bonus Action, 1 minute,
  +1 spell save DC, Advantage on attack rolls for Sorcerer spells, 2 uses/Long Rest).
- Features that grant extra uses, durations, or Metamagic stack with the base class
  and with **Arcane Apotheosis** (level 20) unless noted.
