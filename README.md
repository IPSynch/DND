# Homebrew D&D Subclasses (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Original subclasses built on the 2024 *Player's Handbook* chassis, tuned to be
**strong** and written to be **setting-agnostic** — the flavor works whether your
table runs high-medieval, modern superhero, post-apocalypse, or anything between.

Several entries deliberately push past official power budgets; they are meant for
high-octane, anime-flavored tables. Tune the dice and resource costs down a notch if
your group runs lean. Every file ends with **designer notes** that call out the
intended power level and the cleanest dials to turn it up or down.

---

## Monk — Monastic Orders

Ten Monk subclasses, each granting features at **Monk levels 3, 6, 11, and 17**
(the official 2024 cadence). Each opens with a **setting-agnostic introduction** and
gives every feature a short **flavor line** before its mechanics, in the style of
*D&D Beyond* / WOTC write-ups.

### Conventions used throughout the Monk orders

- **Monk DC** = **8 + Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier**.
- **Focus Points (FP)** are the 2024 Monk resource pool (equal to your monk level
  from level 2 on). Older books call this same pool **Ki** — they are interchangeable,
  so a "Ki point" in the source concepts is a **Focus Point** here.
- **MA die** = your **Martial Arts die** (d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 as you level).
- Features assume and stack with the base 2024 class (Flurry of Blows, Patient
  Defense, Step of the Wind, Stunning Strike, Deflect Attacks, Uncanny Metabolism,
  etc.) unless a feature says otherwise.
- Transformation states (**Gears**, **Gates**, **Cowls**, **stances**) last **1
  minute**, need **no Concentration**, and don't stack with one another unless noted.
