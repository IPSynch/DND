# Homebrew Concept Species (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Twelve original **concept-first** species plus three **Elven** species built for
the *Elven Accuracy* feat. These are tuned to be **strong**, as requested — built
for high-octane, anime-flavored tables. Every species' "Power note" calls out the
spicy parts and gives a lean-table dial-down.

## The core idea: concept, not race

A species here is a **concept**, not an appearance. A War Angel, a Titanborn, or a
Gloaming Elf can *look like anything* — a human, a Tiefling, a Centaur, or a literal
animal (an actual **puma**, not a cat-person). Pick the concept for the mechanics
and the soul; pick the look for the table.

## Design conventions

- Built on the **2024 Player's Handbook** species chassis: Creature Type, Size,
  Speed, and a handful of traits, with a few traits scaling at character levels
  **5 / 11 / 17**.
- **"PB"** = your proficiency bonus. Save DCs are written **8 + PB + the listed
  modifier** unless a feature says otherwise.
- Resistances don't stack with themselves (take the best source).
- These intentionally push past official species budgets. Use the Power notes.

## The three Elven species (Elven Accuracy chassis)

Each one **counts as an Elf** (qualifying for the *Elven Accuracy* feat) and bakes
in a reliable **advantage-on-attack** engine, so the feat's reroll triggers almost
every turn. Pair any of them with Elven Accuracy and a Dex/Wis/Cha attacker.

| Species | Advantage engine | Best for |
|---------|------------------|----------|
| [Gloaming Elf](./elf-1-gloaming-elf.md) | Advantage in/against **dim light or darkness** (you can make your own) | Rogue / shadow striker |
| [Highsun Elf](./elf-2-highsun-elf.md) | **Sun's Mark** — permanent, mobile advantage on a chosen quarry | Ranger / charging attacker |
| [Foreseeing Elf](./elf-3-foreseeing-elf.md) | **Foresight** — advantage on your first attack each turn (always-on) + Portent | Any Elven Accuracy build |

> **Reminder — Elven Accuracy:** when you have Advantage on a D20 Test to attack
> using **Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma**, you can reroll one of the
> dice once. These species are designed to *keep that advantage live* round after
> round.
