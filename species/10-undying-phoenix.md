# Undying (Phoenix-Marked)

*Concept Species — "Death is a doorway you keep walking back through, smelling faintly of ash and cinnamon."*

> Marked by the Phoenix. They burn, they fall, they **come back** — every time a
> little more themselves. Could be a calm human who simply refuses to stay dead, a
> Tiefling wreathed in embers, or a russet puma whose pelt smolders when it's
> angry and reignites when it's killed. The trick is making the second life count.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Celestial (or Elemental, your choice)
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Cinder-Blooded
You have **Resistance to Fire damage** (immunity at **level 11**) and **Necrotic
damage**. You don't age past adulthood and can't die of old age.

### Rekindle
When you drop to **0 hit points but aren't killed outright**, you can choose to
**burst into healing flame**: you immediately stand with **HP equal to half your
maximum**, and each creature within 10 ft. takes **Fire damage equal to twice your
character level** (Dexterity save, your Con-based DC, for half). You can do this
**once per Long Rest** (twice per Long Rest at level 11).

### Embered Strikes
Once per turn when you hit a creature, you deal **extra Fire damage equal to your
proficiency bonus.** If the target is below half its hit points, this damage is
**doubled** (the Phoenix favors finishing).

### Ash and Renewal
When you finish a **Short Rest**, you can shed your form in flame and return:
remove **one** of the following from yourself — a disease, the Poisoned, Blinded,
Deafened, or Charmed condition, or **one level of Exhaustion.** Usable **PB times
per Long Rest.** When you roll Hit Dice on a rest, you may treat each die as its
**maximum** a number of times equal to your PB per Long Rest.

### Warmth of the Reborn
You have **Advantage on death saving throws** and on saves against being reduced to
0 HP by Necrotic damage. Allies who can see you **Rekindle** gain **Temporary HP
equal to your character level.**

### Languages
Common, Ignan or Celestial, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A built-in self-rez with a damage burst, fire immunity by 11,
> and a finisher rider. The "second wind that explodes" is the centerpiece. Lean
> tables: Rekindle returns you at 1 HP + Con instead of half max.
