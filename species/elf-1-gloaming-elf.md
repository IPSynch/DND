# Gloaming Elf

*Elven Species — built-in **advantage** engine #1 (the dark)*

> Elves of the long dusk, who see the world best when the sun gives up on it.
> They could look like a classic moon-pale elf, a shadow-dappled Tiefling-elf
> crossing, or a dusk-grey puma with an elf's soul and an elf's patience. In
> shadow, they do not miss.

**Elf lineage:** This species counts as an **Elf** for any feature, feat, or item
that requires you to be an Elf or Half-Elf (including the **Elven Accuracy** feat).

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Elf)
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** **35 ft.**

### Fey Ancestry
You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed**, and magic can't put
you to sleep. You don't sleep — you Trance 4 hours for a Long Rest's benefit.

### Eyes of the Gloaming
You have **Darkvision 120 ft.**, and you can **see normally in magical darkness.**
Dim light is **not** lightly obscured for you.

### Advantage Engine — Killer in the Dark
While you are in **dim light or darkness**, or while the **target** of your attack
is in dim light or darkness, you have **Advantage on attack rolls against it.**
(With **Elven Accuracy**, this means you reroll one die on essentially every
attack you make from the shadows.) You can create the condition yourself: as a
**Bonus Action**, snuff the light around you into a **15-ft. radius of dim murk**
centered on you for 1 minute, **PB times per Long Rest** — bright light becomes
dim, dim becomes dark, but your own sight is unaffected.

### Veil-Step
**PB times per Long Rest**, while in dim light or darkness, you can use a Bonus
Action to become **Invisible until the end of your turn** or until you attack —
and attacking from this Invisibility doesn't end the advantage above.

### Keen Senses
You have proficiency in **Perception and Stealth**, and **Advantage on Perception
checks that rely on sight or hearing.**

### Languages
Common, Elvish, and one other of your choice.

---

> **How it builds Elven Accuracy:** Your advantage is self-generated (you make your
> own darkness) and travels with you, so you're a Dex/Wis/Cha attacker who has
> advantage nearly always — the perfect Elven Accuracy chassis. Lean tables:
> require the *target* (not just you) to be in shadow for the advantage.
