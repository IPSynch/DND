# Skyclad

*Concept Species — "The Nudist. Armor is a confession that the body isn't enough. Theirs is."*

> A people whose strength is in being **unburdened**. Clothing optional, armor
> refused on principle, possessions kept to nothing. The less they carry, the
> harder they are to stop. They might be a serene human ascetic, a fur-covered
> beast for whom clothes never made sense, or a great cat that is, simply, a great
> cat. Play it as discipline, not a bit.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** **35 ft.**

### Bare Resilience
While you wear **no armor and hold no shield**, your **AC equals 10 + your
Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier.** This is your full Unarmored
Defense and it stacks with nothing else (pick the best). You may still benefit
from a cloak, ring, or other worn magic items — those aren't "armor."

### Nothing to Take
While you are **unarmored and unencumbered** (carrying no more than light gear):
- You have **Advantage on Initiative** and on **Dexterity saving throws.**
- Your **Speed increases by 10 ft.** (in addition to the base above) and you ignore
  Difficult Terrain.
- You can't be **Disarmed**, and grapples against you are made with
  **Disadvantage.**

### Unfettered Body
You have **Resistance to Cold damage** and **Advantage on saving throws against
extreme temperature, exhaustion from the elements, and being Restrained.** You can
also slip free of any nonmagical bonds, manacles, or grapple as a Bonus Action.

### Flow State
The more exposed you are, the sharper you fight. While unarmored, the first time
each turn you hit a creature, you deal **extra damage equal to your proficiency
bonus**, and when a creature misses you with a melee attack, you can **move 5 ft.
without provoking** (no action). At **level 11**, once per turn when a melee
attack misses you, you may make **one weapon or unarmed strike against the
attacker** as part of that same Reaction-free movement.

### Languages
Common and two others of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A Monk-style Con-based AC that can hit absurd numbers, +Speed,
> grapple/disarm immunity, and a free per-turn damage rider. It's a meme on paper
> and a wall in practice. Lean tables: drop the level-11 free strike.
