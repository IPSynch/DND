# Expert Martial Artist

*Concept Species — "Born into the discipline. The body *is* the weapon, and it was finished long before you were."*

> Not a monk class — a **people** who have refined the body across generations.
> They could be wiry humans, a four-armed something, or an actual snow-white puma
> whose paws strike faster than the eye tracks. Hand them a sword and they're
> dangerous; hand them nothing and they're worse.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small (your choice)
- **Speed:** **35 ft.**

### Living Weapon
Your **Unarmed Strikes deal 1d6** damage (Bludgeoning), increasing to **1d8 at
level 5, 1d10 at level 11, and 1d12 at level 17**. You can use **Dexterity**
instead of Strength for your unarmed strikes' attack and damage rolls.

### Flurry
When you take the **Attack action** and attack only with unarmed strikes, you can
make **one extra unarmed strike as a Bonus Action.** (This stacks with the Monk's
Martial Arts / Flurry — a multiclass dream.)

### Deflect & Redirect
When you are hit by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to reduce the damage
by **1d10 + your Dexterity modifier + your proficiency bonus.** If this reduces a
melee attack's damage to 0, you can immediately make **one unarmed strike against
the attacker** if it's within reach. Usable **proficiency bonus times per Long Rest.**

### Master of Leverage
You have **Advantage on ability checks and saving throws to start, escape, or
maintain a Grapple, and to Shove.** You can move at full speed while grappling a
creature your size or smaller, and you ignore Difficult Terrain when you Dash.

### Languages
Common and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** Built to multiclass insanely well with Monk and any martial.
> The free Bonus-Action strike and a Deflect that *counterattacks* are the spicy
> bits. Lean tables: drop Deflect's counterattack rider.
