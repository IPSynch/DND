# Foreseeing Elf

*Elven Species — built-in **advantage** engine #3 (foresight)*

> Elves whose ancestors drank from the well of tomorrow. They see the fight a beat
> before it happens. A serene silver-eyed elf, a star-marked Tiefling-elf, or a
> pale lynx-eyed puma that is already moving toward where you *will* be. Surprise
> is a thing that happens to other people.

**Elf lineage:** This species counts as an **Elf** for any feature, feat, or item
that requires you to be an Elf or Half-Elf (including the **Elven Accuracy** feat).

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Elf)
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Fey Ancestry
You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed**, magic can't put you
to sleep, and you Trance 4 hours for a Long Rest's benefit.

### Eyes That See Ahead
You have **Darkvision 60 ft.**, you can't be **Surprised** while conscious, and
you have **Advantage on Initiative.** You always know if you're being observed by a
creature within 60 ft.

### Advantage Engine — Read the Moment
Your foresight grants **Advantage on attack rolls** in any of these (very common)
situations:
- against any creature that **hasn't taken a turn yet** in the combat;
- on the **first attack roll you make on each of your turns**;
- against any creature that **has missed you** with an attack since your last turn.

Between "first attack each turn" and the other triggers, you'll have advantage on
the great majority of your attacks. (With **Elven Accuracy**, that's a reroll on
your opening — and often best — attack every single round.)

### Glimpse of What Comes
**PB times per Long Rest**, when you or a creature you can see within 60 ft. makes
an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can use your **Reaction** to
**impose Disadvantage** (you saw the misstep) **or grant Advantage** (you saw the
opening). At **level 5**, once per Long Rest you can instead **declare a roll a
hit or a miss** after seeing the d20 but before damage.

### Portent's Gift
After a Long Rest, roll **two d20s** and record them. **Once each** before your
next Long Rest, you can **replace any attack roll, save, or check** (yours or a
creature you can see) with one of the recorded numbers. (As the Diviner's Portent.)

### Languages
Common, Elvish, and one other of your choice.

---

> **How it builds Elven Accuracy:** "First attack each turn" alone guarantees an
> Elven Accuracy reroll **every round** with zero setup, and Portent + the
> Reaction give you control over the *enemy's* dice too. The most "always-on"
> reroll engine of the three. Lean tables: cut Portent to a single d20.
