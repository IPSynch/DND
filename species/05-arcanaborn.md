# Arcanaborn

*Concept Species — "Highly attuned with magic. The Weave doesn't flow *past* you; it flows *into* you."*

> Some people learn magic. These were born standing in it. They could be a serene
> human mystic, a Tiefling whose veins glow, or a puma whose fur crackles with
> static when a spell is cast nearby. They hold more enchanted items than should
> be possible and shrug off hostile magic like rain.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (or Fey, your choice)
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Overflowing Attunement
You can **attune to one additional magic item** (4 total instead of 3) at level 1,
increasing to **5 total at level 5, 6 at level 11, and 7 at level 17.** You can
attune to an item over a **Short Rest of just 1 minute.**

### Weave-Touched
You always have **Detect Magic** and **Mage Hand** prepared, castable at will
without a slot. You can also see the **school of magic** of any spell cast within
60 ft. that you can perceive.

### Spurn the Spell
You have **Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.**
When you succeed on a save against a spell that targets only you, you can use your
**Reaction** to redirect it: the caster rerolls the spell against a different
creature of your choice within 30 ft., using your DC isn't required — the original
caster's effect simply retargets. Usable **PB times per Long Rest.**

### Mantle of the Weave
Choose one damage type from: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison,
Psychic, Radiant, Thunder. You gain **Resistance** to it. At **level 5** you may
change the chosen type after each Long Rest. At **level 11**, the first time each
turn you take damage of your chosen type, you instead **take none and store it** —
your next spell or attack deals **extra damage equal to your character level**, of
your chosen type.

### Soulbound Spark
You learn **one cantrip and one 1st-level spell** of your choice from any class's
list; Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for them
(choose at creation). At **level 5** you can cast the 1st-level spell once per Long
Rest without a slot.

### Languages
Common, Draconic, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** Extra attunement slots scaling to **seven**, advantage on all
> magic saves, and a spell-redirect Reaction make this the ultimate gish/caster
> chassis. Lean tables: cap attunement at 5 and drop the redirect.
