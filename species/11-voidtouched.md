# Voidtouched

*Concept Species — "Something between the stars looked back, and left a little of itself behind your eyes."*

> Bearers of a fragment of the Far Realm / the great Void. Their minds reach where
> hands can't, and reality is politely negotiable in their immediate vicinity. A
> pale human with too-still eyes, a Tiefling whose shadow moves wrong, or a sleek
> black puma that is *always already* looking at the thing you were about to do.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Aberration
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Mind Beyond Flesh
You have **Resistance to Psychic damage**, **Advantage on saving throws against
being Charmed or Frightened**, and **Telepathy** out to **60 ft.** with any
creature that shares a language. You can't be surprised while conscious, and you
have **Darkvision 120 ft.** that pierces magical darkness.

### Reaching Strike
Your reach with melee attacks and unarmed strikes is **+5 ft.** (warped space,
not long limbs). Once per turn when you hit, you can deal **extra Psychic damage
equal to your proficiency bonus** and choose one: the target has **Disadvantage on
its next attack roll**, or it can't take **Reactions** until the start of your next
turn.

### Blink-Step
As a **Bonus Action**, you fold space and **teleport up to 30 ft.** to a space you
can see. Usable a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus per Long
Rest**; at **level 5** you may bring **one willing creature** within 5 ft. with
you. At **level 11**, after you teleport you have **Advantage on your next attack**
before the end of the turn.

### Probability Sink
The Void blurs cause and effect around you. **PB times per Long Rest**, you can use
your **Reaction** to force a creature you can see within 60 ft. to **reroll an
attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** and use the lower result — or to let
**an ally reroll** and keep the higher. ("It simply happened differently.")

### Whispered Knowledge
You have **Advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) and Wisdom (Insight) checks**, and
once per Long Rest you can ask the Void a yes/no question about something within a
mile (DM answers truthfully or "unclear").

### Languages
Common, Deep Speech, and telepathy as above.

---

> **Power note:** Telepathy + 120 ft. darkvision + Blink-Step mobility + a
> reroll-forcing Reaction is a control/skirmisher's dream. Lean tables: limit
> Probability Sink to enemies only and drop the +5 ft. reach.
