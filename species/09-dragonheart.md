# Dragonheart

*Concept Species — "A true dragon's heart beats in a smaller body. For now."*

> Not a Dragonborn cosplaying scales — an actual **dragon's soul** wearing
> whatever shape it was born into. A regal human with slit-pupiled eyes, a Centaur
> whose lower body is armored in scale, or a tawny puma whose breath fogs with heat
> and whose growl drops the temperature of a room. The hoard-instinct is real.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Dragon
- **Size:** Medium (Large available at level 11 — see Apex Growth)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Draconic Element
Choose a damage type at creation: **Fire, Cold, Lightning, Acid, or Poison.** This
is your *element* for all features below, and you have **Resistance** to it.

### Breath Weapon
As an **Action**, exhale a **30-ft. cone** (or 60-ft. line, choose at creation).
Each creature there makes a **Dexterity save (DC 8 + PB + Con mod)**, taking
**2d10** damage of your element on a fail (half on success). The damage rises to
**3d10 at level 5, 4d10 at level 11, 5d10 at level 17.** Usable **PB times per
Long Rest**, and you may also spend a spell slot to use it, adding +1d10 per slot
level.

### Scaled Hide
While unarmored, your **AC = 13 + Dexterity modifier.** You also have **Advantage
on saving throws against being Frightened**, and you can impose your draconic
**Frightful Presence** (15-ft. radius, Wis save vs. your Breath DC, Frightened
until end of your next turn) as a Bonus Action **PB times per Long Rest.**

### Wings & Wrath
At **level 5** you gain a **Fly Speed of 40 ft.** (you must end your turn on the
ground until level 11, when you can hover). At **level 11** you can become
**Large** at will (Bonus Action, 1 minute, PB/day), gaining +5 ft. reach and
treating your Breath Weapon as a level higher while Large.

### Hoarder's Eye
You have **Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) checks when bargaining
or threatening**, and you can sense the **most valuable object** within 60 ft.

### Languages
Common, Draconic, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A scaling cone Breath Weapon, natural armor, fear, and flight
> make this an all-around terror. Lean tables: drop Breath to PB/day only (no
> slot conversion) and delay flight to level 9.
