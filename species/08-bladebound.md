# Bladebound

*Concept Species — "The Swordsman Specialist. One person, one edge, one perfected motion."*

> A lineage that lives and dies by the sword. Not a class — an **identity** burned
> in so deep the body learned the draw before the mind did. They might be a stoic
> human duelist, a Tiefling ronin, or — yes — a puma that has somehow bonded with
> a katana and is, frankly, terrifying with it. Any blade. Total mastery.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Sword-Bond
You can bond with **one weapon that has the Slashing or Piercing edge of a blade**
(any sword, including greatswords, scimitars, rapiers, daggers) over a Short Rest.
While wielding a bonded blade:
- You can **draw or stow it instantly**, and it can't be Disarmed against your
  will. If separated, you can **teleport it to your hand** as a Bonus Action from
  up to 60 ft.
- You add your **proficiency bonus to its damage rolls** (in addition to the
  attack bonus).

### Perfect Edge
Your bonded blade's **critical hit range is 19–20** (and 18–20 at level 11). When
you score a critical hit with it, you can **maximize** the weapon's damage dice
instead of rolling them.

### Iai — The Drawn Cut
Once per turn, when you make the **first attack of combat** or attack a creature
that hasn't acted yet this combat, you have **Advantage** on the roll and deal
**extra damage equal to your character level** on a hit. (Pairs viciously with the
crit range above.)

### Riposte
When a creature you can see misses you with a melee attack, you can use your
**Reaction** to make **one attack with your bonded blade** against it. Usable
**PB times per Long Rest**; from **level 11** there's no per-rest limit, but only
once per turn.

### Reading the Blade
You have **Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that has hit you with a
melee attack since the start of your last turn** ("you showed me your form"), and
**Advantage on Strength or Dexterity saves to keep your footing / resist being
moved.**

### Languages
Common and two others of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A widened, maximizing crit range + a per-PB free damage opener +
> Riposte makes a single sword absurd. Note the built-in **Advantage on attack
> rolls** (Reading the Blade / Iai) — strong on its own and excellent if you want
> to mimic Elven-Accuracy-style reroll stacking. Lean tables: keep crit at 19–20.
