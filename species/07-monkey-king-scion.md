# Monkey King Scion

*Concept Species — "Descendant of the one who fought Heaven to a draw and ate the peaches anyway."*

> Heir to the Handsome Monkey King. Trickster blood, a body like spring steel, and
> a grin that means trouble for somebody. Usually they *look* simian — but the
> bloodline runs strange, so yours might be a wiry human acrobat, a horned imp, or
> a literal golden puma that should not be able to pole-vault a castle wall but
> absolutely just did.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Fey-touched)
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** **35 ft.**, **Climb Speed 35 ft.**

### Body Like Iron, Bones Like Steel
You have **Resistance to Fire damage** (the Furnace couldn't kill your ancestor)
and **Advantage on Constitution and Dexterity saving throws.** Your **jump
distance is tripled**, and you take no falling damage from falls of 30 ft. or less.

### Seventy-Two Transformations
You can cast **Disguise Self** at will, and beginning at **level 3** you can cast
**Alter Self** (PB times per Long Rest) without a slot. At **level 5** you can
also **Polymorph yourself** (and only yourself) once per Long Rest into a Beast of
CR ≤ your proficiency bonus. While transformed you keep your Intelligence, Wisdom,
and Charisma and can speak.

### Hairpin Clones
As a **Bonus Action**, pluck a hair (or whisker) and create up to **two illusory
duplicates of yourself** that last 1 minute (as the *Mirror Image* spell, but you
make them all at once and they don't need a slot). Usable **PB times per Long
Rest.** At **level 11** the duplicates can each make **one weapon attack on your
turn** using your attack bonus, dealing **PB** damage on a hit.

### Somersault Cloud
As a **Bonus Action**, summon a cloud beneath your feet: you gain a **Fly Speed of
40 ft. (hover)** until the end of your turn, and you can take it **once per turn**
— effectively flight in bursts. At **level 5** the cloud lasts **1 minute** (PB
times per Long Rest).

### Mischief in the Marrow
You have **Advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics and Sleight of Hand) and Charisma
(Deception) checks.** Once per Short Rest, when you fail a save, you can **reroll
it** with Advantage — "the King doesn't lose twice."

### Languages
Common, Sylvan, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** Mobility (climb, triple jump, burst flight), self-Polymorph,
> *and* a clone engine that later attacks — this is a Swiss-army trickster.
> Lean tables: cut clone attacks and make Somersault Cloud PB/day from level 1.
