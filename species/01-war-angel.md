# War Angel

*Concept Species — "A soldier of the Host, forged for one war and never told it ended."*

> They do not look alike. One is a winged human in burnished plate; another is a
> Tiefling whose horns burn gold; another is a great white puma whose every step
> rings like a struck bell. What they share is not a shape — it is an **oath**, and
> a light that answers when they raise a weapon.

A War Angel can look like **anything**: human, Tiefling, Centaur, or a literal
animal. The concept is martial-celestial: a being built to fight holy wars.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Celestial
- **Size:** Medium (or Small, your choice)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Radiant Wings
You manifest wings of light at will (Bonus Action). While they are out you have a
**Fly Speed equal to your Speed**, and you can **hover**. At character level 1 you
can stay airborne for a number of minutes per Long Rest equal to your
proficiency bonus; at **level 5** the flight becomes **unlimited**.

### Aegis of the Host
You have **Resistance to Radiant damage**, and while you aren't wearing heavy
armor you gain a **+1 bonus to AC** (the light hardens around you).

### Smite of the Host
When you hit a creature with an attack, you can channel celestial fire: the
target takes extra **Radiant damage equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus**.
The d8 becomes **2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 11, and 4d8 at level 17**.
You can do this a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus per Long
Rest** (and you may spend a 1st-level-or-higher spell slot to use it again,
adding +1d8 per slot level above 1st if you're a caster).

### Unbroken Oath
You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened or Charmed**, and
you can't be **possessed**. Fiends and Undead have **Disadvantage on saving
throws against your Smite of the Host**.

### Languages
Common, Celestial, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** Flight at level 1 (capped, then uncapped at 5), free per-PB
> bonus radiant damage, and a defensive AC bump make this a front-line monster.
> Lean tables: delay uncapped flight to level 9 and drop the +AC.
