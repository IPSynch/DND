# Titanborn

*Concept Species — "Titan Strength. The world is built a size too small and you keep noticing."*

> Beings carrying the raw strength of titans. Could be a giant of a human, a
> Centaur the size of a draft horse, or a literal mountain puma that can drag an
> elk up a cliff in its jaws. They are not subtle. They do not need to be.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Giant
- **Size:** Medium (you may choose Large at level 5 — see Growing Into It)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Titanic Might
Your Strength score **maximum is 22**, and increasing Strength via Ability Score
Improvements ignores the usual 20 cap up to that. You have **Advantage on
Strength checks and Strength saving throws.**

### Powerful Build
You count as **one size larger** for carrying capacity, push/drag/lift, and the
weight you can throw. You have **Advantage on checks and saves to avoid being
moved or knocked Prone.**

### Crushing Blows
Once per turn, when you hit with a melee weapon or unarmed strike, you deal
**extra damage equal to your Strength modifier** (yes, a second time), and you can
force the target to make a **Strength save (DC 8 + PB + Str mod)** or be knocked
**Prone** or pushed **10 feet.** Against creatures larger than you, you have
Advantage on this attack roll.

### Earthshaker
Your unarmed strikes deal **1d8 + Strength** Bludgeoning damage. As an **Action**
(usable **PB times per Long Rest**), you slam the ground: each creature within
**10 feet** makes a Strength save or takes **2d6 Bludgeoning** (scaling +1d6 at
levels 5/11/17) and is knocked Prone on a fail.

### Growing Into It
At **level 5** you may become **Large** at will (Bonus Action) for 1 minute, PB
times per Long Rest; while Large your reach increases by 5 ft. and your slam/melee
damage dice are **maximized once per turn.**

### Languages
Common, Giant, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A 22 Strength cap plus a per-turn free Str-mod rider plus prone
> control is a grappler/striker's paradise. Lean tables: cap Str at 20 and cut
> Growing Into It's maximize.
