# Demon-King Blooded

*Concept Species — "Somewhere up your bloodline sat a throne of skulls. You can feel the seat is still warm."*

> Descendant of a Demon-King. The blood remembers dominion. People kneel a half-
> second before they decide to, and fire treats you like family. You might be a
> charming human noble, a Tiefling with a crown of bone, or a black-furred puma
> whose eyes are two embers — the crown is in the blood, not the face.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Fiend
- **Size:** Medium or Small (your choice)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

### Hellborn Hide
You have **Resistance to Fire and Necrotic damage**, and you don't need to eat,
drink, or breathe (you may still do so for pleasure).

### Tyrant's Presence
You radiate the weight of a ruler. As a **Bonus Action**, each creature of your
choice within **15 feet** that can see you must succeed on a **Wisdom saving
throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier)** or be
**Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.** On a failed save, that
creature also takes **Psychic damage equal to your proficiency bonus.**
You can use this a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus per Long Rest.**

### Crown Command
Once per turn, when a creature **Frightened by you** is hit by an attack (yours or
an ally's), it takes **extra Necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus.**
Starting at **level 5**, when you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you regain **HP
equal to your character level** and your Tyrant's Presence refreshes one use.

### Born to Rule
You have **Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks** and on **saving throws
against being Charmed or Frightened.** Fiends and creatures of CR lower than your
proficiency bonus have **Disadvantage on attack rolls against you** unless they
first succeed on a Wisdom save against your Tyrant's Presence DC.

### Languages
Common, Abyssal or Infernal, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** A no-attack-roll Bonus-Action fear cone that *also* deals
> damage and sets up Crown Command is brutal action economy. Lean tables: make
> Tyrant's Presence cost an Action, or shrink it to a single target.
