# Stormborn

*Concept Species — "Lightning given a heartbeat and told to walk. It walks fast."*

> Children of the storm — speed, thunder, and a temper that arrives as weather. A
> wind-burned human courier, a Centaur that runs like a thunderhead, or a lean
> grey puma that moves between two points without seeming to cross the space
> between. They do not stand still well.

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Elemental (or Humanoid, your choice)
- **Size:** Medium or Small
- **Speed:** **40 ft.**

### Storm-Bodied
You have **Resistance to Lightning and Thunder damage.** You ignore Difficult
Terrain while you Dash, and **Opportunity Attacks against you are made with
Disadvantage** (you're never quite where the blow lands).

### Thunderstep Strikes
Once per turn when you hit a creature, you deal **extra Lightning or Thunder
damage equal to your proficiency bonus.** If you **moved at least 20 ft. before the
attack**, this damage is **doubled** and the target makes a **Constitution save
(DC 8 + PB + Dex mod)** or can't take Reactions until the start of your next turn.

### Flash Step
As a **Bonus Action**, your Speed **doubles until the end of your turn**, and this
movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks. Usable **PB times per Long Rest.**
At **level 5**, the first time you Flash Step each turn you can make **one weapon
attack** as part of it.

### Ride the Lightning
**PB times per Long Rest**, when you take Lightning or Thunder damage, or when a
creature within 30 ft. casts a spell, you can use your **Reaction** to dissolve
into a bolt and **reappear in any unoccupied space within 60 ft.**, taking no
damage from that triggering effect if it was a single-target one aimed at you.

### Eye of the Storm
You have **Advantage on Initiative** and on **Dexterity saving throws**, and you
can't be Surprised. The first turn of each combat, your Speed is **doubled** and
your attacks have **Advantage** (you start before the thunder does).

### Languages
Common, Auran, and one other of your choice.

---

> **Power note:** Best-in-class mobility, a movement-keyed damage rider, a
> reactive 60-ft. teleport, and **Advantage on attacks turn one**. Lean tables:
> limit Eye of the Storm's attack Advantage to your first attack only.
