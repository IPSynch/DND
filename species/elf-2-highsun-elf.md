# Highsun Elf

*Elven Species — built-in **advantage** engine #2 (the mark & the charge)*

> Elves of the open field and the noon duel, who fight in plain sight and never
> blink first. A golden-armored elven knight, a sun-bronzed Centaur-elf, or a
> sand-colored puma that picks one target and simply does not lose it. They burn
> their quarry into focus and then never miss it.

**Elf lineage:** This species counts as an **Elf** for any feature, feat, or item
that requires you to be an Elf or Half-Elf (including the **Elven Accuracy** feat).

---

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Elf)
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** **35 ft.**

### Fey Ancestry
You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed**, magic can't put you
to sleep, and you Trance 4 hours for a Long Rest's benefit.

### Sunsight
You have **Darkvision 60 ft.** and **Advantage on saving throws against being
Blinded**, and you ignore the effects of dazzling or bright light.

### Advantage Engine — Sun's Mark
As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature you can see within 60 ft. (the *Quarry*)
for 1 minute or until it drops; you can move the mark to a new target as a Bonus
Action. **You have Advantage on all attack rolls against your Quarry**, and the
first time you hit it each turn you deal **extra radiant damage equal to your
proficiency bonus.** Only one creature can be your Quarry at a time, and the mark
ends if it leaves your line of sight for a full round. (With **Elven Accuracy**,
your marked target is a guaranteed reroll every turn.)

### Charging Light
Immediately **after you Dash or move at least 20 ft. toward a creature**, you have
**Advantage on the first attack roll** you make against it that turn even if it
isn't your Quarry. If that attack hits, you can't be made to lose the advantage
from Sun's Mark by being Blinded or by the target turning invisible until the
start of your next turn (you've locked on).

### Field Discipline
You have proficiency in **Athletics and Perception**, **Advantage on Perception
checks relying on sight**, and you ignore Difficult Terrain when you Dash.

### Languages
Common, Elvish, and one other of your choice.

---

> **How it builds Elven Accuracy:** Sun's Mark gives **permanent, mobile advantage
> on a chosen target** with no positioning required — the most consistent
> advantage source of the three, ideal for a ranged or charging Dex attacker.
> Lean tables: drop the per-turn radiant rider.
