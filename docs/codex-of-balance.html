The Codex of Balance

How the scales were weighed

Each axis is scored 1–5 and the four are averaged for the rank within a class. Offense = damage & killing control; Defense = AC, resistances, damage reduction, THP; Healing = restoring Hit Points to self or party; Utility = mobility, hard control, versatility, and out-of-combat reach.

Read the average as "how complete," not "how powerful." A pure blaster that scores 1 on Healing is not weak — it is specialized. Well-rounded toolkits (Humoral, Carbon, Coitus) rise to the top precisely because they refuse to leave a scale empty; dedicated specialists (Chronomantic, Beguiling, Dominion, Nourishment) sit lower on average while dominating their one axis. The archetype tells you the role; the average tells you the spread.

Tiers: S = 4.25+  ·  A = 3.75–4.24  ·  B = 3.25–3.74  ·  C = below 3.25. All scores assume the applied dice nerf (signature dice open at 1d8 at level 3).
