The Codex of Balance

Forty-five homebrew subclasses weighed on four scales — Offense, Defense, Healing, and Utility — each scored 1–5, then ranked within its own class by the average. Every subclass earns an archetype and a verdict: hold, nerf, or buff.

Revised edition Reflects recent balance passes — a Sinew overhaul (Muscle die, flat damage reduction, ~2× Con) and Frenzy tweak, earlier buffs/nerfs, and the renames Dung → Fecal and Coitus → Sex. Rows shaded gold were changed by a balance pass; signature dice open at 1d8 at level 3.

How the scales were weighed

Each axis is scored 1–5 and the four are averaged for the rank within a class. Offense = damage & killing control; Defense = AC, resistances, damage reduction, THP; Healing = restoring Hit Points; Utility = mobility, hard control, versatility, out-of-combat reach.

Read the average as "how complete," not "how powerful." A pure blaster that scores 1 on Healing is specialized, not weak. Well-rounded toolkits (Humoral, Carbon, Sex) top the tables because they refuse to leave a scale empty; specialists (Chronomantic, Beguiling, Dominion, Nourishment) sit lower on average while dominating their one axis.

This revision applied 6 buffs and 2 nerfs. Euphoric, Ascendant, Sundering, Foul Gut, Gallows, and Fecal were buffed; Sex (Mend) and Lymph (Flush) were nerfed. The floor came up: Sundering and Fecal both climbed out of the basement, so no subclass is a clear trap pick anymore.
