# The Visceralist

*A Base Class — the Body Unleashed*

Most people spend their lives politely pretending they aren't a wet sack of blood, gas, and
filth held together by skin. The Visceralist made peace with the truth and then made a weapon of
it. You don't borrow power from gods or the Weave — you *wring it out of yourself*, spilling your
own blood to mend a friend, farting a wall of choking gas, packing your own filth into a bomb,
and jumping the length of a battlefield on legs you swelled to bursting. You are gross. You are
usually mostly naked, because clothes just get ruined. And you are far, far harder to kill than
anything this disgusting has any right to be.

> **Tone.** Vulgar and gross by design, written disgusting-but-not-explicit. See
> [Fluidic Abilities](./fluidic-abilities.md) for the shared casting rules and ability list.

## Class features (quick reference)

- **Hit Point Die:** d12 per Visceralist level
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** Constitution and Wisdom
- **Skill Proficiencies:** choose **any 4** skills (free assignment)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons
- **Armor Training:** none (you rely on Unarmored Defense)
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Constitution (Fluidic Casting)

## Level 1: Fluidic Casting

You cast **Fluidic Abilities** using **Constitution**, paying for them with your own Hit Points.
All the rules — DC, HP costs, the can't-drop-below-1 clause, Counterspell interaction, and the
ability list — are in [Fluidic Abilities](./fluidic-abilities.md). You know **2** Fluidic
Abilities and **4** cantrips to start; both grow as shown on the table.

## Level 1: Unarmored Defense

While you wear no armor and no Shield, your base Armor Class equals **10 + your Constitution
modifier + your Dexterity modifier**. (Most Visceralists fight near-naked; cloth only slows the
spill.)

## Level 1: Bottomless Meat

There is simply *more of you* than there should be. Your Hit Point maximum increases by **1 for
each Visceralist level** you have (this keeps pace as you gain levels, giving you a deep well to
burn for casting).

## Level 2: Gorge & Clot

Your body mends itself faster than it should, gorging on its own reserves and clotting shut in
seconds — the engine that makes bleeding yourself for power survivable. You gain both:

- **Sanguine Reserve.** You have a pool of healing equal to **10 × your Visceralist level**,
  which refreshes when you finish a Long Rest. As a **Bonus Action**, you can draw from the pool
  to regain any number of Hit Points left in it (all at once or spread across many turns).
- **Clotting (passive).** At the start of each of your turns, if you are below half your Hit
  Point maximum, you regain Hit Points equal to **your Constitution modifier + half your
  Visceralist level** (rounded down).

## Level 2: Rut (Primed Metabolism)

As a **Bonus Action**, you flood yourself with primed, over-rich fluids for **1 minute**. You can
do this **twice**, and you regain all expended uses on a Long Rest (this increases to **3 uses**
at 10th level and **4 uses** at 18th level). While in **Rut**:

- Your **Fluidic spell save DC increases by 1**.
- You have **Advantage on Constitution saving throws** (including to maintain Concentration).
- Your **subclass (Visceral Discipline)** grants an additional passive benefit and a special
  Bonus Action that function **only while you are in Rut**.

## Level 7: Iron Homeostasis

Your body self-corrects against anything that tries to disrupt it. You add your **Constitution
modifier** (minimum +1) to **all saving throws you make** (this stacks with the fact that
Constitution saves already use it). This is a personal safeguard only — it never extends to
allies.

## Level 14: Feral Efficiency

Your lowest tricks cost you nothing and flow like breathing. You gain both:

- **Effortless Fluids (Spell Mastery).** You can cast any Fluidic Ability or spell of **1st
  level that you know for 0 Hit Points**, and you can cast such an ability using either an
  **Action or a Bonus Action** interchangeably (subject to the normal one-leveled-ability-per-
  turn limit until 20th level).
- **Perfected Metabolism.** You are **immune to poison damage and the Poisoned condition**,
  immune to disease, and you no longer need to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep (you recycle
  yourself endlessly; you still gain the benefits of a Long Rest with 4 hours of light activity).

## Level 20: Apex Organism

You become the perfect, self-devouring machine. You gain the following benefits:

- **Boundless Body.** Your Constitution score increases by 4, to a maximum of **26**. You have
  **Resistance to all damage** you take from your own Fluidic Abilities, and you can't be
  reduced below 1 Hit Point by any effect other than being reduced to 0 directly (you always get
  to keep fighting from 1).
- **Overflowing Output.** You can cast **two leveled Fluidic Abilities on the same turn**
  (for example, one with your Action and one with a Bonus Action). In addition, **once on each of
  your turns, one leveled Fluidic Ability you cast costs 0 Hit Points**.
- **Undying Meat.** You have **Regeneration**: at the start of each of your turns you regain
  **20 Hit Points** if you have at least 1. When you drop to 0 Hit Points but aren't killed
  outright, you can choose to instead drop to 1 (once per Long Rest, no action required).
