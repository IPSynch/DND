# The Secretionist

*A Base Class — the Body as a Chemistry Set*

Where others see a person, you see a laboratory that walks: a network of glands, ducts, and vats
brewing a hundred reagents at once, waiting for a mind clever enough to weaponize them. You are
that mind. You titrate your own blood into a healing draught, distill acid in your gut and spit
it, secrete a hardening resin, brew a cloud of engineered gas, and dose your allies with tailored
fluids that make them stronger. It is grotesque, meticulous, and unnervingly *precise* — and,
because your reagents seep and spray and evaporate off bare skin best, you tend to work with very
little on.

> **Tone.** Vulgar and clinical-gross by design, written disgusting-but-not-explicit. See
> [Fluidic Abilities](./fluidic-abilities.md) for the shared casting rules and ability list.

## Class features (quick reference)

- **Hit Point Die:** d12 per Secretionist level
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** Constitution and Wisdom
- **Skill Proficiencies:** choose **any 4** skills (free assignment)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons
- **Armor Training:** none (you rely on Unarmored Defense)
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Constitution (Fluidic Casting)

## Level 1: Fluidic Casting

You cast **Fluidic Abilities** using **Constitution**, paying for them with your own Hit Points.
All the rules — DC, HP costs, the can't-drop-below-1 clause, Counterspell interaction, and the
ability list — are in [Fluidic Abilities](./fluidic-abilities.md). You know **2** Fluidic
Abilities and **4** cantrips to start; both grow as shown on the table.

## Level 1: Unarmored Defense

While you wear no armor and no Shield, your base Armor Class equals **10 + your Constitution
modifier + your Dexterity modifier**. (Reagents need skin to weep from; armor only gets in the
way.)

## Level 1: Rich Biomass

You cultivate a deep, over-produced reserve of raw material. Your Hit Point maximum increases by
**1 for each Secretionist level** you have, giving you an ever-deeper well to draw on when you
pay for your chemistry in blood.

## Level 2: Transfusion

You can cycle your own biomass back into yourself on demand — the process that lets you spend Hit
Points as fuel without bleeding out. You gain both:

- **Sanguine Reserve.** You have a pool of healing equal to **10 × your Secretionist level**,
  which refreshes when you finish a Long Rest. As a **Bonus Action**, you can draw from the pool
  to regain any number of Hit Points left in it (all at once or spread across many turns).
- **Autoregulation (passive).** At the start of each of your turns, if you are below half your
  Hit Point maximum, you regain Hit Points equal to **your Constitution modifier + half your
  Secretionist level** (rounded down).

## Level 2: Glandular Surge (Primed Metabolism)

As a **Bonus Action**, you kick every gland into overdrive for **1 minute**. You can do this
**twice**, and you regain all expended uses on a Long Rest (this increases to **3 uses** at 10th
level and **4 uses** at 18th level). While surging:

- Your **Fluidic spell save DC increases by 1**.
- You have **Advantage on Constitution saving throws** (including to maintain Concentration).
- Your **subclass (Secretory Specialization)** grants an additional passive benefit and a special
  Bonus Action that function **only while you are surging**.

## Level 7: Chemical Homeostasis

Your internal chemistry auto-corrects against anything that would throw it off balance. You add
your **Constitution modifier** (minimum +1) to **all saving throws you make** (this stacks with
Constitution saves already using it). This is a personal safeguard only — it never extends to
allies.

## Level 14: Refined Secretions

Your simplest formulas become effortless and endlessly repeatable. You gain both:

- **At-Will Reagents (Spell Mastery).** You can cast any Fluidic Ability or spell of **1st level
  that you know for 0 Hit Points**, and you can cast such an ability using either an **Action or a
  Bonus Action** interchangeably (subject to the normal one-leveled-ability-per-turn limit until
  20th level).
- **Purified Body.** You are **immune to poison damage and the Poisoned condition**, immune to
  disease, and you no longer need to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep (your chemistry sustains
  itself; you still gain the benefits of a Long Rest with 4 hours of light activity).

## Level 17: Refined Distillation

Your intermediate formulas become free-flowing as well. You can cast any Fluidic Ability or spell
of **2nd level that you know for 0 Hit Points**, and you can cast such an ability using either an
**Action or a Bonus Action** interchangeably (subject to the normal one-leveled-ability-per-turn
limit until 20th level). This extends **At-Will Reagents** from Refined Secretions, so 1st- and
2nd-level abilities are now both free.

## Level 20: Perfect Chemistry

Your body becomes a flawless, inexhaustible reactor. You gain the following benefits:

- **Optimized Vessel.** Your Constitution score increases by 4, to a maximum of **26**.
- **Catalytic Output.** You can cast **two leveled Fluidic Abilities on the same turn** (for
  example, one with your Action and one with a Bonus Action). In addition, **once on each of your
  turns, a Fluidic Ability of 3rd level or lower that you cast costs 0 Hit Points** (higher-level
  abilities still cost Hit Points as normal).
- **Self-Renewing.** You have **Regeneration**: at the start of each of your turns you regain
  **20 Hit Points** if you have at least 1 Hit Point. In addition, the **first time each round
  that you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points, you instead drop to 1 Hit Point**.
