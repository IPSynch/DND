# Fluidic Abilities — Shared Casting Rules & Ability List

Both the **Visceralist** and the **Secretionist** cast **Fluidic Abilities**: effects that
mimic ordinary spells but are produced by the caster's own body — blood, milk, seed, gas,
filth, acid, sweat, tissue, and worse. This file holds the rules and the ability list they
share; each class doc covers its own features.

> **Tone.** Deliberately vulgar and often gross, written disgusting-but-not-explicit. Reskin
> any name your table dislikes — the rules don't care whether you call it "seed," "milk," or
> "reagent."

## The core casting rules

- **Casting ability:** Constitution. **Spell save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
  Constitution modifier. **Ability attack modifier** = your Proficiency Bonus + your
  Constitution modifier.
- **No slots, no class list.** You don't prepare from a class spell list and you have no spell
  slots. Instead you **know** a number of Fluidic Abilities (see your class table: **1 + your
  level**) chosen from the **Fluidic Abilities list** below, and a number of **cantrips** chosen
  from the **Sorcerer** cantrip list. When you gain a level you can swap one known ability for
  another you qualify for.
- **Maximum ability level** is set by your class table (a full-caster curve — up to 9th-level
  abilities at 17th level). You can only learn a Fluidic Ability whose level you can cast.
- **HP is your fuel.**
  - **Cantrips cost no Hit Points.**
  - A **leveled Fluidic Ability costs (its level × your Proficiency Bonus) Hit Points**, paid the
    instant you cast it. This damage can't be reduced, prevented, or redirected.
  - **You can't be dropped below 1 Hit Point by this cost.** If an ability's HP cost *would*
    reduce you to 0 or below, the ability **still resolves in full**, you drop to **1 Hit Point**
    instead, and you gain **1 level of Exhaustion** as your body strips itself of the fluids,
    fat, and filth it needed — no matter how high-level the ability is.
- **Fluidic Abilities are spells** for every rules interaction: they can be **Counterspelled**,
  **Dispelled**, and **shut down by antimagic**, they trigger effects keyed to spells, and they
  follow their mimicked spell's rules for range, duration, and **Concentration** unless a class
  or subclass feature says otherwise.
- **Components.** Fluidic Abilities need no verbal or material components, but they are **blatant
  and bodily**: you must have your body free to flex, clench, and emit (equivalent to somatic
  components), so being fully Restrained or bound in a way that stops that prevents casting. They
  are always obvious — everyone can see (and smell) what you just did.
- **Two leveled abilities in a turn:** as normal for 2024, you can't cast two leveled abilities
  on the same turn until a feature (such as the level-20 capstone) says you can.

## The Fluidic Abilities list

A representative, curated list of spells reskinned as bodily effects. Your DM can approve other
spells reflavored to fit. **Mimics** is the spell each ability copies.

**Cantrips:** choose from the **Sorcerer cantrip list**, reflavored — e.g. *Acid Splash* as a
spat glob of bile, *Fire Bolt* as an ignited gas jet, *Mind Sliver* as a psychic musk.

### Blood Rush — 1st-level Fluidic Ability *(custom; replaces Cure Wounds)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Casting Time** | 1 Bonus Action |
| **Range / Area** | Self |
| **Components** | S |
| **Duration** | Instantaneous |
| **School** | Transmutation (Sangromancy) |

As part of casting this ability you must **expend one Hit Point Die**, or the casting
automatically fails. Roll that Hit Point Die **twice** and regain Hit Points equal to **the
total of both rolls plus your Constitution modifier**.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast Blood Rush as a higher-level ability, **expend one
additional Hit Point Die for each level above 1st**, rolling every expended die twice and adding
all the rolls to the total.

> **Why it replaces *Cure Wounds*.** Blood Rush keeps the Visceralist's at-will self-healing but
> **caps it to your pool of Hit Point Dice**, so you can no longer spam a cheap heal out of
> combat into effective immortality. It heals **only you**.
