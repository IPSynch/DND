# Warrior of the Inherited Flame

*Monastic Order — Heroic Powerhouse / Inheritor*

> The power was not born in you. It was *given* — handed down a long chain of those
> who carried it before, each one pouring their strength into the next, so that what
> rests in you now is the gathered will of everyone who ever held it. You feel them
> when you clench your fist: a stockpile of borrowed might that you can pour into a
> single blow, or release all at once until your whole body blazes beyond every
> human limit. With it comes a duty heavier than the power itself — to be the one who
> stands smiling between the innocent and the dark, the symbol everyone can look to
> and know, in their bones, that everything is going to be fine.

A heroic juggernaut built on **inherited power** poured through one body. You meter
out percentages of a vast reserve — tapping a little for steady excellence, or
"going beyond" into a full-power state that hits like a natural disaster, with a
recoil cost that scales with how hard you push.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. **Full Cowl** is a stance; "Output" features let you spend extra Focus Points
> to push past your limits, sometimes at a cost to yourself.

---

## One For All — Full Cowl (Level 3)

*Don't pour it all into one fist. Spread the gift across your whole body — and stay
in control.*

- **Full Cowl.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to wreath yourself
  in arcs of borrowed power for **1 minute** (no Concentration). While active: your
  **Speed increases by 15 feet**, you have **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws and
  Athletics/Acrobatics checks**, and your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **MA die**.
- **Detroit Smash (passive).** Once per turn while Full Cowl is active, when you hit a
  creature you can push it up to **15 feet** and, if it strikes a wall or another
  creature, deal it (and that creature) an extra **MA die** of damage from the impact.
- **Smash It Up (passive).** Your Unarmed Strikes ignore **Resistance** to their
  damage type and can damage objects and structures as a siege weapon would.

---

## Going Beyond (Level 6)

*Plus Ultra. When good enough isn't, push past the line — and pay for it after.*

- **Output Control.** While Full Cowl is active, when you take the Attack action you
  can **overclock one Unarmed Strike** by spending **1 to 3 Focus Points**. That
  strike deals an extra **1 MA die per Focus Point** spent and, on a hit, the target
  makes a **Constitution save** or is knocked **Prone** and pushed 10 feet per point.
  If you spend **3**, you take **2d6 Force damage** as the power recoils through your
  arm — true strength always costs the wielder something.
- **Shoot Style (passive).** Your reach with Unarmed Strikes increases by **5 feet**,
  and you can make Unarmed Strikes with kicks at range **15 feet** (whip-like blasts
  of pressurized air) as part of the Attack action.
- **Symbol of Peace (passive).** Allies within **30 feet** of you have **Advantage on
  saving throws against being Frightened**. People are not afraid when you are
  smiling.

---

## One Hundred Percent (Level 11)

*The full inheritance, channeled through a body that can — barely — hold it.*

- **Full Power (passive).** While Full Cowl is active, your Unarmed Strikes deal an
  extra **2 MA dice** (replacing the level 3 die), and you make **one additional
  Unarmed Strike** whenever you take the Attack action.
- **United States of Smash.** As your action, spend **3 Focus Points** to throw your
  full weight into one annihilating blow against a creature within reach (or a
  15-foot Line). It takes **6 MA dice + your Wisdom modifier** of Bludgeoning or Force
  damage, or **half** on a **Constitution save**, and is knocked **Prone** and pushed
  **30 feet**; everything in the Line behind it is caught too. You then take **3d6
  Force damage** of recoil and your Full Cowl benefits are reduced by half until the
  start of your next turn.
- **Reckless Inheritance (passive).** When you score a critical hit or reduce a
  creature to 0 HP, you **regain 1 Focus Point**. The will of those before you presses
  you onward.

---

## The Next Symbol (Level 17)

*One hundred… no. Beyond. For these few seconds, you are everything the chain of
heroes before you ever hoped you would become.*

As a **Bonus Action**, spend **5 Focus Points** to go **Beyond** for **1 minute** (no
Concentration). While Beyond:

- Your **Speed doubles** and you gain a matching **flying Speed**; you have
  **Resistance to all damage**; and you **automatically succeed on Strength and
  Dexterity saving throws**.
- Your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **3 MA dice**, **critically hit on 18–20**, and
  each hit can apply **Detroit Smash** and one **Output Control** effect for free
  (no Focus Point cost and no recoil).
- Once per turn you may unleash a finisher — one creature and everything in a
  **20-foot-radius** Sphere takes **10d12 Force damage**, or **half** on a
  **Constitution save**, is knocked **Prone**, and is **Stunned** until the end of your
  next turn on a failure. Structures in the area are leveled.

When **Beyond** ends, you gain **2 levels of Exhaustion** — the body can only borrow
so much before the bill comes due. You can go Beyond once per **Long Rest**.

---

### Designer notes

- The throughline is **risk-metered output**: Full Cowl is the safe, always-on
  baseline; Going Beyond and One Hundred Percent let the player gamble HP and Focus
  for spikes; The Next Symbol is the all-in finale with a real recoil cost (2
  Exhaustion). Keep the **recoil and Exhaustion** — they are the only thing stopping
  this from being strictly better than every other striker.
- **Reckless Inheritance** (level 11) is the sustain valve that makes the expensive
  Output features affordable in a long fight; without it the kit runs dry fast, which
  is the intended pre-11 texture.
- Tuning lever: if the level 17 **10d12 finisher + Stun + all-damage Resistance** is
  too much for your boss math, drop the finisher to 8d12 or make Beyond last 6 rounds
  instead of a full minute.
