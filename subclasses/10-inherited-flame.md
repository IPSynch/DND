# Warrior of the Inherited Flame

*Monk Subclass*

The power was not born in you. It was given — handed down a long chain of those who carried
it before, each one pouring their strength into the next, so that what rests in you now is
the gathered will of everyone who ever held it. You feel them when you clench your fist: a
stockpile of borrowed might you can pour into a single blow, or release all at once until
your whole body blazes past every human limit. With it comes a duty heavier than the power
itself — to be the one who stands smiling between the innocent and the dark, the symbol
everyone can look to and know, in their bones, that everything is going to be fine.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom
modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You spend **Focus
Points** (called Ki in some traditions). **Full Cowl** is a stance; "Output" features let you
push past your limits at a cost to yourself.*

## Level 3: One For All — Full Cowl

Don't pour it all into one fist. Spread the gift across your whole body, and stay in control.

***Full Cowl.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to wreath yourself in arcs of
borrowed power. This stance lasts for 1 minute. It ends early if you fall Unconscious or if you
end it (no action required). While it is active, your Speed increases by 15 feet, you have
Advantage on Dexterity saving throws and on Strength (Athletics) and Dexterity (Acrobatics)
checks, and your Unarmed Strikes deal extra damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die.

***Detroit Smash.*** Once on each of your turns while Full Cowl is active, when you hit a
creature with an Unarmed Strike you can push it up to 15 feet. If it collides with a wall or
another creature, it (and that creature) takes extra damage equal to one roll of your Martial
Arts die.

***Smash It Up.*** Your Unarmed Strikes ignore Resistance to their damage type and can damage
objects and structures as though you wielded a siege weapon.

## Level 6: Going Beyond

Plus Ultra. When good enough isn't, you push past the line — and pay for it afterward.

***Output Control.*** While Full Cowl is active, when you take the Attack action you can
overclock one Unarmed Strike, spending 1 to 3 Focus Points. That strike deals extra damage
equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die per Focus Point spent, and on a hit the target must
succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be knocked Prone and pushed 10 feet per Focus Point.
If you spend 3 Focus Points, the power recoils through your arm and you take 2d6 Force damage.

***Shoot Style.*** Your reach with Unarmed Strikes increases by 5 feet, and as part of the
Attack action you can make Unarmed Strikes at a range of 15 feet with whip-like blasts of
pressurized air.

***Symbol of Peace.*** Allies within 30 feet of you have Advantage on saving throws against the
Frightened condition. People are not afraid when you are smiling.

## Level 11: One Hundred Percent

The full inheritance, channeled through a body that can — barely — hold it.

***Full Power.*** While Full Cowl is active, your Unarmed Strikes deal extra damage equal to two
rolls of your Martial Arts die (this replaces the extra die from Full Cowl), and you can make
one additional Unarmed Strike whenever you take the Attack action.

***United States of Smash.*** As an action, you can spend 3 Focus Points to throw your full
weight into one annihilating blow against a creature within reach, or against every creature in
a 15-foot-long Line. The target takes Bludgeoning or Force damage equal to six rolls of your
Martial Arts die plus your Wisdom modifier, or half as much on a successful Constitution saving
throw, and is knocked Prone and pushed 30 feet on a failure. You then take 3d6 Force damage,
and your Full Cowl benefits are halved until the start of your next turn.

***Reckless Inheritance.*** When you score a critical hit or reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points,
you regain 1 Focus Point (you can't exceed your maximum).

## Level 17: The Next Symbol

One hundred… no. Beyond. For these few seconds, you are everything the chain of heroes before
you ever hoped you would become.

As a Bonus Action, you can spend 5 Focus Points to go Beyond. This state lasts for 1 minute. It
ends early if you fall Unconscious or if you end it (no action required). While you are Beyond:

- Your Speed doubles and you gain a matching Fly Speed, you have Resistance to all damage, and
  you automatically succeed on Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- Your Unarmed Strikes deal extra damage equal to three rolls of your Martial Arts die and score
  a critical hit on a roll of 18–20, and each hit can apply Detroit Smash and one Output Control
  effect for free (no Focus Point cost and no recoil).
- Once on each of your turns, you can unleash a finisher: one creature you can see, and each
  creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere around it, takes 10d12 Force damage, or half as much on a
  successful Constitution saving throw, and on a failure is knocked Prone and has the Stunned
  condition until the end of your next turn. Structures in the area are leveled.

When this state ends, you gain two levels of Exhaustion. You can go Beyond once per Long Rest.

---

### Designer Notes

- The throughline is risk-metered output: Full Cowl is the safe, always-on baseline; Going Beyond
  and One Hundred Percent let you gamble Hit Points and Focus for spikes; The Next Symbol is the
  all-in finale with a real recoil cost. Keep the recoil and the Exhaustion — they're the only
  thing stopping this from being strictly better than every other striker.
- Reckless Inheritance (Level 11) is the sustain valve that makes the expensive Output features
  affordable in a long fight. If the Level 17 finisher is too much for your boss math, drop it to
  8d12 or make Beyond last six rounds instead of a full minute.
