# Oath of Dominion

*The Tyrant — Rule by Right of Will*

You believe, to the marrow, that some are born to command and the rest to obey — and that
you are the former. Your oath is to **order**: your order, imposed by a will so total that
lesser minds bend to it like grass in wind. You do not ask. You decree, and the world
rearranges itself to comply. Crowns are for men who need a symbol; your authority is written
directly onto the spines of everyone who kneels.

*An overtly authoritarian, usually-evil oath of compulsion and command.*

## Level 3: Imperious Presence

Your voice carries the weight of command. You add your **Charisma modifier** (already core to a
Paladin) and your **Proficiency Bonus** to Charisma (Intimidation) checks, and you have
**Advantage** on them against creatures that can see and hear you. When a creature within 30 feet
of you that can hear you makes an attack against an ally, you can use your **Reaction** to bark a
command: the attacker makes a **Wisdom saving throw** against your spell save DC, and on a failure
its attack is redirected to target **you** instead (if it can reach you), or it has **Disadvantage**
on the attack if it cannot.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Crown of Command

As a Bonus Action, you assert your dominion for 1 minute. For the duration:

- You radiate authority: enemies within 30 feet that can perceive you have **Disadvantage on
  saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened by you**.
- Once on each of your turns (no action required), you can issue a one-word **Command** (as the
  spell, no slot) to a creature within 30 feet that can hear you; it makes a **Wisdom saving
  throw** or obeys on its next turn.
- You gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Paladin level** at the start of each of your turns
  while at least one enemy is Charmed or Frightened by you.

## Level 7: Aura of Submission

While your **Aura of Protection** is active, you and each ally in it have **Advantage on saving
throws against being Charmed or Frightened** and against being moved against their will.

In addition, an enemy in the aura that tries to move **away from you** must spend **1 extra foot
of movement for every foot moved** (the aura is Difficult Terrain for fleeing it), and a Charmed
or Frightened enemy in the aura has its **Speed halved** and **can't take Reactions**.

## Level 15: Will of the Sovereign

Your commands sink deeper. When a creature fails a saving throw against your **Command** (from
Crown of Command) or your enchantment spells, you can have it also take **Psychic damage equal to
your Paladin level**. Once per turn, when an enemy you can see within 30 feet would make an attack,
move, or cast a spell, you can use your **Reaction** to attempt to forbid it: it makes a **Wisdom
saving throw**, and on a failure that action is wasted (it loses that action, Bonus Action, or
Reaction). You can do this a number of times per Long Rest equal to your **Charisma modifier**.

## Level 20: Enthroned

As a Bonus Action, you become an avatar of absolute rule for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- Your **Crown of Command** is active for free, and your one-word **Command** can be issued to
  **every enemy within 30 feet** at once on each of your turns.
- Once on each of your turns as a Magic action, you can attempt to **dominate** one creature you
  can see within 60 feet (as *Dominate Monster*, no concentration, lasting until the end of your
  next turn; a creature that fails by 5 or more is dominated for **1 minute**, repeating the save
  at the end of each of its turns).
- You are **immune to the Charmed and Frightened conditions**, and any creature currently Charmed,
  Frightened, or dominated by you has **Disadvantage on all saving throws**.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a
5th-level spell slot to use it again.
