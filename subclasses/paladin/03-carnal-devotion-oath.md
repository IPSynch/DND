# Oath of Carnal Devotion

*The Bare Flesh — Pleasure as a Sacrament*

You took your oath naked and unashamed. Your creed is that the body is holy, that desire
is a divine current, and that there is no truer act of devotion than to give and receive
ecstasy. You stride onto the field wearing nothing but conviction — and conviction, it
turns out, turns aside blades better than steel. You overwhelm foes with raw allure until
their minds white out, you mend allies with a touch that sets the nerves alight, and at
the peak of your fervor you let go with a holy release that scatters your enemies senseless.

> **A note on tone.** A frank, bawdy, sex-positive oath, written tasteful rather than
> explicit. Reskin "release," "squirt," and "heat" to "radiant effluence," "spray," and
> "fervor" if your table prefers; the mechanics don't change.

## Oath Spells

| Paladin Level | Spells |
|---------------|--------|
| 3 | *Charm Person*, *Healing Word* |

| 13 | *Confusion*, *Dominate Beast* |
| 17 | *Dominate Person*, *Mass Cure Wounds* |

## Level 3: Bare and Unbound

You fight as you were born. You gain the following benefits:

- **Shameless Defense.** While you wear no armor and wield no Shield, your base Armor Class
  equals **10 + your Constitution modifier + your Charisma modifier**.
- **Loving Hands.** Whenever you restore Hit Points to a creature other than yourself with Lay
  on Hands or a spell, it also gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier**.

## Level 3: Rapturous Display

As a **Magic action**, you flood the senses of those before you. Choose a single creature
within 30 feet of you, or a **15-foot Cone** originating from you. Each target makes a
**Wisdom saving throw** against your spell save DC, taking Psychic damage on a failure (half
as much on a success). The damage is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level 9, **4d8** at level
13, and **5d8** at level 17 — this is your **Ardor die total**. A single creature that fails
also has the **Charmed** condition until the end of its next turn (it can't take Reactions and
has Disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you).

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Heat

As a Bonus Action, you enter a state of sacred arousal for 1 minute. For the duration, you gain
a special **Bonus Action — Release**, with which you let go of a holy squirt. Each time you use
it, choose one mode:

- **Drenching Spray.** A **15-foot Cone** of glistening release. Each creature in it makes a
  **Dexterity saving throw**, taking your **Ardor die total as Radiant** damage on a failure
  (half as much on a success).
- **Saturate (single target).** One creature you can see within 30 feet makes a **Constitution
  saving throw**. On a failure, it is **Slowed** (as the *Slow* spell) until the end of its next
  turn, drenched and sluggish.

While Heat is active, you also have **Resistance to Psychic damage** and **Advantage on
Charisma checks**, and the first time each turn you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points or land
your Rapturous Display, you regain Hit Points equal to your Paladin level.

## Level 7: Aura of Intoxication

You exude a heady, dizzying musk. While your **Aura of Protection** is active, you and each ally
in it have **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened** and gain
**Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of each of their turns**.

In addition, each enemy in the aura has **Disadvantage on saving throws against your Charmed
effects and your Psychic damage**, and once on each of your turns when an enemy in the aura fails
a saving throw against one of your features or spells, you or an ally within the aura regains
**Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier**.

## Level 15: Irresistible

Your presence is too much to bear. When a creature you can see within 30 feet hits you with an
attack, you can use your **Reaction** to force it to make a **Wisdom saving throw**; on a
failure, it takes your **Ardor die total** as Psychic damage and has the **Charmed** condition
until the end of its next turn, its aggression dissolving into longing. In addition, you are
**immune to the Charmed condition** and have **Resistance to Psychic damage**, and creatures
Charmed by you have **Disadvantage on saving throws** against your spells.

## Level 20: Avatar of Rapture

As a Bonus Action, you become a living embodiment of ecstasy for 1 minute. You gain the
following benefits:

- Your **Heat** Channel Divinity is active for free for the duration (and its Release Bonus
  Action improves: Drenching Spray becomes a **30-foot Cone**, and Saturate's Slow lasts **1
  minute**, with a repeated save at the end of each of the target's turns).
- When you use your **Rapturous Display**, it affects a **30-foot Cone or every enemy within 30
  feet** of you, and creatures that fail are Charmed for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end
  of each of their turns).
- You and each ally within 10 feet of you regain **Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier**
  at the start of each of your turns, and you have **Resistance to all damage**.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
