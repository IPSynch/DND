# Oath of the Demon Soul

*The Takeover — Holiness Worn as a Devil's Skin*

You made a pact at the altar of your own ruin: the power to protect what you love, paid
for with the shape of a fiend. When you call on your oath, your body answers in horns,
talons, and wings, and the longer the battle rages the deeper the demon sinks its hooks —
each passing moment swelling your strength, until the thing wearing your face is something
angels would flee. You are not evil. You simply refuse to lose, and you found the price
worth paying.

*Inspired by Mirajane Strauss's Satan Soul — a takeover that escalates the longer it runs.*

## Level 3: Fiendish Frame

Touched by the demon within, your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Charisma
modifier** damage of your choice of **Necrotic or Fire**, counts as magical, and reaches **10
feet**. While you aren't wearing heavy armor you have a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed but can't
end your turn aloft** unless you have another way to stay airborne (you may, while transformed —
see below).

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Demon Takeover

As a Bonus Action, you unleash the fiend in your blood, transforming for 1 minute (it ends early
if you're Incapacitated). While transformed:

- You sprout claws and wings: your Unarmed Strike die becomes **1d10**, you gain a **Fly Speed
  equal to your Speed** (and can hover), and you have **Resistance to Necrotic, Fire, and Poison
  damage**.
- You accrue **Demon Power**. You gain **1 Demon Power** when you transform, **1 more at the
  start of each of your turns**, and **1** whenever you score a critical hit or reduce a creature
  to 0 Hit Points. You can have a maximum of **10**.
- You add your current **Demon Power to the damage** of each weapon and Unarmed Strike you make,
  and your Armor Class increases by **+1 for every 4 Demon Power** you have (rounded down).

Demon Power resets to 0 when the transformation ends. You can extend an active transformation by
**1 minute** by spending another use of Channel Divinity (no action required), keeping your
accrued Demon Power.

## Level 7: Aura of Dread

The air around you curdles with menace, sheltering your own in the demon's shadow. While your
**Aura of Protection** is active, you and each ally in it have **Resistance to Necrotic and Fire
damage** and **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened**.

In addition, enemies that start their turn in the aura must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw**
or have the **Frightened** condition until the start of their next turn. While you are
transformed, a creature frightened this way also has **Disadvantage on the first attack roll** it
makes against you each turn.

## Level 15: Deepening Corruption

The demon gives more the further you let it in. While transformed:

- Your maximum **Demon Power increases to 15**, and you gain **2** (instead of 1) at the start of
  each of your turns.
- When you have **10 or more Demon Power**, your weapon and Unarmed attacks deal an extra **2d8**
  damage of your fiendish type and ignore **Resistance** to it.
- When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points while transformed, you can instead drop to **1 Hit Point**
  and lose half your Demon Power (rounded up); you can do this once per transformation.

## Level 20: Demon Sovereign

Your takeover becomes complete and monstrous. Your **Demon Takeover** gains the following while
active:

- You begin the transformation with **10 Demon Power**, your maximum becomes **20**, and you make
  **one additional attack** whenever you take the Attack action.
- Once on each of your turns, you can spend **5 Demon Power** to exhale ruin in a **30-foot Cone**:
  each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Necrotic or Fire damage equal
  to twice your Demon Power** on a failure (half as much on a success).
- You have **Immunity to Necrotic, Fire, and Poison damage** and to the Frightened and Charmed
  conditions.

Once you use **Demon Takeover** through this enhanced state, you can't transform again until you
finish a Long Rest unless you expend a 5th-level spell slot — but the takeover otherwise costs
Channel Divinity as normal.
