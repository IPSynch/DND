# Oath of the Iron Fist

*The Unarmed, Unarmored Bulwark — Fists of Justice*

You wear no plate and carry no blade. You never needed them. Your oath is carried in
your knuckles: that a righteous fist, thrown true, settles any argument, and that the body
a god gave you is the only weapon a real warrior requires. You wade into the worst of it
bare-handed and bare-chested, take the hits meant for everyone behind you, and answer with
a punch that folds steel and cracks stone.

*Inspired by Monkey D. Garp — a marine whose bare fists outclass any weapon.*

## Oath Spells

When you reach a Paladin level specified in the Oath of the Iron Fist Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Iron Body

You fight with your fists and trust your hide. You gain the following benefits:

- **Radiant Knuckles.** Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Strength or
  Charisma modifier (your choice) Radiant** damage instead of its normal damage. This die
  increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at 11th level, and **2d6** at 17th level. Your
  Unarmed Strikes count as **magical** for overcoming Resistance and Immunity.
- **Flurry.** You can make **one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action** on your turn (you don't need
  to have taken the Attack action first).
- **Bare-Knuckle Defense.** While you aren't wearing armor or wielding a Shield, your base
  Armor Class equals **10 + your Constitution modifier + your Charisma modifier**.

You can use your Unarmed Strikes for **Divine Smite** and any feature that requires a melee
weapon attack, treating them as melee weapon attacks.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Fighting Spirit

As a Bonus Action, you flood your body with righteous power for 1 minute. For the duration:

- Your Unarmed Strike die increases by one step along the track **1d8 → 1d10 → 1d12 → 2d6 →
  2d8** (so at 17th level, where Radiant Knuckles is already 2d6, it becomes **2d8**), and you
  add your **Charisma modifier** to the **damage** of every Unarmed Strike (in addition to the
  ability modifier already in the strike).
- When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you can **push it up to 10 feet** away or
  knock it **Prone** (no save) the first time you hit it on each of your turns.
- You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**.

## Level 7: Aura of the Vanguard

You plant yourself between your allies and harm. While your **Aura of Protection** is active,
you and each ally in it have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from
nonmagical attacks** and **can't be knocked Prone or moved against their will** by enemies.

In addition, when an ally within the aura is hit by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to
take **half** of that damage instead (the ally takes the other half), and you can immediately
make **one Unarmed Strike** against the attacker if it is within your reach.

## Level 15: Unbreakable

Punishment only makes you angrier. You gain the following two benefits:

- **Too Angry to Die.** While you have at least 1 Hit Point, you can't be reduced to fewer Hit
  Points than your Paladin level by a single hit.
- **Righteous Retort.** Whenever you take damage, your next Unarmed Strike before the end of your
  next turn deals **extra Radiant damage equal to your Paladin level**. You also gain **Advantage
  on saving throws** against being moved, knocked Prone, Grappled, Restrained, Stunned, or
  Paralyzed.

## Level 20: Galaxy-Splitting Fist

As a Bonus Action, you become a living siege engine for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- Each of your Unarmed Strikes deals an **extra 2d10 Radiant** damage on a hit (this bonus
  stacks on top of your normal Unarmed Strike damage), and you make **one additional Unarmed
  Strike** whenever you take the Attack action.
- Once on each of your turns, when you take the Attack action, you can replace **one** of those
  attacks with a single, world-shaking punch: every creature of your choice in a **60-foot line**
  originating from you makes a **Strength saving throw**, taking **6d10 Radiant** damage and being
  knocked Prone on a failure (half damage and no Prone on a success).
- You have **Resistance to all damage** while this is active.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
