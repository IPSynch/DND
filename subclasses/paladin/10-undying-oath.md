# Oath of the Undying

*The Revenant — The Oath That Death Could Not End*

You died. Somewhere in the cold you found a vow still burning and you held onto it, and it
dragged you back. Now your oath is refusal itself: you will not fall, you will not stay down,
and you will not let death take what you have sworn to keep — not your charge, not your
allies, and not, inconveniently for your enemies, your own corpse. You are grave-cold and
grimly patient. They can kill you all they like. You have stopped finding it persuasive.

*A grim, undead-flavored tank — a wall that resurrects itself.*

## Level 3: Grave-Cold Flesh

Death clings to you and shields you. You have **Resistance to Necrotic damage**, you have
**Advantage on saving throws against being reduced to 0 Hit Points by an effect that would also
kill you outright**, and you automatically **stabilize** if you begin your turn dying. Your
Unarmed Strike and weapon attacks can deal **Necrotic** damage instead of their normal type (your
choice), and when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to
your Paladin level**.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Deathless Vigil

As a Bonus Action (or as a **Reaction** when you are reduced to 0 Hit Points), you deny your own
death for 1 minute. While it lasts:

- When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you instead drop to **1 Hit
  Point** the first time each round.
- You **regenerate Hit Points equal to your Paladin level** at the start of each of your turns
  (so long as you have at least 1 Hit Point).
- You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**, and you are immune to the
  **Frightened** condition and to disease.

## Level 7: Aura of the Wake

While your **Aura of Protection** is active, an ally in it that would drop to 0 Hit Points instead
drops to **1 Hit Point** the first time it would do so each round (the grave will not have them
while you stand). When an enemy in the aura deals damage to one of your allies, that enemy takes
**Necrotic damage equal to your Charisma modifier**.

## Level 15: Return

The first time you are reduced to 0 Hit Points (and aren't already saved by another feature) and
**Deathless Vigil is not active**, you can immediately rise: you stand with Hit Points equal to
**half your Hit Point maximum**, and each enemy within 10 feet of you takes **Necrotic damage
equal to your Paladin level** and is pushed 10 feet away. You regain the use of this feature when
you finish a Long Rest. In addition, you no longer need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe, and you
don't age.

## Level 20: The Oath Outlasts the Grave

As a Bonus Action, you become a thing death has given up on for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- You **cannot drop below 1 Hit Point** by any means, and you can't be killed outright.
- You **regenerate Hit Points equal to twice your Paladin level** at the start of each of your
  turns, and you have **Resistance to all damage** and **immunity to the Poisoned, Frightened,
  Paralyzed, and Stunned conditions**.
- When an enemy within 30 feet hits you or an ally in your aura, you can use your **Reaction** to
  lash out with grave-cold force, dealing **4d8 Necrotic** damage to that enemy and healing the
  ally (or yourself) for the same amount.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a
5th-level spell slot to use it again.
