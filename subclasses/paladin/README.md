# Homebrew Paladin Subclasses (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Ten original **Oaths** built on the 2024 *Player's Handbook* Paladin chassis. Each grants
**Oath Spells** (always prepared) at Paladin levels **3, 5, 9, 13, and 17**, a **Channel
Divinity** option at level 3 that powerfully buffs the paladin (in the spirit of Devotion's
*Sacred Weapon* and Vengeance's *Vow of Enmity*), and Oath features at levels **3, 7, 15, and
20**, matching the official subclass cadence.

These are tuned to be **strong**, as requested — high-octane, anime-flavored, and a touch above
official power budgets on purpose. Dial the dice and save DCs down a notch if your group runs
lean.

## Design conventions used throughout

- **Spell save DC** and **spell attack bonus** always mean your Paladin ones.
- **Channel Divinity** uses the 2024 pool (2 uses, regain 1 on a Short Rest and all on a Long
  Rest; 3 uses at level 11).
- **Aura of Protection** (level 6) and **Aura expansion to 30 feet** (level 18) are the base
  class features each Oath's auras build on; an Oath aura that says "while your Aura of Protection
  is active" simply rides on that same emanation.
- **Lay on Hands** is the base class pool (5 × your Paladin level).
- The level-20 capstones are deliberately big "boss-mode" buttons: a Bonus Action, 1-minute
  duration, once per Long Rest or by expending a 5th-level spell slot.
