# Oath of the Gallows

*The Executioner — Judge, Jury, and Headsman*

You are the sentence at the end of the trial. Your oath is not to mercy but to *finality*:
the guilty will be named, condemned, and ended, and no plea, no rank, and no running will
buy them a single day more. You mark your quarry with a death you have already decided on,
and from that moment everything you do bends toward the drop of the blade. Whether the law
you serve is just is a question you stopped asking long ago.

*A grim, grey oath — equally at home on a lawful headsman or a self-appointed killer.*

## Level 3: The Condemned

You bond bonus damage to those you have sentenced. When you hit a creature you have marked with
**Pronounce Sentence** (below) with an attack, it takes an extra **1d8 Necrotic** damage
(**2d8** at 11th level). If you reduce your Condemned target to 0 Hit Points, you regain a use of
**Channel Divinity** (once per Short or Long Rest).

**Mark of the Noose.** When a creature you can see drops to **fewer Hit Points than your Paladin
level**, you can mark it as Condemned for free (no action), and if it is already your Condemned
target and at fewer Hit Points than **your Paladin level + your Charisma modifier**, your next
hit against it is an automatic **critical hit**.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Pronounce Sentence

As a Bonus Action, you condemn a creature you can see within 30 feet for 1 minute (or until it
drops to 0 Hit Points or you condemn another). While it is your Condemned target:

- You have **Advantage on attack rolls** against it.
- It has **Disadvantage on saving throws against your spells and Smites**.
- The first time each turn it tries to move away from you, it must succeed on a **Wisdom saving
  throw** or its Speed becomes 0 until the start of its next turn — the gallows do not let go.

## Level 7: Aura of the Scaffold

While your **Aura of Protection** is active, your **Condemned target** and any enemy that starts
its turn in the aura while below half its Hit Point maximum must succeed on a **Wisdom saving
throw** or have the **Frightened** condition until the start of its next turn. A creature
Frightened this way has **Disadvantage on attack rolls against everyone but you** (it dares not
take its eyes off the headsman).

## Level 15: Public Execution

A death witnessed breaks the will of those who see it. When your **Condemned target** drops to 0
Hit Points, each enemy within 30 feet of it makes a **Wisdom saving throw** or becomes
**Frightened of you for 1 minute** (repeating the save at the end of each of its turns). You can
also now have your **Mark of the Noose** auto-crit trigger against any creature below **one
quarter** of its Hit Point maximum, Condemned or not.

## Level 20: Final Sentence

As a Bonus Action, you become inevitability made flesh for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- You can have **up to three Condemned targets** at once, and all benefits of The Condemned and
  Pronounce Sentence apply to each.
- Once on each of your turns, you can attempt an execution against a Condemned target within 30
  feet: it makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking **8d8 Necrotic** on a failure (half on a
  success); if this leaves it at **fewer Hit Points than your Paladin level × 3**, it is slain.
- You are **immune to the Frightened condition**, and whenever any creature dies within 30 feet
  of you, you regain **Hit Points equal to your Paladin level**.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
