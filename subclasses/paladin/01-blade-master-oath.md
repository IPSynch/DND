# Oath of the Blade Master

*The Peerless Sword — World's Strongest Swordsman*

You swore your oath to the blade itself. Not to a god, not to a cause — to the perfect
cut. You have crossed swords with every master worth the name and buried them, and the
discipline that took you there is its own creed: never look away, never strike twice
where one stroke serves, and let no distance be a refuge. With a flick of your wrist you
send a crescent of cutting force across a battlefield; the slash that splits a galleon's
hull at a hundred paces is the same slash that opens a throat at arm's reach.

*Inspired by Dracule Mihawk — a duelist whose melee is simply not bound by reach.*

## Oath Spells

When you reach a Paladin level specified in the Oath of the Blade Master Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

| Paladin Level | Spells |
|---------------|--------|
| 3 | *Searing Smite*, *Zephyr Strike* |
| 5 | *Magic Weapon*, *Misty Step* |
| 9 | *Blinding Smite*, *Haste* |
| 13 | *Dimension Door*, *Staggering Smite* |
| 17 | *Banishing Smite*, *Steel Wind Strike* |

## Level 3: Distant Edge

Your strikes are not bound by the length of your blade. When you make a melee weapon
attack, you can target a creature or object within **30 feet** of you instead of within
your normal reach, hurling a crescent of cutting force. This is still a **melee weapon
attack** in every respect — it benefits from Divine Smite, your Weapon Mastery property,
Improved Divine Smite, and any feature that triggers on a melee weapon attack. This range
increases to **60 feet** at 7th level, **90 feet** at 15th, and **120 feet** at 20th.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Unrivaled Stance

As a Bonus Action, you can take a flawless dueling stance for 1 minute. For the duration:

- You add your **Charisma modifier** (minimum +1) to the **damage** of every weapon attack
  you make.
- Your melee weapon attacks score a **critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20**.
- The first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can
  force it to make a **Strength saving throw** against your spell save DC; on a failure it
  can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn (you cut down its guard).

## Level 7: Aura of the Duelist

Your poise spreads to everyone fighting at your side. While your **Aura of Protection** is
active, you and each ally in it gain a **+2 bonus to AC against the first attack roll made
against them each round** (you turn the opening strike aside) and have **Advantage on
opportunity attacks**.

**Deflecting Riposte (Reaction).** When you or a creature you can see within 5 feet of you is
hit by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to parry, reducing the damage by **1d10 + your
Charisma modifier + your Paladin level**. If this reduces the damage to 0 and the attacker is
within your Distant Edge range, you can immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against it
as part of this Reaction.

## Level 15: Crossing Slashes

Your blade moves faster than the eye. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a
melee weapon attack, you can deal an extra cut: each creature of your choice within **10 feet
of the target** takes **slashing damage equal to your Charisma modifier**, and you can choose
one of them to suffer the same Weapon Mastery property you applied to the original target. In
addition, whenever you score a critical hit with a weapon, you can make **one additional weapon
attack** as part of the same action.

## Level 20: World's Strongest Swordsman

As a Bonus Action, you become the apex of the blade for 10 minutes. You gain the following
benefits:

- Your **Distant Edge** range becomes **120 feet**, and your melee weapon attacks ignore
  **Resistance** to their damage and treat **half cover and three-quarters cover as no cover**.
- You make **one additional attack** whenever you take the Attack action (this stacks with
  Extra Attack).
- Each time you hit a creature with a weapon attack, it takes an extra **2d8 Force** damage as
  the cutting pressure tears space itself.
- While this is active, when a creature within your Distant Edge range makes an attack roll or a
  saving throw, you can use your Reaction to cut the attack apart, imposing **Disadvantage** on
  the roll.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
expend a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
