# Oath of the Devourer

*The Maw — Holiness Through Hunger*

You swore your oath to an appetite. Something vast and starving looked back at you from the
dark, and you agreed to be its mouth in the world. You eat the strong to become stronger;
you tear the flesh of your enemies and feel their power knit into your own; and the more you
feed, the larger and more terrible you grow. It is monstrous. You have made your peace with
being a monster, so long as it is your monster.

*A grey, predatory oath — feed to heal, feed to grow, feed to win.*

## Level 3: Ravenous Bite

You grow a maw of fangs. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) can be made as a **Bite** that deals
**1d10 + your Strength or Charisma modifier Piercing** damage, counts as magical, and is a melee
weapon attack for all features. Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one
Bite as a Bonus Action**. Whenever your Bite reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, or you hit a
creature already below half its Hit Points with it, you **regain Hit Points equal to your Paladin
level**.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Feeding Frenzy

As a Bonus Action, you give in to the hunger for 1 minute. While it lasts:

- You gain **Feast** counters: 1 when you activate this, and 1 each time you reduce a creature to
  0 Hit Points or land your Bite on a new target. Maximum **5**.
- You add your **Feast counters to the damage** of every Bite and weapon attack, and you have
  **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Feast counters** (refreshed whenever the count
  rises).
- Your size increases to **Large** if you are smaller (your reach grows accordingly), and you have
  **Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws**.

## Level 7: Aura of the Predator

While your **Aura of Protection** is active, enemies in it that are below half their Hit Point
maximum have **Disadvantage on attack rolls against you** (they sense they are prey). When you
reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, you and each ally in your aura gain **Temporary Hit Points
equal to your Charisma modifier**.

## Level 15: Swallow Whole

When you score a critical hit with your Bite, or hit a **Large or smaller** creature that is below
a quarter of its Hit Points, you can try to engulf it: it makes a **Strength saving throw**, and
on a failure it is **swallowed** — Restrained, Blinded, and unable to breathe, and it takes **4d8
Acid** damage at the start of each of your turns. It can repeat the save at the end of its turns to
claw free into a space within 5 feet of you. While you have a creature swallowed, you regain Hit
Points equal to **half** that Acid damage each turn. You can have one creature swallowed at a time.

## Level 20: Apex Predator

As a Bonus Action, you become the perfect eating machine for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- **Feeding Frenzy** is active for free, your maximum Feast counters become **10**, and you make
  **one additional Bite or weapon attack** whenever you take the Attack action.
- Your Bite die becomes **2d10**, ignores **Resistance**, and whenever it reduces a creature to 0
  Hit Points you regain **Hit Points equal to twice your Paladin level** and gain a Feast counter.
- You have **Resistance to all damage** while you have at least 1 Feast counter, and you can
  **Swallow Whole** a creature on any hit, not only a crit or a low-HP target.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a
5th-level spell slot to use it again.
