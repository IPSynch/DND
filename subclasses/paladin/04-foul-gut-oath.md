# Oath of the Foul Gut

*The Defiler — Holy War Waged From the Bowels*

Yours is a creed of utter contempt. You sanctify the basest matter your body makes and
fling it at the proud and the pure: you shit out blades in the thick of the melee, pack
projectiles of hardened filth and hurl them sizzling with rot, and bury the righteous in
your own corruption. It is humiliating, it is lethal, and that is exactly the point — a
foe laid low by your bowels dies knowing what you thought of them.

> **A note on tone.** Crude by design, written disgusting-but-not-explicit. Reskin "filth"
> and "ordure" to "blight-matter" or "grave-mud" if you like; the necrotic math is the same.

## Level 3: Filth-Forged Arsenal

You arm yourself from within. As a **Bonus Action**, you excrete and harden a mass of filth
into a weapon of your choice (any Simple or Martial melee weapon) in your hand. This weapon:

- is **magical**, deals **Necrotic** damage instead of its normal type, and you can use your
  **Charisma modifier** instead of Strength or Dexterity for its attack and damage rolls;
- has the **Thrown (range 30/120)** property in addition to its normal properties — when you
  throw it, it bursts into reeking sludge on impact and a **new one re-forms in your hand**
  instantly (no action);
- lasts until you finish a Long Rest or manifest another. You can have only one at a time.

When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can channel **Divine Smite** through it as normal.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Septic Surge

As a Bonus Action, you make your every strike a vector of rot for 1 minute. For the duration:

- Your weapon attacks deal an extra **1d8 Necrotic** damage on a hit (**2d8** at 11th level).
- The first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature, it must succeed on a
  **Constitution saving throw** against your spell save DC or have the **Poisoned** condition
  until the start of your next turn.
- A creature that fails the save and is **already Poisoned** instead takes your weapon's damage
  again as the rot festers, and you **regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier**.

## Level 7: Aura of Pestilence

A reek of decay pours off you that sickens your foes but only hardens your friends. While your
**Aura of Protection** is active, you and each ally in it are **immune to disease** and have
**Resistance to Necrotic and Poison damage**.

In addition, each enemy that starts its turn in the aura makes a **Constitution saving throw** or
takes **Necrotic damage equal to your Paladin level** and has **Disadvantage on its next attack
roll** before the start of its next turn.

## Level 15: Scattershot Filth

You lay down a barrage. Once on each of your turns when you take the Attack action, you can
replace one attack with a spray of hardened projectiles: each creature of your choice in a
**30-foot Cone** makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **3d8 Necrotic** damage on a failure
(half as much on a success). A creature that fails also has its **Speed halved** until the start
of your next turn, mired in clinging muck. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with your
Necrotic damage, you can immediately afflict another creature within 30 feet with the disease of
the *Contagion* spell (no save to apply the seed; the normal *Contagion* saves still end it).

## Level 20: Cataclysmic Voiding

As a Bonus Action, you become an engine of rot for 1 minute. You gain the following benefits:

- You make **one additional weapon attack** whenever you take the Attack action, and your
  Filth-Forged weapon's extra damage from Septic Surge increases to **3d8 Necrotic**.
- Once on each of your turns as part of your Attack action, you can erupt: each creature of your
  choice in a **20-foot-radius Sphere** centered on you makes a **Constitution saving throw**,
  taking **6d8 Necrotic** damage and gaining the **Poisoned** and **Blinded** conditions for 1
  minute on a failure (half damage and no conditions on a success; repeats the save at the end
  of each of its turns to end the conditions).
- You have **immunity to the Poisoned condition and to Necrotic damage**, and you regain Hit
  Points equal to **half** the Necrotic damage you deal.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
