# Oath of Black Powder

*The Gunsmith-Saint — Judgment Delivered at Range*

Your oath smells of brimstone and gun oil. Where other paladins trust a blade and a
prayer, you trust a blessed firearm and a steady hand: a single consecrated weapon, sworn
to you, that folds and unfolds into whatever shape the fight demands — a snap-quick pistol
in a hallway, a long rifle across a field, a roaring scattergun in a press of bodies. You
shoot through the chaos of melee without flinching and put your faith, quite literally,
behind every trigger pull.

## Level 3: Oathbound Firearm

You bind a single firearm to your oath. As a **Bonus Action**, you can conjure or summon your
Oathbound Firearm into your hand (if it's a mundane firearm you can instead perform a 1-hour
ritual to bind an existing one). You are proficient with it, it is **magical**, and you use your
**Charisma modifier** for its attack and damage rolls. It never needs ammunition or reloading
(it conjures its own rounds), and **you don't have Disadvantage on ranged attacks from being
within 5 feet of an enemy**.

As a Bonus Action, you can reshape the firearm into one of the following forms (its damage is
based on your tier; see the table):

| Form | Range | Properties | Damage |
|------|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Pistol** | 40/120 | Light, you may draw and fire as one action | **1d8** Piercing |
| **Rifle** | 120/480 | Two-Handed; +2 to one attack roll if you didn't move | **1d10** Piercing |
| **Scattergun** | self (15-ft Cone) | Two-Handed; attack becomes a Dex save vs your spell save DC, hitting all in the cone | **1d8** Piercing |

You can channel **Divine Smite** and any feature requiring a weapon attack through your firearm
(treating the Scattergun's blast as a ranged weapon attack against each target for Smite
purposes, applied once to one creature of your choice). At 11th level, your firearm's damage die
increases by one step.

## Level 3: Channel Divinity — Consecrated Rounds

As a Bonus Action, you bless your chamber for 1 minute. For the duration:

- Each shot deals an extra **1d8 Radiant** damage on a hit (**2d8** at 11th level).
- You ignore **half and three-quarters cover**, and your firearm scores a **critical hit on a
  roll of 19 or 20**.
- Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature, it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or
  is knocked **Prone** by the impact.

## Level 7: Aura of the Marksman

While your **Aura of Protection** is active, you and allies in it ignore **half cover** on ranged
attacks and **don't suffer Disadvantage for firing into melee or at long range**. When an enemy
you can see within 60 feet hits an ally with an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to fire a
covering shot at it: make a ranged attack with your Oathbound Firearm, and on a hit the enemy
takes the weapon's damage and its attack against your ally takes a **−2 penalty** to the result.

## Level 15: Trick Shooting

Your gunplay borders on the supernatural. Whenever you take the Attack action and attack only
with your Oathbound Firearm, you make **one additional attack** with it. In addition, once on
each of your turns when you hit with a shot, you can apply one of these without spending an
action:

- **Disarming Shot.** The target makes a Strength save or drops one held item of your choice.
- **Crippling Shot.** The target makes a Constitution save or has its Speed halved and can't take
  Reactions until the start of your next turn.
- **Ricochet.** The shot leaps to a second creature within 15 feet of the target; make a separate
  attack roll against it, dealing the firearm's damage on a hit.

## Level 20: Final Judgment

As a Bonus Action, you enter a state of perfect aim for 1 minute. You gain the following
benefits:

- You make **one additional attack** with your Oathbound Firearm whenever you take the Attack
  action (stacking with Trick Shooting), and each shot ignores **Resistance** to its damage.
- Once on each of your turns, you can spend your action to line up an execution shot against one
  creature you can see within your firearm's range: it makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking
  **8d8 Radiant plus your firearm's damage** on a failure (half on a success). If this reduces a
  creature with fewer Hit Points than your Paladin level × 2 to 0, it is slain outright.
- You have **truesight out to 60 feet** and can't be Blinded.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend
a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
