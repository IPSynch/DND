# Chronomantic Sorcery

*Sorcerous Origin — Control*

> Time is a river, and everyone else is wading. You are standing on the bank,
> deciding when they get to take their next step. You act in the gaps between
> seconds; you let an enemy's blade fall and then quietly *un-fall* it; and when
> you truly cut loose, the world holds its breath and waits for you.

A control-focused origin built around two pillars the request asked for: **extra
Reactions** (you accumulate more of them as you level) and **crowd control**
(slows, holds, and time-stops). You become the player who is always doing
*something*, even on other people's turns.

> **Reaction rule of thumb used here:** You normally get 1 Reaction per round.
> This subclass raises that ceiling. You can still only spend **one Reaction per
> triggering event** — multiple Reactions just mean you can respond to multiple
> *different* triggers in the same round.

---

## Chronomantic Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Silvery Barbs*, *Feather Fall* |
| 5 | *Hold Person*, *Blur* |
| 7 | *Slow*, *Counterspell* |
| 9 | *Hold Monster*, *Telekinesis* |

(*Silvery Barbs*, *Feather Fall*, and *Counterspell* are all cast with a
**Reaction** — exactly what this origin wants to be doing.)

---

## Out of Time (Level 3)

You exist a half-step ahead of everyone else.

- **Second Reaction.** You can take **two Reactions per round** instead of one
  (one per trigger).
- **Time Skip (Reaction).** When a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an
  attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can use a **Reaction** to skip
  their moment: choose to impose **Disadvantage** on the roll *before* it's made,
  or — *after* you see the result — force them to **reroll** and use the new roll.
  You can use Time Skip a number of times equal to your **Charisma modifier** per
  **Long Rest**; additional uses cost **1 Sorcery Point** each.

---

## Accelerated Existence (Level 6)

Your personal clock runs fast, and you can drag others' clocks to a crawl.

- **Quicktime.** Your **Speed increases by 10 feet**, and once on each of your
  turns you can take the **Disengage** action as part of moving without using an
  action.
- **Dilation Field (active).** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **2 Sorcery Points**
  to create a 20-foot-radius **zone of slowed time** at a point within 120 feet
  for 1 minute (no Concentration required). While in the zone, enemies (your
  choice of which) suffer the effects of the *Slow* spell: **Speed halved, −2 AC,
  −2 to Dexterity saves, can't take Reactions, and can take only one action *or*
  one Bonus Action on their turn** (not both). A creature that leaves the zone
  shakes off the effect at the end of its turn.

---

## Rewind (Level 14)

You can reach back one second and edit what just happened.

- **Third Reaction.** You can now take **three Reactions per round.**
- **Rewind (Reaction).** When you or a creature you can see within 60 feet takes
  damage or fails a saving throw, you can use your **Reaction** to rewind that
  instant: the creature **rerolls the failed save** (and you choose which result
  to keep), **or** the damage is undone and the creature **regains the Hit Points
  it just lost.** You can use Rewind once per **Long Rest** for free; additional
  uses cost **3 Sorcery Points.**
- **Snap Cast.** Whenever you use a Reaction, you can immediately cast a Sorcerer
  **cantrip** as part of that Reaction (no additional action).

---

## Lord of the Hour (Level 18)

Time is no longer a force you bend — it is a tool you hold.

- **First Mover.** You can't be **Surprised**, and at the start of combat you and
  each ally within 30 feet can choose to **act on the highest initiative count**
  (treat your initiative as a 30 if higher than your roll).
- **Stop (capstone — Reaction-fueled Time Stop).** Once per **Long Rest**, you can
  cast *Time Stop* without expending a spell slot. Uniquely for you, during the
  stopped time you **can still take Reactions** (your extra Reactions let you set
  up responses for when time resumes).
- **Temporal Prison (active crowd control).** As a **Magic action**, choose one
  creature within 120 feet. It must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw** or be
  **frozen in time** for 1 minute: it is **Incapacitated**, its Speed is 0, it
  can't be moved, and it can neither affect nor be affected by anything outside
  itself (it can't be targeted or damaged, like a one-creature stasis). It
  repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a
  success. Alternatively, you can spend **4 Sorcery Points** to instead **Stun
  every enemy** in a 30-foot-radius Sphere within 120 feet until the end of your
  next turn (Wisdom save negates).

---

### Designer notes

- The Reaction count climbs **2 → 2 → 3** across the tiers, plus **Time Skip**,
  **Rewind**, and **Snap Cast** give you genuinely impactful things to *do* with
  those Reactions instead of just holding *Shield*.
- **Dilation Field** is the workhorse: a sustained, no-Concentration *Slow* aura
  is brutal control that frees your action and Concentration for *Hold Monster* or
  *Telekinesis* stacked on top.
- **Temporal Prison** is intentionally a "remove one boss-tier threat from the
  fight" button with a save-ends valve so it isn't strictly better than 9th-level
  spells. The AoE Stun mode is the team-fight alternative.
- Tuning lever: if three Reactions feels swingy, cap the table at two and keep
  Rewind as a free-action-once-per-rest instead.
