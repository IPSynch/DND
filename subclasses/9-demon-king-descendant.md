# Warrior of the Demon-King's Blood

*Monk Subclass*

The blood in your veins is older and darker than any monastery's teachings. Some ancestor
of yours sat a throne of bone and ruled by terror, and though their crown is long lost,
their inheritance smolders in you. You feel it when the fighting starts: horns of shadow
crowning your brow, an aura that turns brave soldiers' knees to water, a hunger that grows
the more blood is spilled. You have spent your discipline not on purging this birthright
but on mastering it, bending the tyrant's power to your own will — so that when you finally
let the crown settle, it is you, and not the demon, who rules.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Wisdom
modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You spend **Focus
Points** (called Ki in some traditions). Several features key off a creature being Frightened
by you.*

## Level 3: Crown of Dread

The inheritance announces itself: shadow-horns, and a presence that makes lesser things want
to flee.

***Dread Aura.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to manifest your heritage.
The aura lasts for 1 minute. It ends early if you fall Unconscious or if you end it (no action
required). While it is active, each enemy that starts its turn within 10 feet of you must
succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition until the start of its next
turn.

***Infernal Strikes.*** Your Unarmed Strikes can deal Necrotic or Fire damage (your choice),
and when you hit a creature that is Frightened, it takes extra damage equal to one roll of
your Martial Arts die.

***Fiendish Resilience.*** You have Resistance to Fire and Necrotic damage, and you no longer
need to eat, drink, or sleep (though you still rest to gain the benefits of resting).

## Level 6: Devour and Dominate

The tyrant has two appetites: to feed, and to be obeyed.

***Life Siphon.*** When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, or when you hit a Frightened
creature, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Monk level.

***Command the Fearful.*** While your Dread Aura is active, you can spend 1 Focus Point as a
Bonus Action to issue a wordless command to one creature Frightened by you within 30 feet of
you. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or, on its next turn, either move away from you
and take no actions, or move toward and attack a creature of your choice.

***Hellfire Knuckles.*** Your Unarmed Strikes ignore Resistance to Fire and Necrotic damage,
and when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, each enemy within 5 feet of it takes Fire
damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die.

## Level 11: Tyrant's Dominion

Fear becomes a leash, and pain becomes power.

***Aura of Tyranny.*** Your Dread Aura now extends to 30 feet, and while a creature Frightened
by you is within the aura, its Speed is 0 and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving
throws.

***Crimson Strength.*** While you have Temporary Hit Points, you gain a +2 bonus to attack
rolls and damage rolls, and your Speed increases by 15 feet.

***Soul Harvest.*** As part of the Attack action, you can spend 2 Focus Points to drain a
creature you hit. It must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes Necrotic
damage equal to three rolls of your Martial Arts die, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an
equal amount until it finishes a Long Rest, and you regain Hit Points equal to the damage
dealt.

## Level 17: Demon-King Ascendant

The crown settles, the horns become real, and for a time the throne of bone has a new king —
you.

As a Bonus Action, you can spend 5 Focus Points to ascend. This transformation lasts for 1
minute. It ends early if you fall Unconscious or if you end it (no action required). While
ascended:

- You can become Large (if you wish), you gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed, and you have
  Resistance to all damage except Radiant. Any Radiant damage you take is halved even if you
  would be Vulnerable to it.
- Your Dread Aura is active at all times, and creatures make its saving throw with
  Disadvantage. Whenever a creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points within 30 feet of you, every
  enemy that can see it must make a Wisdom saving throw against the Frightened condition.
- Your Unarmed Strikes deal extra damage equal to two rolls of your Martial Arts die (Necrotic
  or Fire) and score a critical hit on a roll of 19–20. On a critical hit, if the target is
  already below half its Hit Point maximum, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or
  drop to 0 Hit Points.
- At the start of each of your turns, you regain Hit Points equal to your Monk level, and
  creatures Frightened by you can't regain Hit Points while within your aura.

---

### Designer Notes

- The engine of this subclass is mass Fear, then punishing the Frightened: nearly every feature
  improves against a Frightened target, and Tyranny's Speed-0-and-Disadvantage rider turns the
  aura into genuine battlefield lockdown. Creatures immune to the Frightened condition are this
  subclass's natural weakness — a healthy one to preserve.
- Soul Harvest's Hit-Point-maximum drain and the Level 17 execute are deliberately nasty against
  bosses. If your bosses are squishy, raise the execute threshold (for example, one-quarter of
  maximum) or drop it for the cleaner extra dice and crit range. Keep the Radiant weakness so
  clerics and paladins remain the heir's bane.
