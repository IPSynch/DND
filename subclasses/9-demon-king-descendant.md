# Warrior of the Demon-King's Blood

*Monastic Order — Dread Striker / Fiendish Heritage*

> The blood in your veins is older and darker than any monastery's teachings. Some
> ancestor of yours sat a throne of bone and ruled by terror, and though their crown
> is long lost, their inheritance smolders in you. You feel it when the fighting
> starts: horns of shadow crowning your brow, an aura that turns brave soldiers'
> knees to water, a hunger that grows the more blood is spilled. You have spent your
> discipline not on purging this birthright but on *mastering* it — bending the
> tyrant's power to your own will, so that when you finally let the crown settle, it
> is you, and not the demon, who rules.

A dread striker who weaponizes a fiendish inheritance: fear, life-draining strikes,
hellish resilience, and a climactic transformation into a Demon-King incarnate.
Built to dominate the battlefield through terror and overwhelming, growing power.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. Your **Dread Aura** and **Demon-King Ascendant** form are stances you
> activate; several features key off being **Frightened by you**.

---

## Crown of Dread (Level 3)

*The inheritance announces itself: shadow-horns, and a presence that makes lesser
things want to flee.*

- **Dread Aura.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to manifest your
  heritage for **1 minute** (no Concentration). While active, each enemy that **starts
  its turn within 10 feet** of you must succeed on a **Wisdom save** or be
  **Frightened** of you until the start of its next turn.
- **Infernal Strikes (passive).** Your Unarmed Strikes can deal **Necrotic or Fire**
  damage (your choice), and when you hit a creature that is **Frightened**, it takes
  an extra **MA die** of damage. Cruelty rewards itself.
- **Fiendish Resilience (passive).** You have **Resistance to Fire and Necrotic**
  damage, and you don't need to eat, drink, or sleep (though you still rest).

---

## Devour & Dominate (Level 6)

*The tyrant's two appetites: to feed, and to be obeyed.*

- **Life Siphon.** When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, or hit a Frightened
  creature, you **gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your monk level**. The
  battlefield's dead feed the heir.
- **Command the Fearful.** While Dread Aura is active, you can spend **1 Focus
  Point** as a **Bonus Action** to issue a wordless command to one creature Frightened
  by you within 30 feet: it must succeed on a **Wisdom save** or use its next turn to
  **move away from you and take no actions**, or **attack a creature of your choice**
  (your pick). The crown compels.
- **Hellfire Knuckles (passive).** Your Unarmed Strikes ignore **Resistance** to Fire
  and Necrotic damage, and a creature you reduce to 0 HP is wreathed in dark flame —
  each enemy within 5 feet of it takes **MA-die** Fire damage.

---

## Tyrant's Dominion (Level 11)

*Fear becomes a leash, and pain becomes power.*

- **Aura of Tyranny (passive).** Your Dread Aura now reaches **30 feet**, and a
  creature **Frightened by you** has its **Speed reduced to 0** while within the aura
  and makes attack rolls and saving throws with **Disadvantage**. None flee, and none
  fight well.
- **Crimson Strength (passive).** While you have Temporary Hit Points, you gain a
  **+2 bonus to attack and damage rolls** and your Speed increases by **15 feet**.
  Blood spilled makes you stronger.
- **Soul Harvest.** As part of the Attack action, spend **2 Focus Points** to drain a
  creature you hit: it makes a **Constitution save** or takes **3 MA dice** of Necrotic
  damage, has its **maximum Hit Points reduced** by that amount until it finishes a
  Long Rest, and you **regain Hit Points** equal to the damage dealt.

---

## Demon-King Ascendant (Level 17)

*The crown settles, the horns become real, and for a time the throne of bone has a
new king — you.*

As a **Bonus Action**, spend **5 Focus Points** to ascend for **1 minute** (no
Concentration). While ascended:

- You become **Large** (if you wish), gain a **flying Speed equal to your Speed**, and
  have **Resistance to all damage except Radiant**; Radiant damage you take is
  **halved** rather than doubled by any vulnerability.
- Your **Dread Aura** is always on and its save is made with **Disadvantage**; any
  creature reduced to 0 HP within 30 feet causes **every enemy** that can see it to
  make a fresh **Wisdom save** against Frightened.
- Your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **2 MA dice** of Necrotic or Fire damage and
  **critically hit on 19–20**, and on a critical hit the target must succeed on a
  **Constitution save** or **drop to 0 Hit Points** if it is already below half its
  maximum (the tyrant's killing decree).
- At the **start of each of your turns**, you **regain Hit Points equal to your monk
  level**, and creatures Frightened by you can't be healed while within your aura.

---

### Designer notes

- The engine of this subclass is **mass Fear → punish the Frightened**: nearly every
  feature is better against a Frightened target, and Tyranny's Speed-0 + Disadvantage
  rider turns the aura into genuine battlefield lockdown. Enemies immune to Fear
  (Undead, many Fiends/Constructs) are this subclass's natural weakness — a healthy
  one to preserve.
- **Soul Harvest**'s max-HP drain and the level 17 **execute** are intentionally
  nasty against bosses. If your bosses are squishy, raise the execute threshold below
  half (e.g., one-quarter) or drop it for the cleaner 2 MA dice + crit range.
- The **Radiant** weakness is the deliberate "good triumphs over the demon-king"
  pressure valve — keep it so clerics and paladins remain the heir's bane.
