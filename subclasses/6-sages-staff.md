# Warrior of the Sage's Staff

*Monastic Order — Staff Master / Versatile Powerhouse*

> The staff in your hands is no humble walking-stick. It lengthens at a thought,
> stretches across a battlefield, splits into a storm of striking copies, and grows
> so heavy at one end that the earth cracks beneath it — yet you spin it light as a
> reed. You climb the wind on it, vault between the clouds, and bring it down on a
> distant foe as if distance were a polite suggestion. Those who have studied the
> old tales know the type: the irrepressible trickster-king who could not be caged,
> could not be killed, and answered to no heaven. You carry his weapon, and a
> little of his refusal to be told *no*.

A staff-wielding powerhouse and the most versatile striker in the order. With a
quarterstaff in hand you hit harder, reach farther, strike whole crowds, vault into
the sky, and split yourself into clones. This is a deliberately top-tier subclass —
the trade is that nearly everything keys off **wielding a quarterstaff**.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. The features below apply only while you wield a **quarterstaff** (which is a
> Monk weapon). You use your MA die in place of the staff's normal die.

---

## The Cloud-Piercing Staff (Level 3)

*It grows, it shrinks, it weighs as much as you need it to.*

- **As-You-Will Cudgel.** While wielding a quarterstaff, its damage die becomes
  **one size larger than your MA die** (e.g., d8 → d10), and your **reach with it
  increases by 5 feet** (the staff lengthens on command).
- **Heaven-and-Earth (passive).** When you hit a creature with the staff, you can
  make it as heavy as a mountain or as fast as light: choose to either knock the
  target **Prone** (Strength save) or **push it 15 feet** (no save). Once per turn.
- **Far Strike.** As part of the Attack action, one staff attack can extend to a
  target within **30 feet** or strike every creature in a **15-foot Line** (one roll
  against each).

---

## Seventy-Two Transformations (Level 6)

*The trickster's gift: never quite where, or what, the enemy expects.*

- **Hair-Clone Strike.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to conjure
  **two spectral duplicates** of yourself in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet for 1
  minute. Once on each of your turns, each clone can make **one staff attack** (using
  your statistics) against a creature within 5 feet of it as part of your Attack
  action; a clone has AC equal to your Monk DC and vanishes if hit or if it ends a
  turn more than 60 feet from you.
- **Sky-Walking (passive).** You gain a **flying Speed equal to your Speed while you
  have a quarterstaff in hand**, but you fall if you end your turn airborne — unless
  you are at level 11+ (below). You can vault off the staff to **Disengage and Dash**
  for free once per turn.
- **Cloud-Somersault.** Step of the Wind lets you teleport up to your Speed.

---

## Summersault Cloud & Iron Body (Level 11)

*Sworn off no heaven, you simply stop coming down.*

- **Sustained Flight (passive).** Your flying Speed no longer requires you to land at
  the end of your turn, and it increases to **your Speed + 20 feet**.
- **Eighty-One-Pound Smash.** As part of the Attack action, spend **2 Focus Points**
  to bring the staff down with crushing weight: each creature in a **15-foot-radius**
  area within 60 feet takes **3 MA dice** of Bludgeoning damage (or **half** on a
  **Dexterity save**), is knocked **Prone** on a fail, and the ground becomes
  **Difficult Terrain** until the start of your next turn.
- **Body of Iron (passive).** Your unarmored AC increases by **+1**, you have
  **Resistance to Bludgeoning** damage, and you have **Advantage on saves against
  spells** while wielding your staff (the legendary unkillable body).
- **More Clones.** Hair-Clone Strike now conjures **four** duplicates.

---

## The Great Sage, Equal of Heaven (Level 17)

*The title the trickster-king claimed for himself, and dared the heavens to deny.*

- **Length of the Heavens (passive).** Your staff's reach can extend to **60 feet**
  for any single attack, and your staff attacks score a **critical hit on 19–20**.
- **Storm of a Thousand Staves.** As your action, spend **4 Focus Points** to whirl
  the staff into a blur that fills the battlefield. Each creature you choose within
  **60 feet** takes **8 MA dice** of Bludgeoning or Force damage (your choice), or
  **half** on a successful **Dexterity save**. Creatures that fail are knocked
  **Prone** and **pushed 20 feet**, and you may immediately make **one Hair-Clone
  Strike** for free.
- **Equal of Heaven (passive).** While wielding your staff you have **Resistance to
  all damage**, you **can't be Grappled, Restrained, Paralyzed, or Petrified**, and
  if you would drop to 0 Hit Points, you can spend **3 Focus Points** to drop to **1**
  instead (the king who could not be killed). This last use recharges on a **Short or
  Long Rest**.

---

### Designer notes

- This is intentionally the **versatile juggernaut** of the ten: reach, ranged
  strikes, AoE, flight, clones, and tankiness all in one kit. The leash that keeps it
  fair is the **"only while wielding a quarterstaff"** clause — disarm or sunder the
  staff and most of the subclass switches off (a fun vulnerability for a clever DM).
- The **clones** (2 → 4) are a real action-economy multiplier; if they prove too
  swingy, cap each clone to a single attack per *round* rather than per turn, or have
  them share one attack between them.
- Tuning lever: **Equal of Heaven**'s anti-condition list is broad on purpose for the
  "unkillable monkey king" fantasy. Trim Petrified/Paralyzed first if you want bosses
  to retain their lockdown tools.
