# Warrior of the Sorcerer's Bane

*Monk Subclass*

The world gave you nothing — no spark, no gift, no whisper of the power others wield
like it was owed to them. So you turned that emptiness into a blade. Where they sense the
currents of magic, you are a blind spot, a hole in the world that no spell can find and
no enchantment can grip. You studied the ones who lean on power until you knew exactly
where the power isn't — the soft, mortal body beneath it — and you learned to put your
fist precisely there. To a sorcerer you are the worst thing imaginable: someone their
magic simply does not work on, already inside their guard.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
Wisdom modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You
spend **Focus Points** (called Ki in some traditions). "Spellcasting feature" means any
feature a creature uses to cast spells or take Magic actions.*

## Level 3: Heavenly Restriction

A body utterly without magic is one that magic struggles even to perceive.

***Blind Spot.*** You have Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical
effects, and whenever you succeed on a saving throw against a spell that deals damage, you
take no damage from it (you take half damage on a failed save). In addition, divination
magic can't locate you or read your thoughts unless you allow it.

***Voidwalk.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to gain Resistance to all
damage from spells until the start of your next turn and move up to your Speed toward a
creature without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

***Cursed-Tool Strikes.*** Your Unarmed Strikes count as magical for the purpose of
overcoming Resistance and Immunity, and they ignore Resistance to their damage type.

## Level 6: Mage Killer

Find the instant a spell is born, and end it before it draws breath.

***Disrupt.*** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you casts a spell or takes a
Magic action, you can take a Reaction and spend 1 Focus Point to make one Unarmed Strike
against it. On a hit, the creature takes the strike's damage and must succeed on a
Constitution saving throw or the spell or action fails and is wasted, along with any
resources it required.

***Antibody.*** You have Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that is
Concentrating on a spell or that has cast a spell since the start of your last turn, and a
creature you hit has Disadvantage on saving throws it makes to maintain Concentration.

## Level 11: Sever the Weave

Magic is just another thing with a throat to cut.

***Dispelling Blows.*** When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you can either end
one spell of level 3 or lower that is active on it or that it is Concentrating on (no check
required), or suppress one magical effect or benefit on it until the end of its next turn.

***Null Field.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 Focus Points to project a field of
absence in a 15-foot-radius Sphere centered on you. It lasts for 1 minute, moving with you.
Within it, spells of level 3 or lower fail, magical effects of that level can't begin, and
you and your allies have Advantage on saving throws against magic. A creature that tries to
cast a higher-level spell inside the field must first succeed on a spellcasting ability
check against your Monk DC, or the spell fails.

***Inevitable.*** You can't gain the Charmed or Frightened conditions or be put to sleep by
magic, and magical teleportation can't move you against your will.

## Level 17: The Untouched One

To the powers of the world, you were never here at all.

***Sorcerer's Bane.*** You have Immunity to damage from spells (a spell's nonmagical
byproducts — a conjured boulder's weight, a created fire's mundane heat — can still affect
you, at the DM's discretion). Creatures have Disadvantage on spell attack rolls against
you, and a spell that targets only you and allows no saving throw simply fails.

***Execute the Caster.*** As part of the Attack action, you can spend 4 Focus Points to make
a single Unarmed Strike against a creature with a spellcasting feature. On a hit, the
creature takes extra damage equal to eight rolls of your Martial Arts die (Force), loses
Concentration, and can't cast spells or take Magic actions until the end of its next turn
(no save). It also has the Stunned condition for that duration if it fails a Constitution
saving throw.

***Closing the Distance.*** Your Voidwalk can also teleport you to an unoccupied space within
5 feet of a creature you can see within 60 feet of you, ignoring magical barriers such as a
*wall of force* along the way.

---

### Designer Notes

- This subclass is a hard counter, and it will feel oppressive to enemy casters — that's the
  fantasy, so lean in, but in caster-heavy campaigns it can trivialize whole fights. The main
  dials are the level cutoffs on Sever the Weave (3rd-level cap) and the Reaction economy of
  Disrupt.
- Blind Spot's "no damage on a successful save" is essentially Evasion against every spell
  save. If that's too much before Level 11, restrict it to Dexterity saves at Level 3 and
  widen it to all saves at Level 11.
