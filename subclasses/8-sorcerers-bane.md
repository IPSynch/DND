# Warrior of the Sorcerer's Bane

*Monastic Order — Anti-Magic / Mage Killer*

> The world gave you nothing — no spark, no gift, no whisper of the power that
> others wield like it was owed to them. So you turned that emptiness into a blade.
> Where they sense the currents of magic, you are a blind spot, a hole in the world
> that no spell can find and no enchantment can grip. You studied the ones who rely
> on power until you knew exactly where the power *isn't* — the soft, mortal body
> underneath — and you learned to put your fist precisely there. To a sorcerer you
> are the worst thing imaginable: someone their magic simply does not work on, who
> is already inside their guard.

A specialist mage-killer for the hunter who answers magic with absence. You are
nearly impossible to target with spells, you punch through magical defenses, and you
shut casters down — the natural predator of every wizard, cleric, and warlock.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. "Spellcasting feature" means any feature a creature uses to cast spells or
> use spell-like Magic actions.

---

## Heavenly Restriction (Level 3)

*A body utterly without magic — and therefore one magic struggles to even perceive.*

- **Blind Spot (passive).** You have **Advantage on saving throws against spells and
  other magical effects**, and **spells that allow a saving throw deal no damage to
  you on a successful save** (and half on a failure). Detection and divination magic
  can't locate you or read your mind unless you allow it.
- **Voidwalk.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to gain
  **Resistance to all damage from spells** until the start of your next turn, and to
  move up to your Speed toward a creature without provoking Opportunity Attacks — the
  classic closing-the-distance rush.
- **Cursed-Tool Strikes (passive).** Your Unarmed Strikes count as **magical** for
  overcoming Resistance and Immunity, and ignore **Resistance** to their damage type.

---

## Mage Killer (Level 6)

*Find the instant the spell is born, and end it before it draws breath.*

- **Disrupt.** When a creature within **30 feet** you can see casts a spell or uses a
  Magic action, you can use your **Reaction** and spend **1 Focus Point** to make one
  **Unarmed Strike** against it. On a hit, the creature takes your strike's damage and
  must succeed on a **Constitution save** or the spell **fails and is wasted** (its
  action and any resources are spent). This is a free, melee-range Counterspell that
  works on anything you can reach.
- **Antibody (passive).** You have **Advantage on attack rolls against any creature
  that is Concentrating on a spell or has cast a spell since the start of your last
  turn**, and your hits force Concentration saves with **Disadvantage**.

---

## Sever the Weave (Level 11)

*Magic is just another thing with a throat to cut.*

- **Dispelling Blows (passive).** When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you
  can choose to **end one spell of 3rd level or lower** active on it or that it is
  Concentrating on (no check), or **suppress one magical effect or buff** on it until
  the end of its next turn. Your fists unravel the weave.
- **Null Field.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **2 Focus Points** to project a
  **15-foot-radius** field of absence centered on you for 1 minute. Within it, **spells
  of 3rd level or lower fail**, magical effects of that level can't start, and you and
  allies have **Advantage on saves against magic**. A creature that tries to cast a
  higher-level spell inside the field must first succeed on a **spellcasting-ability
  check** against your Monk DC or the spell fails.
- **Inevitable (passive).** You are **immune to being Charmed, Frightened, or put to
  sleep by magic**, and magical Teleportation can't move you against your will.

---

## The Untouched One (Level 17)

*To the powers of the world, you were never here at all.*

- **Sorcerer's Bane (passive).** You have **Immunity to damage from spells**
  (mundane components of an effect — a conjured boulder's *weight*, a fire's mundane
  heat — still affect you normally, at your DM's discretion), and any creature has
  **Disadvantage on spell attack rolls against you**. Spells that don't allow a save
  and target only you simply **fail**.
- **Execute the Caster.** As part of the Attack action, spend **4 Focus Points** to
  make a single Unarmed Strike against a creature with a spellcasting feature. On a
  hit, it takes an extra **8 MA dice** of Force damage, **loses all Concentration**,
  and can't cast spells or take Magic actions until the **end of its next turn**
  (no save) — and is **Stunned** for that duration if it fails a **Constitution save**.
- **Closing the Distance (passive).** Your Voidwalk also lets you **teleport** to a
  spot within 5 feet of any creature you can see within **60 feet**, and you ignore
  magical barriers (such as *wall of force*) when doing so. No caster's distance is
  safe from you.

---

### Designer notes

- This subclass is a hard counter, and it will **feel oppressive to enemy casters** —
  that's the entire fantasy, so lean in, but be aware in caster-heavy campaigns the
  player can trivialize whole fights. The dials are the **level** ceilings on Sever
  the Weave (3rd-level cutoff) and the Reaction economy of **Disrupt**.
- **Heavenly Restriction**'s "no damage on a successful save" is Evasion for *every*
  spell save (Dex, Con, Wis, etc.), which is strong but central to the concept. If
  it's too much before level 11, restrict it to Dexterity saves at level 3 and widen
  it at 11.
- Against PCs, the level 17 "spells targeting only you fail" can frustrate enemy
  spellcasters into irrelevance — entirely intended, but warn your DM.
