# Discipline of the Elements (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Logia — Your Body Is Not Made of Meat Anymore. It's Made of That.*

You have rewritten what you're made of. Where there should be blood and organs there is instead a
churning mass of your chosen element — flame, frost, sparking charge, drifting toxin — held in the
shape of a person by nothing but your will. Blades pass through your smoke. Fists sink into your
cinders. And when you strike back, you strike *as* the element itself: an avalanche of cold, a
gout of magma, a bolt from a clear sky. You are not a person who wields the element. You are the
element wearing a face.

*Inspired by the Logia of One Piece — bodies that become an element, intangible to the ordinary
and made of the extraordinary.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Reflexive Hide
(Absorb Elements)*, *Scalding Rays (Scorching Ray)*, *Humor Orb (Chromatic Orb)*, *Bodily Breath
(Dragon's Breath)*, *Acid Sphere (Vitriolic Sphere)*.

Your **Element die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at
15th.

## Level 3: Elemental Body

Choose your **element** when you take this feature (you can't change it later). This sets your
**damage type** and a **rider** it inflicts:

| Element | Damage type | Rider (on a failed save) |
|---|---|---|
| Ice / Cold | Cold | target's Speed is halved until the end of its next turn |
| Fire / Magma | Fire | target takes your Element die again at the start of its next turn (burning) |
| Light | Radiant | target is Blinded until the end of its next turn |
| Lightning | Lightning | target can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn |
| Thunder | Thunder | target is pushed 10 feet and knocked Prone |
| Poison | Poison | target is Poisoned until the end of its next turn |
| Acid | Acid | target's AC is reduced by 1 until the end of its next turn |

You gain **Immunity to your element's damage type**, and because your flesh is diffuse, you have
**Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from nonmagical attacks** while you
wear no armor.

**Element Blast.** You gain an at-will ranged attack that costs no Hit Points. As a **Magic
action** (or as an attack when you take the Attack action), make a ranged spell attack out to
**60 feet**, dealing **Element die + your Constitution modifier** damage of your element; on a
hit, the target also suffers your element's **rider** (no save). This blast's die scales with
your Element die.

## Level 3: Elemental Surge (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you loosen fully into your element:

- **Passive — Logia Body.** You have **Immunity to nonmagical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and
  Slashing damage** (weapons pass through you), and your elemental damage **ignores Resistance**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Elemental Burst.** Choose a **15-foot Cone** or a **10-foot-radius
  Sphere** centered on you. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking
  **Element die + your Constitution modifier** damage of your element and suffering its **rider**
  on a failure (half damage, no rider, on a success).

## Level 6: Elemental Mastery

Your control sharpens and your defenses thicken.

- **Intangible Reflex (Reaction).** When you're hit by a **magical** attack or effect, or by a
  nonmagical one you didn't already ignore, you can dissolve into your element to take **no
  damage** from it. You can do this a number of times equal to your **Constitution modifier** per
  Long Rest.
- Your **Element Blast** launches **two** bolts (two attack rolls) whenever you use it, and you
  become **immune** to any condition associated with your element (you can't be Blinded by light,
  Poisoned by poison, and so on).
- You gain a movement mode fitting your element (choose one, usable at will): a **Fly Speed equal
  to your Speed** (Fire, Light, Thunder, Lightning) or the ability to move across and through
  liquids and Difficult Terrain unhindered and squeeze through any gap the element could (Ice,
  Poison, Acid).

## Level 10: One With the Element

You cross the threshold into a true Logia.

- You are now **permanently immune to all nonmagical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage** —
  physical weapons pass through you whether or not you're in Rut. (Magical attacks and effects
  still affect you normally.)
- You can turn your whole body into a mobile mass of your element as a **Bonus Action** (fly or
  flow as above, squeeze through any opening, and you gain **Resistance to all magical damage**
  for as long as you stay dispersed; you can't make weapon attacks while fully dispersed, but you
  can still cast and use your Element Blast).
- **Elemental Field.** As a **Magic action**, you saturate a **20-foot-radius** area around you
  with your element for 1 minute (no Concentration). Each enemy that starts its turn in the area,
  or enters it for the first time on a turn, takes **Element die + your Constitution modifier**
  damage of your element and suffers its **rider**. You can move the field to remain centered on
  you at the start of each of your turns.

## Level 18: Elemental Apotheosis (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you stop pretending to be a person at all and become a walking disaster of
your element for **1 minute**. For the duration:

- You have **Immunity to your element's damage type** (already), **Immunity to all nonmagical
  damage**, and **Resistance to all other damage**. You **can't be Grappled, Restrained, or
  Paralyzed** (there's nothing solid to hold).
- At the start of each of your turns, an **elemental cataclysm** erupts — a **30-foot-radius
  Sphere** centered on you or a point within 120 feet (a blizzard, an eruption, a supercell,
  whatever your element demands). Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking
  **8d8** damage of your element and suffering its **rider** on a failure (half damage, no rider,
  on a success).
- Your **Element Blast** and **Elemental Burst** deal an additional **Element die** and treat
  **Immunity** to your element as **Resistance**.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
