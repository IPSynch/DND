# Discipline of the Demon (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Take Over — Three Demons Sleeping in One Body*

You don't summon a demon. You don't bargain with one. You *become* one — dragging a
sleeping horror up out of your own marrow and wearing it like a second, better skin. And you
have learned not one demon but three, each grown for a different kind of killing: a shrieking
brute of claws and fangs, a black-winged artillery piece, and a cold, commanding thing that
bends the battlefield to its will. When you go into your **Rut**, you choose which one wakes up.

*Inspired by Mirajane Strauss's Satan Soul — a Take Over mage who wears a different demon for
every fight.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Clench
(Shield)*, *Scalding Rays (Scorching Ray)*, *Blood Puppetry (Hold Monster)*, *Flesh Reshaping
(Polymorph)*, *Ascended Form (Draconic Transformation)*.

Your **Demon die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Demon Blood

Even out of your full forms, the demon leaks through. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals
**1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Slashing or Force damage (raking claws), counts as
**magical**, and increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at 11th, and **2d6** at 15th.
Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus
Action**, and you can use Unarmed Strikes for any Fluidic feature that calls for a melee weapon
attack.

You also gain **Darkvision out to 120 feet** (or extend your existing Darkvision by 60 feet),
**Resistance to Necrotic damage**, and **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or
Frightened** — nothing so small frightens a thing like you.

## Level 3: Demonic Rut — Take Over (Primed)

Your **Rut** is a demonic transformation. Each time you enter Rut, choose **one** of the three
**Demon Souls** below. You gain that Soul's **Passive** and its **Rut Bonus Action** for the
duration (in addition to the normal Rut benefits). You can choose a different Soul each time you
enter Rut.

- **Demon of Wrath (melee).** *Passive — Ravening:* your Speed increases by **10 feet**, your
  Unarmed Strike die increases by one step, and the first time you hit a creature on each of
  your turns it takes an extra **Demon die** of damage. *Rut Bonus Action — Rend:* make one
  Unarmed Strike; on a hit the target also takes an extra **Demon die** and makes a **Strength
  saving throw** or is knocked **Prone** and pushed **10 feet**.
- **Demon of Ruin (ranged blaster).** *Passive — Black Wings:* you gain a **Fly Speed equal to
  your Speed** (you fall at the end of your turn if you can't hover), and you gain a ranged
  attack — the **Demon Bolt** (a lance of black fire): as part of your Attack action you can,
  in place of a melee attack, make a ranged spell attack out to **90 feet** dealing **Demon die
  + your Constitution modifier** Fire or Force damage. *Rut Bonus Action — Evil Spark:* a
  **15-foot Cone** or a single creature within 90 feet makes a **Dexterity saving throw**,
  taking **Demon die + your Constitution modifier** Fire or Force damage (half on a success).
- **Demon of Guile (utility / command).** *Passive — Dominion:* you gain a **Fly Speed equal
  to your Speed**, **Resistance to Psychic damage**, and enemies within 30 feet have
  **Disadvantage on saving throws** against your Fluidic Abilities that impose the Charmed,
  Frightened, or Restrained condition. *Rut Bonus Action — Command Word:* one creature within
  30 feet makes a **Wisdom saving throw** or is compelled (as the *Command* spell — Approach,
  Flee, Drop, Grovel, or Halt) and takes **Demon die** Psychic damage; on a success it takes
  half damage and no compulsion. You may instead grant an ally within 30 feet **Temporary Hit
  Points equal to your Visceralist level** and Advantage on its next attack.

## Level 6: Full Take Over & Empowered Souls

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack
action), and when you roll Initiative you can enter **Demonic Rut** without using an action and
without spending a use of Rut.

Each Demon Soul also grows stronger:

- **Wrath** — your claws gain **Reach 5 feet** beyond normal and you have **Resistance to
  Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**.
- **Ruin** — your **Demon Bolt** fires **twice** whenever you use it in place of an attack (two
  separate attack rolls), and your Fire and Force damage **ignores Resistance**.
- **Guile** — when a creature you can see within 30 feet targets you or an ally with an attack,
  you can use your **Reaction** to force a **Wisdom saving throw**; on a failure it must choose
  a new target or waste the attack, and it takes **Demon die** Psychic damage.

## Level 10: Twin Souls

Your body is roomy enough for two demons at once. When you enter **Demonic Rut**, you now choose
**two** different Demon Souls and gain **both** of their Passives **and both** of their Rut Bonus
Actions (you can still take only one Bonus Action per turn). In addition:

- The extra **Demon die** damage from any Soul's features increases by **your Constitution
  modifier**, and **Evil Spark** and **Command Word** can each target **two** creatures.
- While in Demonic Rut you have **Resistance to the damage type of your last Demon Bolt or Evil
  Spark** and cannot be **Frightened**.

## Level 18: Sitri, the Demon King (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you tear all three demons loose at once and stand as their crowned union
for **1 minute**. For the duration:

- You gain **all three Demon Souls simultaneously** — every Passive and every Rut Bonus Action
  — and your size can increase to **Large**.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, a **Fly Speed equal to twice your Speed**, and you are
  **immune to the Charmed, Frightened, and Stunned conditions**.
- Once on each of your turns you can take **one additional Bonus Action**, letting you fire two
  of your Souls' Bonus Actions in the same turn.
- **Soul Annihilation.** Once on each of your turns, when you take the Attack action, you can
  replace one attack with a devastating release: a **60-foot Cone** or a **20-foot-radius
  Sphere** within 120 feet. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking
  **8d8** Fire, Force, or Necrotic damage (your choice) on a failure and being knocked **Prone**
  (half damage, no Prone, on a success).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
