# Discipline of the Lymph (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Antibody — The Body That Rejects All Poison, Curse, and Spell*

You command the clear fluid of the immune system — lymph, plasma, antibodies — and you have turned
your body into a system that identifies threats and neutralizes them. You flush poison and curse
from your allies, adapt on the fly to resist whatever just hit you, and clot enemy magic mid-cast
like a swarm of white cells strangling an infection. You are the party's living cure.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Purge (Lesser
Restoration)*, *Blood Seizure (Counterspell)*, *Unmake Magic (Dispel Magic)*, *Membrane Globe
(Globe of Invulnerability)*, *Total Restoration (Heal)*.

Your **Lymph die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Antibodies

You purge threats on reflex. As a **Bonus Action**, you can touch a creature (or yourself) and end
one **disease** or one of the following conditions affecting it: **Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed,
Poisoned,** or **Stunned**. In addition, you and allies within 10 feet of you have a **bonus to
saving throws against spells and magical effects equal to your Constitution modifier's sign**
(minimum +1). You are immune to disease and poison.

## Level 3: Immune Response (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Adaptive Resistance.** Whenever you take damage of a type, you gain **Resistance to
  that damage type** until the end of your next turn (the body learns). You also have **Advantage
  on saving throws against spells**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Flush.** Choose a creature within 30 feet; it ends one condition or disease
  on itself (as Antibodies) **and** regains **Lymph die** Hit Points, or — if you target an enemy —
  it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or takes your **Lymph die** in Necrotic damage as you
  turn its own lymph against it.

## Level 6: White-Cell Swarm

When a creature you can see within 60 feet casts a spell, you can use your **Reaction** to try to
smother it: make an ability check using your spell save DC's math (d20 + Proficiency Bonus +
Constitution modifier) against a DC of **10 + the spell's level**; on a success the spell fails and
has no effect (this works like *Counterspell* but costs no Hit Points and no ability). You can do
this a number of times per Long Rest equal to your **Constitution modifier**.

## Level 10: Herd Immunity

Your protective field becomes a 15-foot Emanation. Allies in it gain the **Antibodies** save bonus
and have **Advantage on saving throws against being Poisoned, Diseased, Charmed, or Frightened**.
Once on each of your turns when an ally in the aura fails a saving throw, you can use no action to
let them **reroll** it. In addition, when you hit a creature with a Fluidic Ability, you can end
one **beneficial** spell effect on it (as *Dispel Magic* against one effect).

## Level 18: Perfect Immunity (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you raise your body's defenses to a flawless peak for 1 minute. For the
duration:

- You are **immune to all conditions** except Unconscious (from dropping to 0 HP), and you have
  **Resistance to all damage**.
- Whenever a creature within 30 feet casts a spell or uses a magical effect that targets you or an
  ally in your aura, you can use your **Reaction** to attempt to **neutralize** it as White-Cell
  Swarm — and if you succeed, you **absorb** it, regaining **Hit Points equal to 5 × the spell's
  level**.
- Allies within your aura are **immune to disease and the Poisoned condition** and can use your
  Reaction-free reroll from Herd Immunity once each on their own turns.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** to use it again.
