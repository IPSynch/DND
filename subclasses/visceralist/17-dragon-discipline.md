# Discipline of the Dragon (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Wyrm-Flesh — Scales Where Skin Should Be, and a Furnace in the Gut*

Somewhere in the meat of you sleeps something enormous and old. You've learned to wake it in
pieces — scales that ripple up your arms, a jaw that unhinges too far, wings that tear wet from
your shoulder blades — and, when you truly let go, to shed your human shape entirely and stand as
a dragon. You spit ruin from a furnace behind your ribs, rake with claws that split stone, and
weather blows that would pulp a lesser body. Some call you a monster. You prefer *apex*.

*Inspired by Kaido of the Beasts — a body that shifts from man to man-beast hybrid to a full,
sky-blotting dragon.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Bodily Breath
(Dragon's Breath)*, *Reflexive Hide (Absorb Elements)*, *Hardened Hide (Stoneskin)*, *Fart
Flight (Fly)*, *Ascended Form (Draconic Transformation)*.

Your **Dragon die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Draconic Scales

Choose a damage type when you take this feature: **Fire, Cold, Lightning, Acid,** or **Poison**.
This is your **draconic element**, and you gain **Resistance** to it. (You can't change this
choice later.)

Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Slashing damage
(claws and tail), counts as **magical**, and increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at
11th, and **2d6** at 15th. Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one
Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action**, and you can use Unarmed Strikes for any Fluidic feature that
calls for a melee weapon attack.

**Breath Weapon.** As a **Magic action**, you exhale destruction in a **15-foot Cone** or a
**30-foot Line**. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Dragon die +
your Constitution modifier** damage of your draconic element on a failure (half on a success).
You can use your Breath Weapon a number of times equal to your **Constitution modifier** per
Long Rest.

## Level 3: Hybrid Form (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you take the man-beast hybrid shape:

- **Passive — Wyrmscale.** You gain a **+1 bonus to AC** (overlapping scales), a **Fly Speed
  equal to your Speed** (you fall at the end of your turn if you can't hover), and your claws
  gain **Reach 5 feet** beyond normal and deal an extra **Dragon die** on the first hit each
  turn.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Rend or Roar.** Either make one Unarmed Strike that deals an extra
  **Dragon die** and pushes the target 10 feet on a failed **Strength saving throw**, **or** use
  your **Breath Weapon** as this Bonus Action (this doesn't expend a Breath Weapon use).

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Dragon's Fury

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).

Your Breath Weapon roars back faster: at the start of each of your turns, roll a **d6**; on a
**5 or 6** you regain one expended use of your Breath Weapon (in addition to the uses you regain
on a Long Rest). Your damage of your draconic element **ignores Resistance**, and you gain
**Resistance to Frightened** and impose it — when you first hit a creature with a claw or Breath
Weapon on your turn, it makes a **Wisdom saving throw** or is **Frightened** of you until the end
of its next turn.

## Level 10: Full Dragon Form

As a **Bonus Action**, you shed your human shape and become a **dragon** for **10 minutes**. For
the duration:

- Your size becomes **Large** (or **Huge** if space allows), your reach increases by **5 feet**,
  you have a **Fly Speed of 60 feet** with hovering, and your Hit Point maximum increases by
  **twice your Visceralist level** (and you gain that many Hit Points).
- You gain **Multiattack**: whenever you take the Attack action you make a **bite** (an Unarmed
  Strike that deals its damage as Piercing plus an extra **Dragon die** of your draconic
  element) **and** up to two **claw** Unarmed Strikes.
- You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**, and your **Breath
  Weapon** recharges on a **d6 roll of 4–6** at the start of your turn (on top of its normal
  uses) and its Cone becomes **30 feet** / Line becomes **60 feet**.
- Once on each of your turns you can beat your wings: each creature within **15 feet** makes a
  **Strength saving throw** or is knocked **Prone** and pushed **10 feet**.

You can use **Full Dragon Form** twice per Long Rest, or by spending **20 Hit Points** (subject
to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).

## Level 18: The Beast King (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you unleash the whole dragon at last, an ancient horror of scale and
flame that blots the sky, for **1 minute**. For the duration (this supersedes Full Dragon Form):

- Your size becomes **Huge** (or **Gargantuan** if space allows), your reach increases by **10
  feet**, your Fly Speed is **90 feet**, and your **Multiattack** grows to a **bite and three
  claws**. Each of your attacks deals an extra **2d8** damage of your draconic element on a hit.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, and you are **immune** to the **Frightened** condition
  and to your draconic element.
- **Endless Breath.** Your **Breath Weapon** recharges at the start of **each** of your turns
  and its area doubles (**60-foot Cone** or **120-foot Line**), dealing **4d8 + your
  Constitution modifier** of your draconic element on a failed Dexterity save (half on a
  success). Structures and unattended objects caught in it are destroyed.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
