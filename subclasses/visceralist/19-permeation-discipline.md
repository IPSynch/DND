# Discipline of Permeation (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Phantom — Solid When It Counts, and Never When It Doesn't*

You have learned to let the world pass through you. On a whim your flesh goes ghostly and a
sword, a wall, or the ground itself sweeps through you like you were made of fog. Then, in the
instant it matters, you snap solid again — a fist rising out of the floor, a strike from inside a
foe's guard, an ambush from a direction that shouldn't exist. Most enemies never land a blow.
The ones who do find nothing there to hit.

*Inspired by Mirio Togata's Permeation — phasing through matter, striking from impossible angles.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Blurred Sweat
(Blur)*, *Reflexive Hide (Absorb Elements)*, *Muscle Leap (Dimension Door)*, *Loose Joints
(Freedom of Movement)*, *Body Ghost (Etherealness)*.

Your **Permeation die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at
15th.

## Level 3: Permeable Body

You can loosen your flesh out of the physical world at will. You have a **phase Speed equal to
your walking Speed**: you can move through creatures, objects, and structures as though they were
Difficult Terrain, and your movement **doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks**. You can't willingly
**end** your movement inside a solid object or creature; if you're forced to, you're shunted to
the nearest unoccupied space and take **1d6 Force** damage per 5 feet traveled.

Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning or
Force damage, counts as **magical**, and increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at 11th,
and **2d6** at 15th. Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one Unarmed
Strike as a Bonus Action**.

**Ghost Reflex (Reaction).** When you're hit by an attack or fail a Dexterity saving throw, you
can phase so it passes through you, taking **no damage** and suffering none of its effects. You
can do this a number of times equal to your **Constitution modifier** per Long Rest.

## Level 3: Intangible (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you flicker constantly between solid and not:

- **Passive — Ghosting.** You have **Resistance to all damage from nonmagical sources**, you
  automatically ignore **nonmagical Difficult Terrain**, and you are **immune to being Grappled,
  Restrained, or knocked Prone**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Sink & Strike.** You sink into a surface or creature and re-emerge in any
  unoccupied space you can reach within **30 feet**, then make one Unarmed Strike with
  **Advantage** that deals an extra **Permeation die** on a hit (an ambush from an impossible
  angle).

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Selective Solidity

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).

You choose exactly which parts of you are solid at the moment of impact. Your Unarmed Strikes
ignore **cover** and any **AC bonus from a shield** (you strike the flesh, not the guard), and
on the turn you emerge from **Sink & Strike** or from any phasing movement your attacks **ignore
Resistance**. You can also carry a creature you're Grappling with you as you phase (it must be
your size or smaller). Your **Ghost Reflex** uses now refresh on a **Short or Long Rest**.

## Level 10: Living Ghost

You can hold the half-real state and still fight from it.

As a **Bonus Action**, you become **intangible** for up to **1 minute** while remaining fully
able to act (you concentrate on it as though on a spell). While intangible:

- You can move through any creature, object, or structure freely (you still can't willingly end
  inside one), you have a **fly Speed equal to your Speed** (drifting), and you **can't be
  targeted by attacks** or affected by nonmagical physical effects, difficult terrain, or
  environmental hazards.
- Once on each of your turns you can **solidify a strike**: reach a hand into a creature within 5
  feet and snap solid inside it. The target makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking
  **Permeation die + your Constitution modifier** Force damage on a failure — this internal
  strike **ignores AC, cover, and Resistance** and can't be reduced by armor (half damage on a
  success).

You can use **Living Ghost** twice per Long Rest, or by spending **20 Hit Points** (subject to
the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).

## Level 18: The Phantom (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you push almost entirely out of the world for **1 minute**, existing as a
striking ghost that reality can barely touch. For the duration:

- You are **immune to all nonmagical damage** and have **Resistance to all magical damage**, you
  **can't be Grappled, Restrained, Paralyzed, or knocked Prone**, and you have a **fly (phase)
  Speed equal to twice your Speed** that lets you pass through anything at will.
- You are hard to even see coming — attack rolls against you have **Disadvantage**, and once each
  round you can ignore an attack or effect entirely (as **Ghost Reflex**, for free).
- Your **solidify a strike** from Living Ghost no longer requires being intangible and can be used
  as part of **each** of your attacks; each such strike deals **Permeation die + your
  Constitution modifier** Force damage and **bypasses all AC, cover, Resistance, and Immunity to
  nonmagical damage**. Against a Large or larger creature you may instead phase halfway through
  it and detonate: a **20-foot Line** through its body, **8d8** Force damage, Constitution save
  for half.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
