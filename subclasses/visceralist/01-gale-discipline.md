# Discipline of the Gale (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Gaped Vent — War Waged on a Roar of Wind*

You have trained your gut and your gaping backside into a bellows that never stops. You ride a
perpetual jet of your own gas across the sky, blanket the field in a reeking storm that clings to
you wherever you go, and vent hurricane cones and cutting lines of pressurized filth. Nobody wants
to stand near you. That is rather the point.

**Discipline Abilities.** You always have these Fluidic Abilities known; they don't count against
your Abilities Known, and you can cast each once you can cast abilities of its level: *Gas Cloud
(Fog Cloud)*, *Reeking Guardians (Spirit Guardians)*, *Bodily Breath (Dragon's Breath)*, *Fart
Flight (Fly)*, *Killing Fog (Cloudkill)*.

Your **Gale die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Jet Vents (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Perpetual Jets.** You have a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** (you can hover), and
  you ignore Difficult Terrain.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Gale Vent.** You blast a **15-foot Cone or 30-foot Line**. Each creature
  there makes a **Dexterity saving throw** against your spell save DC, taking your **Gale die** in
  Thunder or Poison damage (your choice) on a failure (half on a success) and being **pushed 10
  feet** away on a failure.

## Level 3: Clinging Reek

Any Fluidic Ability you cast that creates an **emanation or aura centered on yourself** (such as
*Reeking Guardians*) is cast **without requiring Concentration**; instead it lasts up to 1 minute
and moves with you. You can have one such ability sustained this way at a time.

## Level 6: Backdraft

Your storm chokes and corrodes. Enemies that fail a save against your **Gale Vent** or your
self-centered emanations gain the **Poisoned** condition until the end of their next turn, you add
your **Constitution modifier** to that damage, and you gain **Resistance to Poison and Thunder
damage**. Your fly speed also increases by **10 feet**.

## Level 10: Hurricane Bowels

The first **Gale Vent** you use on each of your turns costs no Bonus Action (you may use it and
still take another Bonus Action), and your Gale Vent's area becomes a **30-foot Cone or 60-foot
Line**. In addition, while you're in Rut, enemies within any emanation you sustain have their
**Speed halved** and have **Disadvantage on ranged attack rolls** (they're fighting a headwind of
filth).

## Level 18: Category-Five Flatus (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you erupt into a self-sustaining storm for 1 minute (no Concentration). A
**30-foot-radius Emanation** of shrieking, reeking gale roars around you and moves with you:

- You and creatures you choose are unaffected. Each enemy that enters the area for the first time
  on a turn or starts its turn there makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking your **Gale die
  + your Constitution modifier** in Thunder or Poison damage (your choice) on a failure and having
  the **Poisoned** condition until the start of its next turn; on a failure it is also **pushed to
  the edge** of the emanation and knocked **Prone**.
- The area is **Difficult Terrain** for your enemies, ranged attacks into or through it have
  **Disadvantage**, and your Fly Speed doubles.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** (this can't reduce you below 1; if it would, you gain a level of Exhaustion as
normal) to use it again.
