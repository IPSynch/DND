# Discipline of the Brood (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Broodmother — A Womb That Answers Every Question With Teeth*

You have made your body a hatchery. Between your hips is a second, hungrier mouth that gestates
and disgorges little horrors of clotted blood and gristle on command — wet, shrieking things that
exist for one purpose and last exactly one heartbeat. You fling them out and they *pop*: a
lunging bite, a burst of gore, and then nothing but a stain. And when a real fight comes, you
labor out a single true child — a huge, loyal brute that fights at your side until it's carved
apart. Your spawn never crowd the turn order. They live and die inside *your* action, on *your*
command, and then they're gone.

*Inspired by Suguru Geto's swallowed-curse arsenal — an army carried inside one body — reimagined
as a brood birthed, not eaten.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Rotting Touch
(Inflict Wounds)*, *Reeking Guardians (Spirit Guardians)*, *Bile Tendrils (Evard's Black
Tentacles)*, *Swarmspawn (Insect Plague)*, *Ring of Rot (Circle of Death)*.

Your **Brood die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Birth Spawnling

As a **Bonus Action**, you birth a **Spawnling** — a Small blood-creature that immediately hurls
itself at a point you can see within **30 feet** and bursts. It does not persist, take turns of
its own, or roll Initiative — it lives and dies entirely within this Bonus Action. Choose one:

- **Burster.** Each creature within 5 feet of the point makes a **Dexterity saving throw**,
  taking **Brood die + your Constitution modifier** Necrotic or Acid damage (half on a success).
- **Latcher.** One creature within 5 feet of the point makes a **Dexterity saving throw**;
  on a failure it takes **Brood die** damage and is **Grappled** (escape DC equal to your spell
  save DC) as the Spawnling clamps on. At the start of that creature's next turn the Spawnling
  bursts for another **Brood die** and releases.

You can birth one Spawnling per Bonus Action.

## Level 3: Gravid (Primed)

While in **Rut**, your womb quickens and never quite empties:

- **Passive — Teeming.** When you birth a Spawnling with your Bonus Action you birth **two**
  (each targets its own point), and each Spawnling's damage increases by **your Constitution
  modifier**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Convulsive Birth.** You expel a Spawnling in a **15-foot Cone**. Each
  creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Brood die + your Constitution
  modifier** Necrotic or Acid damage (half on a success). You then gain **Temporary Hit Points
  equal to your Visceralist level** from the afterbirth.

## Level 6: The Prime

As a **Magic action**, you labor out your **Prime** — a single Large blood-born beast that fights
at your side for up to **1 hour**. Unlike your Spawnlings it persists, but it does **not** roll
Initiative and never clutters the turn order: it acts on **your** turn, and you command it (its
move and one action) as part of taking a **Bonus Action**. If you don't command it, it Dodges.

The Prime uses these statistics:

- **AC** 14 + your Constitution modifier · **HP** equal to **five times your Visceralist level**
  · **Speed** 40 ft · it uses **your spell save DC** and **your ability attack modifier**.
- **Immunities:** Poison, and the Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion, and Poisoned conditions.
- **Gore (attack).** Reach 10 ft: **2d8 + your Constitution modifier** Piercing damage.
- **Death Throes.** When the Prime drops to 0 Hit Points or you dismiss it (no action), it
  bursts: each creature within 10 feet makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Brood die**
  Necrotic or Acid damage (half on a success).

You can have only **one** Prime at a time. Once the Prime is gone, you can birth a new one after
you finish a Short or Long Rest, or immediately by spending **15 Hit Points**.

## Level 10: Prolific Womb

Your fertility becomes monstrous.

- **Litter.** When you birth Spawnlings with a Bonus Action (or Convulsive Birth), you birth a
  number equal to **your Constitution modifier** (minimum two), dividing them among any points
  within range as you choose.
- **Harvest.** Whenever a creature you can see drops to 0 Hit Points within 30 feet of you, you
  can birth **one Spawnling immediately** (no action required), once per turn.
- **Empowered Prime.** Your Prime gains **Multiattack** (two Gore attacks), its Hit Points
  become **seven times your Visceralist level**, and its **Death Throes** deals your **Brood
  die + your Constitution modifier** in a 15-foot radius.

## Level 18: Ravenous Brood (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you give yourself over to a screaming birthing-frenzy for **1 minute**.
For the duration:

- At the start of each of your turns, you birth a **wave** of Spawnlings: choose a **30-foot
  Cone** or a **20-foot-radius Sphere** within 60 feet. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity
  saving throw**, taking **6d8** Necrotic or Acid damage (half on a success) — and this wave
  costs you no action.
- Your **Prime** is reborn at full Hit Points if it had fallen, its **Gore** deals an extra
  **2d8**, and its **Death Throes** doubles in radius.
- You gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Visceralist level** at the start of each
  of your turns, and while you have any of these Temporary Hit Points you have **Resistance to
  all damage**.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
