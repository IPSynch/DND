# Discipline of Velocity (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Blur — You Don't Go Around People. You Go Through Them.*

Your blood runs at a boil and your muscles fire faster than a heartbeat should allow. You don't
trade blows — you *pass* through them. Your whole style is the charge: you pick a line, you drop
your shoulder, and you run clean through everything standing on it, leaving a wake of broken
people and a couple of afterimages that haven't caught up to where you already are. Stand still
and you're a target. So you never do.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Fast Twitch
(Expeditious Retreat)*, *Bounding Legs (Jump)*, *Blurred Sweat (Blur)*, *Muscle Leap (Dimension
Door)*, *Overclock (Time Stop)*.

Your **Velocity die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at
15th.

## Level 3: Run-Through

Your walking Speed increases by **10 feet** while you wear no armor. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage
option) deals **1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning or Slashing damage and counts as
**magical**.

Once on each of your turns, you can make a **Run-Through**: move in a straight line up to your
Speed. This movement **doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks**, and you can move through the space
of any creature (its space is Difficult Terrain for this movement, but you don't stop). The first
time you pass through or within 5 feet of each creature during the movement, it makes a
**Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Velocity die + your Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning or
Slashing damage on a failure (half on a success). A creature can be struck only once per
Run-Through. You must end this movement in an unoccupied space.

## Level 3: Overdrive (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you redline:

- **Passive — Redline.** Your Speed increases by an additional **15 feet**, you can take the
  **Dash** action as a **Bonus Action**, your Run-Through deals an extra **Velocity die**, and
  you have **Advantage on saving throws against being Grappled, Restrained, or knocked Prone**
  (you simply aren't there anymore).
- **Rut Bonus Action — Second Pass.** Make an **additional Run-Through** this turn.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Afterimage

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack
action), and you can **replace any one of those attacks with a Run-Through**.

You leave streaks of yourself behind: if you moved at least **20 feet** on your turn, attack
rolls against you have **Disadvantage** until the start of your next turn. In addition, during a
Run-Through you can **change direction once** (bending your line into an L-shape), and each
creature that fails its save against your Run-Through is also knocked **Prone**.

## Level 10: Sound Barrier

Your speed crosses into the uncanny.

- Your walking Speed **doubles**, you can move across **liquids and up vertical surfaces** during
  your movement without falling, and you can take **two Run-Throughs** on each of your turns
  (each still only once per creature, but a creature can be caught by both).
- When a creature you can see makes an attack roll against you, you can use your **Reaction** to
  blur out of the way, imposing **Disadvantage** on the attack; if it still hits, you take **no**
  damage from it and immediately move up to half your Speed without provoking Opportunity
  Attacks. You can do this a number of times equal to your **Constitution modifier** per Long
  Rest.
- Once on each of your turns, when a creature fails its save against your Run-Through, its Speed
  is **halved** and it **can't take Reactions** until the start of its next turn (you've knocked
  the wind and the footing out of it).

## Level 18: Godspeed (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you accelerate past what flesh should survive for **1 minute**. For the
duration:

- You can take **one additional action** on each of your turns, your Speed **triples**, and every
  creature struck by your Run-Through takes the **maximum** result of your Velocity dice instead
  of rolling.
- You have **Resistance to all damage** (the blow never quite lands where you are), and once each
  round when you are hit by an attack or fail a Dexterity saving throw, you can choose to **take
  no damage and suffer none of its effects**, then move up to half your Speed.
- **Sonic Run.** Once on each of your turns you can make a **supersonic Run-Through** that ignores
  the "once per creature" limit and can pass through the same creature repeatedly along a **120-
  foot line**: each creature on the line makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **8d8**
  Bludgeoning or Force damage on a failure (half on a success), and you may end anywhere along
  the line.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
