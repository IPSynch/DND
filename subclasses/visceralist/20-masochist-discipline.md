# Discipline of the Masochist (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Flagellant — Every Wound Is a Gift, and You Are Very Generous With Yourself*

Pain doesn't slow you. Pain *tunes* you. Where other fighters flinch from the killing blow, you
lean into it, grinning through the blood, because the closer you are to death the more terribly
alive you become. A gash makes your fists heavier; a punctured lung makes your skin harder; and
at the ragged edge of dying you are the deadliest thing in the room. You are not reckless. You
have simply run the numbers, and the numbers say: *bleed*.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Caustic
Reprisal (Hellish Rebuke)*, *Clench (Shield)*, *Bloodletting Grasp (Vampiric Touch)*, *Adrenal
Rush (Haste)*, *Battle Metabolism (Tenser's Transformation)*.

Your **Wound die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Suffering Made Strength

Your power rises as your Hit Points fall. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your
Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning or Force damage, counts as **magical**, and you can make
**one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action** on your turn (you don't need to have taken the Attack
action first). You benefit from the following thresholds:

- **Bloodied (current Hit Points at or below half your maximum):** you gain a **+2 bonus to
  damage rolls**, you have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**, and the
  first time you hit a creature on each of your turns it takes an extra **Wound die**.
- **Broken (current Hit Points at or below one-quarter your maximum):** in addition to the
  Bloodied benefits, you have **Advantage on attack rolls** and a **+2 bonus to AC and to all
  saving throws**.

In addition, the first time you take damage on each of your turns, your **next** attack before
the end of your next turn deals an extra **Wound die**.

## Level 3: Ecstatic Agony (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you feed on your own suffering:

- **Passive — Pain Is Pleasure.** You count as **Bloodied** for all your Masochist features even
  when your Hit Points are above half, and you have **Advantage on saving throws against being
  Frightened or Charmed** — you're already somewhere they can't follow.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Offer Flesh.** You wound yourself for Hit Points equal to **five times
  your Constitution modifier** (this can't reduce you below 1). Until the end of your next turn
  you count as **Broken** regardless of your actual Hit Points, and your attacks deal an extra
  **Wound die**.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Numb to Pain

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).

You endure what should end you. While **Bloodied**, reduce all damage you take by your
**Constitution modifier** (per instance); while **Broken**, reduce it by **twice your
Constitution modifier** instead. And when you would drop to 0 Hit Points but aren't killed
outright, you can drop to **1** instead; you can do this once per **Short or Long Rest**.

## Level 10: Death's Door Power

At the brink you become something that shouldn't still be standing. While you are **Broken**:

- Your attacks **ignore Resistance**, your critical hits land on a roll of **19 or 20**, and the
  first time you hit a creature on each of your turns you deal the **maximum** result of your
  Unarmed Strike and Wound dice instead of rolling.
- You have **Advantage on all saving throws**.

You also gain a third threshold. While your current Hit Points are **at or below your Visceralist
level** (**On Death's Door**), you have **Resistance to all damage**, and the first time each
round you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points, you instead drop to **1**.

## Level 18: Apotheosis of Agony (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you throw yourself wholly onto the altar of your own pain for **1
minute** — the transcendent state you've been chasing every time you bled. For the duration:

- You count as being at **1 Hit Point** for **all** of your Masochist features (Bloodied, Broken,
  and On Death's Door are **all active** no matter your actual Hit Points), so every bonus above
  applies at once.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, you **can't be reduced below 1 Hit Point** by anything,
  and each of your hits deals an extra **2d8** Force damage.
- **Reprisal Nova.** Whenever you take damage, the wound answers back: each creature of your
  choice within **15 feet** takes **Wound die + your Constitution modifier** Necrotic or Force
  damage (no save, once per instance of damage you take).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule). *(Yes — using
your last resort by nearly killing yourself is entirely in character.)*
