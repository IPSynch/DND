# Discipline of Coitus (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Lover — Ecstasy Given and Taken*

You have made a sacrament of sex. Your seed is a reagent of raw vitality: you anoint your allies'
weapons until they bite like the divine, fill your friends with borrowed stamina, mend their wounds
with a warm slick of yourself, and — when the moment peaks — let go with a holy climax that leaves
your enemies senseless and adoring. You fight nude and shameless, because every part of you is a
tool, and every tool of yours is generous.

> **Tone.** A frank, bawdy, sex-positive discipline, written tasteful rather than explicit. Reskin
> "seed," "release," and "climax" to "essence," "effluence," and "surge" if your table prefers.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Fluid Mending
(Cure Wounds)*, *Steady Nerves (Enhance Ability)*, *Invigorating Musk (Aura of Vitality)*, *Adrenal
Rush (Haste)*, *Word of Vigor (Power Word Fortify)*.

Your **Ardor die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Anointing Release

As a **Bonus Action**, you anoint a creature or weapon within 30 feet with your seed. Choose one
mode (each lasts 1 minute, no Concentration, unless noted):

- **Lather (a weapon).** The wielder's attacks with it deal an extra **Ardor die** damage (your
  choice of the weapon's type or Radiant) on a hit.
- **Fill (a creature).** The target gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Ardor die** and has
  **Advantage on its next attack roll or saving throw**.
- **Mend (a creature).** The target regains **Ardor die + your Constitution modifier** Hit Points
  (instantaneous).

The three modes above are an **unlimited ability — you can use them any number of times, with no
cost and no daily limit.**

**Deep Coat (lasting).** Instead of the quick modes above, you (or a willing ally) can spend **1
minute** working a **fist** or a **bludgeoning weapon** thoroughly inside your own vagina or anus,
painting it to the hilt in a heavy, lasting glaze of seed. Doing so costs **10 Hit Points** (the
stretch hurts as much as it thrills — subject to the class can't-drop-below-1 rule). **Until you
finish a Long Rest**, the coated **Unarmed Strikes** (if you used a fist) or the coated
**bludgeoning weapon** deal an extra **1d6** damage on a hit (the weapon's own type or Radiant,
chosen when coated). This bonus is **weaker than Lather but far longer-lasting**, needs no
Concentration, and a creature can benefit from only **one Deep Coat** at a time.

## Level 3: In the Throes (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Generous Body.** Whenever you anoint or heal a creature (including with Anointing
  Release), you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier**, and you have
  **Resistance to Psychic damage**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Climax.** Let go in a **15-foot Cone** *or* at a single creature within 30
  feet. Each target makes a **Wisdom saving throw**, taking your **Ardor die** in Psychic or
  Radiant damage on a failure (half on a success). A single creature that fails is also **Charmed**
  by you (or, your choice, **Incapacitated**) until the end of its next turn.

## Level 6: Prolific & Refreshing

Your output grows copious and restorative. When you use **Anointing Release**, you can anoint
**two** targets at once.

**Restorative Coupling.** **Only outside of combat**, you can share a few minutes of sex with a
willing creature; when you do, you spend **10 Hit Points** to let it **regain one expended limited
resource of its choice** — a use of a class feature, a maneuver/Superiority die, a Bardic
Inspiration die, a point of Ki/Focus, a Rage, or a spent spell slot of **3rd level or lower**
(subject to the class can't-drop-below-1 rule). A given creature can benefit from Restorative
Coupling only **once per Short or Long Rest**.

## Level 10: Aura of Ecstasy

You radiate an intoxicating warmth in a 15-foot Emanation. Allies in it add a **d6** to their
attack rolls and saving throws and have **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened**,
while enemies in it have **Disadvantage on saving throws against your Climax and your Fluidic
Abilities**. Once on each of your turns, when an enemy in the aura fails a save against one of your
features, you or an ally in the aura regains **Hit Points equal to your Ardor die**.

## Level 18: The Grand Climax (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you release everything at once in overwhelming, radiant rapture. First,
choose any number of allies within 30 feet: each is simultaneously **Lathered** (on a weapon it
wields) **and Filled**, and regains **Hit Points equal to your Ardor die + your Visceralist level**.
Then every enemy within a **30-foot Emanation** around you makes a **Wisdom saving throw**:

- **On a failure:** it takes **10d8** Psychic or Radiant damage (your choice) and is **Charmed by
  you** for 1 minute; a creature that fails by 5 or more is instead **Incapacitated** with rapture
  until the end of its next turn.
- **On a success:** half damage, and it is **Frightened** of you until the start of its next turn.

A Charmed creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again.
