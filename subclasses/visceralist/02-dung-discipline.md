# Discipline of the Dung (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Filthsmith — A Blade Drawn From the Bowels*

You are an artist, and your medium is shit. You pack it, harden it, and shape it into blades,
bombs, and shackles faster than the eye can follow, dancing through a fight with a reeking weapon
in hand and a fresh one always ready. Your filth crushes like a maul and rots like the grave, and
when you need someone to stop moving, you simply encase them in your own quick-setting waste.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Filth Bomb
(Fireball)*, *Burning Filth Blade (Flame Blade)*, *Bile Tendrils (Evard's Black Tentacles)*,
*Giant Filth Hand (Bigby's Hand)*, *Petrifying Secretion (Flesh to Stone)*.

Your **Dung die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Filth-Forged Arsenal

As a **Bonus Action**, you excrete and harden a mass of filth into a weapon of your choice (any
Simple or Martial melee weapon). It is **magical**, deals **Force or Necrotic** damage (your
choice when made), and you use **Constitution** for its attack and damage rolls. It has the
**Reach** and **Thrown (30/120)** properties, and when thrown it bursts into sludge and a new one
**re-forms in your hand** instantly. It lasts until you finish a Long Rest or manifest another.

## Level 3: Bladefilth Dance (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Caked Plating.** Hardened filth armors you: your **AC increases by 2**, and you have
  **Advantage on saving throws to avoid being disarmed or knocked Prone**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Setting Bomb.** You lob a flash-hardening lump at a point within 60 feet;
  each creature in a **10-foot radius** makes a **Strength saving throw** or is **Restrained** in
  quick-setting filth (a Restrained creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns; you
  can also choose to have it take your **Dung die** in Force or Necrotic damage on a fail).

## Level 6: Filth-Dancer

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).
In addition, when you take the Attack action, you can **cast a cantrip in place of one of those
attacks**, and each hit with your Filth-Forged weapon deals an extra **1d8 Force or Necrotic**
damage (this die is included in, and scales with, your Dung die progression: 1d8 → 2d8 → 3d8 →
4d8 by the same levels).

## Level 10: Shrapnel Burst

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your Filth-Forged weapon, filth explodes
off the blow: each other creature of your choice within **10 feet** of the target takes your
**Constitution modifier** in Force or Necrotic damage. When a creature Restrained by your Setting
Bomb fails its save to escape, it also takes your **Dung die** in damage as the shell crushes
inward.

## Level 18: Colossal Ordure (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you sheathe yourself in a titanic, animate suit of hardened filth for 1
minute. For the duration:

- Your **size becomes Large** (or larger, if already Large), your reach increases by **5 feet**,
  and you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Visceralist level** (refreshed at the
  start of each of your turns).
- You make **one additional attack** with your Filth-Forged weapon whenever you take the Attack
  action, and each hit deals an **extra 2d8 Force or Necrotic** damage.
- Once on each of your turns, you can slam the ground as part of your Attack action: each creature
  of your choice within **20 feet** makes a **Strength saving throw**, taking **6d8 Force** damage
  and being knocked **Prone** and **Restrained** on a failure (half damage, no conditions, on a
  success).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** to use it again.
