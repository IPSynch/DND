# Discipline of the Flood (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Wellspring — Drowning Foes and Blessing Friends*

Your bladder is a bottomless font, and you have learned to make it sacred. You hose down the
battlefield in acrid torrents that put enemies on their backs, flood the ground into an impassable
mire, and — because your fluids carry more than filth — you douse your allies in warm, invigorating
streams that leave them stronger, faster, and stubbornly hard to kill. Undignified? Absolutely.
Effective? Ask the people still standing in your puddle.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Slick (Grease)*,
*Endless Flow (Create or Destroy Water)*, *Sludge Field (Slow)*, *Invigorating Musk (Aura of
Vitality)*, *Killing Fog (Cloudkill)*.

Your **Flood die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Pressure Bladder (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Slipstream.** You can't be knocked **Prone**, **Grappled**, or **Restrained**, and a
  15-foot Emanation around you is **Difficult Terrain** for your enemies (a slick of your making).
- **Rut Bonus Action — Pressure Stream.** You fire a **30-foot Line** of high-pressure discharge;
  each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking your **Flood die** in Acid damage
  and being knocked **Prone** on a failure (half damage, no Prone, on a success).

## Level 3: Blessed Waters

As a **Bonus Action**, you douse a creature within 30 feet (including yourself) in invigorating
fluid. It gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier + half your Visceralist
level**, and until the start of your next turn it adds a **d4** to its attack rolls, ability
checks, and saving throws (this doesn't require Concentration). **This is an unlimited ability —
you can use it any number of times, with no cost and no daily limit.**

## Level 6: Fields of Flow

Your area abilities discriminate friend from foe. Whenever you create an area with a Fluidic
Ability or a Flood feature, you can choose any creatures in it to be unaffected by its harmful
effects; further, **allies of your choice** in that area gain **Resistance to the ability's damage
type** (if any) and regain **Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier** the first time each
turn they start there. You also gain **Resistance to Acid damage**.

## Level 10: Golden Deluge

When you use **Blessed Waters**, you can splash **up to three** creatures at once, and its d4
becomes a **d6**. In addition, as a **Magic action**, you can unleash a cleansing flood in a
**20-foot-radius** area within 60 feet: allies there are cured of the **Blinded, Deafened,
Poisoned,** or **one** other condition of your choice and regain **Flood die** Hit Points, while
enemies there make a **Constitution saving throw** or are **Slowed** (as the *Slow* spell) until
the start of your next turn.

## Level 18: The Great Flood (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you rupture into a standing tidal surge for 1 minute (no Concentration). A
**30-foot-radius Emanation** of churning fluid surrounds you and moves with you:

- The area is **Difficult Terrain** for your enemies and open water for your allies (they ignore
  it). Allies in it have **Advantage on saving throws** and regain **Hit Points equal to your
  Constitution modifier** at the start of each of their turns.
- For as long as an ally remains in the emanation, it adds a bonus **2d4** to its **attack rolls,
  saving throws, ability checks, and its damage rolls** (add the 2d4 to each such roll's total).
- Each enemy that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a
  **Strength saving throw**, taking your **Flood die + your Constitution modifier** in Acid damage
  and being knocked **Prone** and pushed 10 feet on a failure; a creature that fails is also
  **Slowed** until the start of its next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** to use it again.
