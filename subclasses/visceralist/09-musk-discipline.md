# Discipline of the Musk (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Pheromancer — Sweat, Scent, and Absolute Seduction*

Your sweat and secretions carry engineered pheromones so potent they rewrite the minds that breathe
them. You exude an intoxicating musk that leaves enemies weak-kneed and adoring and allies flushed
with courage. You don't need to win the fight — you need only make everyone *want* you to.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Nauseating Mist
(Ray of Sickness)*, *Nerve Lock (Hold Person)*, *Invigorating Musk (Aura of Vitality)*, *Blood
Puppetry (Hold Monster)*, *Melt Mind (Feeblemind)*.

Your **Musk die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Intoxicating Cloud

You emit a 10-foot Emanation of heady scent that you can turn on or off (no action required). While
active, allies of your choice in it have **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened**,
and each enemy in it has **Disadvantage on saving throws against your Charmed and Frightened
effects**. Your Charm and Fear effects **ignore immunity to the Charmed/Frightened conditions**,
treating such immunity as merely Advantage on the save instead.

## Level 3: In Heat (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Overpowering.** Enemies within 30 feet of you have **Disadvantage on Wisdom saving
  throws** against your Fluidic Abilities, and you have **Resistance to Psychic damage**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Waft.** Choose a creature within 30 feet; it makes a **Wisdom saving throw**
  or takes your **Musk die** in Psychic damage and is **Charmed or Frightened** (your choice) until
  the end of its next turn. A creature Charmed this way can't take Reactions and has Disadvantage on
  attacks against creatures other than you.

## Level 6: Bewitching Presence

When a creature you can see within 30 feet targets you with an attack, you can use your
**Reaction** to force a **Wisdom saving throw**; on a failure it must choose a new target (if any)
or lose the attack, and it takes your **Musk die** in Psychic damage. Creatures Charmed or
Frightened by you have **Disadvantage on all saving throws** against your Fluidic Abilities.

## Level 10: Rapturous Haze

Your aura grows to a **20-foot Emanation**, and once on each of your turns, when an enemy fails a
saving throw against one of your Fluidic Abilities or Musk features, you can either **Charm** it
(as above) until the end of its next turn or heal an ally within the aura for your **Musk die**. In
addition, you are **immune to the Charmed and Frightened conditions**.

## Level 18: Avatar of Allure (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you saturate the field with irresistible musk for 1 minute (no
Concentration). Every enemy within a **30-foot Emanation** around you makes a **Wisdom saving
throw** at the start of each of its turns:

- **On a failure:** it takes your **Musk die + your Constitution modifier** Psychic damage and is
  **Charmed by you** (your thralls attack whom you choose and can't target you); a creature that
  fails by 5 or more instead falls **Incapacitated** with adoration until the end of its next turn.
- **On a success:** half damage, and it is instead **Frightened** of you until the start of its
  next turn.

Allies within the emanation have **Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws**. Once you use this
feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend **50 Hit Points** to
use it again.
