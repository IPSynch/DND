# Discipline of Elasticity (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Rubber Body — Every Limb a Whip, a Spring, and a Cannon*

You have rendered your own flesh into living rubber. You stretch your limbs across a room to strike,
snap back to dodge, inflate yourself to absorb a blow, and pump your blood into "gears" that make
you faster, bigger, and more absurd by the second. Blunt weapons bounce off you and you find the
whole thing rather funny.

*Inspired by Monkey D. Luffy — a body that stretches, bounces, and shifts through gears.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Tissue Swell
(Enlarge/Reduce)*, *Bounding Legs (Jump)*, *Fluidic Grip (Telekinesis)*, *Battle Metabolism
(Tenser's Transformation)*, *Flesh Reshaping (Polymorph)*.

Your **Stretch die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Rubber Body

Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning damage,
counts as **magical**, and has **Reach 15 feet** (your limbs stretch). You can Grapple creatures up
to 15 feet away, and you have **Resistance to Bludgeoning damage** and **Advantage on saving throws
against being Grappled or Restrained** (you just slip out). Immediately after you take the Attack
action, you can make **one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action**.

## Level 3: Gear Second (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you shift into **Gear Second**, pumping your blood at high pressure:

- **Passive — Gear Second.** Your Speed increases by **15 feet**, your Unarmed reach increases to
  **20 feet**, and your Unarmed Strike die increases by one step.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Pistol/Bazooka.** Wind up and fire a limb: make one Unarmed Strike against a
  creature within 30 feet; on a hit it takes your **Stretch die + your Constitution modifier**
  extra Bludgeoning damage and is pushed **15 feet** (or you can pull yourself to it).

## Level 6: Extra Attack, Balloon & Gear Third

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).

**Balloon (Reaction).** When you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can inflate to
absorb it, **halving that damage**. You can do this a number of times per Long Rest equal to your
Constitution modifier.

**Gear Third (giant limbs).** Once on each of your turns, you can enlarge a limb for a single
strike: choose one Unarmed Strike to affect a **15-foot Cone or 30-foot Line** (or to hit a
Huge-or-smaller creature as a single giant fist). Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving
throw**, taking your **Stretch die + your Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning damage and being
knocked **Prone** on a failure (half damage, no Prone, on a success).

## Level 10: Gear Fourth

As a **Bonus Action**, you sheathe your rubber body in compressed muscle and take to bouncing on
the air itself for **1 minute** (a lesser transformation than your capstone). While it lasts:

- You gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Visceralist level** (refreshed at the start of each
  of your turns), a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** (you bounce off the air and can hover), and
  your Unarmed reach becomes **25 feet**.
- Your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **1d8 Bludgeoning or Force** damage and, on a hit, can bounce
  the target up to **15 feet** in a direction of your choice.
- When you're hit by an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to rebound: reduce the damage by your
  **Stretch die + your Constitution modifier**, and if any damage is prevented you may snap back to
  make one Unarmed Strike against the attacker if it's within reach.

You can use **Gear Fourth** twice per Long Rest, or by spending **20 Hit Points** (subject to the
usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).

## Level 18: Gear Fifth (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you flood yourself into a gleefully unreal "awakened" state for 1 minute.
For the duration:

- Your size becomes **Large** (or larger), your Unarmed reach becomes **30 feet**, your Speed
  doubles, and you make **two additional Unarmed Strikes** whenever you take the Attack action.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, and once per round when you're hit, you can **reduce the
  damage to 0** and bounce the attacker 15 feet (cartoon-rubber logic).
- Each of your Unarmed Strikes deals an extra **2d8** Bludgeoning or Force damage and can affect
  the Cone/Line of **Gear Third** without limit on how many times per turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again.
