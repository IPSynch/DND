# Discipline of Bone (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Osseous — Every Skeleton Is a Weapon Rack Waiting to Be Opened*

Where the Sinew brawler pours power into muscle, you reach one layer deeper — to the frame. Your
skeleton is not a fixed thing. You grow it, sharpen it, and push it out through your own skin:
blades sprouting from your forearms, a palisade of spikes bursting up out of the ground, a ribcage
of plate that turns aside a greataxe. Bone is the last thing to rot and the first thing you reach
for. You will never be disarmed, because your arms *are* the armory.

*A body-horror bladesmith in the vein of Kimimaro — the skeleton reshaped into an endless supply
of weapons and walls.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Clench
(Shield)*, *Reflexive Hide (Absorb Elements)*, *Hardened Hide (Stoneskin)*, *Bone Bramble (Wall
of Thorns)*, *Bodyquake (Earthquake)*.

Your **Bone die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Bone Weaponry

You extrude blades of living bone from your body. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals
**1d8 + your Constitution modifier** Piercing or Slashing damage, counts as **magical**, and
increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at 11th, and **2d6** at 15th. Immediately after you
take the Attack action, you can make **one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action**, and you can use
Unarmed Strikes for any Fluidic feature that calls for a melee weapon attack.

You bristle with spurs: while you wear no armor and no shield, whenever a creature within 5 feet
hits you with a melee attack it takes **your Constitution modifier** in Piercing damage. And you
can fire a shard as a ranged option — as part of your Attack action, in place of a melee attack,
you can throw a **Bone Dart** (a ranged spell attack out to 30/60 feet) dealing **Bone die + your
Constitution modifier** Piercing damage.

## Level 3: Osseous Bulwark (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you sheathe yourself in overgrown bone:

- **Passive — Bone Armor.** You gain a **+2 bonus to AC** (interlocking plates), you have
  **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**, and your retaliatory spurs deal
  your **Bone die** instead of your Constitution modifier.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Impale.** A spike of bone erupts beneath a creature within 15 feet, which
  makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Bone die + your Constitution modifier** Piercing
  damage and being **Restrained** (skewered) on a failure. A Restrained creature can use an
  action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC to tear free, or repeat the save at
  the end of each of its turns.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Exoskeleton

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack
action).

Dermal bone hardens you permanently: while you wear no armor you gain a **+1 bonus to AC**, and
you have **Resistance to Piercing damage** (your marrow is dense as stone). As a **Reaction** when
you or a creature within 5 feet is targeted by an attack, you can raise a slab of bone: the target
gains **half cover** (+2 AC and +2 to Dexterity saves) against that attack, and if the attack
misses because of it, the bone shatters into shards dealing **Bone die** Piercing damage to the
attacker if it's within 5 feet.

## Level 10: Living Ossuary

Your skeleton becomes an inexhaustible arsenal.

- **Blade Storm.** When you take the Attack action, you can forgo your normal attacks to launch a
  volley of **Bone Darts** at up to a number of creatures equal to your **Constitution modifier**
  (one dart each, separate attack rolls, each dealing **Bone die + your Constitution modifier**
  Piercing).
- **Bone Field.** As a **Magic action**, you erupt a **20-foot-radius** thicket of jagged bone at
  a point within 60 feet. It is **Difficult Terrain**, and each creature caught in the eruption
  (and each that enters or ends its turn there) makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **Bone
  die** Piercing damage and being **Restrained** on a failure until it tears free (as **Impale**).
  The field lasts 1 minute.
- **Regrowing Frame.** At the start of each of your turns while in Rut, you knit fresh bone,
  gaining **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Bone die**.

## Level 18: Bone Cathedral (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you turn your whole skeleton inside-out into a fortress of blades, and the
ground with it, for **1 minute**. For the duration:

- You are encased in a bone exoskeleton: your size can increase to **Large**, your reach grows by
  **5 feet**, you have **Resistance to all damage** and a **+2 bonus to AC**, and you make **one
  additional Unarmed Strike** whenever you take the Attack action (each dealing an extra **2d8**
  Piercing on a hit).
- The battlefield answers you: at the start of each of your turns you can raise or reshape a
  **40-foot-radius** cathedral of spikes centered on you or a point within 60 feet. Each enemy
  there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **6d8** Piercing damage and being
  **Restrained** on a failure (half damage, no Restrain, on a success), and the area is Difficult
  Terrain for your enemies only.
- Once on each of your turns you can fire a **Bone Barrage**: a **60-foot Line**, **Dexterity
  saving throw**, **6d8** Piercing on a failure (half on a success).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
