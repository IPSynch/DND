# Discipline of the Ooze (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Slime-Bag — Mucus, Phlegm, and Digestive Bile*

You have given up on being solid. You weep slime, hock corrosive phlegm, and vomit digestive bile
that eats through armor, flesh, and stone alike. You mire the ground into a slick trap, glue foes
in place, and slough your own edges away so blades find nothing to bite. Fighting you is like
fighting a puddle that dissolves you back.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Sticky Strands
(Web)*, *Caustic Spray (Tasha's Caustic Brew)*, *Sludge Field (Slow)*, *Acid Sphere (Vitriolic
Sphere)*, *Killing Fog (Cloudkill)*.

Your **Ooze die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Slime Body

You weep a constant slick. A 10-foot Emanation around you is **Difficult Terrain** for your
enemies, you have **Advantage on saving throws and checks to escape a Grapple or the Restrained
condition**, and you have **Resistance to Acid damage**. As part of casting a Fluidic Ability that
deals Acid damage, you can spit corrosively: one creature that takes the damage also has its **AC
reduced by 1** until the end of its next turn (this stacks, to a maximum of −5).

## Level 3: Corrosive Bile (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Digestive.** Your Acid damage **ignores Resistance** and treats **Immunity as
  Resistance**, and a creature that takes Acid damage from you is coated: it takes your
  **Constitution modifier** in Acid damage at the start of its next turn.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Bile Hock.** Spit at a single creature within 30 feet or in a **15-foot
  Cone**; each target makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking your **Ooze die** in Acid damage
  on a failure (half on a success) and having its **AC reduced by 1** on a failure.

## Level 6: Engulfing Mire & Acid Skin

A creature that starts its turn in your Slime Body emanation makes a **Strength saving throw** or
has its **Speed halved** and can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn. In addition,
when a creature within 5 feet hits you with a melee attack, it takes your **Constitution modifier**
in Acid damage (reactive slime), and you gain **immunity to being Grappled or Restrained**.

## Level 10: Dissolve

Your acid ruins everything it touches. When a creature fails a save against your Acid damage, you
can force it to make a **Constitution saving throw**; on a failure, one nonmagical weapon or armor
it wears or holds takes a **permanent −2 penalty** (armor to AC, weapon to damage) until repaired,
and the creature is **Restrained** in clinging sludge until the end of its next turn. You can also
turn any Acid-dealing Fluidic Ability's area into lingering caustic pools that persist until the
start of your next turn, dealing your **Ooze die** to any enemy that ends its turn there.

## Level 18: The Living Deluge (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you liquefy into a semi-solid horror for 1 minute. For the duration:

- You gain **Resistance to all damage** except Force, you can move through any space as narrow as 1
  inch without squeezing, and you can't be knocked Prone, Grappled, or Restrained.
- Your Slime Body emanation grows to **20 feet**, and any enemy that enters it for the first time
  on a turn or starts its turn there takes your **Ooze die + your Constitution modifier** in Acid
  damage (Dexterity save halves) and has its AC reduced by 1.
- Once on each of your turns, you can try to **engulf** a Large or smaller creature within 10 feet:
  it makes a **Strength saving throw** or is pulled inside you, **Restrained and Blinded**, taking
  **6d8 Acid** at the start of each of your turns until it escapes (Strength save at end of its
  turns).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** to use it again.
