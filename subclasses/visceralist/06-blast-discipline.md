# Discipline of the Blast (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Detonator — Nitroglycerin Sweat and a Very Short Fuse*

Your sweat isn't salt water — it's an unstable, nitroglycerin-like oil that you can set off at will.
You clap it into concussive blasts, spray it in searing cones, and rocket across the battlefield on
the recoil of your own explosions. You are loud, you are volatile, and you leave scorch marks
everywhere you've been.

*Inspired by Katsuki Bakugo — mobility and murder, both powered by explosions.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Scalding Spray
(Burning Hands)*, *Scalding Rays (Scorching Ray)*, *Filth Bomb (Fireball)*, *Pore Burst
(Destructive Wave)*, *Fermenting Filth Bomb (Delayed Blast Fireball)*.

Your **Blast die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Detonation

You can ignite your sweat as a weapon. As a **Magic action**, you set off a blast — choose a single
creature within 60 feet or a **15-foot Cone** from you. Each target makes a **Dexterity saving
throw** against your spell save DC, taking your **Blast die + your Constitution modifier** in Fire
damage on a failure (half on a success). Whenever you deal Fire damage with this or a Fluidic
Ability, you can immediately **launch yourself up to 15 feet** in any direction (this movement
doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks and can send you upward).

## Level 3: Blastpack (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Recoil Flight.** You have a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** (you fall at the end of
  your turn if you can't hover), and your Fire damage **ignores Resistance**.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Kick Blast.** Trigger an extra Detonation as a Bonus Action (its cone is
  **15 feet** or single target), and it can propel you an additional 15 feet.

## Level 6: Bigger Bang

Your explosions escalate. Your Detonation's single-target range increases to 120 feet and its cone
becomes **30 feet**, and your Fire damage (from any source) scores its dice as if you rolled at
**Advantage** — roll each Fire damage die twice and take the higher — once on each of your turns.
You also gain **Resistance to Fire damage** and **immunity to your own explosions'** damage.

**Blast Away (Reaction).** When you take damage, you can use your Reaction to detonate beneath
yourself, **halving that damage** and launching yourself up to **15 feet** in a direction of your
choice (this movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks and can send you upward). You can do this
a number of times per Long Rest equal to your Constitution modifier.

## Level 10: Howitzer

Once on each of your turns, when you deal Fire damage to a creature, it must succeed on a
**Constitution saving throw** or be knocked **Prone** and **Deafened** until the end of its next
turn. In addition, you can **overcharge** a Detonation: spend an extra **10 Hit Points** to make it
deal **twice your Blast die** and become a **20-foot-radius Sphere** at a point within 120 feet.

## Level 18: Cluster Detonation (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you vent every gland and set off the mother of all blasts in a
**40-foot-radius Sphere** centered on a point within 150 feet (you can exclude yourself and chosen
allies). Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**:

- **On a failure:** **12d8 Fire** damage, knocked **Prone**, and **Stunned** until the end of its
  next turn.
- **On a success:** half damage, and none of the other effects.

The blast's recoil launches you up to **60 feet**. Until the start of your next turn, any creature
that failed continues to burn, taking your **Blast die** in Fire damage at the start of its next
turn. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
spend **50 Hit Points** to use it again.
