# Discipline of Frenzy (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Adrenal — Turn Pain Into Power*

Your glands flood you with adrenaline and battle-hormones until every wound only makes you stronger.
Where other Visceralists carefully budget the blood they spill to cast, you *want* to bleed — every
Hit Point you burn stokes a rising fury that you dump straight back into your fists. You are a
self-immolating engine of violence that runs hottest right at death's door.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Steady Nerves
(Enhance Ability)*, *Clench (Shield)*, *Adrenal Rush (Haste)*, *Battle Metabolism (Tenser's
Transformation)*, *Ascended Form (Draconic Transformation)*.

## Level 3: Bloodrage

You turn self-harm into strength. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your
Constitution modifier** Bludgeoning or Force damage and counts as magical, and you can make **one
Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action** on your turn (you don't need to have taken the Attack action
first). Whenever you
**spend Hit Points to cast a Fluidic Ability** (or lose Hit Points to an enemy), you gain
**Temporary Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier**, and your next attack before the end
of your next turn deals **extra damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus**.

## Level 3: Adrenal Surge (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Berserk.** You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**,
  your Speed increases by **10 feet**, and you add your **Constitution modifier** to the damage of
  every weapon and Unarmed Strike.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Reckless Fury.** Make one Unarmed Strike with **Advantage**; until the start
  of your next turn, your attacks have Advantage but attacks against you do too.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Relentless

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature. In addition, when you would drop to 0 Hit Points but aren't
killed outright, you can drop to **1** instead; you can do this once per Short or Long Rest.

## Level 10: Rising Fury

You keep a **Fury** counter that starts at 0 each combat. You gain 1 Fury the first time each turn
you take damage or spend Hit Points to cast, to a maximum of **5**. You add your current Fury to
all your weapon and Unarmed damage rolls, and while you have **maximum Fury** you have **Advantage
on attack rolls** and your Speed doubles. Fury resets when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

## Level 18: Ascendant Frenzy (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you ascend into a screaming, secretion-wreathed apex state for 1 minute
(this is the *Ascended Form* fantasy made flesh). For the duration:

- You can take **one additional action** on each of your turns (it can be used to Attack, Dash,
  Disengage, or cast a Fluidic Ability), and you begin the transformation with **maximum Fury**,
  which can no longer drop.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, immunity to the **Frightened, Charmed, Stunned, and
  Paralyzed** conditions, and you **regenerate Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier** at
  the start of each of your turns.
- Each of your weapon and Unarmed Strikes deals an extra **2d8** Force damage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again.
