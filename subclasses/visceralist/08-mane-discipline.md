# Discipline of the Mane (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Hair-Weaver — A Thousand Living Strands*

Every hair on your body answers you — the mane on your head, yes, but also the coarse hair of your
armpits, your groin, and your backside, which you are entirely willing to deploy. You lash, snare,
and skewer with prehensile strands, harden them into blades, and soak them in your own reagents to
make them burn, rot, or freeze. There is always more hair, and it is always growing.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Sticky Strands
(Web)*, *Bile Tendrils (Evard's Black Tentacles)*, *Fluidic Grip (Telekinesis)*, *Bone Bramble
(Wall of Thorns)*, *Blood Puppetry (Hold Monster)*.

Your **Mane die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Prehensile Hair

Your hair is a weapon with **Reach 15 feet**. As an Unarmed Strike, it deals **1d8 + your
Constitution modifier** Slashing or Piercing damage (your choice) and counts as **magical**. You
can use your hair to Grapple, and you can have it Grapple **up to two creatures** at once (each
within reach). Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one hair Unarmed Strike
as a Bonus Action**.

## Level 3: Imbued Mane (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Bladed Bristles.** Your hair hardens and drinks in your fluids: your hair strikes
  deal an extra die of damage (your Mane die is treated as one step higher for these strikes) and
  you can change their damage type to **Acid, Fire, Cold, or Necrotic**. Creatures Grappled by
  your hair take your **Constitution modifier** in that damage at the start of each of your turns.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Lash.** Your strands whip out: make one hair Unarmed Strike against **each**
  of up to three creatures within reach, or attempt to Grapple all of them.

## Level 6: Mane Mastery & Extra Attack

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature. In addition, you have **Advantage on checks to Grapple**
with your hair, a creature Grappled by your hair is also **Restrained**, and you can make hair
Unarmed Strikes and cast Fluidic Abilities without needing free hands (the hair does the work).

**Constricting Drain.** At the start of each of your turns, each creature Grappled by your hair
takes your **Mane die** in Slashing or Piercing damage (or your imbued damage type, if you're in
Rut), and you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to the number of creatures you currently have
Grappled** with your hair. (This replaces the smaller Constitution-modifier drain from Imbued
Mane.)

## Level 10: Strangling Storm

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a hair strike, you can lash outward: each creature of
your choice within 15 feet of you or the target takes your **Mane die** in your chosen imbued
damage type, and one creature of your choice must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** or be
**Restrained** until the end of its next turn. You can also, as a **Magic action**, conjure a
20-foot cube **thicket of living hair** (as *Bone Bramble/Wall of Thorns*, but it moves at your
command up to 15 feet on your turn).

## Level 18: Ten-Thousand Strands (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you erupt into a vast, roiling nimbus of hair for 1 minute. For the
duration:

- Your hair's reach becomes **30 feet**, you can Grapple **any number of creatures** within reach,
  and you make **two additional hair Unarmed Strikes** whenever you take the Attack action.
- At the start of each of your turns, every creature Grappled by your hair takes your **Mane die +
  your Constitution modifier** in your chosen imbued damage type.
- You gain **three-quarters cover** from the surrounding mane, and once on each of your turns as
  part of your Attack action you can sweep the field: each creature of your choice within 30 feet
  makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **6d8** of your imbued damage type and being knocked
  **Prone** on a failure (half, no Prone, on a success).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**40 Hit Points** to use it again.
