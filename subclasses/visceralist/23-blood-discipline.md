# Discipline of Blood (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Sanguine — Everyone in This Room Is Already Full of Your Weapon*

Every Visceralist pays for power in blood. You are the one who learned to take it *back* — and to
take everyone else's besides. You crystallize spilled gore into whips and lances, you boil the
blood inside a foe until it betrays them, and at the height of your art you seize the red rivers
running through your enemies and make their own bodies march, flail, and stop at your word. You
never run dry in a fight. A battlefield is a reservoir, and you are thirsty.

*A hemomancer / bloodbender — the class's own blood-fuel identity turned into a weapon aimed
outward.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Rotting Touch
(Inflict Wounds)*, *Bloodletting Grasp (Vampiric Touch)*, *Blood Seizure (Counterspell)*, *Blood
Puppetry (Hold Monster)*, *Heartstop (Power Word Kill)*.

Your **Blood die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Bloodcraft

You bend blood — spilled or still-flowing. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your
Constitution modifier** Slashing or Necrotic damage (a lash of hardened blood), counts as
**magical**, has **Reach 10 feet**, and increases to **1d10** at 7th level, **1d12** at 11th, and
**2d6** at 15th. Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make **one Unarmed Strike
as a Bonus Action**, and you can use Unarmed Strikes for any Fluidic feature that calls for a
melee weapon attack.

**Hemorrhage.** The first time on each of your turns that you deal damage to a creature that has
blood (not a Construct, Undead, Ooze, or Elemental), it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or
begins to **bleed**, taking **Blood die** Necrotic damage at the start of its next turn. A
creature can bleed from only one source of yours at a time (a fresh trigger refreshes it).

**Transfusion.** Whenever a creature within 30 feet takes your **Hemorrhage** bleed damage, you
regain Hit Points equal to your **Constitution modifier**.

## Level 3: Crimson Rite (Primed)

While in **Rut**, the blood in the room grows restless:

- **Passive — Boiling Blood.** Your **Hemorrhage** bleed damage increases by your **Constitution
  modifier**, bleeding creatures **can't regain Hit Points**, and you have **Resistance to
  Necrotic damage**. When a creature hits you with a melee attack, it is spattered and must
  succeed on a Constitution save or begin to **bleed** (as Hemorrhage).
- **Rut Bonus Action — Blood Puppet.** Choose a creature within 30 feet that is **bleeding** or
  below its Hit Point maximum. It makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking **Blood die + your
  Constitution modifier** Necrotic damage and being **Restrained** (its own blood locks its
  muscles) until the end of its next turn on a failure. If it fails by 5 or more, you may also
  move it up to **15 feet** or force it to drop what it's holding.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Bloodbender

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (your blood-lashes count as melee attacks).

You read and rule the wounded. You have **Advantage on attack rolls** against any creature that is
**Bloodied** (at or below half its Hit Points) or currently **bleeding** from you. You can also
give blood as easily as you take it: as a **Bonus Action**, you can spend up to **half your
current Hit Points** to heal a creature you touch (or that is bleeding within 30 feet) for the
same amount plus your **Blood die**.

**Crimson Ward (Reaction).** When you take damage, you can harden a skin of blood to blunt it,
reducing the damage by **Blood die + your Constitution modifier**. You can do this a number of
times equal to your **Constitution modifier** per Long Rest.

## Level 10: Lord of the Red

Your dominion over blood becomes near-absolute.

- **Full Bloodbend.** As a **Magic action**, target a creature within 60 feet that is bleeding
  from you. It makes a **Constitution saving throw** or is **Blood-Puppeted** for 1 minute (no
  Concentration): its Speed becomes 0, it is **Restrained**, and once on each of its turns you can
  use your Reaction to make it walk up to its Speed or make one weapon attack against a target of
  your choice (using its own attack bonus). It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns,
  ending the effect on a success. You can control only one creature this way at a time.
- **Sanguine Aura.** You radiate a **20-foot Emanation**. Whenever an enemy in it takes damage
  from you or an ally, it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or begins to **bleed** (as
  Hemorrhage).
- **Wide Transfusion.** You regain Hit Points from **any** creature that takes your bleed damage
  within **60 feet** (not just 30), and you can redirect that healing to an ally within the
  emanation instead.

## Level 18: Scarlet Sovereign (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you seize command of every drop of blood in sight for **1 minute**. For
the duration:

- Each enemy that has blood within a **60-foot Emanation** around you makes a **Constitution
  saving throw** at the start of each of its turns. On a failure it takes **6d8** Necrotic damage,
  begins to **bleed**, and is **Restrained** by its own coursing blood until the start of its next
  turn; on a success it takes half damage and isn't Restrained. You regain Hit Points equal to
  your **Constitution modifier** for each creature that fails.
- You are sheathed in a mantle of crimson: you have **Resistance to all damage** and cannot be
  **Charmed, Frightened, or Paralyzed**.
- **Heartstop.** Once on each of your turns, you can clench your fist and crush the blood in a
  single foe within 60 feet. It makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking **8d8** Necrotic
  damage on a failure (half on a success); if this damage reduces a creature to **25 Hit Points
  or fewer**, its heart stops and it drops to **0 Hit Points** instead.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
