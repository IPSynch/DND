# Discipline of the Titan (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Colossus — Meat Piled Into a Mountain That Walks*

Where other Visceralists refine their flesh, you simply grow *more of it*. On command your body
balloons into a towering titan of steaming muscle and plated skin, tall enough to look over
walls and dense enough to shrug off a ballista bolt. You regenerate faster than anyone can carve
you down, harden your hide to living armor at the point of impact, and bring a fist the size of a
cart down on whatever's left. There is no elegant way to fight you. There is only being stepped
on.

*Inspired by the Titans of Attack on Titan — colossal, steaming, and near-impossible to keep
down.*

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Tissue Swell
(Enlarge/Reduce)*, *Clench (Shield)*, *Pore Burst (Destructive Wave)*, *Hardened Hide
(Stoneskin)*, *Bodyquake (Earthquake)*.

Your **Titan die** is **1d8** at 3rd level, **2d8** at 7th, **3d8** at 11th, and **4d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Colossal Frame

There is too much of you. Your Unarmed Strike (Damage option) deals **1d8 + your Constitution
modifier** Bludgeoning damage, counts as **magical**, and increases to **1d10** at 7th level,
**1d12** at 11th, and **2d6** at 15th. Immediately after you take the Attack action, you can make
**one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action**, and you can use Unarmed Strikes for any Fluidic feature
that calls for a melee weapon attack.

You count as **one size larger** for determining your carrying capacity and whether you can
**Grapple or Shove** a creature, you can Grapple or Shove as a **Bonus Action**, and you have
**Advantage** on those checks. In addition, your **Clotting** class feature now triggers at the
start of **every** turn (not only while below half your Hit Points).

## Level 3: Titan Shift (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you swell toward true titan size:

- **Passive — Hardening.** You gain a **+2 bonus to AC** (bone and skin flash-plating), you have
  **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage**, and you gain **Temporary Hit
  Points equal to your Visceralist level** at the start of each of your turns (venting steam).
- **Rut Bonus Action — Crushing Blow.** Make one Unarmed Strike that deals an extra **Titan
  die** on a hit; the target and each creature of your choice within 10 feet of it makes a
  **Strength saving throw** or is knocked **Prone**.

## Level 6: Extra Attack & Steaming Regeneration

You gain the **Extra Attack** feature (you can attack twice whenever you take the Attack action).

Your wounds boil shut. At the start of each of your turns, if you have at least 1 Hit Point, you
regain Hit Points equal to your **Titan die** (this stacks with Clotting). If you are subjected
to an effect that would **sever or cripple** you (an effect that would reduce your Speed to 0, or
the Blinded, Deafened, or Restrained condition from a nonmagical source), you can end it at the
start of your turn by venting steam. You gain no regeneration on any turn after you take **Fire
or Acid** damage (the searing kinds titans fear), until the start of your next turn.

## Level 10: Titan Form

As a **Bonus Action**, you erupt to full size for **10 minutes**. For the duration:

- Your size becomes **Large** (or **Huge**, your choice, if space allows), your reach increases
  by **5 feet**, your walking Speed increases by **10 feet**, and your **Titan die** is added to
  every Unarmed Strike you hit with.
- Your Hit Point maximum increases by **your Visceralist level × 3**, and you gain that many Hit
  Points immediately. You have a **damage threshold of 10** (you ignore any instance of damage
  totaling less than 10 after other reductions).
- Once on each of your turns you can **stomp**: each creature of your choice within **15 feet**
  makes a **Strength saving throw**, taking **Titan die + your Constitution modifier**
  Bludgeoning damage and being knocked **Prone** on a failure. The ground within 15 feet becomes
  **Difficult Terrain** until the start of your next turn.

You can use **Titan Form** twice per Long Rest, or by spending **20 Hit Points** (subject to the
usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).

## Level 18: Colossal Titan (Capstone)

As a **Bonus Action**, you become a walking cataclysm of meat for **1 minute** — the largest,
hottest, hardest thing on the field. For the duration:

- Your size becomes **Huge** (or **Gargantuan** if space allows), your reach increases by **10
  feet**, your Speed doubles, and you make **two additional Unarmed Strikes** whenever you take
  the Attack action. Each Unarmed Strike deals an extra **2d10** Bludgeoning damage on a hit.
- You have **Resistance to all damage**, a **damage threshold of 20**, and you regain **20 Hit
  Points** at the start of each of your turns. The first time each round you would be reduced to
  0 Hit Points, you instead drop to **1** (steam floods the wound shut).
- **Titanic Devastation.** Once on each of your turns, when you take the Attack action, you can
  replace one attack with a colossal stomp or sweep: choose a **60-foot Line** or a **30-foot
  Cone**. Each creature there makes a **Strength saving throw**, taking **8d10** Bludgeoning
  damage, being knocked **Prone**, and being pushed up to **15 feet** on a failure (half damage,
  no other effects, on a success). Unattended objects and structures in the area are shattered.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again (subject to the usual can't-drop-below-1 rule).
