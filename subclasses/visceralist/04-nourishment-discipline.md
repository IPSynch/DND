# Discipline of Nourishment (Visceralist Subclass)

*The Wet Nurse — No One Dies On Your Watch*

Your body is a wellspring of life-giving fluid — warm milk from the breast, or rich seed, that
mends wounds, purges poison, and pours strength back into the failing. You mother the whole party:
a squirt closes a gash, a mouthful hauls a dying friend back to their feet, and a fine mist of your
own nourishment keeps everyone standing long after they should have fallen. You out-heal a Life
Cleric without breaking a sweat — well, without breaking *that* kind of sweat.

**Discipline Abilities.** Always known, not counted against your Abilities Known: *Fluid Mending
(Cure Wounds)*, *Group Squirt (Mass Healing Word)*, *Invigorating Musk (Aura of Vitality)*, *Mass
Mending (Mass Cure Wounds)*, *Word of Renewal (Power Word Heal)*.

Your **Milk die** is **2d8** at 3rd level, **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 11th, and **5d8** at 15th.

## Level 3: Overflowing Care

Your fluids mend better than any priest's magic. Whenever you restore Hit Points to a creature
with a Fluidic Ability or a Discipline feature, add **your Visceralist level** to the Hit Points
restored. (This is stronger than, and does not stack with, the Life Domain's Disciple of Life.)

## Level 3: Let-Down (Primed)

While in **Rut**, you gain:

- **Passive — Rich Flow.** Any creature you restore Hit Points to also gains **Temporary Hit
  Points equal to your Constitution modifier**, and your own **Sanguine Reserve** healing is
  doubled.
- **Rut Bonus Action — Nurse.** Choose to either heal one creature within 30 feet for your **Milk
  die + your Constitution modifier**, or spray a **15-foot Cone**, healing every ally in it for
  your **Milk die**.

## Level 6: Guardian Teat

You catch the falling. When a creature you can see within 30 feet drops to 0 Hit Points or takes
damage, you can use your **Reaction** to heal it for your **Milk die + your Constitution
modifier** (if it was at 0, it's no longer dying). You can do this a number of times equal to your
**Constitution modifier** per Long Rest for free. In addition, a creature you heal gains
**Advantage on its next saving throw** before the start of your next turn.

## Level 10: Font of Life

You emit a 15-foot Emanation of nourishing mist that you can turn on or off (no action required).
While it's active, allies in it regain **Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier** at the
start of each of their turns, and the **first time each round** that an ally in it would drop to 0
Hit Points, it instead drops to **1**. In addition, when you cast a healing Fluidic Ability that
rolls dice, you can **use the maximum result** instead of rolling, once on each of your turns.

## Level 18: Miracle of the Body (Capstone)

As a **Magic action**, you pour out a flood of life in a **30-foot Emanation**. Each creature of
your choice in the area **regains all lost Hit Points**, ends the **Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed,
Poisoned,** and **Stunned** conditions and one level of Exhaustion on itself, and gains
**Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Visceralist level**. Furthermore, each creature of your
choice that **died within the last minute** and is in the area **returns to life** with Hit Points
equal to your Visceralist level (as *Raise Dead*, but instantaneous and with no penalty).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend
**50 Hit Points** to use it again.
