# Barbarian: Path of the Hollow Crown

*Primal Path — Offense / Utility — (bonus concept)*

> *You asked for an interesting Barbarian power concept. Here it is.*

> Other barbarians rage with their own fury. You rage with everyone else's. Your
> line is a graveyard of kings, warlords, champions, and monsters — every one of
> them dead, none of them quietly. When you enter your rage you don't summon
> *your* anger; you crack open the crown that sits, invisible and heavy, on your
> brow, and one of *them* climbs up behind your eyes to fight again. A different
> dead ruler answers each time. You never know who you'll be until the blood is
> up.

**The pitch.** The Hollow Crown is a *rage that rerolls its own identity every
time you use it.* Each rage, you channel a different **Echo** — the lingering will
of a dead warrior-king bound to your bloodline — and gain a randomly determined
(or, later, chosen) package of benefits on top of normal Rage. It's a barbarian
built around variety, gambling, and the fantasy of an entire dynasty of monsters
fighting *through* one person. It pairs raw rage with a rotating toolkit that
keeps every fight feeling different.

This is a sketch in the same house style as the Fighter subclasses, on the
**Barbarian 2024 cadence (levels 3, 6, 10, 14)** — flesh out the Echo table to
taste.

---

## The Hollow Crown (Level 3)

> The crown is empty so that anyone can wear it. Anyone who is already dead.

When you enter a Rage, roll on or choose from the **Echoes of the Crown** table
(below) to determine which dead ruler answers. You gain that Echo's benefit until
the Rage ends. You can hold only one Echo at a time.

**Echoes of the Crown (d6).**

| d6 | Echo | While raging, you gain… |
|----|------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **The Iron Tyrant** | Resistance to **all** damage (not just B/P/S) and you can't be knocked Prone. |
| 2 | **The Red Reaver** | Once per turn, extra damage equal to your **Rage Damage bonus + your Barbarian level** on a hit; you must move toward the nearest enemy each turn. |
| 3 | **The Ghost-King** | A Fly (hover) Speed equal to your Speed, and your attacks deal **Necrotic** instead of their type and ignore Resistance to it. |
| 4 | **The Mountain-Mother** | Your size increases by one (to Huge), your reach increases by 5 ft, and you have Advantage on Strength checks and saves. |
| 5 | **The Whispering Queen** | When a creature within 30 ft attacks you, it makes a **Wisdom save (DC 8 + PB + CON)** or attacks a different creature of your choice. |
| 6 | **The Hollow King** | At the **start** of each of your turns, regain hit points equal to your Barbarian level; the first creature to drop you to 0 takes that much Necrotic damage. |

You can choose the Echo instead of rolling a number of times per Long Rest equal
to your **Proficiency Bonus**; otherwise it's random.

## Crowded Skull (Level 6)

> The throne room behind your eyes gets loud. You learn to let two of them talk
> at once.

You can hold **two Echoes** at the start of a Rage (roll/choose twice; reroll
duplicates). In addition, once per turn while raging, as part of an attack you
can **briefly surface a third Echo** — apply the *one-line on-hit benefit* of any
Echo not currently active (e.g., a burst of the Red Reaver's bonus damage, a
single Whispering Queen save) — then it sinks back down.

You also gain **Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, Frightened, or
Possessed.** The crown is already full; there's no room for anyone new.

---

## Dynasty Unbound (Level 10)

> A line of kings is a line of credit. You've stopped asking permission to spend
> it.

Your Echoes intensify. While raging, **all** benefits from your active Echoes that
deal damage, restore hit points, or set a save DC are **increased** — damage and
healing dice are rolled twice and you add your **Constitution modifier**; save
DCs gain **+2.**

Additionally, when you score a Critical Hit or reduce a creature to 0 hit points
while raging, you can **swap one active Echo** for a different one immediately —
the dead trade places mid-fight, and the battle never stops shifting under your
enemies' feet.

---

## The Crown Takes a Head (Level 14)

> When the dynasty agrees on something, the world tends to lose an argument.

Once per **Long Rest**, when you enter a Rage, you can convene the **whole
Crown**: you gain the benefits of **every Echo on the table at once** until the end
of your next turn, and **one Echo of your choice** for the rest of the Rage. While
the full Crown is convened, you can't be reduced below 1 hit point, your attacks
**ignore Resistance and Immunity**, and each enemy that starts its turn within
30 feet of you makes a **Wisdom save (DC 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
Constitution modifier)** or is **Frightened of the dynasty wearing your face**
until the end of its turn.

When this Rage ends, you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Barbarian
level** — the kings linger a moment before they sink back into the crown to wait
for the next war.

---

### Designer notes

- The fun is the **variance**: an Iron Tyrant rage plays like an unkillable wall,
  a Red Reaver rage like a glass cannon, a Ghost-King rage like a flying striker.
  Tables that hate randomness can use the "choose" budget from level 3; tables
  that love it can rage blind and adapt.
- **Crowded Skull**'s "surface a third Echo" line is deliberately fuzzy — let
  players cherry-pick the single most evocative one-liner from each Echo rather
  than the whole package. It rewards system mastery without doubling the bookkeeping.
- **The Crown Takes a Head** is the once-a-day "everything everywhere" button.
  It's strong on purpose — it's the capstone of a dynasty, not a cantrip.
