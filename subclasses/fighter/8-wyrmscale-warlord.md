# Wyrmscale Warlord

*Martial Archetype — Transformation / Offense*

> The first time the scales came in, you thought you were sick. Then you breathed
> out and the wall in front of you was gone. There is a dragon in your blood —
> coiled, patient, enormous — and you have spent your life learning to let a
> little of it out at a time without losing yourself entirely. A clawed hand
> here. A lash of tail there. And, when the fight is truly worth it, all of it:
> wings unfurling, the air going hot, and something the size of a building taking
> your place on the field.

The Wyrmscale Warlord is a transforming juggernaut who fights in a **hybrid
dragon-humanoid form** from early on and ascends to a **full draconic
transformation** at the highest levels. Flavor it as draconic ancestry awakened,
a beast-fruit power, a kaiju bloodline, or a curse you've turned into a weapon.
The arc: **start strong and grow into a literal monster** — flight, breath, and a
body that hits like a siege engine.

---

## Draconic Awakening (Level 3)

> Half-out is still more than enough for most rooms.

Choose a **Damage Type** from this list — your *draconic element*: Acid, Cold,
Fire, Lightning, or Poison. You gain **Resistance** to it permanently.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can enter **Hybrid Form** for **10 minutes** (or until
you end it). You can do so a number of times equal to your **Second Wind uses**,
regaining all expended uses on a **Long Rest** (or by spending a Second Wind
use). While in Hybrid Form:

- You grow claws, fangs, and a lashing tail. Your **unarmed strikes deal 1d10**
  damage (your draconic element or Slashing, your choice) and have **reach
  10 feet.**
- When you take the **Attack** action and attack with an unarmed strike, you can
  make **one tail attack** as a **Bonus Action** (1d10, same rules).
- You have **Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened**, and you
  radiate menace — see *Dragon Fear* below.

You also gain a **Breath Weapon**: as part of the **Attack** action (replacing one
attack), exhale a **15-foot Cone** or **30-foot Line.** Each creature there makes
a **Dexterity saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking **2d10
damage of your draconic element** on a failure, half on a success. The dice
increase to **3d10 at level 7, 4d10 at level 10, 5d10 at level 15, and 7d10 at
level 18.**

---

## Dragon Fear (Level 7)

> The room remembers, somewhere old in its spine, what it owes to dragons.

While in Hybrid Form, you project terror. When you enter Hybrid Form, and as a
**Bonus Action** on later turns, each enemy of your choice within **30 feet**
makes a **Wisdom saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC** or becomes
**Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.**

While a creature is Frightened of you, your attacks against it deal **extra
damage of your draconic element equal to your Proficiency Bonus.** In addition,
your Breath Weapon now **recharges**: after using it, roll a d6 at the start of
each of your turns; on a **5–6** you can use it again that turn.

---

## Wyrm Ascendant (Level 10)

> The wings come in. The ceiling becomes a suggestion.

Your Hybrid Form grows monstrous.

- Your **size increases to Large** (if there's room), and you sprout wings: you
  gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** while in Hybrid Form.
- You gain **Resistance to a second damage type** of your choice while
  transformed, and your unarmed/tail strikes count as **magical** for overcoming
  Resistance.
- Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee attack in Hybrid Form, you
  can **knock it Prone** (if Large or smaller) or **grasp it in your claws**
  (a Grapple, escape DC equal to your Fighter Subclass DC) — your choice.

---

## Wings of Calamity (Level 15)

> Less and less of you is the small shape now. You don't miss it.

Your draconic might overwhelms.

- Your Hybrid Form's unarmed and tail strikes deal **2d10** damage, and you make
  **two** tail attacks as your Bonus Action instead of one.
- When you use your **Breath Weapon**, you can spend a use of **Second Wind** to
  **double its area** and impose **Disadvantage** on the saving throw.
- While transformed, you are immune to the **Frightened** condition and have
  **Advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws.**

---

## Apex Dragon (Level 18)

> All of it. Now.

Once per **Long Rest**, instead of Hybrid Form, you can fully transform into a
**Dragon** for **1 minute** as a **Bonus Action** (a use of Hybrid Form refreshes
this once per Long Rest as well). While in this form, in addition to all Hybrid
Form benefits:

- Your **size becomes Huge** (if there's room), your **Speed and Fly Speed both
  become 60 feet**, and your melee reach is **15 feet.**
- You have **Resistance to all damage except your draconic element, against which
  you have Immunity.**
- Your unarmed and tail strikes deal **3d10** damage and **score Critical Hits on
  19–20.**
- Your **Breath Weapon has no recharge** — you can use it as part of your Attack
  action every turn, and its area is a **60-foot Cone** or **120-foot Line.**
- The first time each turn you hit a Bloodied creature, it must succeed on a
  **Constitution saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC** or be
  **swallowed/pinned** (Restrained, repeating the save at the end of its turns).

When the form ends, you gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Fighter
level** — the dragon's vitality lingers in the smaller shape.
