# The Overtime

*Martial Archetype — Burst / Critical Specialist*

> You are a professional. You clock in, you do the work, and you do it with a
> precision that borders on contempt for the people who treat violence as
> passion. You know exactly where a thing breaks. You hit it there. The ratio is
> always the same — a little extra effort, a lot more result — and when the
> moment truly demands it, you stop holding back and put in the *overtime* that
> ends careers, monsters, and very bad days.

The Overtime fighter is a critical-hit specialist. Where the Champion broadens
its crit range a little, you build an entire engine around landing, lowering, and
weaponizing the critical hit — and a once-a-day "overtime" that simply *decides*
to crit when it matters most. Reskin it as a salaryman exorcist, a tired
hitman-for-hire, a clock-punching monster hunter, or a duelist who treats combat
like a contract. The throughline: **understated, exact, and catastrophic on the
right roll.**

---

## The 7:3 Ratio (Level 3)

> Maximum efficiency. Find the line, cut along it, go home on time.

Your Critical Hits land more easily and hit harder. Your weapon attacks and
unarmed strikes **score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20.**

In addition, whenever you score a Critical Hit, you deal **extra damage of the
weapon's type equal to 1d8 + your Fighter level.** (This is the "overtime" on top
of the doubled dice — the ratio always pays out.)

---

## Collapse Point (Level 7)

> You don't get angry. You get *accurate*. The job decides, not your mood.

You can manufacture openings. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a
weapon attack, you can mark it as a **Collapse Point** until the start of your
next turn. While a creature is a Collapse Point:

- Your attacks against it **score a Critical Hit on a roll of 18–20.**
- The first time you Critically Hit it, it must succeed on a **Constitution
  saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC** or be **Stunned until the end
  of your next turn.**

You can mark a creature this way a number of times equal to your **Proficiency
Bonus**, regaining all uses on a **Short or Long Rest.**

---

## Cleave the Line (Level 10)

> The cut continues. So does the paycheck.

Your crit range improves permanently to **18–20** against **all** creatures (this
supersedes the 19–20 from The 7:3 Ratio, and combines with **Collapse Point** to
make a marked target crit on **17–20**).

Additionally, when you score a Critical Hit, you can immediately make **one extra
weapon attack** against the same target or a different creature within reach as
part of the same action (no action required). This can occur **once per turn.**

---

## Overtime (Level 15)

> When the contract says "no matter what," you read the fine print and bill for
> it.

You can simply *decide* to land the decisive blow. When you hit a creature with a
weapon attack, you can declare **Overtime** to turn that hit into a **Critical
Hit.**

You can use Overtime a number of times equal to your **Strength or Constitution
modifier** (your choice, minimum once), and regain all uses on a **Long Rest.**
You also regain **one** expended use whenever you roll **Initiative** and have
none remaining.

---

## Clocking Out (Level 18)

> The last entry in the ledger. It is always paid in full.

Your critical hits become genuinely terminal.

- **Maximum Effort.** When you score a Critical Hit, you **don't roll the weapon
  damage dice — you deal maximum damage with them** (the doubled dice from
  critting are both maxed), then add your usual modifiers and **The 7:3 Ratio**
  bonus on top.
- **Severance.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a Critical Hit,
  or score a Critical Hit against a creature that is already **Bloodied**, it
  must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC
  with Disadvantage** or **drop to 0 hit points.** Creatures immune to the
  Frightened condition or with Legendary Resistances available may spend a
  resistance to ignore this. This has no effect on creatures with more hit points
  than five times your Fighter level.
