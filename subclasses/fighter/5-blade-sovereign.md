# Blade Sovereign

*Martial Archetype — Offense / Reach*

> There is no greatest swordsman by accident. The title is taken, defended, and
> never put down. You have honed a single discipline past the point other people
> believe possible: the cut. Not a spell, not a trick — a *cut*, so clean and so
> committed that distance stops mattering. The arc of your blade leaves the edge
> and keeps traveling, and somewhere far away a thing that thought it was safe
> simply comes apart.

The Blade Sovereign is a pure offensive duelist whose slashes reach across the
battlefield. From level 3 you throw a wave of cutting force from your melee
weapon; everything after sharpens that core fantasy. Flavor it as a world's-best
swordsman, a flying-slash sword-saint, an energy-blade vanguard, or a monk of the
edge. The promise is simple and relentless: **the deadliest weapon attacks in the
party, at a range no one expects.**

---

## Flying Slash (Level 3)

> The blade stays in your hand. The cut does not.

When you take the **Attack** action with a Slashing melee weapon (or one you've
trained with as your signature blade), you can make any of those attacks as a
**ranged attack with a range of 30/60 feet**, hurling a crescent of cutting force
at a target you can see. These attacks use your normal melee attack bonus and
deal the weapon's normal damage.

In addition, you adopt a peerless offensive stance: while wielding your blade in
two hands (or a one-handed Slashing weapon with nothing in your other hand), you
gain a **+2 bonus to damage rolls** with it. This bonus increases to **+3 at
level 10** and **+4 at level 15.**

---

## Honed Edge (Level 7)

> Mastery is just refusing to leave any waste in the motion.

Your Flying Slashes sharpen. You can apply any one **Weapon Mastery property** you
know to a Flying Slash, and your signature blade gains the **Vex** property in
addition to its own.

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a Flying Slash, you can extend the
cut into a **Line**: each creature in a 5-foot-wide Line between you and the
target (or directly beyond it, your choice) makes a **Dexterity saving throw
against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking **Slashing damage equal to your
Strength or Dexterity modifier + your Proficiency Bonus** on a failure.

---

## Severing Arc (Level 10)

> The longer you fight, the longer the blade gets. The world calls it
> impossible. You call it Tuesday.

The reach of your art expands dramatically.

- Your **Flying Slash** range increases to **120/240 feet.**
- Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with any weapon attack, that
  hit deals **extra Slashing damage equal to one roll of your weapon's damage
  die** (the cut runs deeper than it should).
- Your Flying Slashes ignore **half cover and three-quarters cover** — your cut
  bends around obstacles to reach the mark.

---

## Black Sword (Level 15)

> The strongest blade is the one that has decided nothing is worth missing for.

Your most committed strikes become irresistible.

- Your weapon attacks (melee and Flying Slash) **ignore Resistance to Slashing
  damage**, and **treat Immunity to Slashing as Resistance.**
- When you score a **Critical Hit** with any attack, you can immediately make
  **one Flying Slash** against a different creature you can see within range as
  part of the same action (no action required), once per turn.
- Your crit range with your signature blade improves to **19–20.**

---

## World's Strongest (Level 18)

> Look up. The horizon is closer than my blade.

You stand at the summit of the cut.

- **Cross-Slash.** Once on each of your turns, when you make a Flying Slash, you
  can spend no action to make it a **20-foot-wide Line up to your Flying Slash
  range.** Each creature in the Line makes a **Dexterity saving throw against
  your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking the weapon's damage (rolled once, plus your
  modifiers and bonuses) on a failure, or half on a success. A creature that fails
  by 5 or more is also **Bloodied to the result** — if this damage would leave it
  above half its hit points but it failed by 5+, it instead drops to exactly half
  (the cut finds the line). This Line counts as a single attack for features that
  trigger on a hit.
- **Unbreakable Title.** Your signature blade can **never be disarmed,
  sundered, or knocked from your grip** against your will, and your weapon
  attacks count as **magical** for overcoming Resistance and Immunity.
