# Titan's Wrath

*Martial Archetype — Bruiser / Offense*

> Somewhere inside you there is a larger person, and they are angry. Most of
> your life is spent keeping the door shut. But when the stakes are high enough —
> when someone weaker is about to be hurt, or when *you* are — the door comes
> off its hinges. Your frame swells, your skin hardens, your voice drops into
> the floor, and the thing that walks out of the smoke fights with its bare
> hands because it doesn't need anything else.

The Titan's Wrath fighter is a transforming brawler. They begin a fight as an
ordinary combatant and *become* a juggernaut, growing stronger the longer and
harder the battle goes. Whether you flavor it as a gamma mutation, a Symbol of
Peace standing tall so others don't have to, an heirloom curse, or a will so
fierce it reshapes flesh — the loop is the same: **transform, hit things with
your fists, and refuse to fall down.**

---

## Titanic Form (Level 3)

> *"You wouldn't like me when I'm calm, either."* The change starts in the jaw
> and ends in the fists.

You gain the **Unarmed Fighting** style if you don't have it (your unarmed
strikes deal **1d8** bludgeoning damage, or **1d10** with no weapon or shield in
either hand). If you already have it, you instead learn one additional Fighting
Style of your choice.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can enter **Titanic Form** for **1 minute**. It ends
early if you fall Unconscious or end it (no action). You can transform a number
of times equal to your **Second Wind uses**, and regain all expended
transformations when you finish a **Long Rest**; you can also spend one **Second
Wind** use to transform when you have none left.

While transformed:

- You gain **Temporary Hit Points** equal to **twice your Fighter level** when
  you transform (and only then).
- Your unarmed strikes' damage die increases by one step (**1d8 → 1d10**, or
  **1d10 → 1d12** for the two-free-hands option), and they count as a weapon you
  have **Mastery** with — you may apply the **Push** or **Topple** mastery
  property to them.
- When you take the **Attack** action and attack with an unarmed strike, you can
  make **one additional unarmed strike** as a **Bonus Action**.
- You have **Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.**

## Smash Through (Level 7)

> The first rule of the bigger person: nothing is a wall, everything is a door.

While in Titanic Form, your unarmed strikes gain **reach 10 feet**, and when you
hit a creature with one you can choose to **knock it back 10 feet** and, if it is
Large or smaller, knock it **Prone** (no save, once per turn).

Additionally, your strikes shatter the scenery. You can **move through the space**
of any creature at least one size smaller than you, treating it as Difficult
Terrain, and you ignore Difficult Terrain caused by rubble, foliage, or debris.
When you move at least 10 feet in a straight line and then hit a creature with an
unarmed strike, that strike deals **extra bludgeoning damage equal to your
Strength modifier.**

---

## Unbreakable (Level 10)

> The angrier it gets, the harder it is to put down. That is the whole trick,
> and it has never once stopped working.

Your Titanic Form grows truly monstrous. While transformed:

- Your **size increases by one category** (to a maximum of Huge) if there is room.
- You gain **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from
  nonmagical sources.**
- The **first time each turn** you take damage, you gain **Temporary Hit Points
  equal to your Constitution modifier** (minimum 1) — the pain only feeds it.

If you are reduced to 0 hit points while transformed but not killed outright, you
can **end Titanic Form as a Reaction** to instead drop to **1 hit point** and
gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Fighter level. Once you use this, you
can't again until you finish a **Short or Long Rest.**

## Gamma Spiral (Level 15)

> Every blow that lands on you is a deposit. Sooner or later you make a
> withdrawal, and it is enormous.

While in Titanic Form, you build **Fury**. Each time you take damage, gain
**1 Fury** (max equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**). You can spend Fury in two
ways:

- **Overwhelm (no action).** When you hit with an unarmed strike, spend any
  amount of Fury to deal **+1d12 bludgeoning damage per Fury** spent.
- **Stand Tall (no action).** When you would be Frightened, Stunned, Paralyzed,
  knocked Prone, or moved against your will, spend **1 Fury** to ignore it.

In addition, your **Action Surge** now grants an additional action that can only
be used to take the **Attack** action — but that Attack action lets you make
**one extra unarmed strike** beyond your normal number.

---

## One For All (Level 18)

> Now it's your turn. Plus Ultra.

Your transformation reaches its absolute ceiling. While in Titanic Form:

- Your unarmed strikes deal **maximum damage on a hit against any creature that
  is Bloodied**, and they **score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20.**
- Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you
  can unleash a named smash: the target and each creature in a **15-foot Cone
  behind it** make a **Strength saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC**,
  taking **6d10 bludgeoning damage** on a failure (half as much on a success) and
  being knocked **Prone** and pushed to the far end of the cone on a failure.
- When you enter Titanic Form, **all your transformations refresh if you have
  none remaining** — once per Long Rest. The Symbol of Peace does not run out at
  the worst moment.
