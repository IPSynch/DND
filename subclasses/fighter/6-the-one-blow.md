# The One Blow

*Martial Archetype — Burst / Single-Target*

> You trained until the training stopped being interesting. Now there is only
> one move left in you, and it is the only one you have ever needed: you hit the
> thing, once, and the thing is over. No combos. No flurries. No dramatic
> exchange of blows. Other fighters learn to attack *more*. You learned to
> attack *once*, correctly, with everything — and to make that single moment so
> total that a second swing would be an insult to the first.

The One Blow fighter rewrites the Fighter's signature **Extra Attack** into
fuel. Instead of swinging more times, you fold every additional attack into one
overwhelming strike. It is a high-skill, high-payoff fantasy of the bored
invincible hero, the one-shot gunslinger, the "I only need a moment" master.
Reskin it as a caped baldy, a quick-draw legend, or a monk who has transcended
technique. The deal you make: **fewer swings than anyone — and the hardest single
hit in the game.**

---

## Serious Punch (Level 3)

> Wind up. That's the whole technique. There isn't a part two.

When you take the **Attack** action, you can declare a **Serious Strike**: you
make **only one attack** this turn (with a weapon or unarmed strike), but that
attack gains a stacking bonus. For each attack you *gave up* to make it — now and
from features you gain later — the strike deals **extra damage equal to one roll
of your weapon's damage die** (or **1d8** for an unarmed strike).

At level 3 you have one attack per Attack action, so this adds nothing yet — but
your **Action Surge** already lets you fold a second Attack action's attack into
the same blow (see *Concentrated Force* below). Every future source of "an extra
attack" you possess becomes **+1 die on the Serious Strike** instead.

**Concentrated Force.** When you use **Action Surge**, instead of taking a second
action you can pour it into a declared Serious Strike this turn, adding **+2 dice**
to it.

## Consecutive Normal Punches (Level 7)

> "Normal." The understatement is the point.

Your Serious Strike sharpens into a guaranteed connection.

- A Serious Strike **ignores Disadvantage** and treats any natural roll of
  **10 or higher on the d20 as a hit** against creatures whose AC is no greater
  than 25 (you are simply faster than the dodge).
- The bonus dice from Serious Strike are now **d10s** for unarmed strikes (instead
  of d8s), and **d12s** for melee weapons that already roll a d10 or larger.
- You gain **Extra Attack** as normal at level 5 — and feeding it into a Serious
  Strike adds its **+1 die** as described above. Your Serious Strike now adds at
  least **+1 die** by default.

## I'm Here For Fun (Level 10)

> You could end it sooner. You don't, until you do.

You can wind up. On your turn, you can forgo your **Bonus Action and your
movement** to **Charge** your next Serious Strike. A Charged Serious Strike on
your next turn:

- Adds **+3 dice** beyond its normal total.
- **Automatically scores a Critical Hit** if it hits.
- On a hit, releases a shockwave: each creature within **15 feet** of the target
  makes a **Strength saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking
  **damage equal to your Fighter level** and being knocked **Prone** on a failure.

You can also Charge as a **Reaction** when a creature you can see starts its turn
within 60 feet — readying the blow for when it closes.

## Serious Series (Level 15)

> The named techniques. You only ever needed the one, but the one has moods.

When you make a **Serious Strike**, choose one of the following riders (you can
choose a different one each time):

- **Serious Side Hops → Tableflip.** The strike's damage **ignores Resistance**
  and treats **Immunity as Resistance.**
- **Serious Headbutt.** On a hit, the target makes a **Constitution saving throw
  against your Fighter Subclass DC** or is **Stunned until the end of your next
  turn.**
- **Serious Punch.** The strike deals **maximum damage** with its **base weapon
  die** (the un-multiplied one), before bonus dice.

Additionally, your Serious Strike now adds bonus dice from **both** your Extra
Attack **and** a folded Action Surge by default, without needing to spend Action
Surge — you simply choose to strike once, and the math assumes the rest.

---

## One Punch (Level 18)

> *"...okay."* It is already done.

You become the answer to every fight.

- **Now I'm Motivated.** When you make a **Charged Serious Strike**, you can spend
  your **Action Surge** to add it to the charge for a total of roughly **the
  entire turn's worth of attacks in one hit** — the strike adds **+8 dice** beyond
  its base damage and deals **maximum damage on every die** against any creature
  that is **Bloodied.**
- **Genuine Article.** Once per **Long Rest**, when a single Serious Strike
  reduces a creature to 0 hit points, you can immediately make a **fresh Serious
  Strike** against another creature you can see within movement range (you may
  move up to your Speed first, no action required). The hero is, frustratingly,
  never even slightly winded.
