# Blink Duelist

*Martial Archetype — Mobility / Duelist*

> The battlefield, to you, is a set of marked positions — and you are never
> standing where the enemy thinks you are. You tap a target, you tap a friend,
> and the two of them swap places mid-swing. You step *through* the air and
> arrive shoulder-to-shoulder with the strongest thing on the field, grinning,
> because the only fight worth having is the one against the best. To you,
> teleportation is not an escape. It is how you pick the duel.

The Blink Duelist rearranges the battlefield by snapping creatures and allies
across it, then locks the deadliest enemy into a one-on-one fight nobody else can
interrupt. Flavor it as a clap-and-swap technique, a short-range warp gift, a
trickster's relic, or sheer impossible speed. The fantasy is **control of
position plus supremacy in the duel** — you decide who fights whom, and you win
the matchup you chose.

---

## Swap (Level 3)

> *Clap.* Now you're over there, and your problem is over here.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can teleport. Choose yourself and one willing creature
within 60 feet, **or** two creatures within 60 feet that you can see (at least
one of which must be willing or yourself) — the two chosen creatures instantly
**swap positions.** An unwilling creature can resist by succeeding on a
**Charisma saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC.**

When you teleport yourself with Swap, the first attack you make before the end of
your turn has **Advantage**, and on a hit deals **extra damage equal to your
Proficiency Bonus.**

You can Swap a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**, regaining all
uses on a **Short or Long Rest.**

---

## Best Match (Level 7)

> "You're strong. *Finally.*" The compliment is the threat.

You excel against a single chosen foe. As a **Bonus Action** (or for free when you
use Swap), you can declare one creature you can see your **Best Match** until the
end of the encounter or until you choose a new one. While a creature is your Best
Match:

- You have **Advantage on attack rolls against it whenever you are within 5 feet
  of it** and no other enemy is within 5 feet of you (a true duel).
- The first time each turn you hit it, you deal **extra damage equal to 1d8 + your
  Strength or Dexterity modifier.**
- You can use **Swap** to teleport directly adjacent to your Best Match from up to
  **60 feet** away as part of the same Bonus Action.

---

## Boogie Step (Level 10)

> Two taps and the whole board rotates. The enemy's careful formation becomes a
> punchline.

Your Swap becomes a battlefield-altering art:

- Swap's range increases to **120 feet**, and you can swap **two pairs** of
  creatures with a single use (four creatures total).
- When you swap an enemy into a space, you can choose to have it arrive
  **Prone**, or to provoke an **Opportunity Attack** from one ally adjacent to
  its new location (no Reaction required from the ally).
- You can use Swap as a **Reaction** when you or an ally within range is hit by an
  attack — swap the target with another valid creature; if the attack can now
  reach the new occupant, it redirects to them, otherwise it misses.

---

## Unbroken Duel (Level 15)

> The ring is sealed. No one walks in, no one walks out, until it's settled.

While you are within 5 feet of your **Best Match**, you seal the duel. Other
creatures have **Disadvantage on attack rolls against you**, and you have
**Resistance to all damage dealt by creatures other than your Best Match.**

Furthermore, if your Best Match tries to move more than 30 feet away from you, you
can use your **Reaction** to immediately **Swap to its destination**, keeping the
duel alive — and you make one weapon attack against it with Advantage.

---

## Black Flash (Level 18)

> Time hiccups. Space folds wrong. For a quarter-second you and your fist are
> everywhere you need to be.

Your mastery of position becomes overwhelming.

- **Endless Swap.** You regain a use of **Swap** at the start of each of your
  turns if you have none remaining.
- **Resonant Strike.** Once per turn, when you hit your **Best Match** with a
  weapon attack immediately after teleporting (via any feature), the attack is a
  **Critical Hit**, and the thunderous resonance deals an extra **4d6 Force
  damage** to it and **Stuns it until the end of your next turn** on a failed
  **Constitution saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC.**
- **Tag Out.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your **Reaction**
  to Swap with a willing ally within 120 feet and **drop to 1 hit point**
  instead, appearing in their old space. The ally takes your place but not your
  damage. Once you use this, you can't again until you finish a **Long Rest.**
