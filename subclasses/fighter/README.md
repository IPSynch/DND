# Homebrew Fighter Subclasses (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Eleven original martial concepts built on the 2024 *Player's Handbook* Fighter
chassis. Each grants features at **Fighter levels 3, 7, 10, 15, and 18**,
matching the official 2024 subclass cadence.

These are tuned to be **strong** — noticeably above official Fighter subclasses
in raw output — while staying inside the *shape* of the class (resource pools,
once-per-turn riders, save DCs, and short/long-rest economies). They are written
for high-octane, cinematic, anime-flavored tables. If your group runs lean,
shave a die size or a use per rest off the apex features.

Every subclass intro is **setting-agnostic**: nothing in the flavor assumes a
medieval world. Swap "armor" for "exo-suit," "tournament" for "title bout," or
"warband" for "strike team" and the fantasy still lands in a cape-comic city, a
post-collapse wasteland, or a far-future colony.

## Design conventions used throughout

- **Save DC** for a subclass feature = `8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
  Strength **or** Constitution modifier` (whichever the feature names, or your
  choice if it names neither). This is the *Fighter Subclass DC*.
- **Second Wind** refers to the 2024 level-1 feature and its pool of uses
  (2 at level 1, 3 at level 4, 4 at level 10), which several subclasses spend
  to power transformations.
- **Weapon Mastery** is the 2024 level-1 feature (Push, Sap, Slow, Topple,
  Vex, etc.). Some subclasses widen the mastery properties you can use.
- **Bloodied** means a creature is at or below half its hit point maximum.
- "Unarmed strike" follows the 2024 rule: it can deal damage, **Grapple**, or
  **Shove**, and a transformation that improves "your unarmed strike's damage"
  improves only the damage option.
