# Heaven's Restriction

*Martial Archetype — Anti-Caster / Skirmisher*

> You were born with a deal already signed. No spark of magic will ever live in
> you — not a cantrip, not a blessing, not the smallest enchantment. In exchange
> the world handed your body everything it took back from your soul: speed past
> seeing, strength past sense, instincts that read a fight one move before it
> happens. You are the empty space magic cannot fill, and you have made yourself
> the specialist hunter of everyone who relies on it. Sorcerers fear a great
> many things. You are the one they don't see coming.

Heaven's Restriction is a weapon-master skirmisher and dedicated **anti-magic
hunter**. You wield an entire arsenal with equal mastery, close distance in an
instant, and shut casters down — punishing spells, ignoring enchantments, and
hitting harder the more magical your target is. Flavor it as a magic-null
mercenary, a binding-vow assassin, a null-field meta-human, or a sworn
witch-hunter. The role: **the caster's worst matchup and the party's answer to
anyone who thinks distance or magic will save them.**

---

## Heavenly Restriction (Level 3)

> The trade was lopsided in your favor. They just don't know it yet.

Your body is the price and the prize.

- **Cursed Null.** You can't cast spells or be the target of beneficial spells
  cast by others *unless you choose to allow them*. In exchange, you have
  **Advantage on saving throws against spells**, and **Resistance to damage from
  spells.**
- **Master of Arms.** You gain **Weapon Mastery with all Simple and Martial
  weapons** (you can use the Mastery property of any such weapon you wield), and
  you can change your Weapon Mastery choices when you finish a **Short Rest**, not
  just a Long Rest.
- **Inverted Spear.** Your weapon attacks deal **extra damage equal to your
  Proficiency Bonus against any creature that is concentrating on a spell, or that
  has cast a spell since the end of your last turn.**

---

## Cursed Speed (Level 7)

> You arrive before the incantation finishes. That's usually the end of it.

You close on casters like a thrown blade.

- Your **Speed increases by 15 feet**, and you ignore Difficult Terrain.
- When a creature you can see within 30 feet **casts a spell** or **takes the
  Magic action**, you can use your **Reaction** to move up to your Speed toward
  it; if you end adjacent to it, you can make **one weapon attack** against it. On
  a hit, it must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw against your Fighter
  Subclass DC** or **lose the spell** (it is wasted; any slot or use is spent).
- You can make this Reaction a number of times equal to your **Proficiency
  Bonus**, regaining all uses on a **Short or Long Rest.**

---

## Domain Crusher (Level 10)

> Whatever they conjure, you are the part of the room that doesn't believe in it.

You tear magic apart on contact.

- When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can attempt to **dispel one
  spell effect of 3rd level or lower on it** (as *Dispel Magic*; for higher-level
  effects, make an ability check using your attack bonus against a DC of 10 + the
  spell's level). No action required, once per turn.
- You can move through and attack creatures or barriers made of magical force
  (such as *Wall of Force* or *Bigby's Hand*) as though they were a creature with
  AC 15, dealing your weapon damage to them; **summoned and conjured creatures
  take an extra 1d10 damage** from your attacks.
- You are **immune to being Charmed, Frightened, or Possessed by magical means.**

---

## Soul-Cleaving Arsenal (Level 15)

> A weapon for every wall they put up. You brought all of them.

Your mastery of arms becomes supernatural — without an ounce of the supernatural.

- You can **draw or stow any number of weapons** whenever you make an attack, and
  you treat **all your weapons as having the Vex and Sap properties** in addition
  to their own.
- Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature, you can switch your weapon
  mid-strike to deal **maximum damage with the weapon's die.**
- Your weapon attacks **ignore Resistance** to their damage type, and treat
  **Immunity as Resistance**, against any creature that is a caster, summoned, or
  has Legendary or Lair actions.

---

## Beyond the Reach of Heaven (Level 18)

> They prayed for a way out. You are the proof the prayer went unanswered.

You become the absolute negation of magic on the battlefield.

- **Null Field.** Spells of **5th level or lower fail automatically if they would
  target only you** or if you are the sole creature in their area (you simply
  aren't there for the magic to find). Against area spells that catch others, you
  always take the "successful save / half or none" result.
- **No Escape.** Creatures within 30 feet of you can't **teleport** or leave the
  current plane unless you allow it, and **Reactions used to cast spells** within
  that range provoke an **Opportunity Attack from you** (which, on a hit, forces
  the **Constitution save to lose the spell** from *Cursed Speed*).
- **Heaven's Punisher.** Once per **Long Rest**, you can declare a creature your
  **Quarry** as a Bonus Action. For 1 minute, your weapon attacks against it
  **ignore Resistance and Immunity**, **crit on 18–20**, and the first time each
  turn you hit it you **end one spell or magical effect it is sustaining** and deal
  an extra **4d10 Force damage.**
