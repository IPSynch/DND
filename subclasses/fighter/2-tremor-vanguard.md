# Tremor Vanguard

*Martial Archetype — Offense / Control*

> You learned the secret no one else believes: the air is solid. The space
> between you and a distant enemy is just another surface to grab, crack, and
> shatter. When you punch, the punch keeps going. When you swing, the swing
> arrives somewhere you never touched. Mountains have weak points. So do
> fortress walls, frozen seas, and the sky itself — and you have made a career
> out of finding them.

The Tremor Vanguard turns vibration into a weapon. Your strikes — fist or
weapon — send shockwaves rippling outward, letting you knock down crowds, strike
foes you can't reach, and fold the battlefield like a bedsheet. Flavor the power
however you like: a quake-channeling relic gauntlet, a meta-human resonance
field, a martial art that hits the "fault lines" in everything. The role is
constant: **the strongest opener in the room, and the one who controls where
everyone stands.**

---

## Seismic Strikes (Level 3)

> Tap the world and feel where it's brittle. Then break it there.

Your attacks carry a percussive shockwave. When you hit a creature with a melee
weapon or an unarmed strike, you can choose to deal **Thunder damage** instead of
the weapon's normal type, and the strike "rings" — you can apply the **Push** or
**Topple** Weapon Mastery property to that attack even if the weapon doesn't
normally have it.

In addition, once per turn when you hit with a melee attack you can release a
**Tremor**: choose a 5-foot-wide **Line** extending **15 feet** from the target
directly away from you. Each creature in the Line makes a **Strength saving throw
against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking **1d6 Thunder damage** on a failure
(this die increases to **1d8 at level 7, 1d10 at level 10, and 1d12 at level
15**).

---

## Crack the Air (Level 7)

> Distance is a courtesy you no longer extend to anyone.

You can attack things you aren't touching. When you take the **Attack** action,
any melee weapon or unarmed strike you make gains a **range of 30 feet** for that
attack — you swing or punch and a quake-blade of compressed air arrives at the
target. These ranged attacks use your normal melee attack bonus and deal their
normal damage (Thunder if you choose), and they can apply your **Seismic
Strikes** Tremor.

When you score a **Critical Hit** with any attack, the Tremor's Line widens to a
**15-foot Cone** and the damage die is rolled twice.

---

## Quakeguard (Level 10)

> What shakes can also *steady*. The same hands that crack the sky can hold it
> shut.

You gain **Resistance to Thunder damage**, and you can vibrate incoming force
apart. When you or a creature within **30 feet** of you takes damage, you can use
your **Reaction** to reduce that damage by **1d12 + your Fighter level.**

You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**,
regaining all uses on a **Short or Long Rest.**

Additionally, your **Tremor** now knocks creatures **Prone** on a failed save (in
addition to its damage).

---

## Seaquake (Level 15)

> Whitebeard's signature: grab the whole field at once and tilt it.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can slam a quake into the ground or air around you.
Each creature of your choice within **30 feet** makes a **Strength saving throw
against your Fighter Subclass DC.** On a failure, a creature takes **4d8 Thunder
damage**, is knocked **Prone**, and is pushed **10 feet** away; on a success it
takes half damage and isn't moved. Structures and unattended objects in the area
take **double damage**.

You can use Seaquake a number of times equal to your **Strength modifier**
(minimum once), regaining all uses on a **Long Rest.**

---

## Worldbreaker (Level 18)

> The man who can destroy the world. Stand somewhere else.

Your tremors ignore the rules that protect everything else.

- **Unstoppable Resonance.** Your Thunder damage **ignores Resistance**, and
  treats **Immunity as Resistance.**
- **Reach of the Quake.** The range of your **Crack the Air** attacks increases
  to **120 feet**, and your **Seismic Strikes** Tremor can originate from any
  point within that range rather than only from a creature you hit.
- **The Whole Island.** Once per **Long Rest**, you can spend your action to
  unleash a catastrophic quake. Choose a **60-foot-radius Sphere** centered on a
  point you can see within 120 feet. Each creature there makes a **Strength
  saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking **10d10 Thunder
  damage** and being knocked **Prone** and **Restrained** (buried) on a failure,
  or half damage on a success. A Restrained creature repeats the save at the end
  of each of its turns. The terrain in the area becomes **Difficult Terrain**,
  and any structure in it is **reduced to rubble.**
