# Adamant Bulwark

*Martial Archetype — Defense / Tank*

> Hit you. Go ahead. Their best swing rings off your shoulder like a hammer off
> an anvil and leaves you bored. Somewhere in you is a vein of something harder
> than steel, and when you call on it your skin sets into facets that catch the
> light and turn aside the blade. You are not the fastest. You are not the
> flashiest. You are the wall the enemy breaks themselves against, and you have
> never once needed to be moved.

The Adamant Bulwark turns their own body into crystal — a tank built around
absurd AC, layered resistances, and the simple, infuriating refusal to take
damage. Flavor it as diamond skin, a living-mineral physiology, nano-plate that
blooms across your body, or stone-flesh inherited from something old. The role:
**the immovable object** — you hold the line, soak the alpha strike, and make the
enemy's biggest hits feel like a waste of everyone's time.

---

## Crystalline Body (Level 3)

> The shine isn't decoration. It's load-bearing.

You can harden your body into a gemstone lattice. While you aren't wearing Heavy
armor, your skin counts as a natural defense: your **AC equals 13 + your
Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier** (you can still use a shield).

As a **Bonus Action**, you can fully crystallize for **1 minute** (or until you
end it, no action). While crystallized:

- You gain a **+1 bonus to AC.**
- You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.**
- You have **Advantage on saving throws against being moved, knocked Prone, or
  Grappled.**

You can crystallize a number of times equal to your **Second Wind uses**, and
regain all expended uses on a **Long Rest** (you can also spend a Second Wind use
to crystallize when you have none).

---

## Facet Guard (Level 7)

> Every face of the crystal points the damage somewhere it isn't.

Your hardened body deflects blows onto its angles. While crystallized, when you
are hit by an attack you can use your **Reaction** to reduce that attack's damage
by **1d10 + your Constitution modifier**; if this reduces the damage to 0, the
attacker takes **that prevented amount as Piercing damage** (shrapnel) up to a
maximum of your Fighter level.

You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**,
regaining all uses on a **Short or Long Rest.**

Additionally, you can crystallize as a **Reaction** when you are hit by an attack
or forced to make a saving throw (applying its benefits before the triggering
roll resolves).

---

## Unyielding Lattice (Level 10)

> They can break the surface. They cannot break *you.* Those are different
> projects.

Your durability becomes structural.

- Your crystallized **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing now
  applies even against magical sources**, and you gain **Resistance to one
  additional damage type of your choice** while crystallized (you can change the
  choice each time you crystallize).
- While crystallized you have **Immunity to the Stunned and Paralyzed
  conditions** — a statue does not flinch.
- When a creature within 5 feet hits you with a melee attack while you're
  crystallized, it takes **Piercing damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.**

---

## Diamond Body (Level 15)

> The hardest natural substance in the world, wearing your face.

Your crystal form reaches gemstone perfection. While crystallized:

- Your **AC bonus increases to +3** (from +1), and you can't be made to have
  Disadvantage on saving throws.
- Once per turn, when you would take damage, you can **reduce that damage by an
  amount equal to your Fighter level** (before resistances apply).
- You have **Immunity to Critical Hits** — your facets simply have no soft spot to
  find.

You also gain a **Brace** reaction: when an ally within 5 feet is hit by an
attack, you can use your **Reaction** to take the damage **in their place**,
applying all your resistances and reductions to it.

---

## Adamantine Apex (Level 18)

> Mountains weather. You don't.

You become very nearly indestructible.

- **Flawless.** While crystallized, the **first 10 damage from each instance of
  damage you take is ignored** (applied before resistances). Combined with your
  resistances and reductions, most hits simply slough off.
- **Perfect Cleavage.** Once per turn while crystallized, when a creature within
  reach hits you or an adjacent ally, you can use your **Reaction** to counter
  with a single weapon attack; on a hit, the target is knocked **Prone** and
  **pushed 15 feet.**
- **Unbroken.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points while crystallized, you
  instead drop to **a number of hit points equal to your Fighter level** and your
  crystallization ends. Once you use this, you can't again until you finish a
  **Long Rest.**
