# Demon-King's Heir

*Martial Archetype — Offense / Control*

> There is a throne in your blood that you never asked to inherit. Most days you
> keep it cold and empty. But push you far enough — threaten what you love,
> corner you, spill enough of your blood — and the heir takes the seat. Horns.
> Wings of shadow. Eyes that make brave people sit down. The form is monstrous
> and it is *yours*, and the worst part, the part you don't say out loud, is how
> good it feels to stop holding back.

The Demon-King's Heir transforms into a demonic sovereign — a terror-projecting,
hard-hitting controller who rules the battlefield through fear and dominion.
Flavor it as a sealed demon soul, a devil-fruit monarch, a fiendish bloodline, or
a hero who borrows the enemy's power and barely gives it back. The fantasy:
**become the thing nightmares answer to** — strong, frightening, and in command
of everyone foolish enough to still be standing.

---

## Demon Form (Level 3)

> The crown is heavier than it looks. You wear it anyway.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can unleash your **Demon Form** for **1 minute** (or
until you end it, no action; it also ends if you fall Unconscious). You can do so
a number of times equal to your **Second Wind uses**, regaining all expended uses
on a **Long Rest** (or by spending a Second Wind use). When you transform, you
gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Fighter level.**

While in Demon Form:

- You sprout claws and shadow-wings: your **unarmed strikes deal 1d10** (Slashing
  or Necrotic, your choice), and you gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed.**
- The first time each turn you hit a creature with an attack, it takes **extra
  Necrotic damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.**
- You exude a **Dread Aura**: when you transform, and as a **Bonus Action**
  afterward, each enemy within **30 feet** makes a **Wisdom saving throw against
  your Fighter Subclass DC** or is **Frightened of you until the end of your next
  turn.**

---

## Sovereign's Presence (Level 7)

> Kneel. Most of them already want to; you're only making it official.

Your fear becomes a weapon and a leash.

- Creatures **Frightened of you** have **Disadvantage on all saving throws**
  against your features and take the **full effect** of your Dread Aura each turn
  (no save to shake it while within 30 feet — they may only look away, gaining the
  benefits of being unable to see you but suffering Disadvantage on attacks).
- Once per turn, when you hit a creature that is **Frightened of you**, you can
  **drain its vigor**: it takes an extra **2d6 Necrotic damage** and you gain
  Temporary Hit Points equal to the amount.

---

## Demonic Dominion (Level 10)

> The throne extends an invitation. It is not the kind you decline.

Your control over the field deepens. While in Demon Form:

- You gain **Resistance to Necrotic, Cold, and nonmagical Bludgeoning, Piercing,
  and Slashing damage.**
- As a **Bonus Action**, you can attempt to **dominate a Frightened creature**
  within 30 feet: it makes a **Wisdom saving throw against your Fighter Subclass
  DC**, and on a failure you can use your **Reaction**, when it would act, to
  dictate a single creature it targets or a direction it moves on its next turn
  (a one-command compulsion). A creature can be subject to this once at a time.
- When a creature Frightened of you drops to 0 hit points within 30 feet, you
  regain a use of **Demon Form** (once per Short Rest).

---

## The Crown Descends (Level 15)

> You stop borrowing the power. You start *being* it.

Your transformation reaches its terrible maturity. While in Demon Form:

- Your unarmed strikes deal **2d10** and **score Critical Hits on 19–20.**
- Your **Dread Aura expands to 60 feet**, and creatures that start their turn
  Frightened of you within it take **Necrotic damage equal to your Fighter
  level.**
- You are **immune to the Frightened and Charmed conditions**, and you have
  **Advantage on Strength, Constitution, and Charisma saving throws.**
- When you are reduced to 0 hit points while transformed, you can use your
  **Reaction** to feed on the surrounding terror, dropping to **1 hit point** and
  gaining **Temporary Hit Points equal to the number of creatures Frightened of
  you times your Proficiency Bonus.** Once per Long Rest.

---

## Demon King (Level 18)

> The seat is no longer empty, and it never will be again. Long live the king.

You become a true Demon King.

- **Absolute Dread.** Enemies that can see you within **60 feet** are
  **Frightened of you** unless they succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw against
  your Fighter Subclass DC** at the start of each of their turns (they may repeat
  it each turn). Your **Sovereign's Presence** and **Demonic Dominion** apply to
  all of them.
- **Reign of Ruin.** Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with a
  melee attack in Demon Form, you can erupt with dark power: the target and each
  creature in a **20-foot-radius Sphere** centered on it make a **Constitution
  saving throw against your Fighter Subclass DC**, taking **6d8 Necrotic damage**
  on a failure (half on a success). You **regain hit points equal to the highest
  amount dealt** to any single creature.
- **The King Does Not Fall.** While in Demon Form, you have **Advantage on Death
  Saving Throws**, and your transformation no longer ends when you fall
  Unconscious.
