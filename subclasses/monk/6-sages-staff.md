# Warrior of the Sage's Staff

*Monk Subclass*

The staff in your hands is no humble walking-stick. It lengthens at a thought, stretches
across a battlefield, splits into a storm of striking copies, and grows so heavy at one
end that the earth cracks beneath it — yet you spin it light as a reed. You climb the
wind on it, vault between the clouds, and bring it down on a distant foe as if distance
were a polite suggestion. Those who know the old tales know the type: the irrepressible
trickster-king who could not be caged, could not be killed, and answered to no heaven.
You carry his weapon, and a little of his refusal to be told *no*.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
Wisdom modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You
spend **Focus Points** (called Ki in some traditions). The features below function only
while you wield a Quarterstaff, which is a Monk weapon; use your Martial Arts die in place
of the staff's normal damage die.*

## Level 3: The Cloud-Piercing Staff

It grows, it shrinks, it weighs as much as you need it to.

***As-You-Will Cudgel.*** While you wield a Quarterstaff, its damage die becomes one size
larger than your Martial Arts die (for example, d8 becomes d10), and your reach with it
increases by 5 feet.

***Heaven-and-Earth.*** Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with the staff,
you can make it as heavy as a mountain or as swift as light: either knock the target Prone
(it can resist with a successful Strength saving throw) or push it up to 15 feet away (no
save).

***Far Strike.*** As part of the Attack action, one staff attack can extend to a target
within 30 feet of you or strike every creature in a 15-foot-long Line (make one attack
roll against each).

## Level 6: Seventy-Two Transformations

The trickster's gift: never quite where, or what, the enemy expects.

***Hair-Clone Strike.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to conjure two
spectral duplicates of yourself in unoccupied spaces you can see within 30 feet. They last
for 1 minute. Once on each of your turns, as part of your Attack action, each duplicate can
make one staff attack (using your game statistics) against a creature within 5 feet of it.
A duplicate has an Armor Class equal to your Monk DC and vanishes if it takes any damage or
ends a turn more than 60 feet from you.

***Sky-Walking.*** While you wield a Quarterstaff, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed,
but you fall if you end your turn airborne (this limitation is removed at Level 11). You can
also vault off the staff to take the Disengage and Dash actions, once on each of your turns
without using an action.

***Cloud-Somersault.*** When you take the Step of the Wind Bonus Action, you can teleport up
to your Speed instead of moving normally.

## Level 11: Somersault Cloud and Iron Body

Sworn off no heaven, you simply stop coming down.

***Sustained Flight.*** Your Fly Speed no longer requires you to land at the end of your
turn, and it increases to your Speed + 20 feet.

***Eighty-One-Pound Smash.*** As part of the Attack action, you can spend 2 Focus Points to
bring the staff down with crushing weight on a point within 60 feet. Each creature in a
15-foot-radius Sphere centered there takes Bludgeoning damage equal to three rolls of your
Martial Arts die, or half as much on a successful Dexterity saving throw, and is knocked
Prone on a failure. The area becomes Difficult Terrain until the start of your next turn.

***Body of Iron.*** Your Armor Class increases by 1 while you wear no armor, you have
Resistance to Bludgeoning damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws against spells
while you wield your staff.

***More Hands.*** Your Hair-Clone Strike now conjures four duplicates instead of two.

## Level 17: The Great Sage, Equal of Heaven

The title the trickster-king claimed for himself, and dared the heavens to deny.

***Length of the Heavens.*** Your staff's reach can extend to 60 feet for any single
attack, and your staff attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.

***Storm of a Thousand Staves.*** As an action, you can spend 4 Focus Points to whirl the
staff into a blur that fills the battlefield. Each creature you choose within 60 feet of
you takes Bludgeoning or Force damage (your choice) equal to eight rolls of your Martial
Arts die, or half as much on a successful Dexterity saving throw. A creature that fails is
knocked Prone and pushed 20 feet. You can then make one Hair-Clone Strike without spending
a Focus Point.

***Equal of Heaven.*** While you wield your staff, you have Resistance to all damage and
can't gain the Grappled, Restrained, Paralyzed, or Petrified conditions. In addition, if
you would drop to 0 Hit Points, you can spend 3 Focus Points to drop to 1 Hit Point
instead. You regain the use of this last benefit when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

---

### Designer Notes

- This is the versatile juggernaut of the set: reach, ranged strikes, area damage, flight,
  duplicates, and durability all in one kit. The leash that keeps it fair is the
  "only while you wield a Quarterstaff" clause — disarm or sunder the staff and most of the
  subclass switches off, a fun vulnerability for a clever foe.
- The duplicates (two → four) are a real action-economy multiplier. If they prove too
  swingy, limit each duplicate to a single attack per round rather than per turn.
