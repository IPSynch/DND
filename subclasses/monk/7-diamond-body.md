# Warrior of the Diamond Body

*Monk Subclass*

Pain taught you the lesson early, and you took it further than anyone intended: if a
body can be hardened, why stop at calluses? You learned to clench your skin into stone,
then stone into something harder — to fold your flesh along invisible facets until a
blade meets you and shatters. In your hardened form you gleam like a struck gem, you
weigh like a mountain, and the strongest blow in the room rings off you and leaves you
grinning. You are the wall your friends stand behind, and the last thing a charging
enemy expected to find unmovable.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
Wisdom modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You
spend **Focus Points** (called Ki in some traditions). Your **Diamond Body** is a stance
you turn on and off; several features function only while it is active.*

## Level 3: Crystalline Skin

Harden, and the world's edges find nothing to bite.

***Diamond Body.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to harden your body.
This stance lasts for 1 minute. It ends early if you fall Unconscious or if you end it
(no action required). While hardened, you gain a +2 bonus to your Armor Class, you have
Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and your Unarmed Strikes deal
extra damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die.

***Facet Guard.*** While hardened, when you are hit by a melee attack, the attacker takes
damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die as the strike rings off your faceted
skin.

## Level 6: Immovable Object

A mountain does not flinch, and it does not let you past.

***Unmovable.*** While hardened, you have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks
you make to avoid or end the Grappled, Restrained, and Prone conditions, and you can't be
moved against your will unless you allow it.

***Bulwark.*** While hardened, the space within 5 feet of you is Difficult Terrain for
your enemies to leave. In addition, when a creature you can see within 5 feet of you makes
an attack against a target other than you, you can take a Reaction to make that attack
target you instead.

***Diamond Knuckle.*** While hardened, your Unarmed Strikes ignore Resistance to their
damage type and treat any bonus to a target's Armor Class from Cover as 0.

## Level 11: Brilliant Adamant

Beyond stone, beyond steel — the one substance that cuts everything and is cut by nothing.

***Adamant Form.*** While hardened, your Resistance extends to all damage except Psychic
and Force, and any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit instead.

***Refraction.*** While hardened, when you succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against an
effect that deals damage, you take no damage instead of half; on a failed save, you take
half damage instead of full.

***Pillar of the Party.*** Your Armor Class increases by 1 while you wear no armor, even
when you are not hardened, and your Hit Point maximum increases by an amount equal to twice
your Monk level.

## Level 17: Living Diamond

The transformation is no longer something you do. It is something you are.

***Perfected Lattice.*** While hardened, you have Resistance to all damage (including
Psychic and Force), you can't gain the Stunned, Paralyzed, or Petrified conditions, and
the bonus to your Armor Class from Diamond Body increases to +3.

***Earthshatter Fist.*** As part of the Attack action, you can spend 3 Focus Points to
drive a diamond fist into the ground or a foe. One creature you hit, and each creature in a
15-foot-radius Sphere around it, takes Bludgeoning damage equal to six rolls of your
Martial Arts die and is knocked Prone; a successful Constitution saving throw halves the
damage and prevents the Prone condition. The terrain in the area becomes Difficult Terrain.

***Unbreakable.*** While hardened, the first time each round you would drop to 0 Hit
Points, you drop to 1 Hit Point instead.

---

### Designer Notes

- This subclass is built to be the party's anchor: high Armor Class, all-damage Resistance,
  crit cancellation, and a Reaction taunt let it soak punishment that would delete other
  Monks, while Facet Guard and Earthshatter Fist keep its damage relevant.
- The defensive numbers are stacked on purpose. If your table finds it unkillable rather
  than merely very tanky, the cleanest dial is to make Diamond Body cost 1 Focus Point per
  round to sustain rather than running a full minute on one point.
