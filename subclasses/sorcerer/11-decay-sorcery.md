# Decay Sorcery

*Magic That Unmakes*

Everything you touch comes apart. Stone crumbles to grit, steel flakes to rust,
flesh withers from the bone—your magic is the simple, terrible principle that all
things fall to ruin, hurried along by your will. To you, a fortress is already
dust; it simply hasn't finished collapsing yet.

## Level 3: Decay Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Decay Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Decay Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Catapult*, *Ray of Sickness* |
| 5 | *Shatter*, *Blindness/Deafness* |
| 7 | *Vitriolic Sphere*, *Blight* |
| 9 | *Disintegrate*, *Cloudkill* |

## Level 3: Unmaking Touch

Your magic accelerates the ruin of everything it meets. You gain the following
benefits.

**Entropy.** Whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell that deals damage, you can change
that spell's damage type to Force or Necrotic.

**Crumbling.** Your spells deal double damage to objects and structures and ignore
their Damage Thresholds.

**Decaying Grasp.** As a Magic action, make a melee spell attack against a creature
or object within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes Necrotic damage equal to
your Decay die total, and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to that
damage until it finishes a Long Rest. The damage increases as you gain Sorcerer
levels: it is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level 7, **4d8** at level 11, and
**5d8** at level 15. This amount is your **Decay die total**. If this reduces a
Large or smaller nonmagical object or creature's Hit Point maximum to 0, it crumbles
to fine dust.

## Level 6: Accelerated Decay

Ruin spreads from your spells like rot through fruit. You gain the following benefits.

**Inexorable.** Your Force and Necrotic damage ignores Resistance.

**Withering.** Once on each of your turns when a creature takes Force or Necrotic
damage from one of your Sorcerer spells, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an
amount equal to your Charisma modifier until it finishes a Long Rest. Whenever you
reduce a creature's Hit Point maximum, you regain 1 Sorcery Point (no more than once
per turn).

**Dust to Dust.** A creature whose Hit Point maximum is reduced to 0 by your magic
dies and crumbles to dust, and it can be restored to life only by a *True
Resurrection* or *Wish* spell.

## Level 14: Field of Ruin

As a Magic action, you collapse an area into entropy. Choose a 20-foot-radius Sphere
within 120 feet of yourself; it becomes a field of decay for 1 minute, requiring no
Concentration. The area is Difficult Terrain, and unattended objects and structures
in it crumble to dust. Whenever a creature enters the area for the first time on a
turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw against your
spell save DC. On a failed save, it takes 6d8 Necrotic damage and its Hit Point
maximum is reduced by that amount until it finishes a Long Rest; on a successful
save, it takes half as much damage and no reduction.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest,
unless you spend 4 Sorcery Points to use it again.

## Level 18: Total Disintegration

The principle of ruin bends fully to your hand. You gain the following benefits.

**Absolute Entropy.** Your Force and Necrotic damage ignores Resistance and treats
Immunity to those damage types as Resistance instead.

**Annihilate.** As a Magic action, choose a creature or object you can see within 120
feet of you. A creature makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC,
taking 14d6 Force damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. If
this damage reduces the creature to 0 Hit Points, it and everything it is wearing and
carrying (other than magic items) is disintegrated into dust. A nonmagical object, or
a creation of magical force, is automatically disintegrated. Once you use this
feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend 6
Sorcery Points to use it again.
