# Breastmilk Sorcery

*Magic of the Milk — the Sorcery That Sustains*

Some sorcerers learn to kill. You learned to *keep people alive*. Your body is a
wellspring of warm, glowing milk that mends flesh, washes out poison, and pours
strength back into the dying. A spray closes a wound; a mouthful purges a plague; a
flood at the right moment hauls a fallen friend back from the dark. Where the
battlefield healers ration their spell slots, you simply never run dry — you are a
mother to the whole party, and nobody bleeds out on your watch.

> **A note on tone.** A nurturing, generous origin with a frankly maternal,
> unabashedly bodily flavor — bawdy but never explicit. Reskin "milk" to "blessed
> water," "sap," or "elixir" if your table prefers; the healing math is unchanged.

## Level 3: Breastmilk Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the table, you thereafter always have the
listed spells prepared.

**Breastmilk Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Cure Wounds*, *Healing Word* |
| 5 | *Aid*, *Lesser Restoration* |
| 7 | *Aura of Vitality*, *Mass Healing Word* |
| 9 | *Greater Restoration*, *Death Ward* |

## Level 3: Nurturing Vitality

You are soft, warm, and far hardier than you look. While you aren't wearing armor,
your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 3: Mother's Milk

You let down a flow of restorative milk at will, no spellcasting required. The potency
of your milk is your **Milk die total**: it is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level 7,
**4d8** at level 11, and **5d8** at level 15. (Your Font of Magic feature turns spell
slots into Sorcery Points, so you can heal all day instead of casting.) Each option
below is used as a **Bonus Action** unless noted, and effects with a range use a range
of **30 feet**.

### Nurse (Burst) — 1 Sorcery Point

A creature you can see regains Hit Points equal to your **Milk die total + your
Charisma modifier**.

### Nourish — 1 Sorcery Point

A creature you can see gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Milk die total +
your Charisma modifier**, lasting 1 hour, and has **Advantage on the next saving throw
it makes** before the temporary Hit Points are gone.

### Cleanse — 1 Sorcery Point

A creature you can see ends **one** of the following on itself: the Blinded, Deafened,
or Poisoned condition; one effect causing it to be Charmed or Frightened; one disease;
or one level of Exhaustion.

### Suckling Font (Sustained) — 3 Sorcery Points

A 15-foot Emanation of warm mist surrounds you and moves with you for 1 minute (no
Concentration). At the start of each of your turns, each creature of your choice in the
area regains Hit Points equal to **1d8 + your Charisma modifier**.

### Flood of Life (Overcharged) — 4 Sorcery Points

A rushing tide of milk washes over the field. Up to **six** creatures you can see each
regain Hit Points equal to your **Milk die total**, and each one also ends the Poisoned
condition on itself.

## Level 6: Bountiful Bosom

Your glands run deep and your reflexes turn protective. You gain the following
benefits.

**Hardy Constitution.** You have **Resistance to Necrotic damage**, you are immune to
disease, and a creature you restore Hit Points to with Mother's Milk or a Sorcerer
spell has **Advantage on its next death saving throw** before the start of your next
turn.

**Potent Milk.** Whenever you roll dice to restore Hit Points with a feature or spell,
you can **reroll any 1s and 2s** and must use the new rolls. When you restore Hit
Points to a creature above its Hit Point maximum, the **excess becomes Temporary Hit
Points** (up to your Sorcerer level).

**Letdown Reflex (Reaction).** When a creature you can see within 30 feet takes damage
or drops to 0 Hit Points, you can use your Reaction to let down a healing spray: the
creature regains Hit Points equal to your **Milk die total + your Charisma modifier**,
and if it was at 0 Hit Points it is no longer dying. You can do this a number of times
equal to your **Charisma modifier** per Long Rest for free; after that, you can do it
again by spending **2 Sorcery Points**.

## Level 14: Aura of Nurture

You become a font of constant, motherly sustenance. You emit a 15-foot Emanation that
you can turn on or off at will (no action required). While the aura is active:

- At the start of each of its turns, each creature of your choice in the aura regains
  **Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier** and has **Resistance to Necrotic
  damage**.
- The **first time** each round that a creature of your choice in the aura would drop
  to 0 Hit Points, it instead drops to **1 Hit Point** (the milk keeps it on its feet).

In addition:

**Free Letdown.** The first option of **Mother's Milk** you use on each of your turns
costs **no Sorcery Points**.

## Level 18: Font of Life

Your milk becomes a miracle. You gain the following benefits.

**Maximized Letdown.** Once on each of your turns, when you restore Hit Points with
Mother's Milk or a Sorcerer spell, you can use the **highest number possible** for each
healing die instead of rolling.

**Perfected Body.** You are immune to the Poisoned condition and to disease, you no
longer age, and you can't be aged magically.

**Miracle of the Milk.** As a Magic action, you pour out a flood of life in a 30-foot
Emanation. Each creature of your choice in the area **regains all lost Hit Points**,
ends the Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, and Stunned conditions and one level
of Exhaustion on itself, and gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Sorcerer
level**. Furthermore, each creature of your choice that **died within the last minute**
and is in the area **returns to life** with Hit Points equal to your Sorcerer level
(this works like *Raise Dead* but is instantaneous and imposes none of that spell's
penalties). Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long
Rest**, unless you spend **6 Sorcery Points** to use it again.
