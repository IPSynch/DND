# Bowel Sorcery

*Magic of the Gut, the Bowels, and the Anus*

Your power comes out of your ass. Farts, shit, and the rancid gas your gut has been
brewing—you weaponize all of it. You blast it in clouds, spray it in sheets, and jet it
hard enough to fly. It's vile, it's humiliating, and it drops bodies dead: your bowels
are a swamp of rot, and rot, in a sorcerer's hands, kills as fast as any blade. Best
part—you never need to cast a damn spell. Just a full gut and the Sorcery Points to let
it rip.

## Level 3: Bowel Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Bowel Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Bowel Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Ray of Sickness*, *Grease* |
| 5 | *Spirit Guardians*, *Stinking Cloud*, *Ray of Enfeeblement* |
| 7 | *Vitriolic Sphere*, *Blight* |
| 9 | *Cloudkill*, *Contagion* |

When you cast *Spirit Guardians*, your spirits are rotting, reeking things: the spell
deals only Necrotic damage for you (you can't choose Radiant), and any creature that
fails its saving throw against the spell also has the Poisoned condition until the start
of its next turn.

## Level 3: Foul Discharge

You channel the contents of your bowels into magic, no spellcasting required. Your
damaging Bowel Sorcery effects deal either Poison or Necrotic damage (your choice each
time you use them), and the amount increases as you gain Sorcerer levels: it is **2d8**
at level 3, **3d8** at level 7, **4d8** at level 11, and **5d8** at level 15. This amount
is your **Bowel die total**, and all effects below use your spell save DC.

As a Bonus Action, you can release one of the following discharges, paying its Sorcery
Point cost. (Remember that your Font of Magic feature lets you turn spell slots into
Sorcery Points, so you can fuel these maneuvers all day instead of casting spells.)

### Burst (cheap, instantaneous)

- **Backblast (1 Sorcery Point).** A cloud of foul gas erupts in a 15-foot Cone. Each
  creature in it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking your Bowel die total on a
  failed save (half as much on a success) and having the Poisoned condition until the end
  of its next turn on a failure.
- **Putrid Slumber (2 Sorcery Points).** *Knockout gas, as the* Sleep *spell.* Each
  creature in a 15-foot-radius Sphere within 60 feet of you makes a Constitution saving
  throw. On a failed save, a creature has the Incapacitated condition and falls Prone,
  overcome by sleep, until the end of its next turn; it wakes if it takes damage or a
  creature uses an action to rouse it. A creature repeats the save at the end of each of
  its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Creatures that don't sleep are
  unaffected.
- **Feces Bomb (3 Sorcery Points).** *An explosive blast, as the* Fireball *spell.* Hurl
  a packed ball of volatile filth at a point within 120 feet of you; each creature in a
  20-foot-radius Sphere there makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking twice your Bowel die
  total on a failed save (half as much on a success). The blast coats the area and every
  creature that failed in clinging filth until the end of your next turn.

### Sustained (1 minute, no Concentration)

- **Lingering Stench (3 Sorcery Points).** A 15-foot Emanation of reeking gas surrounds
  you and moves with you; you and creatures you choose are unaffected. The Speed of any
  other creature in it is halved. Whenever an affected creature enters the area for the
  first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw,
  taking your Bowel die total on a failed save (half as much on a success) and having the
  Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn on a failure.
- **Explosive Diarrhea (2 Sorcery Points).** *A repeating spray, as the* Dragon's Breath
  *spell.* For the duration, you can take a Bonus Action on each of your turns to spray a
  30-foot Cone of liquid filth. Each creature in the Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw,
  taking your Bowel die total on a failed save (half as much on a success).
- **Sludge Field (2 Sorcery Points).** *Miring muck, as the* Slow *spell.* You flood a
  20-foot-radius area on the ground within 60 feet of you with liquid feces. A creature
  that is in contact with the ground in the area is subjected to the *Slow* spell (Speed
  halved; −2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws; can't take Reactions; takes either
  one action or one Bonus Action on its turn, not both). Flying creatures are unaffected. A
  creature shakes off the effect at the end of any turn it ends outside the area.
- **Hardening Filth (2 Sorcery Points).** Feces erupts around your foes and sets like
  stone. Choose a point within 60 feet of you; each creature in a 15-foot-radius Sphere
  there makes a Strength saving throw, gaining the Restrained condition on a failure. A
  Restrained creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect
  on itself on a success.

### Overcharged (expensive, devastating)

- **Filth Armament (4 Sorcery Points).** You force out a mass of feces through your
  anus—highly moldable yet remarkably sturdy—and, as part of this Bonus Action, mold it in
  your hand into a weapon of your choice (a blade, club, whip, or the like). The weapon
  lasts until you finish a Long Rest, as the matter hardens and keeps. When you create it,
  choose Poison or Necrotic; that is its damage type for as long as it exists. The weapon
  has the Reach property, and you can dismiss it (no action required).

  You attack with the weapon as a Bonus Action. The attack is a spell attack that uses
  your spell attack modifier, and it benefits from your Innate Sorcery feature's Advantage
  on attack rolls as though it were a Sorcerer spell. On a hit, it deals your Bowel die
  total plus your Charisma modifier damage of its chosen type. Once on each of your turns
  when you hit with it, you can force the target to make a Constitution saving throw or
  have the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.

  You can hand the weapon to another creature. While another creature wields it, that
  creature can attack with it as a Bonus Action, making a spell attack with a bonus equal
  to its proficiency bonus plus its Charisma modifier; on a hit, the weapon deals 2d8 plus
  that creature's Charisma modifier damage of the weapon's chosen type. You can have only
  one Filth Armament at a time.

- **Bowel Breath (4 Sorcery Points).** You unleash a torrent of gas and filth in a 60-foot
  Line that is 5 feet wide, or a 30-foot Cone (your choice). Each creature in the area
  makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking three times your Bowel die total on a failed save
  (half as much on a success) and having the Poisoned and Blinded conditions until the end
  of its next turn on a failure.

## Level 3: Bloated Hide

Your gut is thick, distended, and remarkably hard to pierce. While you aren't wearing
armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Jet Propulsion

Your bowels become both engine and weapon. You gain the following benefits.

**Rot Piercing.** The Poison and Necrotic damage of your Bowel Sorcery ignores Resistance
and treats Immunity to those damage types as Resistance instead. A creature that is immune
to the Poisoned condition isn't immune to the Poisoned condition from your features;
instead, it makes the saving throw against that effect with Advantage.

**Flatulent Flight.** When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you gain a Fly Speed
equal to your Speed for the feature's duration, propelled by jets of gas. Whenever a
creature of your choice starts its turn within 5 feet of you, or you fly within 5 feet of
a creature of your choice during your movement, that creature makes a Constitution saving
throw, taking 1d8 Poison or Necrotic damage on a failed save. This save is made
separately from any other Bowel Sorcery effect—so a creature caught in your Lingering
Stench (or a similar aura) as well rolls a Constitution save for each effect, and the
damage stacks.

**Hardened Gut.** You have Resistance to Poison and Necrotic damage and immunity to the
Poisoned condition.

## Level 14: Aura of Filth

A reek pours from your anus—not from your body, which remains as alluring as ever; it is
purely the magic of your bowels—blanketing everything nearby. You emit a 10-foot Emanation
of foul air that you can turn on or off at will (no action required). While the aura is
active, whenever an enemy starts its turn in it, that creature makes a Constitution saving
throw, taking 2d8 Poison or Necrotic damage on a failed save and having the Poisoned
condition until the end of its next turn. While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, the
aura expands to 20 feet and a creature that fails the save also can't take Reactions until
the start of its next turn.

## Level 18: Cataclysmic Bowel Movement

Your gut empties with apocalyptic force. As a Magic action, you erupt with gas and filth in
a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on yourself. Each creature in the area makes a
Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10d8 Poison damage plus 10d8
Necrotic damage and has the Poisoned, Blinded, and Prone conditions for 1 minute; on a
successful save, it takes half as much damage and has the Prone condition only. A creature
repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the Poisoned and Blinded
conditions on itself on a success.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
