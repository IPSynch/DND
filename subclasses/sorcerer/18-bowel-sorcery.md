# Bowel Sorcery

*Magic of the Gut, the Bowels, and the Anus*

Your power comes out the back. You weaponize your own farts and feces—jets of foul gas,
sprays of filth, blasts strong enough to lift you off the ground. It is disgusting, it
is undignified, and it is genuinely deadly: the gut is full of rot, and rot, channeled
by a sorcerer, kills as surely as any blade.

## Level 3: Bowel Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Bowel Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Foul Discharge

You channel the contents of your bowels into magic. Your damaging Bowel Sorcery effects
deal either Poison or Necrotic damage (your choice each time), and the amount increases
as you gain Sorcerer levels: it is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level 7, **4d8** at
level 11, and **5d8** at level 15. This amount is your **Bowel die total**, and all
effects below use your spell save DC.

As a Bonus Action, you can release one of the following discharges, paying its Sorcery
Point cost.

***Burst (cheap, short-lived).***

- **Backblast (1 Sorcery Point).** A cloud of foul gas erupts in a 15-foot Cone. Each
  creature in it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking your Bowel die total as Poison
  or Necrotic damage on a failed save (half as much on a success) and having the Poisoned
  condition until the start of your next turn on a failure.

***Sustained (moderate, lasting).***

- **Lingering Stench (3 Sorcery Points).** A 15-foot Emanation of reeking gas surrounds
  you for 1 minute (no Concentration); you and creatures you choose are unaffected. The
  Speed of any other creature in it is halved. Whenever an affected creature enters the
  area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution
  saving throw, taking your Bowel die total as Poison or Necrotic damage on a failed save
  (half as much on a success) and having the Poisoned condition until the start of its
  next turn on a failure.

***Overcharged (expensive, devastating).***

- **Filth Armament (4 Sorcery Points).** You compress feces into a magical weapon—a
  reeking blade, club, or whip—that appears in your hand for 1 minute (no Concentration).
  It uses your spell attack modifier and deals 2d8 plus your Charisma modifier Poison or
  Necrotic damage on a hit, has the Reach property, and you can draw or dismiss it as a
  Bonus Action. Once on each of your turns when you hit with it, you can force the target
  to make a Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition until the end of its
  next turn. You can make one attack with it as a Bonus Action on each of your turns.
- **Bowel Breath (5 Sorcery Points).** You unleash a torrent of gas and filth in a
  60-foot Line that is 5 feet wide, or a 30-foot Cone (your choice). Each creature in the
  area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking twice your Bowel die total as Poison or
  Necrotic damage on a failed save (half as much on a success) and having the Poisoned
  and Blinded conditions until the end of its next turn on a failure.

## Level 6: Jet Propulsion

Your bowels become both engine and weapon. You gain the following benefits.

**Rot Piercing.** The Poison and Necrotic damage of your Bowel Sorcery ignores Resistance
and treats Immunity to those damage types as Resistance instead. A creature that is immune
to the Poisoned condition isn't immune to the Poisoned condition from your features;
instead, it makes the saving throw against that effect with Advantage.

**Flatulent Flight.** When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you gain a Fly Speed
equal to your Speed for the feature's duration, propelled by jets of gas. While flying this
way, a creature within 5 feet of you when you start your flight or when you move away from
it makes a Constitution saving throw or takes 1d8 Poison or Necrotic damage.

**Hardened Gut.** You have Resistance to Poison and Necrotic damage and immunity to the
Poisoned condition.

## Level 14: Aura of Filth

You are wrapped in a permanent reek that withers those who come near. You emit a 10-foot
Emanation of foul air. Whenever an enemy starts its turn in the aura, it makes a
Constitution saving throw, taking 2d8 Poison or Necrotic damage on a failed save and
having the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. While your Innate Sorcery
feature is active, the aura expands to 20 feet and a creature that fails the save also
can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.

## Level 18: Cataclysmic Bowel Movement

Your gut empties with apocalyptic force. As a Magic action, you erupt with gas and filth in
a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on yourself. Each creature in the area makes a
Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10d8 Poison damage plus 10d8
Necrotic damage and has the Poisoned, Blinded, and Prone conditions for 1 minute; on a
successful save, it takes half as much damage and has the Prone condition only. A creature
repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the Poisoned and Blinded
conditions on itself on a success.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
