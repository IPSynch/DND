# Euphoric Sorcery

*Magic of Overwhelming Sensation*

Your magic floods the nervous system until thought drowns in feeling. You drive your own
senses to a white-hot peak and ride the high into battle, and you force that same crushing
wave of sensation onto your enemies until their minds simply give out. Pleasure, pain, and
raw overload are all the same current to you—and you are always reaching for more.

## Level 3: Euphoric Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Euphoric Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Euphoric Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Dissonant Whispers*, *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* |
| 5 | *Phantasmal Force*, *Hold Person* |
| 7 | *Confusion*, *Fear* |
| 9 | *Synaptic Static*, *Dominate Person* |

## Level 3: Heightened Senses

Every nerve is alight; you feel a blow coming before it lands. While you aren't wearing
armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 3: Ecstatic Overload

You convert overwhelming sensation into power, building toward release. You gain the
following benefits.

**Psychic Surge.** Whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell that deals damage, you can change
that spell's damage type to Psychic. The damage of your Euphoric Sorcery features increases
as you gain Sorcerer levels: it is **1d8** at level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level
11, and **4d8** at level 15. This amount is your **Euphoria die total**.

**Ecstasy.** You have a pool of stacks called Ecstasy, and you can have a number of Ecstasy
equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 3) at a time. The first time on each of your
turns that you deal Psychic damage to a creature, you gain 1 Ecstasy. You can also gain
Ecstasy through your Indulgences. You lose all Ecstasy when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Heightened Bliss.** While you have at least 1 Ecstasy, you add the number of Ecstasy you
currently have to each Psychic damage roll you make. While you have your maximum Ecstasy,
you also have Advantage on saving throws.

**Climax.** As a Magic action, you can expend all your Ecstasy (you must have at least 1) to
release a crashing wave of sensation in a 30-foot Cone. Each creature in the area makes a
Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, a creature takes Psychic
damage equal to your Euphoria die total plus 1d8 for each Ecstasy you expended and has the
Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, it takes half
as much damage and isn't Incapacitated.

## Level 3: Indulgences

You can flood your senses, or someone else's, on command. As a Bonus Action, you can use one
of the following Indulgences, paying its Sorcery Point cost. (Your Font of Magic feature
turns spell slots into Sorcery Points, so you can fuel these all day instead of casting
spells.)

### Burst (cheap, instantaneous)

- **Overstimulate (1 Sorcery Point).** Choose a creature within 60 feet of you; it makes a
  Wisdom saving throw, taking your Euphoria die total as Psychic damage on a failed save
  (half as much on a success). Whether or not it succeeds, you gain 1 Ecstasy.
- **Lose Yourself (1 Sorcery Point).** You let a wave of overwhelming sensation crash over
  you, blanking the world for an instant. You gain 2 Ecstasy and Temporary Hit Points equal
  to your Sorcerer level.

### Sustained (1 minute, no Concentration)

- **Tide of Sensation (3 Sorcery Points).** A 15-foot Emanation of dizzying sensation
  surrounds you and moves with you; you and creatures you choose are unaffected. Whenever an
  affected creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it
  makes a Wisdom saving throw, taking your Euphoria die total as Psychic damage on a failed
  save (half as much on a success) and being unable to take Reactions until the start of its
  next turn on a failure. The first creature that fails this save on each of your turns
  grants you 1 Ecstasy.
- **Numbing Haze (2 Sorcery Points).** *Sensory overload, as the* Slow *spell.* You flood a
  20-foot-radius area within 60 feet of you with disorienting sensation for the duration.
  Whenever a creature of your choice is in the area, it is subjected to the *Slow* spell
  (Speed halved; −2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws; can't take Reactions; takes
  either one action or one Bonus Action on its turn, not both). A creature shakes off the
  effect at the end of any turn it ends outside the area.

### Overcharged (expensive, devastating)

- **Euphoric Detonation (4 Sorcery Points).** You overload every mind nearby at once. Each
  creature of your choice in a 20-foot-radius Sphere within 120 feet of you makes a Wisdom
  saving throw, taking twice your Euphoria die total as Psychic damage on a failed save (half
  as much on a success) and having the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn on
  a failure. For each creature that fails, you gain 1 Ecstasy (up to your maximum).

## Level 6: Mind-Melting Bliss

Your sensation magic becomes impossible to numb against. You gain the following benefits.

**Mind-Piercing.** Your Psychic damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Psychic
damage as Resistance instead.

**Greater Ecstasy.** Your maximum Ecstasy increases by 2. In addition, whenever you score a
Critical Hit with a Sorcerer spell or reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with Psychic damage,
you gain 1 Ecstasy.

**Overwhelm.** When a creature fails a saving throw against your Climax or against a Sorcerer
spell of yours that deals Psychic damage, it has Disadvantage on the saving throws it makes
until the end of your next turn.

## Level 14: Rapture

Your release becomes a mind-shattering crescendo. You gain the following benefits.

**Mind-Shattering Climax.** When you use your Climax, you can center it on yourself as a
30-foot-radius Sphere instead of a Cone, and a creature that fails its save against your
Climax is Incapacitated for 1 minute instead of until the end of its next turn. An affected
creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a
success.

**Aura of Euphoria.** You emit a 10-foot Emanation of overwhelming sensation. Whenever an
enemy starts its turn in the aura, it makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC,
taking 3d8 Psychic damage on a failed save; the first creature to fail this save on each of
your turns grants you 1 Ecstasy.

## Level 18: Endless Ecstasy

You exist at a permanent, blinding peak of sensation. You gain the following benefits.

**Absolute Overload.** Your Psychic damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Psychic
damage as Resistance instead, and your Climax, Euphoric Detonation, and Sorcerer spells can
impose the Incapacitated or Frightened condition on a creature even if it would normally be
immune, unless that creature is a Construct or an Undead.

**Boundless Ecstasy.** Your maximum Ecstasy increases by 3, you begin each combat with 3
Ecstasy, and once on each of your turns you can use an Indulgence without spending Sorcery
Points. In addition, once per Long Rest you can use your Climax without expending any
Ecstasy, treating it as though you had expended your maximum.
