# Radiant Light Sorcery

*Magic at the Speed of Light*

You don't move toward your enemies—you simply arrive, a streak of blinding gold
already past their guard. You kick like a falling star, fire lances of light that cross
a battlefield in an instant, and leave only afterimages where you stood. Nothing in
the world is faster than you, because nothing in the world is faster than light.

## Level 3: Radiant Light Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Radiant Light Sorcery Spells table,
you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Radiant Light Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Faerie Fire*, *Guiding Bolt* |
| 5 | *Scorching Ray*, *Misty Step* |
| 7 | *Sickening Radiance*, *Dimension Door* |
| 9 | *Wall of Light*, *Far Step* |

## Level 3: Speed of Light

You move and strike with the swiftness of light itself. You gain the following
benefits.

**Lightfoot.** Your Speed increases by 15 feet. While your Innate Sorcery feature is
active, you can also teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see as a
Bonus Action.

**Radiance.** Whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell that deals damage, you can change that
spell's damage type to Radiant.

**Photon Lance.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, you can take a Magic
action to fire a beam of light at a creature within 120 feet of you (ranged spell
attack). On a hit, the target takes Radiant damage equal to
your Light die total and, unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw against
your spell save DC, has the Blinded condition until the start of your next turn. Your
Light die total is **1d8** at level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and
**4d8** at level 15.

## Level 3: Lightstep Defense

You drift just out of reach, quick as a sunbeam. While you aren't wearing armor, your
base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity modifier plus your Charisma modifier.
You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Faster Than Sight

You act before others can react. You gain the following benefits.

**Always First.** You have Advantage on Initiative rolls, you can't be Surprised, and
your teleport from Lightfoot can be used twice on each of your turns.

**Blink Step.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, immediately after you cast
a Sorcerer spell, you can teleport up to 15 feet (no action required).

**Searing Speed.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, your Radiant damage
ignores Resistance, and the first time on each of your turns that you deal Radiant
damage to a creature, it takes extra Radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

## Level 14: Light Barrage

As a Magic action, you unleash a storm of light beams across the battlefield. Choose
up to four creatures you can see within 120 feet of you, or a 30-foot Cone. Make a
ranged spell attack against each chosen creature, or have each creature in the Cone
make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. A creature takes 6d8 Radiant
damage on a hit or failed save (half as much on a successful save), and a creature that
takes this damage has the Blinded condition until the end of your next turn unless it
is immune.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless
you spend 4 Sorcery Points to use it again.

## Level 18: Avatar of Light

You become light made flesh, all but impossible to catch or harm. You gain the
following benefits.

**Luminous Body.** You have Resistance to all damage, and as a Reaction when you take
damage you can teleport up to 60 feet, leaving an afterimage; if you teleport at least
30 feet this way, you take no damage from the triggering effect if it required an
attack roll. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Charisma
modifier per Long Rest.

**Light Speed.** Once on each of your turns, you can take one additional Magic action,
used only to cast a Sorcerer spell of level 5 or lower or to use Photon Lance or Light
Barrage. Your Radiant damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Radiant damage
as Resistance instead.
