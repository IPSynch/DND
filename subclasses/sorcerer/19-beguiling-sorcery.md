# Beguiling Sorcery

*Magic of Irresistible Allure*

You crawl into people's heads and make yourself at home. One look leaves an enemy
slack-jawed and stupid; one whispered word turns them on whoever's standing next to
them; and once you've sunk your hooks in, they can't shake you loose—burn through their
thoughts all you like, they just keep adoring you for it. You don't really beat your
enemies. You make them forget they ever wanted to fight you, right up until it's too
damn late.

## Level 3: Beguiling Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Beguiling Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Beguiling Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Charm Person*, *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* |
| 5 | *Hold Person*, *Suggestion* |
| 7 | *Charm Monster*, *Confusion* |
| 9 | *Dominate Person*, *Synaptic Static* |

## Level 3: Enthralling Presence

Your magic slips past mental defenses and binds the will. You gain the following benefits.

**Psychic Allure.** Whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell that deals damage, you can change
that spell's damage type to Psychic.

**Irresistible.** Your effects that impose the Charmed condition overwhelm defenses against
it. A creature that is immune to the Charmed condition isn't immune to it from your
features; instead, it makes the saving throw with Advantage. A creature that would have
Advantage on a saving throw against being Charmed by you rolls without Advantage, and a
creature that would roll normally has Disadvantage instead.

**Lingering Charm.** Damage dealt by you or your effects to a creature Charmed by you
doesn't end the Charmed condition.

**Mesmerize.** As a Magic action, choose a creature within 60 feet of you that can see or
hear you. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it
takes Psychic damage equal to your Beguiling die total and has the Charmed condition until
the end of its next turn; on a success, it takes half as much damage. Your Beguiling die
total is **1d8** at level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and **4d8** at level
15.

## Level 3: Beguiling Grace

Foes hesitate to strike something so captivating. While you aren't wearing armor, your
base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity modifier plus your Charisma modifier. You
can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Overwhelming Mind

Your psychic power becomes difficult to ward against. You gain the following benefits.

**Mind-Piercing.** Your Psychic damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Psychic
damage as Resistance instead.

**Exploited Adoration.** When you deal damage to a creature Charmed by you, it takes extra
Psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier, and it has Disadvantage on the next saving
throw it makes before the start of your next turn.

**Entrancing Strike.** When a creature fails a saving throw against your Mesmerize or
against a Sorcerer spell of yours that deals Psychic damage, you can cause it to have the
Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn (no more than once per turn).

## Level 14: Captivating Aura

You radiate an aura of overpowering presence in a 15-foot Emanation. Whenever an enemy
starts its turn in the aura, it makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On
a failed save, it takes 3d8 Psychic damage and has the Charmed condition until the start of
its next turn. While a creature is Charmed by this aura, it can't make Opportunity Attacks
and treats your allies as it treats you for the purpose of your other charm effects. A
creature that succeeds on the save is immune to this aura until the start of your next turn.

## Level 18: Total Domination

Your control over mind and will becomes nearly absolute. You gain the following benefits.

**Absolute Allure.** Your Psychic damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Psychic
damage as Resistance instead (if it didn't already), and your effects that impose the
Charmed or Paralyzed conditions affect a creature even if it would normally be immune,
provided it isn't a Construct or Undead.

**Enthrall.** As a Magic action, choose up to three creatures you can see within 60 feet of
you. Each makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, a
creature takes 8d8 Psychic damage and has the Paralyzed condition for 1 minute; on a
success, it takes half as much damage and has the Charmed condition until the end of its
next turn. A Paralyzed creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending
the effect on itself on a success—but while it remains Charmed by any of your effects, it
makes this save with Disadvantage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
