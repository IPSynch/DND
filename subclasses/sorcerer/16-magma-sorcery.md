# Magma Sorcery

*Magic of Absolute Magma*

Your magic is molten rock and merciless heat. You do not warn, and you do not relent;
where you walk, the ground turns to liquid fire, and what you strike does not recover.
Other fire-callers scorch. You bury your enemies in the earth's own burning blood.

## Level 3: Magma Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Magma Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Magma Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Burning Hands*, *Catapult* |
| 5 | *Scorching Ray*, *Flaming Sphere* |
| 7 | *Wall of Fire*, *Fireball* |
| 9 | *Immolation*, *Wall of Stone* |

## Level 3: Molten Body

You are wreathed in liquid stone and searing heat. You gain the following benefits.

**Heat.** You have Resistance to Fire damage, and whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell
that deals damage, you can change that spell's damage type to Fire.

**Searing Resonance.** Once on each of your turns when a creature takes Fire damage
from one of your Sorcerer spells, it takes extra Fire damage equal to your Charisma
modifier and bursts into flame, taking 1d8 Fire damage at the start of its next turn
unless someone uses an action to douse it.

**Magma Fist.** As a Magic action, make a melee spell attack against a creature within
10 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes Fire damage equal to your Magma die total,
and the space it occupies becomes a pool of lava (see Lava Fields). Your Magma die
total is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level 7, **4d8** at level 11, and **5d8** at
level 15.

## Level 3: Molten Hide

A crust of cooling rock sheathes your body. While you aren't wearing armor, your base
Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma modifier. You
can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Eruption

You turn the battlefield itself into a furnace. You gain the following benefits.

**Lava Fields.** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to coat the ground
in a 15-foot-radius area within 60 feet of you with lava for 1 minute (no Concentration
required). The area is Difficult Terrain, and a creature takes 2d8 Fire damage when it
enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there. You are immune to
your own lava, and you ignore Difficult Terrain created by lava or magma.

**Absolute Heat.** Your Fire damage ignores Resistance, and your Searing Resonance burn
increases to 2d8 at the start of the creature's next turn.

## Level 14: Great Eruption

As a Magic action, you call up a column of magma. Choose a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high
Cylinder within 120 feet of yourself. Each creature in that area makes a Dexterity
saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 12d6 Fire damage and being launched 10
feet upward on a failed save (taking falling damage as normal), or half as much damage
on a successful one. The area becomes a Lava Field (as the level 6 feature) for 1 minute.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless
you spend 4 Sorcery Points to use it again.

## Level 18: Magma Incarnate

You become living magma, an unstoppable force of heat. You gain the following benefits.

**Body of Magma.** You have Immunity to Fire damage and Resistance to Bludgeoning,
Piercing, and Slashing damage from nonmagical sources. A creature that touches you or
hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you takes Fire damage equal to your
Charisma modifier.

**Inferno Unbound.** Your Fire damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Fire
damage as Resistance instead, and once on each of your turns you can deal maximum damage
instead of rolling for a Sorcerer spell that deals Fire damage. In addition, whenever a
creature dies while taking Fire damage from your magic, you can have it erupt, creating a
Lava Field centered on it (as the level 6 feature).
