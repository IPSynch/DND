# Chronomantic Sorcery

*Magic That Bends the Flow of Time*

Time is a river, and everyone else is wading. You stand on the bank, deciding when
they take their next step. You act in the gaps between seconds, let an enemy's
blade fall and then quietly un-fall it, and—when you truly cut loose—make the world
hold its breath and wait for you.

> **Multiple Reactions.** Several features of this subclass let you take more than
> one Reaction per round. You can still take only one Reaction per triggering
> event; additional Reactions let you respond to separate triggers in the same
> round.

## Level 3: Chronomantic Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Chronomantic Sorcery Spells table,
you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Chronomantic Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Silvery Barbs*, *Feather Fall* |
| 5 | *Hold Person*, *Blur* |
| 7 | *Slow*, *Counterspell* |
| 9 | *Hold Monster*, *Telekinesis* |

## Level 3: Out of Time

You exist a half-step ahead of everyone else. You gain the following benefits.

**Second Reaction.** You can take two Reactions per round instead of one (no more
than one per triggering event).

**Time Skip.** When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes an attack
roll, an ability check, or a saving throw, you can take a Reaction to alter that
moment. Choose one of the following: impose Disadvantage on the roll before it is
made, or—after seeing the result—force the creature to reroll and use the new roll.

You can use Time Skip a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of
once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can also
use it by spending 1 Sorcery Point.

## Level 6: Accelerated Existence

Your personal clock runs fast, and you can drag others' clocks to a crawl. You gain
the following benefits.

**Quicktime.** Your Speed increases by 10 feet. In addition, once on each of your
turns you can take the Disengage action as part of your movement (no action
required).

**Dilation Field.** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 2 Sorcery Points to create a
20-foot-radius zone of slowed time centered on a point within 120 feet of yourself.
The zone lasts for 1 minute and requires no Concentration. Whenever a creature of
your choice enters the zone for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there,
it is subjected to the *Slow* spell (Speed halved; −2 penalty to AC and Dexterity
saving throws; can't take Reactions; can take either one action or one Bonus
Action on its turn, not both). A creature shakes off the effect at the end of any
turn it ends outside the zone.

## Level 14: Rewind

You can reach back one second and edit what just happened. You gain the following
benefits.

**Third Reaction.** You can take three Reactions per round instead of two (no more
than one per triggering event).

**Rewind.** When you or a creature you can see within 60 feet of you takes damage
or fails a saving throw, you can take a Reaction to rewind that instant. Choose one:
the creature rerolls the failed save and you choose which result to use, or the
damage is undone and the creature regains the Hit Points it just lost.

You can use Rewind once without cost, and you regain that use when you finish a
Long Rest. You can also use it by spending 3 Sorcery Points.

**Snap Cast.** Whenever you take a Reaction, you can cast a Sorcerer cantrip as
part of that Reaction (no action required).

## Level 18: Lord of the Hour

Time is no longer a force you bend but a tool you hold. You gain the following
benefits.

**First Mover.** You can't be Surprised, and at the start of combat you and each
ally within 30 feet of you can choose to act on the highest Initiative count in the
encounter (treat your Initiative as that count if it is higher than your roll).

**Stop.** You can cast *Time Stop* without expending a spell slot. Uniquely for
you, you can take Reactions during the stopped time. Once you cast it with this
feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Temporal Prison.** As a Magic action, choose one creature within 120 feet of
yourself. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or be
frozen in time for 1 minute. While frozen, the creature has the Incapacitated
condition, its Speed is 0, it can't be moved, and it can neither affect nor be
affected by anything outside itself. It repeats the save at the end of each of its
turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Alternatively, you can spend 4
Sorcery Points to instead force each enemy of your choice in a 30-foot-radius
Sphere within 120 feet to succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Stunned
condition until the end of your next turn.
