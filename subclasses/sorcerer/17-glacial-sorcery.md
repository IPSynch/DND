# Glacial Sorcery

*Magic of Endless Ice*

You carry winter in your veins. With a breath you still a river; with a gesture you
raise walls of ice or seal a foe in a tomb of frost. You are patient and absolute, the
quiet cold that outlasts every fire, and the battlefield freezes to your tempo.

## Level 3: Glacial Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Glacial Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Glacial Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |

| 5 | *Hold Person*, *Snilloc's Snowball Swarm* |

## Level 3: Ice Age

Frost answers your will and creeps into everything you touch. You gain the following
benefits.

**Frost.** You have Resistance to Cold damage, you ignore Difficult Terrain made of ice
or snow, and whenever you cast a Sorcerer spell that deals damage, you can change that
spell's damage type to Cold.

**Frostbite.** Once on each of your turns when a creature takes Cold damage from one of
your Sorcerer spells, its Speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of its next turn,
and if its Speed is reduced to 0 this way, it has the Restrained condition until the
start of its next turn as ice locks it in place.

**Glacial Spike.** As a Magic action, choose a point within 90 feet of you; each
creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered there makes a Constitution saving throw
against your spell save DC, taking Cold damage equal to your Frost die total on a failed
save (half as much on a success) and having the Restrained condition until the end of
its next turn on a failure. Your Frost die total is **2d8** at level 3, **3d8** at level
7, **4d8** at level 11, and **5d8** at level 15.

## Level 3: Frostplate

A rime of ice plates your skin. While you aren't wearing armor, your base Armor Class
equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma modifier. You can use a
Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Deep Freeze

The cold you command grows merciless. You gain the following benefits.

**Absolute Cold.** Your Cold damage ignores Resistance.

**Flash Freeze.** When a creature that is Restrained by your features fails a saving
throw against one of your Sorcerer spells, it instead has the Paralyzed condition until
the start of its next turn, frozen solid.

**Frozen Armor.** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to gain Temporary Hit
Points equal to your Frost die total plus your Charisma modifier; while you have these,
any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes Cold damage equal to your Charisma
modifier and has its Speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.

## Level 14: Glacial Epoch

As a Magic action, you plunge a wide area into deep winter. Choose a 30-foot-radius
Sphere within 120 feet of yourself; it becomes a field of ice and freezing wind for 1
minute, requiring no Concentration. The area is Difficult Terrain for your enemies, and
whenever a creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there,
it makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it
takes 6d8 Cold damage and has the Restrained condition until the start of its next turn;
on a successful save, it takes half as much damage. A creature that fails this save while
already Restrained by the field instead has the Paralyzed condition until the start of its
next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you
spend 4 Sorcery Points to use it again.

## Level 18: Eternal Winter

The cold becomes yours to command without limit. You gain the following benefits.

**Body of Ice.** You have Immunity to Cold damage and to the Paralyzed condition, and you
can't be Restrained against your will. You no longer need to breathe.

**Absolute Zero.** Your Cold damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to Cold damage
as Resistance instead, and once on each of your turns you can deal maximum damage instead
of rolling for a Sorcerer spell that deals Cold damage. In addition, a creature that is
reduced to 0 Hit Points while taking Cold damage from your magic is frozen into a solid
statue of ice and dies.
