# Soul Sorcery

*Granting Life to the Lifeless*

You can pull the soul out of a living thing and pour it into an object, waking the
inanimate into loyal, hungry life. A frightened foe surrenders a sliver of its
spirit; a cooking pot, a sword, or a storm cloud rises up to serve you. The braver
the soul you take, the mightier the servant you make.

## Level 3: Soul Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Soul Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Soul Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Unseen Servant*, *Command* |
| 5 | *Enlarge/Reduce*, *Spiritual Weapon* |
| 7 | *Fear*, *Bigby's Hand* |
| 9 | *Animate Objects*, *Dominate Person* |

## Level 3: Soul Snatch

You can frighten a creature into yielding part of its soul. As a Bonus Action, choose
a creature within 30 feet of you that can hear you; it makes a Wisdom saving throw
against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it has the Frightened condition until
the end of its next turn, and you harvest a **Soul Fragment** (you can hold a number
of Soul Fragments equal to your Charisma modifier at a time). A creature that
succeeds is immune to your Soul Snatch for 24 hours.

You also harvest 1 Soul Fragment whenever an enemy you can see within 60 feet of you
is reduced to 0 Hit Points.

You can spend Soul Fragments to animate objects, as described in Animate the
Lifeless.

## Level 3: Animate the Lifeless

As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Soul Fragment or 1 Sorcery Point to pour a soul
into a Tiny, Small, or Medium nonmagical object within 30 feet of you, creating a
**Soul Homunculus** in its space. It is an ally to you and your allies and uses the
statistics below. You can have a number of Soul Homunculi active at a time equal to
your Charisma modifier.

In combat, your Homunculi share your Initiative count but take their turns
immediately after yours. They can move and use their actions on their own, obeying
your mental commands (giving commands never requires an action from you). A Soul
Homunculus lasts for 1 hour, until it drops to 0 Hit Points, or until you dismiss it
(no action required).

> **Soul Homunculus.** Object (its size matches the animated object). **AC** 13. **HP**
> equal to twice your Sorcerer level. **Speed** 30 ft. (it also gains a Fly or Swim
> Speed of 30 ft. if its form suits). It is immune to Poison and Psychic damage and
> the Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion, and Poisoned conditions. **Slam (its action).**
> Melee attack roll using your spell attack modifier, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 plus your
> Charisma modifier Force damage.

## Level 3: Soul-Wrought Resilience

The souls you command knit themselves into a living ward. While you aren't wearing armor,
your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Soul Harvest

Your hold over souls grows, and your servants strike harder. You gain the following
benefits.

**Greater Harvest.** Whenever you harvest one or more Soul Fragments, you regain 1
Sorcery Point (no more than once per turn). Your maximum number of Soul Fragments
increases to twice your Charisma modifier.

**Empowered Servants.** Your Soul Homunculi's Hit Points increase to three times your
Sorcerer level, and their Slam attacks deal an extra 1d8 Force damage. When you
create a Homunculus, you can have it make one attack immediately (no action required
from you).

**Soul Burst.** When a Soul Homunculus drops to 0 Hit Points, it explodes; each
creature within 10 feet of it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save
DC, taking 3d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

## Level 14: Special Homunculi

You can craft singular servants of tremendous power. As a Magic action, you can spend
3 Soul Fragments or 5 Sorcery Points to create one **Special Homunculus** in an
unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. It uses the Soul Homunculus rules, except its
size can be Large or Huge, its AC is 16, its Hit Points equal four times your
Sorcerer level, and it makes two attacks with its action (each dealing 2d8 plus your
Charisma modifier Force damage). Choose one of the following essences when you create
it; you can have only one Special Homunculus at a time.

- **Sun (ember).** Its attacks deal Fire damage, and as a Bonus Action it can spew
  flame in a 15-foot Cone (Dexterity save against your spell save DC; 4d8 Fire damage,
  half on a success).
- **Storm (cloud).** Its attacks deal Lightning damage, it has a Fly Speed of 40 ft.,
  and creatures within 10 feet of it that start their turn there take 1d8 Lightning
  damage.
- **Blade (sword).** Its attacks deal Slashing damage, score a Critical Hit on a roll
  of 19–20, and ignore Resistance to Slashing damage.

## Level 18: Soul Kingdom

You rule a host of awakened things. You gain the following benefits.

**Tireless Court.** Your maximum number of Soul Homunculi increases by 3, and your
Homunculi and Special Homunculus no longer have a time limit—they persist until
destroyed or dismissed.

**Mass Animation.** As a Magic action, you flood the area with souls. Choose up to
six nonmagical objects you can see within 60 feet of you and animate each as a Soul
Homunculus (these don't count against your maximum and last for 1 minute), or empower
your existing servants so that all of them deal maximum damage on their next attack.
Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest,
unless you spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
