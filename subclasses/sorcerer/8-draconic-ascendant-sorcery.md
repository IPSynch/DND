# Draconic Ascendant Sorcery

*Become the Dragon*

The blood of dragons doesn't merely color your magic—it waits to consume you, and
you let it. When you unleash your Innate Sorcery, scales erupt across your skin,
wings tear free, and your shape swells into something vast and terrible. Each time
you ascend, the dragon within you grows mightier, until at last there is little left
of the mortal you once were.

## Level 3: Draconic Ascendant Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Draconic Ascendant Spells table,
you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Draconic Ascendant Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Chromatic Orb*, *Command* |
| 5 | *Dragon's Breath*, *Fly* |
| 7 | *Fear*, *Vitriolic Sphere* |
| 9 | *Summon Draconic Spirit*, *Hold Monster* |

## Level 3: Draconic Form

Choose a kind of dragon from the Draconic Ancestry table in the Sorcerer class
(this determines your damage type, listed in parentheses). You gain Resistance to
that damage type, and you can speak, read, and write Draconic.

When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you can take on a Draconic Form for
the feature's duration. While in this form, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Sorcerer level (refreshed each
  time you activate Innate Sorcery).
- You sprout wings and gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed.
- You grow natural weapons. You can make an unarmed strike as part of the Bonus
  Action used to activate Innate Sorcery, and your unarmed strikes deal 1d10 plus
  your Charisma modifier damage of your chosen type (Slashing for the claws is also
  permitted) and count as magical.
- Once on each of your turns when you deal damage with a Sorcerer spell of your
  chosen damage type, you can deal extra damage of that type equal to your Charisma
  modifier.

## Level 6: Greater Draconic Form

Your ascended form grows larger and more dangerous. While in your Draconic Form, you
gain the following benefits.

**Towering Size.** Your size becomes Large (if it wasn't already), you have Advantage
on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and your reach increases by 5 feet.

**Rending Multiattack.** When you take the Attack action, you can make two unarmed
strikes with your natural weapons.

**Breath Weapon.** As a Magic action, you exhale destructive energy in a 30-foot
Cone or a 60-foot Line that is 5 feet wide (your choice). Each creature in that area
makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 6d8 damage of your
chosen type on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. This damage
increases to 8d8 at Sorcerer level 11 and 10d8 at level 17. You can use this Breath
Weapon once per activation of Innate Sorcery for free, or by spending 2 Sorcery
Points.

## Level 14: Ascendant Wyrm

The dragon all but eclipses the mortal. While in your Draconic Form, you gain the
following benefits.

**Frightful Presence.** When you activate Innate Sorcery, each enemy of your choice
within 60 feet of you makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or has
the Frightened condition for 1 minute (it repeats the save at the end of each of its
turns, ending the effect on itself on a success).

**Tyrant's Scales.** Your Temporary Hit Points from Draconic Form increase to three
times your Sorcerer level, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and
Slashing damage, and your Breath Weapon recharges at the start of each of your turns
(you can use it once per turn without spending Sorcery Points).

**Wyrm's Might.** Your size can become Huge, and your natural weapon strikes deal an
extra 1d10 damage of your chosen type.

## Level 18: Avatar of the Dragon

Your Draconic Form becomes the truest expression of who you are. You gain the
following benefits.

**Eternal Ascension.** You can activate your Draconic Form even when your Innate
Sorcery feature isn't active, sustaining the form for up to 1 hour. Once you do so,
you can't again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Cataclysmic Form.** While in your Draconic Form, your Sorcerer spells and natural
weapons of your chosen damage type ignore Resistance and treat Immunity as
Resistance, and once on each of your turns you can deal maximum damage instead of
rolling for your Breath Weapon or for one Sorcerer spell of your chosen damage type.
