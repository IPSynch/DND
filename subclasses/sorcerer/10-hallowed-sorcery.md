# Hallowed Sorcery

*Surrounded by Vengeful Spirits*

You are never alone. Radiant spirits wheel around you, drawn from some brighter
plane and bound to your will. They shield those you love, savage those you hate, and
spread outward into a storm of wrath that only you can walk through unharmed.

## Level 3: Hallowed Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Hallowed Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Hallowed Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Bless*, *Spirit Shroud* |
| 5 | *Spirit Guardians*, *Beacon of Hope* |
| 7 | *Guardian of Faith*, *Banishment* |
| 9 | *Summon Celestial*, *Circle of Power* |

The above versions of *Spirit Shroud*, *Spirit Guardians*, *Guardian of Faith*,
*Beacon of Hope*, *Summon Celestial*, and *Circle of Power* are added to the Sorcerer
spell list for you.

## Level 3: Wheeling Spirits

Spirits answer your call more readily than anyone's. You gain the following benefits.

**Ready Guardians.** You always have *Spirit Guardians* prepared, and it doesn't
count against the number of spells you can prepare. You can cast it as a Bonus
Action, and when you cast it you choose its damage type from Radiant, Necrotic, Cold,
or Force.

**Sheltering Aura.** Whenever you cast *Spirit Guardians* or a spell that creates an
aura or Emanation, you can choose any number of creatures within it; your spirits
never harm those creatures, and each of them gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your
Charisma modifier at the start of each of its turns spent within the area.

## Level 3: Warding Spirits

Attendant spirits turn aside blows meant for you. While you aren't wearing armor, your
base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma modifier.
You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Empowered Spirits

Your spirits strike harder and hold the line. While you are concentrating on *Spirit
Guardians* (or it is otherwise active on you), you gain the following benefits.

**Greater Guardians.** The radius of your *Spirit Guardians* increases to 20 feet,
its damage increases by 1d8, and a creature that fails its saving throw against it
also can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Vengeful Surge.** Once on each of your turns, when an enemy within your *Spirit
Guardians* takes damage from it, you can deal extra damage to that creature equal to
your Charisma modifier (of the spell's damage type).

**Unbroken Concentration.** You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws to
maintain Concentration on *Spirit Guardians* and other Hallowed Sorcery Spells.

## Level 14: Storm of the Faithful

Your spirits become a roaming tempest. You gain the following benefits.

**Twin Hosts.** You can concentrate on two spells at once, provided at least one of
them is *Spirit Guardians* or another spell from your Hallowed Sorcery Spells.

**Tireless Wheel.** While *Spirit Guardians* is active, you can use a Bonus Action to
move its Emanation up to 30 feet to a new point centered on a location you can see
within range (the spell otherwise normally stays centered on you), and creatures of
your choice that start their turn within it regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma
modifier.

## Level 18: Avatar of Wrath

You become the eye of a holy storm. You gain the following benefits.

**Eternal Guardians.** *Spirit Guardians* cast by you requires no Concentration and
lasts until you end it (no action required) or are Incapacitated. Its radius
increases to 30 feet, and its damage ignores Resistance and treats Immunity to its
damage type as Resistance instead.

**Cataclysm of Spirits.** As a Magic action, your spirits erupt outward. Each enemy
of your choice within 60 feet of you makes a Constitution saving throw against your
spell save DC, taking 12d8 damage of your *Spirit Guardians*' damage type on a failed
save (half as much on a success) and having the Stunned condition until the end of
your next turn on a failure. Each ally of your choice in that area regains 4d8 Hit
Points. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long
Rest, unless you spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
