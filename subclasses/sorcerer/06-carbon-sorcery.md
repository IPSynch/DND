# Carbon Sorcery

*Magic Forged from the Element of Life*

Carbon is the spine of all living things, and it answers you in every shape it
takes. You harden your skin to diamond, ooze adhesive resin from your pores,
exhale soot and ember, and knit torn flesh back together with raw protein. No other
sorcerer is so versatile, so durable, or so hard to put down.

## Level 3: Carbon Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Carbon Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Carbon Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Grease*, *Burning Hands* |
| 5 | *Web*, *Heat Metal* |
| 7 | *Wall of Fire*, *Stoneskin* |
| 9 | *Wall of Stone*, *Wall of Force* |

## Level 3: Diamond Body

Your flesh crystallizes into a living lattice. While you aren't wearing armor, your
base Armor Class equals 13 plus your Charisma modifier, and you can use a Shield and
still gain this benefit. While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, that base Armor
Class instead equals 13 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma modifier.
In addition, whenever you take the Dodge action, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning,
Piercing, and Slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

## Level 3: Allotropes

You can reshape the carbon in your body into many forms. As a Bonus Action, you can
spend Sorcery Points to manifest one of the allotropes below. Each uses your spell
save DC.

The healing and damage of these effects increase as you gain Sorcerer levels: the
amount is **1d8** at level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and **4d8**
at level 15. This amount is referred to below as your **Carbon die total**.

***Diamond (defense).*** As a Bonus Action (1 Sorcery Point), you or a creature you
can see within 30 feet of you gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Carbon die
total plus your Charisma modifier. While the creature has any of these Temporary Hit
Points, it has Resistance to one damage type of your choice chosen when you grant
them.

***Adhesive (control).*** As a Bonus Action (1 Sorcery Point), choose a point within
60 feet of you; each creature in a 15-foot-radius Sphere centered there makes a
Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is stuck fast and has the
Restrained condition for 1 minute. A Restrained creature repeats the save at the end
of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Ember (offense).*** As a Bonus Action (1 Sorcery Point), you exhale soot and fire
in a 30-foot Cone. Each creature in the Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking
your Carbon die total as Fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a
successful one. A creature that fails takes 1d8 Fire damage at the start of its next
turn as embers cling to it.

***Protein (restoration).*** As a Bonus Action (1 Sorcery Point), you or a creature
you can see within 30 feet of you regains Hit Points equal to your Carbon die total
plus your Charisma modifier, and you can end one of the following conditions
affecting it: Blinded, Deafened, or Poisoned.

## Level 6: Allotropic Mastery

Your control over carbon deepens. You gain the following benefits.

**Versatile Surge.** When you manifest an allotrope, you can spend 1 additional
Sorcery Point to manifest a second, different allotrope as part of the same Bonus
Action.

**Hardened Form.** You have Resistance to Fire and Poison damage, and you can't be
moved against your will or knocked Prone unless you choose to allow it.

**Reactive Lattice.** When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to manifest the
**Diamond** allotrope on yourself (you must still pay its cost), gaining its
Temporary Hit Points before the damage is applied.

## Level 14: Indestructible Carbon

The bonds within you become nearly impossible to break. You gain the following
benefits.

**Crystalline Resilience.** Your Carbon die total increases as normal, and whenever
you would drop to 0 Hit Points but not be killed outright, you can instead drop to a
number of Hit Points equal to your Carbon die total (rolled). Once you use this
benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Empowered Allotropes.** The areas of your **Adhesive** and **Ember** allotropes
double in size, and the Temporary Hit Points and healing from your **Diamond** and
**Protein** allotropes increase by an amount equal to your Sorcerer level.

## Level 18: Living Diamond

You become a perfected lattice of carbon, radiant and all but unbreakable. You gain
the following benefits.

**Perfected Lattice.** Your base Armor Class from Diamond Body increases to 15 plus
your Charisma modifier, and you have Resistance to all damage except Force and
Psychic.

**Allotropic Eruption.** As a Magic action, you erupt with every allotrope at once.
Choose any number of creatures you can see within 60 feet of you. For each, you can
apply the full effect of one allotrope of your choice (Diamond, Adhesive, Ember, or
Protein), using your highest Carbon die total. Once you use this feature, you can't
use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you spend 6 Sorcery Points to use
it again.
