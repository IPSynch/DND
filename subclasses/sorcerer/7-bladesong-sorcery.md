# Bladesong Sorcery

*The Blade and the Spell as One*

You have learned to pour your innate magic into steel, weaving spellcraft and
swordplay into a single, lethal dance. Where other sorcerers stand back and hurl
their power, you close the distance, your blade and your cantrips striking as one
unbroken motion.

## Level 3: Bladesong Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Bladesong Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Bladesong Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Shield*, *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* |
| 5 | *Shadow Blade*, *Misty Step* |
| 7 | *Haste*, *Greater Invisibility* |
| 9 | *Steel Wind Strike*, *Far Step* |

## Level 3: Sorcerous Blade

Your body and your magic are tuned for battle. You gain the following benefits.

**Martial Training.** You gain proficiency with Light and Martial weapons that have
the Finesse or Light property, and you gain proficiency in Constitution saving
throws.

**Arcane Edge.** You can use your Charisma modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity
for the attack and damage rolls of weapons you're proficient with. When you take the
Attack action on your turn, you can replace one attack with casting a Sorcerer
cantrip that has a casting time of an action.

## Level 3: Bladesong

You can invoke an ancient elven magic called the Bladesong, a tradition you have
made your own. As a Bonus Action, you can start the Bladesong, which lasts for 1
minute. It ends early if you have the Incapacitated condition, if you don Medium or
Heavy armor or a Shield, or if you use two hands to make a weapon attack. You can
also end it (no action required).

While your Bladesong is active, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain a bonus to your Armor Class equal to your Charisma modifier.
- Your Speed increases by 10 feet.
- You have Advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- You gain a bonus to any Constitution saving throw you make to maintain
  Concentration; the bonus equals your Charisma modifier.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you
regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can also activate it by
spending 2 Sorcery Points.

## Level 6: Extra Attack and War Magic

Your strikes and spells reinforce each other. You gain the following benefits.

**Extra Attack.** You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the
Attack action on your turn.

**Sorcerous Strike.** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a
weapon attack, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to deal an extra 2d8 Force damage. This
extra damage increases to 3d8 at Sorcerer level 11 and 4d8 at level 17.

**Quickened Blade.** While your Bladesong is active, when you cast a Sorcerer cantrip
that has a casting time of an action, you can make one weapon attack as a Bonus
Action.

## Level 14: Song of Defense

The magic of your Bladesong can be drawn upon to absorb harm. While your Bladesong
is active, when you take damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce that damage by
twice the number of Sorcery Points you spend (to a maximum of 10 points spent). In
addition, while your Bladesong is active you have Resistance to Force damage and
Advantage on saving throws against spells.

## Level 18: Blade Ascendant

Your union of steel and sorcery reaches its peak. You gain the following benefits.

**Third Attack.** You can attack three times, instead of twice, whenever you take
the Attack action on your turn.

**Perfected Song.** While your Bladesong is active, your weapon attacks score a
Critical Hit on a roll of 18–20, and the first time each turn you reduce a creature
to 0 Hit Points or hit it with both a weapon attack and a spell, you regain 2
Sorcery Points (up to your maximum). Your Bladesong no longer ends as a result of
using two hands to make a weapon attack.
