# Demonic Soul Sorcery

*Take Over the Power of Demons*

The souls of demons answer your call, and you wear them like a second skin. When you
loose your innate magic, horns and wings and claws erupt from your body, and you take
on a form sculpted to your need—an armored juggernaut, a storm of ranged ruin, or a
balanced predator. Each transformation cuts you a little closer to the demon you are
becoming.

## Level 3: Demonic Soul Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Demonic Soul Sorcery Spells table,
you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Demonic Soul Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Hellish Rebuke*, *Command* |
| 5 | *Darkness*, *Misty Step* |
| 7 | *Fire Shield*, *Fear* |
| 9 | *Dominate Person*, *Wall of Fire* |

## Level 3: Soul Takeover

When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you take over the soul of a demon,
transforming for the feature's duration. Choose one of the Demon Souls below; you can
choose a different one each time you transform. While transformed, you gain Temporary
Hit Points equal to twice your Sorcerer level (refreshed each time you transform),
grow demonic wings that give you a Fly Speed equal to your Speed, and gain the form's
benefits.

The damage of these forms increases as you gain Sorcerer levels: it is **1d8** at
level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and **4d8** at level 15. This
amount is your **Demon die total**.

- **Juggernaut Soul (melee tank).** Your size can become Large, you gain a +2 bonus to
  AC, and you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. You gain
  a claw attack: as a Magic action you make two melee spell attacks (reach 10 ft.),
  each dealing 1d10 plus your Charisma modifier Slashing or Necrotic damage.
- **Tempest Soul (ranged blaster).** When you deal damage with a Sorcerer spell that
  targets only one creature, you can have it instead affect each creature of your
  choice in a 15-foot-radius Sphere centered on the original target. Once on each of
  your turns, as a Bonus Action, you can hurl a demonic bolt at a creature within 120
  feet (ranged spell attack dealing your Demon die total as Fire or Necrotic damage).
- **Predator Soul (versatile).** Your Speed increases by 10 feet, you have Advantage
  on attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its Hit Points, and once
  on each of your turns when you deal damage with a Sorcerer spell or a claw attack,
  you deal extra Necrotic damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

## Level 3: Demonic Hide

Demonic flesh hardens against harm even in your mortal shape. While you aren't wearing
armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 6: Greater Takeover

Your command over demon souls deepens. You gain the following benefits.

**Swift Shift.** While transformed, you can change to a different Demon Soul as a
Bonus Action.

**Hardened Soul.** You have Resistance to Fire and Necrotic damage and immunity to
the Frightened condition. While transformed, the Temporary Hit Points from Soul
Takeover increase to three times your Sorcerer level.

**Form Mastery.** Each form grows stronger: the Juggernaut's claws gain a +your-Demon
die-total bonus to damage once per turn; the Tempest's demonic bolt can be loosed
twice on your turn; and the Predator's extra damage applies to every hit, not just
once per turn.

## Level 14: Demon Sovereign

Your transformations approach their final, monstrous shape. While transformed, you
gain the following benefits.

**Sovereign Form.** Your size can become Huge, your Fly Speed doubles, and you have
Resistance to all damage except Radiant.

**Overwhelming Presence.** When you transform, each enemy of your choice within 60
feet of you makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 6d8 Psychic
damage and gaining the Frightened condition for 1 minute on a failure (it repeats the
save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success), or
half as much damage on a success.

## Level 18: Satan Soul

You take over the soul of an archdemon, becoming a force of pure dread. When you
activate Innate Sorcery, your transformation lasts for 1 minute, requires no
Concentration, and grants the benefits of all three Demon Souls at once, plus the
following:

**Archdemon's Might.** Your Sorcerer spells, claws, and demonic bolts ignore
Resistance and treat Immunity to their damage types as Resistance instead, and once
on each of your turns you can deal maximum damage instead of rolling for one Sorcerer
spell or attack.

Once you transform this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest,
unless you spend 6 Sorcery Points.
