# Humoral Sorcery

*Sorcerous Origin — Offense / Utility*

> The ancient physicians taught that the body is a vessel of four humors — blood,
> phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile — and that health is their balance. You
> learned the *other* lesson: that every fluid the body makes is a reagent, and
> that a sorcerer who has mastered their own secretions need never go unarmed,
> unlubricated, or unaccompanied by a truly memorable stench.

A gleefully gross but mechanically serious origin. It maps each crude bodily
fluid the request asked for onto a real damage type or condition — acid, poison,
grease (Prone), nausea (Poisoned), a buff/heal essence, and even a spawned ally —
so the table can lean into the comedy without breaking the math. Played straight,
it's a controller/debuffer that drowns the battlefield in **Acid and Poison** and
slick, reeking hazards.

> **A note on tone.** The flavor below is deliberately tasteful-disgusting: the
> fluids are named, the effects are described, and nothing is explicit. Reskin
> any name your table dislikes — the mechanics don't care what you call the goo.

## Humoral Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Grease*, *Ray of Sickness*, *Tasha's Caustic Brew* |
| 5 | *Melf's Acid Arrow*, *Stinking Cloud* |
| 7 | *Evard's Black Tentacles* (bile tendrils), *Vitriolic Sphere* |
| 9 | *Cloudkill*, *Contagion* |

## The Four Humors (Level 3)

You can consciously weaponize your secretions. As a **Bonus Action**, you can
spend **1 Sorcery Point** to vent a **Humor** of your choice. Each uses your spell
save DC. The dice scale: **2d6 at level 3, 3d6 at 7, 4d6 at 11, 5d6 at 15.**

- **Sanguine (blood).** Your veins run open without harm. Until the end of your
  turn, whenever you deal damage with a Sorcerer spell, you **regain Hit Points
  equal to your Charisma modifier** (once per spell).
- **Phlegm (mucus / sweat / slick).** You exude a slippery film. A 15-foot-radius
  area around you becomes **Difficult Terrain** that you're immune to, and each
  enemy in it makes a **Dexterity saving throw** or falls **Prone**. Until your
  next turn you also have **Advantage on checks and saves to escape a grapple or
  the Restrained/Grappled condition** — you simply slither free.
- **Yellow Bile (acid / caustic).** You spit a corrosive jet in a 15-foot **Cone**.
  **Dexterity saving throw**: the scaling damage as **Acid** on a failure (half on
  a success), and on a failure the target's **AC is reduced by 1** until the end
  of its next turn as its armor and hide corrode.
- **Black Bile (toxic gas / filth).** A reeking miasma billows from you in a
  10-foot-radius **Sphere** centered on you or a point within 30 feet. Each
  creature there makes a **Constitution saving throw** or is **Poisoned** for 1
  minute (repeat save at end of turn) and **can't take Reactions** while in the
  cloud. The cloud lingers until the start of your next turn.

## Glandular Surge (Level 6)

Your body becomes a far more productive — and far more resilient — chemical plant.

- **Hardened Constitution.** You gain **Resistance to Acid and Poison damage**,
  you're **immune to the Poisoned condition**, and you can't be nauseated or
  sickened by nonmagical means.
- **Empowered Secretions.** When you vent a Humor, you can spend **1 additional
  Sorcery Point** to either **double the area** of the effect *or* **reroll any
  1s and 2s** on its damage dice.
- **Quickening Ichor (new Humor — buff / spawn).** A potent, generative essence.
  - *Anoint:* As a **Bonus Action**, spend 1 SP to anoint yourself or a willing
    creature within 5 feet. They gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your
    sorcerer level** and **Advantage on their next attack roll or spell attack.**
  - *Spawn:* As a **Magic action**, spend **3 Sorcery Points** to birth a
    **Humoral Spawnling** — a Small ooze ally with HP equal to your sorcerer
    level, AC 12, and a +your-spell-attack reach attack dealing 2d6 Acid. It acts
    on your turn (you command it as a Bonus Action) and, when it drops to 0 HP or
    you dismiss it, **bursts** for 3d6 Acid in a 10-foot radius (Dex save halves).
    You can have one Spawnling at a time.

## Pandemic Body (Level 14)

You are no longer a person who *makes* toxins — you *are* one.

- **Plague Aura.** You radiate a 10-foot **Aura of filth**. Each enemy that starts
  its turn in it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or takes **2d6 damage**
  (Acid or Poison, your choice) and is **Poisoned** until the start of its next
  turn.
- **Infectious Failure.** When a creature fails a saving throw against one of your
  Humors or Sorcerer spells, you can **curse it with a disease** (as the *Contagion*
  spell, no concentration) **once per Long Rest** per creature.
- **Free Vent.** The **first Humor you vent on each of your turns costs 0 Sorcery
  Points.**

---

## Font of Filth (Level 18)

Your mastery over the body's fluids becomes total and grotesquely sublime.

- **Reflexive Secretion.** You can vent any **Humor as part of casting a Sorcerer
  spell**, requiring no action and no Bonus Action.
- **Perfected Body.** You're **immune to Acid and Poison damage**, you no longer
  need to eat, drink, or breathe (you endlessly recycle your own humors), and you
  gain **Blindsight out to 30 feet** via scent and vibration.
- **The Great Purge (capstone).** As a **Magic action**, you erupt with every
  humor at once in a **30-foot-radius Sphere** centered on you. Each creature
  there makes a **Constitution saving throw**.
  - **On a failure:** 10d6 Acid plus 10d6 Poison damage, and the creature is
    knocked **Prone** (slick), **Poisoned** and **Blinded** for 1 minute (corrosive
    filth in the eyes), and **Frightened** until the end of its next turn (the
    sheer stench). It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending
    Poisoned, Blinded, and Frightened on a success.
  - **On a success:** half damage and Prone only.

  You can use The Great Purge once per **Long Rest** for free, or by spending
  **6 Sorcery Points**.

### Designer notes

- The crude prompt is honored without making the sheet unusable: **urine/bile →
  Yellow Bile (acid)**, **gas/feces → Black Bile (poison cloud)**, **sweat/mucus →
  Phlegm (grease/escape)**, **blood → Sanguine (leech heal)**, and the generative
  fluid is recast as **Quickening Ichor**, a buff that can even *spawn* an ally —
  the tasteful read on "creating life from your own secretions."
- It front-loads area denial and debuff but stays a real blaster thanks to the
  scaling Humor dice plus *Vitriolic Sphere* and *Cloudkill* on the always-prepared
  list.
- The Acid/Poison immunity at 18 plus the standing **Plague Aura** make this origin
  excellent at simply *standing inside its own hazards* while everyone else suffers.
