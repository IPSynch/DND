# Humoral Sorcery

*Sorcerous Origin — Offense / Utility*

> The old physicians swore the body held four humors — blood, phlegm, yellow
> bile, and black bile — and that health was their balance. You learned the
> practical lesson buried under the theory: every fluid your body makes is a
> weapon. You bleed to heal yourself; you flood the ground with sweat, mucus,
> and urine until your enemies can't keep their feet; you spit stomach bile that
> eats armor down to rust; and you choke a battlefield in the gas and filth of
> your own gut. None of it disgusts you anymore. Your foes won't get the time to
> be disgusted either.

A gross-out origin played mechanically straight. Each crude fluid maps onto a
real damage type or condition — **blood → self-healing**, **mucus/sweat/urine →
slick Bludgeoning hazards and knockdowns**, **stomach bile → Acid that corrodes
armor**, **gut gas/feces → a Poison fog**. Played straight it's a
controller/blaster that drowns the field in **Acid and Poison** and slick,
reeking terrain.

> **Tone.** This is written plainly, no euphemisms — urine, bile, gut gas, and
> filth are named for what they are. Rename anything your table dislikes; the
> mechanics don't care what you call the fluid.

**How the Humors now work.** Each Humor has two ways to vent it:

- A **cheap, short-lived burst** (1 Sorcery Point, a single immediate effect that
  fades by the start of your next turn — the same snappy tempo as before), and
- A **costly, sustained version** (3 Sorcery Points, lasts **1 minute and
  requires no Concentration**) for when you want to plant a hazard and leave it
  running.

## Visceral Defense (Level 3)

Your body is your armor — thick-skinned, fluid-slick, and hard to truly hurt.
While you aren't wearing armor, your **Armor Class equals 10 + your Constitution
modifier + your Charisma modifier.** You can use a Shield and still gain this
benefit.

> **Why Constitution and Charisma?** Constitution is the humoral stat — this whole
> origin runs on the resilience and output of your own body — and you already
> pump Charisma to cast. Stacking the two is deliberately strong. For a more
> conventional spread, use **Dexterity + Charisma** instead (see Designer notes).

## The Four Humors (Level 3)

You can consciously weaponize your secretions. As a **Bonus Action**, you vent a
**Humor** of your choice, paying its cost. Each effect uses your **Sorcerer spell
save DC**. The damage dice scale: **2d6 at level 3, 3d6 at 7, 4d6 at 11, 5d6 at
15.**

### Sanguine — blood

Your veins open without harming you, and the spilled blood feeds you back.

- **Burst (1 SP).** Until the end of your turn, whenever you deal damage with a
  Sorcerer spell you **regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier** (once
  per spell).
- **Sustained (3 SP).** For **1 minute** (no Concentration), that leech persists:
  whenever you deal damage with a Sorcerer spell you regain Hit Points equal to
  your Charisma modifier (once per spell), **and** at the start of each of your
  turns you regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier.

### Phlegm — mucus, sweat, and urine

You drench the ground in slick fluid. Enemies lose their footing; you slip free
of anything that grabs you.

- **Burst (1 SP).** A 15-foot **Emanation** of slick fluid floods out from you
  until the start of your next turn; you're unaffected and the area is **Difficult
  Terrain** for everyone else. Each enemy in it makes a **Dexterity saving
  throw**, taking the scaling damage as **Bludgeoning** and falling **Prone** on a
  failure (half damage, no Prone, on a success). Until the start of your next turn
  you also have **Advantage on checks and saving throws to escape or avoid the
  Grappled and Restrained conditions** — you simply slither loose.
- **Sustained (3 SP) — Floodground.** This is a secretion-fueled *Tremor*. For
  **1 minute** (no Concentration), a 15-foot **Emanation** of churning fluid
  surrounds you and moves with you; you are unaffected, and the area is **Difficult
  Terrain**. Whenever the Emanation enters a creature's space, a creature enters
  the Emanation, or a creature ends its turn there, that creature makes a
  **Dexterity saving throw**. On a failure it takes the scaling damage as
  **Bludgeoning** and falls **Prone**. A creature makes this save only **once per
  turn**.

### Yellow Bile — stomach bile and acrid urine

You spit a jet of caustic stomach bile, sharpened with concentrated urine. It
burns flesh and rots armor off the body.

- **Burst (1 SP).** A 15-foot **Cone**. Each creature makes a **Dexterity saving
  throw**, taking the scaling damage as **Acid** (half on a success). On a
  failure, the target's armor or hide corrodes and its **AC is reduced by 1** (see
  **Corrosion**, below).
- **Sustained (3 SP) — Caustic Bath.** Choose one creature you can see within 30
  feet; it is coated in clinging bile for **1 minute** (no Concentration). At the
  start of each of its turns it makes a **Constitution saving throw**, taking half
  the scaling damage as **Acid** (half again on a success), and each turn it
  remains coated its **AC is reduced by a further 1** (cumulative, to a maximum
  penalty of −5). When the effect ends, the accumulated AC loss resolves under
  **Corrosion**.

> **Corrosion.** When a creature's AC drops from your Yellow Bile:
> - If the AC comes from **mundane, nonmagical worn armor** (leather, chainmail,
>   plate, and the like), the reduction is **permanent** until the armor is
>   repaired — the *Mending* cantrip restores 1 point of lost AC, and a proficient
>   smith working over a Long Rest restores all of it. Dead metal and hide don't
>   heal.
> - If the AC comes from **magic armor**, **natural armor**, or an **Unarmored
>   Defense–style feature**, the reduction lasts only until the creature finishes a
>   **Short or Long Rest** — living tissue knits and enchantment reasserts itself
>   over time.
> - Repeated failures **stack**, to a maximum total penalty of **−5**.

### Black Bile — gut gas and filth

You loose the gas and filth of your own gut as a reeking, clinging fog.

- **Burst (1 SP).** A 10-foot-radius **Sphere** of choking stench billows from you
  or a point within 30 feet until the start of your next turn. Each creature in it
  makes a **Constitution saving throw** or is **Poisoned** until the start of your
  next turn and **can't take Reactions** while inside the cloud.
- **Sustained (3 SP) — Miasma Guardians.** A 15-foot **Emanation** of toxic gas
  and flung filth surrounds you for **1 minute** (no Concentration), moving with
  you; you and any creatures you choose when you vent it are unaffected, and enemy
  **Speed is halved** inside it. When an enemy enters the Emanation for the first
  time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a **Constitution saving
  throw**, taking the scaling damage as **Poison** (half on a success) and, on a
  failure, also being **Poisoned** until the start of its next turn. (This is a
  pocket *Spirit Guardians* that also nauseates — and it needs no Concentration.)

## Glandular Surge (Level 6)

Your body becomes a far more productive — and far more resilient — chemical plant.

- **Hardened Constitution.** You gain **Resistance to Acid and Poison damage**,
  you're **immune to the Poisoned condition**, and you can't be nauseated or
  sickened by nonmagical means.
- **Empowered Secretions.** When you vent a Humor (burst or sustained), you can
  spend **1 additional Sorcery Point** to either **double the area** of the effect
  *or* **reroll any 1s and 2s** on its damage dice. In addition, your **sustained**
  Humors now cost **2 Sorcery Points** instead of 3.
- **Quickening Ichor (new Humor — buff / spawn).** A potent, generative essence.
  - *Anoint:* As a **Bonus Action**, spend 1 SP to anoint yourself or a willing
    creature within 5 feet. They gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your
    sorcerer level** and **Advantage on their next attack roll or spell attack.**
  - *Spawn:* As a **Magic action**, spend **3 Sorcery Points** to birth a
    **Humoral Spawnling** — a Small ooze ally with HP equal to your sorcerer
    level, AC 12, and a +your-spell-attack reach attack dealing 2d6 Acid. It acts
    on your turn (you command it as a Bonus Action) and, when it drops to 0 HP or
    you dismiss it, **bursts** for 3d6 Acid in a 10-foot radius (Dex save halves).
    You can have one Spawnling at a time.

## Pandemic Body (Level 14)

You are no longer a person who *makes* toxins — you *are* one.

- **Plague Aura.** You radiate a 10-foot **Aura of filth**. Each enemy that starts
  its turn in it makes a **Constitution saving throw** or takes **2d6 damage**
  (Acid or Poison, your choice) and is **Poisoned** until the start of its next
  turn.
- **Infectious Failure.** When a creature fails a saving throw against one of your
  Humors or Sorcerer spells, you can **curse it with a disease** (as the *Contagion*
  spell, no concentration) **once per Long Rest** per creature.
- **Free Vent.** The **first Humor you vent on each of your turns costs 0 Sorcery
  Points** — whether you vent its burst or its sustained form.

---

## Font of Filth (Level 18)

Your mastery over the body's fluids becomes total and grotesquely sublime.

- **Reflexive Secretion.** You can vent any **Humor (burst form) as part of casting
  a Sorcerer spell**, requiring no action and no Bonus Action.
- **Perfected Body.** You're **immune to Acid and Poison damage**, you no longer
  need to eat, drink, or breathe (you endlessly recycle your own humors), and you
  gain **Blindsight out to 30 feet** via scent and vibration.
- **The Great Purge (capstone).** As a **Magic action**, you erupt with every
  humor at once in a **30-foot-radius Sphere** centered on you. Each creature
  there makes a **Constitution saving throw**.
  - **On a failure:** 10d6 Acid plus 10d6 Poison damage, and the creature is
    knocked **Prone** (slick), **Poisoned** and **Blinded** for 1 minute (corrosive
    filth in the eyes), and **Frightened** until the end of its next turn (the
    sheer stench). It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending
    Poisoned, Blinded, and Frightened on a success.
  - **On a success:** half damage and Prone only.

  You can use The Great Purge once per **Long Rest** for free, or by spending
  **6 Sorcery Points**.

### Designer notes

- **Burst vs. sustained.** Every Humor keeps its cheap, end-of-turn burst for
  tempo, and gains a 3 SP (2 SP after level 6), 1-minute, **non-Concentration**
  sustained form for board control. Because the sustained Humors don't use
  Concentration, you can run a Floodground *and* concentrate on a real spell at the
  same time — that's the engine of this rework, and it's deliberately strong. A DM
  who wants it leaner can halve the dice on the sustained forms or restore
  Concentration to them.
- **Phlegm is a *Tremor* engine.** The Floodground form is built on the *Tremor*
  spell's once-per-turn, enter/start-of-turn save structure, swapped onto a moving
  Emanation that knocks foes Prone in slick fluid.
- **Yellow Bile eats armor for real.** Mundane armor that corrodes **stays**
  corroded until somebody repairs it; living hide, natural armor, and magic armor
  recover after a rest. Against a heavily-armored mundane foe, a sustained Caustic
  Bath can permanently strip up to −5 AC.
- **Black Bile is a poison *Spirit Guardians*.** Half speed in the cloud, a
  recurring CON-save for Poison damage and the Poisoned condition, no
  Concentration required.
- **Visceral Defense uses CON + CHA** — thematic (Constitution is the humoral
  stat) and powerful (you already raise both). Swap to **DEX + CHA** for a more
  balanced, conventional caster AC if your table prefers.
- The origin front-loads area denial and debuff but stays a real blaster thanks to
  the scaling Humor dice plus *Vitriolic Sphere* and *Cloudkill* on the
  always-prepared list, and the Acid/Poison immunity at 18 plus the standing
  **Plague Aura** let it simply stand inside its own hazards while everyone else
  suffers.
