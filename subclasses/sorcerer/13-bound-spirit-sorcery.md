# Bound Spirit Sorcery

*Shadowed by a Vengeful Spirit*

Something walks at your shoulder—a powerful spirit bound to you by love, by tragedy,
or by a curse you may not fully understand. It guards you without being asked, lashes
out at those who threaten you, and grows more terrible whenever your own power surges.
You are never truly alone, and your enemies are never truly safe.

## Level 3: Bound Spirit Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Bound Spirit Sorcery Spells table,
you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Bound Spirit Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Bane*, *Shield* |
| 5 | *Spiritual Weapon*, *Misty Step* |
| 7 | *Spirit Shroud*, *Fear* |
| 9 | *Bigby's Hand*, *Telekinesis* |

## Level 3: The Bound Spirit

A spirit is bound to you and manifests at your side. It uses the statistics below, is
an ally to you and your allies, and occupies a space adjacent to you when it appears.
It manifests when you finish a Long Rest (no action required) and reappears in your
space if dismissed or reduced to 0 Hit Points (you can return it in this way once per
Short or Long Rest).

In combat, the spirit shares your Initiative count. On your turn, you can command it
to move and take one action, using either an action or a Bonus Action to do so,
interchangeably. It can't act on its own without your command.

> **Bound Spirit.** Medium (or smaller) spirit. **AC** equal to 13 plus your Charisma
> modifier. **HP** equal to five times your Sorcerer level. **Speed** 40 ft., fly 40
> ft. (hover). It is immune to the Poisoned and Exhaustion conditions and to
> Necrotic and Poison damage. **Spectral Strike.** Melee attack roll using your spell
> attack modifier, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d6 plus your Charisma modifier Force damage.

**Guardian.** When you take damage while the spirit is within 10 feet of you, you can
take a Reaction to have the spirit take that damage instead.

## Level 6: Surging Bond

Your spirit answers your power. You gain the following benefits.

**Manifest Limb.** Once on each of your turns when you cast a Sorcerer spell, the
spirit can make one Spectral Strike against a creature within its reach (no command
required).

**Empowered Spirit.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, the spirit's
Spectral Strike deals an extra 1d6 Force damage and it can make two attacks when it
takes the Attack action.

## Level 14: Full Manifestation

The spirit's true might begins to show. You gain the following benefits.

**Towering Spirit.** The spirit's size can be Large, its reach increases to 15 feet,
and its Hit Points increase to eight times your Sorcerer level.

**Cursed Surge.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, the spirit grows
immense and overwhelming: it makes three attacks when it takes the Attack action, and
once during this time, as part of its action, it can unleash a burst of cursed energy
in a 30-foot Cone (each creature makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell
save DC, taking 8d6 Force damage on a failure or half as much on a success).

## Level 18: Two as One

You and your spirit reach perfect accord. You gain the following benefits.

**Reverse Curse.** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 3 Sorcery Points to have the
spirit pour healing energy into a creature within its reach, restoring Hit Points
equal to 6d8 plus your Sorcerer level and ending one condition of your choice
affecting it from the following list: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or
Stunned.

**Unleashed Spirit.** While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, the spirit acts on
its own turn immediately after yours with its own action, Bonus Action, and movement
(without requiring a command from you), in addition to any actions you command on
your turn. Its attacks ignore Resistance to Force damage, and the spirit's size can
become Huge.
