# Ejaculation Sorcery

*Magic of the Seed — the Sorcery That Serves*

Other sorcerers hoard their power and spend it on themselves. You learned the
opposite art: every drop you spill makes someone *else* stronger. You lather your
seed along a blade and it bites like a god's own sword. You pour your essence into an
ally — or pull theirs into you — and their spells land truer and hit harder. And when
a friend has burned through their rage, their ki, their last spell slot, you top them
back up with a well-timed release. You are the filthiest, most generous battery a
party ever fought beside. You don't win the fight. You make *everyone else* win it.

> **A note on tone.** This is a deliberately crude, deliberately *supportive* origin.
> The flavor is bawdy but never explicit; the mechanics are what matter. Reskin any
> name your table dislikes — call it "anointing oil," "blessed unguent," or "essence"
> and the numbers don't change.

## Level 3: Ejaculation Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the table, you thereafter always have the
listed spells prepared.

**Ejaculation Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Bless*, *Heroism* |
| 5 | *Aid*, *Enhance Ability* |
| 7 | *Haste*, *Aura of Vitality* |
| 9 | *Holy Weapon*, *Greater Restoration* |

## Level 3: Slick Vitality

Your body runs hot, primed, and slippery-tough. While you aren't wearing armor, your
base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution modifier plus your Charisma
modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

## Level 3: Anointing

You channel your magic into your secretions and lavish them on your allies, no
spellcasting required. The potency of your anointings is your **Unction die total**:
it is **1d8** at level 3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and **4d8** at
level 15. (Your Font of Magic feature turns spell slots into Sorcery Points, so you
can fuel these all day.) Each Anointing is used as a **Bonus Action** and lasts 1
minute with **no Concentration** unless noted.

### Lather (Anoint a Weapon) — 2 Sorcery Points

You smear glistening essence along a weapon or up to 20 pieces of ammunition within 5
feet of you. For the duration, the wielder gains a **+1 bonus to attack rolls** with
it and each hit deals extra damage equal to your **Unction die total** (your choice of
the weapon's damage type or Force), and the weapon's damage **ignores Resistance**.
While your **Innate Sorcery** is active, this bonus to attack rolls is **+2** and the
weapon scores a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.

### Communion (Anoint a Spellcaster) — 3 Sorcery Points

You pour your essence into a willing creature, or draw theirs into yourself — either
way the channel runs both directions and crackles with power. For the duration, the
creature gains a **+1 bonus to its spell save DC and spell attack rolls** and has
**Advantage on saving throws to maintain Concentration**. (This bonus becomes **+2**
at level 6; see Prolific Glands.)

### Replenishing Release (Refuel) — variable

As a Bonus Action, you touch a creature and flood it with restorative essence,
returning a single expended resource. The Sorcery Point cost depends on the resource
— martial wells run cheap; the deep arcane ones cost dearly:

| Resource restored | Cost |
|---|---|
| One Rage, one Superiority Die, one Bardic Inspiration die, one use of Channel Divinity or Wild Shape, one use of Second Wind, **2 Focus/Ki Points**, or **5 points of Lay on Hands** | **2 SP** |
| One **Pact Magic** slot | **3 SP** |
| One **leveled spell slot** (its level can be no higher than half your Sorcerer level, rounded down, max 5th) | **3 SP × the slot's level** |

A creature that regains a **Pact Magic slot or a leveled spell slot** this way can't do
so again until it finishes a **Short or Long Rest**. Martial wells in the top row have
no such limit — they're capped only by your Sorcery Points.

## Level 6: Prolific Glands

Your output becomes copious, potent, and self-renewing.

**Potent Seed.** Your **Communion** bonus increases to **+2**, and the cost of every
Anointing is reduced by **1 Sorcery Point** (minimum 1).

**Refractory Surge.** Whenever a creature affected by your **Lather** scores a critical
hit, or a creature you anointed since the start of your last turn reduces an enemy to 0
Hit Points, you regain **2 Sorcery Points**. This can happen only once per turn.

**Twin Anointing.** When you use an Anointing, you can spend **2 additional Sorcery
Points** to apply it to a second weapon or creature within range at the same time.

## Level 14: Aura of Potency

You radiate a heady, empowering musk that lifts everyone fighting beside you. You emit
a 15-foot Emanation. While a creature of your choice is in the aura, it gains a **+1
bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws** and has **Advantage on saving
throws to maintain Concentration**.

In addition:

**Reach of the Seed.** Your Anointings now have a range of **30 feet** and no longer
require you to touch the target.

**Free Release.** The first Anointing you use on each of your turns costs **no Sorcery
Points**, whatever it is.

## Level 18: Font of Ecstasy

Your generosity becomes godlike. You gain the following benefits.

**Reflexive Anointing.** You can use one Anointing as part of casting a Sorcerer spell,
requiring no action, once on each of your turns.

**Overflowing Vitality.** Whenever you use **Replenishing Release**, the creature also
gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Sorcerer level and **Advantage on its next
attack roll, ability check, or saving throw**.

**The Grand Climax.** As a Magic action, you release everything at once. Choose any
number of allies within 30 feet of you. For 1 minute (no Concentration), each chosen
ally is simultaneously affected by **Lather** (on a weapon it wields) **and**
**Communion**, gains **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Sorcerer level**, and
immediately regains **one expended resource of its choice** from the Replenishing
Release table (you pay no cost for these refuels, and the slot-level cap still applies
to spell slots). Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a
**Long Rest**, unless you spend **6 Sorcery Points** to use it again.
