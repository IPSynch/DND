# Humoral Sorcery

*Magic Drawn from the Body's Own Fluids*

Four humors, the old quacks said—blood, phlegm, yellow bile, black bile—and health
was keeping them balanced. You learned the part they were too squeamish to say out
loud: every disgusting thing your body squeezes out is a weapon. Your blood feeds
you. Your piss and sweat flood the floor until nobody can stand. Your shit hardens
into bombs and blades. Your farts choke, blind, and jet you off your feet. And the
whole time you reek so good your friends crowd in close while your enemies' heads
spin. It's revolting. It also works.

## Level 3: Humoral Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Humoral Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Humoral Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Grease*, *Ray of Sickness*, *Tasha's Caustic Brew* |
| 5 | *Melf's Acid Arrow*, *Stinking Cloud* |
| 7 | *Evard's Black Tentacles*, *Vitriolic Sphere* |
| 9 | *Cloudkill*, *Contagion* |

## Level 3: Visceral Defense

Your body is your armor—thick-skinned, fluid-slick, and hard to truly hurt. While
you aren't wearing armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Constitution
modifier plus your Charisma modifier. You can use a Shield and still gain this
benefit.

## Level 3: The Humors

You weaponize every fluid your body makes, no spellcasting required. Your damaging
Humoral effects deal either Acid or Bludgeoning damage (your choice each time you use
them), and the amount increases as you gain Sorcerer levels: it is **1d8** at level
3, **2d8** at level 7, **3d8** at level 11, and **4d8** at level 15. This amount is
your **Humoral die total**, and all effects below use your spell save DC.

As a Bonus Action, you can vent one of the Humors below, paying its Sorcery Point
cost. (Your Font of Magic feature turns spell slots into Sorcery Points, so you can
fuel these all day instead of casting spells.) Each Humor offers a **Burst** (cheap,
instantaneous), a **Sustained** option (1 minute, no Concentration), and an
**Overcharged** option (expensive, devastating).

### Sanguine (Blood)

You open your own veins without flinching, and every drop you spill comes straight
back—into you, or into those you fight beside.

- **Burst (1 Sorcery Point).** Until the end of your turn, whenever you deal damage
  with a Sorcerer spell, you regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier (once
  per spell).
- **Sustained (3 Sorcery Points).** For 1 minute, whenever you deal damage with a
  Sorcerer spell you regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier (once per
  spell), and you regain Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of
  each of your turns.
- **Overcharged — Transfusion (4 Sorcery Points).** Choose up to three creatures
  within 30 feet of you. Each regains Hit Points equal to twice your Humoral die
  total and gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Sorcerer level, which last for 1
  hour.

### Urine (Mucus, Sweat, and Piss)

You sweat, drool, and piss out a slick, acrid flood that yanks the ground out from
under anyone in it.

- **Burst (1 Sorcery Point).** A 15-foot Emanation of slick fluid spreads from you
  until the start of your next turn; it is Difficult Terrain for creatures of your
  choice. Each creature of your choice in the area makes a Dexterity saving throw,
  taking your Humoral die total as Bludgeoning damage and gaining the Prone condition
  on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Until the start of
  your next turn, you have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks to escape or
  avoid the Grappled and Restrained conditions.
- **Sustained — Floodground (3 Sorcery Points).** A 15-foot Emanation of churning
  fluid surrounds you and moves with you; you are unaffected, and the area is
  Difficult Terrain for creatures of your choice. Whenever the Emanation enters a
  creature's space, a creature enters the Emanation, or a creature ends its turn
  there, that creature makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking your Humoral die total
  as Bludgeoning damage and gaining the Prone condition on a failed save. A creature
  makes this save only once per turn.
- **Overcharged — Acid Deluge (4 Sorcery Points).** You hose a 30-foot Cone (or
  60-foot Line that is 5 feet wide) with a pressurized stream of acrid piss. Each
  creature in the area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking three times your Humoral
  die total as Acid damage on a failed save (half as much on a success). A creature
  that fails also has its AC reduced by 2 (see **Corrosion** below).

### Feces (Shit)

You pack and harden your own filth into ammunition—bombs, spikes, and brutal melee
weapons.

- **Burst — Feces Bomb (2 Sorcery Points).** Hurl a packed ball of filth at a point
  within 120 feet of you; each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere there makes a
  Dexterity saving throw, taking twice your Humoral die total on a failed save (half
  as much on a success). The blast leaves the area coated and Difficult Terrain until
  the end of your next turn.
- **Sustained — Dung Caltrops (2 Sorcery Points).** You scatter a 20-foot-radius area
  on the ground within 60 feet of you with hardened, jagged filth for the duration.
  The area is Difficult Terrain, and when a creature enters it for the first time on a
  turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking your
  Humoral die total as Bludgeoning damage on a failure.
- **Overcharged — Feculent Armament (4 Sorcery Points).** You force out a mass of
  shit—moldable but iron-hard—and, as part of this Bonus Action, mold it in your hand
  into a weapon of your choice. The weapon lasts until you finish a Long Rest and has
  the Reach property; choose Acid or Bludgeoning when you make it. You attack with it
  as a Bonus Action; the attack is a spell attack that uses your spell attack modifier
  and benefits from your Innate Sorcery feature's Advantage on attack rolls as though
  it were a Sorcerer spell. On a hit, it deals your Humoral die total plus your
  Charisma modifier damage of its chosen type, and once on each of your turns when you
  hit, you can reduce the target's AC by 1 (see **Corrosion**). Another creature can
  wield it, attacking with it as a Bonus Action (a spell attack with a bonus equal to
  its proficiency bonus plus its Charisma modifier) for 2d8 plus its Charisma modifier
  damage of the weapon's type. You can have only one Feculent Armament at a time, and
  you can dismiss it (no action required).

> **Corrosion.** When a creature's AC is reduced by your Humoral Sorcery, the
> reduction's duration depends on the source of that AC. If it comes from nonmagical
> armor the creature is wearing, the reduction is permanent until the armor is repaired
> (the *Mending* cantrip restores 1 point; a proficient smith working over a Long Rest
> restores all of it). If it comes from magic armor, natural armor, or an Unarmored
> Defense–style feature, the reduction lasts until the creature finishes a Short or
> Long Rest. Repeated reductions stack, to a maximum total penalty of −5.

### Flatus (Fart)

You loose the gas of your gut as a weapon—a choking cloud, a swarm of reeking
spirits, a sphere of black smog, or a blast strong enough to flatten a crowd.

- **Burst (1 Sorcery Point).** A cloud of foul gas erupts in a 15-foot Cone. Each
  creature in it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking your Humoral die total on a
  failed save (half as much on a success) and being unable to take Reactions until the
  start of its next turn on a failure.
- **Sustained — Reeking Guardians (3 Sorcery Points).** A 15-foot Emanation of
  swirling stench surrounds you and moves with you; you and creatures you choose are
  unaffected, and enemy Speed is halved in it. When an enemy enters the area for the
  first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw,
  taking your Humoral die total on a failed save (half as much on a success) and
  gaining the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn on a failure. (This is
  a stinking *Spirit Guardians* that needs no Concentration.)
- **Sustained — Black Shroud (3 Sorcery Points).** You belch out a 15-foot-radius
  Sphere of pitch-black smog centered on a point within 60 feet of you. The area is
  Heavily Obscured for the duration, but you and any creatures you choose can see
  through it normally. The Sphere's Heavy Obscurement spreads around corners, and a
  light source of 2nd level or lower can't illuminate it.
- **Overcharged — Flatal Breath (4 Sorcery Points).** You unleash a torrent of gas in
  a 60-foot Line that is 5 feet wide, or a 30-foot Cone. Each creature in the area
  makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking three times your Humoral die total on a failed
  save (half as much on a success) and gaining the Blinded condition until the end of
  its next turn on a failure.

## Level 6: Glandular Surge

Your body becomes a more productive and more resilient chemical engine. You gain the
following benefits.

**Hardened Constitution.** You have Resistance to Acid and Poison damage, you are
immune to the Poisoned condition, and you can't be nauseated or sickened by
nonmagical means.

**Corrosive Potency.** The damage of your Humors ignores Resistance and treats
Immunity to its damage type as Resistance instead.

**Empowered Secretions.** When you vent a Humor, you can spend 1 additional Sorcery
Point to either double the size of the effect's area or reroll any 1s and 2s on its
damage dice (using the new rolls). In addition, the cost to vent a Humor's Sustained
option is reduced by 1 Sorcery Point.

**Flatulent Flight.** When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you gain a Fly
Speed equal to your Speed for the feature's duration, propelled by jets of gas.
Whenever a creature of your choice starts its turn within 5 feet of you, or you fly
within 5 feet of a creature of your choice during your movement, that creature makes a
Constitution saving throw, taking 1d8 Acid or Bludgeoning damage on a failed save.

**Quickening Ichor.** You gain a generative essence with two uses.

- **Humoral Inspiration.** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to anoint
  yourself or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature gains a
  Humoral Inspiration, a d20 that functions exactly like Heroic Inspiration: the
  creature can expend it to reroll any d20 it just rolled and use either result. A
  creature can hold only one at a time, and it is lost if unused after 1 hour.
- **Humoral Spawnling.** As a Magic action, you can spend 3 Sorcery Points to create a
  Humoral Spawnling—a Medium ooze—in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. It is
  an ally to you and your allies and uses the statistics below. In combat, the
  Spawnling shares your Initiative count but takes its own turn, immediately after
  yours, with its own action, Bonus Action, and movement. On your turn, you can also
  command it to move up to its Speed (no action required). It obeys your mental
  commands, and commanding it never requires an action from you. The Spawnling lasts
  for 1 hour, until it drops to 0 Hit Points, or until you dismiss it (no action
  required), and you can have only one at a time.

  > **Humoral Spawnling.** Medium Ooze. **AC** 13. **HP** equal to three times your
  > Sorcerer level. **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft. It has Resistance to Bludgeoning,
  > Piercing, and Slashing damage and is immune to Acid and Poison damage. **Caustic
  > Lash (its action).** The Spawnling makes two attacks, each using your spell attack
  > modifier, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 1d10 plus your Charisma modifier Acid damage.
  > **Burst (when it drops to 0 HP or is dismissed).** Each creature in a
  > 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your
  > spell save DC, taking 4d8 Acid damage on a failed save or half as much on a
  > successful one.

## Level 14: Sublime Body

You are a living wellspring of fluids, wreathed in a scent that intoxicates the worthy
and overwhelms the rest. You gain the following benefits.

**Intoxicating Aura.** You emit an aura of heady, perfumed scent in a 10-foot
Emanation. Whenever a creature of your choice enters the aura for the first time on a
turn or starts its turn there, it gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Sorcerer
level (you can grant this to a given creature only once per turn). Whenever an enemy
starts its turn in the aura, it makes a Constitution saving throw; on a failed save,
it takes 2d8 Psychic damage—its head swimming with your overpowering scent—and has the
Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.

**Infectious Failure.** When a creature fails a saving throw against one of your Humors
or Sorcerer spells, you can curse it with the disease created by the *Contagion* spell
(no Concentration required). Once you curse a particular creature this way, you can't
do so again to that creature until you finish a Long Rest—unless you spend 3 Sorcery
Points, which lets you curse a creature you've already cursed since your last Long Rest.

**Free Vent.** The first Humor you vent on each of your turns costs no Sorcery Points,
whatever option you choose.

## Level 18: Font of Filth

Your mastery over the body's fluids becomes total. You gain the following benefits.

**Reflexive Secretion.** You can vent a Humor's Burst option as part of casting a
Sorcerer spell, requiring no action.

**Perfected Body.** You are immune to Acid and Poison damage, you no longer need to
eat, drink, or breathe, and you have Blindsight with a range of 30 feet.

**The Great Purge.** As a Magic action, you erupt with every humor at once in a
30-foot-radius Sphere centered on yourself. Each creature in the area makes a
Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10d8 Acid damage plus
10d8 Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition; it also has the Poisoned and Blinded
conditions for 1 minute and the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.
On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and has the Prone condition only.

A creature affected by the Poisoned, Blinded, or Frightened conditions from this
feature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. On a success, it ends only
one of those conditions on itself (its choice).

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless
you spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
