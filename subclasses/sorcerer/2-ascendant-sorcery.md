# Ascendant Sorcery

*Sorcerous Origin — Offense*

> Every sorcerer carries a spark. Yours is a bonfire barely held in a lantern.
> When you let **Innate Sorcery** off its leash, the air warps, your eyes burn,
> and the laws that bind lesser casters bend out of your way. Each year you live,
> the leash grows longer — until at last there is no leash at all.

This origin is built around one idea: take the level 1 **Innate Sorcery**
feature and make it the centerpiece of your entire career. Every subclass tier
makes the active state stronger, longer, and easier to keep running.

> **Reminder — base Innate Sorcery (level 1):** Bonus Action to activate for
> 1 minute; +1 to your spell save DC and Advantage on attack rolls for your
> Sorcerer spells; 2 uses per Long Rest.

---

## Ascendant Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Tasha's Hideous Laughter*, *Magnify Gravity* (or *Chaos Bolt*) |
| 5 | *Scorching Ray*, *Mirror Image* |
| 7 | *Fireball*, *Counterspell* |
| 9 | *Synaptic Static*, *Far Step* |

---

## Kindled Power (Level 3)

Your spark runs hotter and recovers faster.

- **Third Surge.** You now have **three** uses of Innate Sorcery per Long Rest
  (instead of two).
- **Instant Ignition.** When you roll Initiative, you can activate Innate Sorcery
  **without using an action** and without expending a use of it.
- **Overflow (while Innate Sorcery is active):**
  - You gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your sorcerer level** the
    instant you activate it (refreshed each activation).
  - Your **Sorcerer cantrips deal an extra 1d8 damage** of the cantrip's type.

---

## Overflowing Aura (Level 6)

Your active state spills outward as a visible nimbus of raw magic.

While your **Innate Sorcery** is active:

- The bonus to your **spell save DC increases to +2**, and you gain a **+1 bonus
  to spell attack rolls** *in addition to* the feature's Advantage.
- **Self-feeding engine.** The first time on each of your turns that you cast a
  Sorcerer spell of level 1 or higher, you **regain 1 Sorcery Point** (you can't
  exceed your maximum). The fire feeds itself.
- Enemies that start their turn within 10 feet of you have **Disadvantage on
  saving throws against the next Sorcerer spell you cast against them this turn.**

---

## Sorcerous Ascension (Level 14)

The boundary between you and your own magic thins.

While your **Innate Sorcery** is active:

- **Free Metamagic.** Once on each of your turns, you can apply **one Metamagic
  option to a spell without spending Sorcery Points.** (This stacks with Arcane
  Apotheosis at level 20, letting you apply two free Metamagic options per turn.)
- **Unbreakable.** Your Innate Sorcery can't be ended early by any effect you
  didn't choose, and you have **Advantage on Constitution saving throws to
  maintain Concentration.**

You also gain a **fourth** use of Innate Sorcery per Long Rest, and you can
activate it even with no uses remaining by spending **3 Sorcery Points** when you
take the Bonus Action.

---

## Avatar of Innate Magic (Level 18)

You stop being a person who *uses* magic and become a vessel that magic pours
*through*.

- **Boundless Spark.** You regain **all** expended uses of Innate Sorcery when
  you finish a **Short or Long Rest**, and the spend-Sorcery-Points option from
  level 14 now costs only **2 Sorcery Points.**
- **Avatar State (while Innate Sorcery is active):**
  - Your bonus to **spell save DC becomes +3** (and the +1 spell attack bonus
    from level 6 still applies).
  - Your Sorcerer spells **ignore Resistance** to their damage types.
  - Once on each of your turns, you can **deal maximum damage** instead of
    rolling for a Sorcerer **cantrip, or a 1st- or 2nd-level spell.**

Because your Innate Sorcery is now nearly always available, every other feature
in this origin is effectively *on* for the entire adventuring day.

---

### Designer notes

- This subclass turns Innate Sorcery from "a nice opener you forget about" into a
  resource you want to keep lit at all times. The **self-feeding Sorcery Point**
  return at level 6 is the keystone that makes the +DC aura sustainable.
- **Sorcerous Ascension**'s free Metamagic comes online at 14, four levels before
  the base class's level-20 version, and then *stacks* with it — a deliberate
  "this is your whole identity" reward.
- Power-tuning lever: if "maximize a 2nd-level spell every turn" is too much for
  your table, restrict the level 18 maximize to cantrips and 1st-level spells.
