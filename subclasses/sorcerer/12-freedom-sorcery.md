# Freedom Sorcery

*The Magic of Boundless Freedom*

Your body answers to no rule but your own joy. It stretches, springs, and bounces
where others break; chains slide off you, lightning passes through you, and the
harder the world pushes, the higher you bounce back. When your power crests, even
the laws of the world seem to play along, as if reality itself wanted to have fun
with you.

## Level 3: Freedom Sorcery Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Freedom Sorcery Spells table, you
thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

**Freedom Sorcery Spells**

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 3 | *Longstrider*, *Thunderwave* |
| 5 | *Enlarge/Reduce*, *Misty Step* |
| 7 | *Freedom of Movement*, *Stoneskin* |
| 9 | *Far Step*, *Telekinesis* |

## Level 3: Rubber Body

Your flesh becomes resilient, elastic, and impossible to pin down. You gain the
following benefits.

**Elastic.** You have Resistance to Bludgeoning damage and Immunity to Lightning
damage. You have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks to escape or avoid
the Grappled and Restrained conditions, and your reach with unarmed strikes
increases by 10 feet.

**Stretch Strike.** Your unarmed strikes deal 1d10 plus your Charisma modifier
Bludgeoning damage and count as magical. On each of your turns, you can make one
unarmed strike as a Bonus Action.

## Level 3: Gears

When you activate your Innate Sorcery feature, you can shift into one of the
following Gears for the feature's duration. You can change your Gear when you
reactivate Innate Sorcery.

- **Gear Second (Speed).** Your Speed doubles, you have Advantage on Dexterity
  saving throws, and once on each of your turns you can take one extra action that
  is used only to make unarmed strikes or to cast a Sorcerer cantrip.
- **Gear Third (Power).** Your size becomes Large, your reach increases by an
  additional 10 feet, and your unarmed strikes deal an extra 2d8 Bludgeoning damage
  and can target creatures or objects in a 10-foot Cube on a hit (each makes a
  Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC for half).

## Level 6: Limber Power

Your body bends the rules of battle. While your Innate Sorcery feature is active, you
gain the following benefits.

**Bounce Back.** When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce that damage
by 1d12 plus your Sorcerer level; if you reduce the damage to 0, you can fling the
attacker 10 feet away if it is Large or smaller.

**Gear Boost.** Your Gear Second extra action can now also be used to cast a Sorcerer
spell of level 1 or 2, and your Gear Third strikes deal an extra 3d8 (up from 2d8).

## Level 14: Gear Fourth

When you activate Innate Sorcery, you can instead enter Gear Fourth for the
feature's duration. While in Gear Fourth, you gain the following benefits.

- You have Resistance to all damage except Psychic.
- You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed and can't be knocked Prone.
- As a Magic action, you launch a barrage of compressed strikes: each creature of
  your choice in a 60-foot Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell
  save DC, taking 8d8 Bludgeoning or Force damage (your choice) on a failed save or
  half as much on a successful one.

## Level 18: Gear Fifth — Liberation

You reach the peak of your freedom, where the world itself becomes your plaything.
When you activate Innate Sorcery, you can instead enter Gear Fifth. It lasts for 1
minute and requires no Concentration, and while it lasts you gain the following
benefits.

**Anything Goes.** You have Immunity to Bludgeoning damage and Resistance to all
other damage, you have a Fly Speed equal to twice your Speed, and your Sorcerer
spells and unarmed strikes ignore Resistance and treat Immunity as Resistance.

**Cartoon Logic.** Once on each of your turns, you can deal maximum damage instead of
rolling for a Sorcerer spell or unarmed strike. In addition, as a Bonus Action you
can warp the environment around a point within 120 feet: choose a 20-foot Cube to
become bouncy, stretched terrain. It is Difficult Terrain for your enemies, and a
creature of your choice that starts its turn there is launched up to 20 feet in a
direction you choose and has the Prone condition unless it succeeds on a Strength
saving throw against your spell save DC.

Once you use Gear Fifth, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless
you spend 6 Sorcery Points to use it again.
