# Warrior of the Diamond Body

*Monastic Order — Tank / Defender*

> Pain taught you the lesson early, and you took it further than anyone intended:
> if a body can be hardened, why stop at calluses? You learned to clench your skin
> into stone, then stone into something harder — to fold your flesh along invisible
> facets until a blade meets you and *shatters*. In your hardened form you gleam
> like a struck gem, you weigh like a mountain, and the strongest blow in the room
> rings off you and leaves you grinning. You are the wall your friends stand behind,
> and the last thing a charging enemy expected to find unmovable.

A near-unkillable defender who turns their body to living **diamond**. You boast
the highest survivability in the order — soaring AC, damage Resistance, and tools to
glue every threat onto yourself — while still hitting like a falling boulder.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. Your **Diamond Body** is a stance you can turn on and off; many features key
> off it.

---

## Crystalline Skin (Level 3)

*Harden, and the world's edges find nothing to bite.*

- **Diamond Body.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to harden for
  **1 minute** (no Concentration). While hardened: you gain a **+2 bonus to AC**, you
  have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing** damage, and your Unarmed
  Strikes deal an extra **MA die** (your fists are now mineral). It ends early at no
  cost.
- **Facet Guard (passive).** When you are hit by a melee attack while hardened, the
  attacker takes **MA-die** damage as their weapon rings off your faceted skin.

---

## Immovable Object (Level 6)

*A mountain does not flinch, and it does not let you past.*

- **Unmovable (passive).** While hardened, you have **Advantage on saves and checks
  against being moved, knocked Prone, Grappled, or Restrained**, and you can't be
  pushed or pulled unless you allow it.
- **Bulwark.** While hardened, creatures within **5 feet** of you treat the area
  around you as **Difficult Terrain** to leave, and when a creature you can see within
  5 feet makes an attack against someone other than you, you can use your **Reaction**
  to force that attack to **target you instead** (the blow rings off your diamond).
- **Diamond Knuckle.** While hardened, your Unarmed Strikes ignore **Resistance** to
  their damage type and treat a target's **AC bonus from cover as 0**.

---

## Brilliant Adamant (Level 11)

*Beyond stone, beyond steel — the one substance that cuts everything and is cut by
nothing.*

- **Adamant Form (passive).** While hardened, your **Resistance extends to all
  damage except Psychic and Force**, and any **critical hit against you becomes a
  normal hit** (your facets refuse the lucky angle).
- **Refraction.** While hardened, when you succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw**
  against an effect that deals damage, you take **none** instead of half; on a fail,
  you take half. Light, fire, lightning — all of it scatters across your facets.
- **Pillar of the Party (passive).** Your unarmored **AC increases by +1** even while
  not hardened, and your maximum Hit Points increase by an amount equal to **twice
  your monk level**.

---

## Living Diamond (Level 17)

*The transformation is no longer something you do. It is something you are.*

- **Perfected Lattice (passive).** While hardened, you have **Resistance to all
  damage** (including Psychic and Force), you are **immune to being Stunned,
  Paralyzed, or Petrified**, and your AC bonus from Diamond Body increases to **+3**.
- **Earthshatter Fist.** As part of the Attack action, spend **3 Focus Points** to
  drive a diamond fist into the ground or a foe: one creature you hit, plus each
  creature in a **15-foot-radius** area, takes **6 MA dice** of Bludgeoning damage and
  is knocked **Prone** (a successful **Constitution save** halves the damage and
  prevents Prone). The terrain in the area is shattered into **Difficult Terrain**.
- **Unbreakable (passive).** While hardened, the **first time** each round you would
  drop to 0 Hit Points, you instead drop to **1 Hit Point**. Nothing in this room is
  hard enough to break you.

---

### Designer notes

- This subclass is built to be the party's **anchor**: the AC, the all-damage
  Resistance, the crit-cancel, and the Reaction taunt let it soak punishment that
  would delete other Monks, while **Facet Guard** and **Earthshatter Fist** keep its
  damage relevant so it isn't a pure speed bump.
- The defensive numbers are deliberately stacked (Resistance + high AC + crit
  immunity + drop-to-1). If your table finds it *unkillable* rather than merely very
  tanky, the cleanest dial is to make **Diamond Body** cost **1 Focus Point per round**
  to sustain instead of running a full minute on one point.
- **Bulwark**'s redirect-the-attack Reaction is the heart of the defender fantasy —
  protect it, and let the player feel like a literal wall.
