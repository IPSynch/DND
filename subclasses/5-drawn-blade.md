# Warrior of the Drawn Blade

*Monastic Order — Bladed Striker*

> Other monks made peace with the empty hand. You made peace with steel. To you the
> sword is not a weapon held *apart* from the body but an extension of it — three of
> them, if your discipline runs deep enough, one in each hand and one clenched in
> your teeth. You move through a fight the way water finds the low ground: every cut
> a single unbroken motion, every guard already a counter, every breath a step
> closer to the one stroke that cleaves whatever stands in front of you, be it flesh,
> steel, or stone.

A bladed Monk for the warrior who never set down the sword. Your swords *are* your
Martial Arts weapons, you can wield more of them than anyone should, and your
discipline turns a drawn blade into a force that cuts through steel and the world
itself.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. This subclass treats **Shortswords, Scimitars, Longswords, and other
> Slashing martial melee weapons** as **Monk weapons** for you (see below).

---

## Bladed Discipline (Level 3)

*The sword and the open hand were never two different arts.*

- **Swordsman's Forms.** You gain proficiency with all **martial weapons that deal
  Slashing damage**, and they count as **Monk weapons** for you regardless of their
  properties (so you can use Dexterity, Martial Arts, Flurry of Blows, and your MA
  die with them). You **ignore the Two-Handed and Heavy properties** of such weapons.
- **Three-Sword Style.** You can wield a **second weapon in your off hand and a third
  in your teeth (or with your tail, prehensile grip, etc.)**. When you take the
  Attack action, you can make one **extra weapon attack** as a Bonus Action without
  the usual two-weapon-fighting penalty, and you add your ability modifier to its
  damage.
- **Iron-Cutter (passive).** Your weapon attacks ignore **Resistance to Slashing**
  and can damage objects and barriers as though you wielded a siege weapon.

---

## Flowing Steel (Level 6)

*A guard is only a counter you haven't thrown yet.*

- **Tempest Kick / Air Slash.** When you take the Attack action, one of your weapon
  attacks can become a **ranged slash**, striking a target within **30 feet** or
  every creature in a **15-foot Line** (roll the attack once against each). The wind
  itself carries your edge.
- **Parry (Reaction).** When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can
  use your **Reaction** and spend **1 Focus Point** to add your **MA die + Wisdom
  modifier** to your AC against that attack, potentially turning the hit into a miss.
  If the attack misses because of this, you may immediately make **one weapon attack**
  against the attacker.
- **Asura Pressure (passive).** While you wield two or more weapons, creatures have
  **Disadvantage on saving throws against your Stunning Strike**, and you can make
  **Stunning Strike** with weapon attacks.

---

## Three Thousand Worlds (Level 11)

*Three blades, one will — and for an instant the foe is facing a crowd of you.*

- **Master Swordsman (passive).** Your weapon attacks score a **critical hit on a
  roll of 19–20**, and your **Three-Sword Style** Bonus Action now makes **two** extra
  weapon attacks.
- **Onigiri.** As part of the Attack action, spend **2 Focus Points** to unleash a
  whirling three-blade cut: each creature in a **15-foot Cone** or a **30-foot Line**
  takes **3 MA dice + your Wisdom modifier** of Slashing damage (one roll for all),
  or **half** on a successful **Dexterity save**, and is knocked **Prone** on a fail.
- **Unbreakable Stance (passive).** When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not
  killed outright, you can spend **1 Focus Point** to **drop to 1 Hit Point instead**
  (once per Short or Long Rest). "Nothing happened."

---

## King of Hell / Asura (Level 17)

*Nine blades. Three faces. Six arms wreathed in killing intent. For a few breaths,
the swordsman becomes the demon the legends warned of.*

As a **Bonus Action**, spend **4 Focus Points** to enter the **Asura** stance for
**1 minute** (no Concentration). While it lasts:

- You manifest **phantom arms and blades**: when you take the Attack action, you make
  **three additional weapon attacks**, and your reach with all weapon attacks
  increases by **10 feet**.
- Your weapon attacks deal an extra **2 MA dice** of Force damage and **critically hit
  on a roll of 18–20**.
- Once on each of your turns you can spend your action on a single annihilating
  stroke — **King of Hell** — striking one creature and everything in a **20-foot-wide,
  60-foot Line** behind it for **10d12 Slashing damage**, or **half** on a successful
  **Constitution save**. Walls, gates, and lesser obstacles in the Line are simply
  **cut in two**.

While in this stance you have **Resistance to all damage** and cannot be **Frightened**.

---

### Designer notes

- The point of this subclass is to make a **weapon-using Monk feel fully native** to
  the 2024 chassis rather than bolted on. Folding Slashing martials into Monk weapons
  and stripping Two-Handed/Heavy lets a greatsword-style blade run on Dexterity and
  the MA die without breaking the bounded-accuracy math.
- **Three-Sword Style** scaling (1 → 2 → 3 → 4+ attacks) is the engine; it stacks with
  Flurry of Blows for genuinely absurd action-economy turns at high levels, which is
  the intended power ceiling.
- Tuning lever: **King of Hell**'s "cut any wall in two" is mostly narrative, but if
  it trivializes your set-pieces, restrict it to non-magical barriers.
