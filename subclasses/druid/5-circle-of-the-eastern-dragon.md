# Circle of the Eastern Dragon

*Druid Circle — Aerial controller / blaster*

> In the old stories the dragon is not a hoard-sitting lizard but a river given a
> spine, a storm with whiskers, a thing that *rides* the wind and *is* the
> weather. That is the shape coiled inside you. When you let it out you do not
> sprout from the earth — you pour off it, serpentine and endless, and the sky
> becomes a road only you may walk.

Inspired by the dragons of Eastern myth — Shenlong, Kaido, the river-kings.
This Circle turns Wild Shape into a long, sky-swimming **Eastern Dragon**:
a flying serpent that controls the battlefield from above and exhales
devastating breath.

---

## Circle Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Druid Level | Spells |
|-------------|--------|
| 3 | *Fog Cloud*, *Thunderwave* |
| 5 | *Gust of Wind*, *Dragon's Breath* |
| 7 | *Fly*, *Sleet Storm* |
| 9 | *Cloudkill*, *Control Winds* |

## Draconic Blood & Dragon Shape (Level 3)

> The river runs in your veins, and the first time you breathe out, you understand
> what you are.

When you choose this Circle, pick a breath element — **Cold, Fire, or Lightning** —
used by all your draconic features.

- **Breath Weapon (at will).** As a **Magic action** you exhale a **15-foot Cone**
  of your element. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking
  **2d8** damage of that type on a failure (half on a success). The die increases
  with your druid level: **3d8** at 7th, **4d8** at 13th, **5d8** at 17th. There is
  no limit on how often you can use it.
- **Stormrider.** You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by wind, weather, or water,
  and you have **Resistance to your chosen element.**
- **Dragon Shape.** You gain a new use for Wild Shape: rather than a beast, you
  become an **Eastern Dragon** for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert), using
  your own statistics with these changes — your size becomes **Large** and
  serpentine (you occupy a 15-foot line of space), you gain a **Fly Speed equal to
  your Speed (hover)** and a **Swim Speed** equal to your Speed, **temporary Hit
  Points equal to your druid level**, and natural attacks dealing **1d10 + Wisdom**
  (Slashing or your element, 10-foot reach). You retain spellcasting and can still
  use your Breath Weapon while transformed. While in Dragon Shape and not wearing
  armor, your Armor Class equals **14 + your Wisdom modifier** (thick dragon
  scales); you can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

## Sky Serpent (Level 6)

> You've grown into the shape. The wind no longer carries you — it *obeys* you.

Your Dragon Shape comes into its full power:

- **Coil & Claw (Extra Attack).** While in Dragon Shape, when you take the Attack
  action you make **two attacks**, and each time you hit a Large or smaller creature
  you can move it 10 feet or knock it Prone.
- **Breath on the Wing.** While in Dragon Shape your **Breath Weapon** becomes a
  **30-foot Cone** or **60-foot Line** (your choice each use) **and you can use it
  as a Bonus Action**, so you can breathe and still act.
- **Greater Wings.** Your Dragon Shape's Fly Speed becomes **twice your Speed**
  (hover), and the temporary Hit Points it grants increase to **twice your druid
  level.**

## Wind King (Level 10)

> The sky is your kingdom and the weather attends you.

- **On the Wind.** While in Dragon Shape your Fly Speed increases by a further **20
  feet**, you gain **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws**, and opportunity attacks
  against you have **Disadvantage** (you flow out of reach).
- **Tempest Coil (at will).** As a **Bonus Action**, you whip the air into a
  spiraling gale in a **20-foot-radius** area within 60 feet. Each enemy there makes
  a **Strength saving throw**, taking **2d8** of your element and being pushed 15
  feet on a failure; the area becomes lightly obscured Difficult Terrain for flying
  creatures until the start of your next turn.
- **Pressure Breath.** Once per turn, when a creature fails the save against your
  Breath Weapon, it is also knocked **Prone** and can't take Reactions until the
  start of its next turn.

---

## Heavenly Dragon Ascendant (Level 14)

> You are no longer a dragon that flies through storms. You are the storm, and it
> has a name, and the name is yours.

- **Apex Wyrm.** In Dragon Shape your size can become **Huge**, your temporary Hit
  Points on transforming increase to **three times your druid level**, and you
  become **Immune to your breath element.**
- **Cataclysm Breath (active).** Once per **Long Rest**, you exhale catastrophe: a
  **90-foot Line** or **60-foot Cone**. Each creature there makes a **Dexterity
  saving throw**, taking **12d8** of your element on a failure (half on a success).
  Creatures that fail are also knocked **Prone** and suffer a lingering effect until
  the end of their next turn: **Cold** → Speed 0; **Fire** → Burning (2d10/turn);
  **Lightning** → can't take Reactions and Speed halved.
- **Lord of Weather.** As a **Magic action** you can replicate *Control Weather*
  without a spell slot, once per Long Rest; while in Dragon Shape you can also
  summon or dismiss a 20-foot-radius cloud you can stand on as a Bonus Action.

### Designer notes

- The dragon **Wild Shape arrives at level 3** alongside an **at-will Breath
  Weapon** — the form is gated by Wild Shape uses, the breath never runs dry, and
  **Sky Serpent** (level 6) upgrades the same form (Extra Attack, Bonus-Action
  breath, double-speed flight) rather than handing you a second one.
- Choosing the element at level 3 lets one Circle cover Shenlong (storm), an ice
  river-dragon, or a Kaido-style brawler-wyrm.
- The only rationed pieces are the level-14 **Cataclysm Breath** and **Control
  Weather** (once per Long Rest); everything below is at-will or Wild-Shape-gated.
  If level-3 flight is too much, start Dragon Shape's Fly Speed at "equal to half
  your Speed" until Greater Wings.
