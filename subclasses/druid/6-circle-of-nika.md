# Circle of Nika

*Druid Circle — Brawler / chaos*

> They say there was a warrior of legend who made people laugh — who fought like
> the world was a toy and every wall was a joke. Your body remembers him. Your
> joints don't lock the way other people's do; punches bounce off you and bounce
> *back*; and when you really cut loose, the ground goes soft, the air goes
> springy, and the whole fight starts playing by your rules instead of physics'.

Inspired by the Sun God Nika and the rubber-bodied fighting of Monkey D. Luffy:
a riotous, freewheeling brawler Circle. It rewrites Wild Shape into an
ever-escalating set of **Gears**, building to a final **Awakening** where you stop
stretching the rules and start *breaking* them.

## Rubber Body (Level 3)

> Stretch first, ask questions never. Steel slides off you and stone forgets it
> was supposed to hurt.

- **Gum-Gum Resilience.** You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning damage** and
  **Immunity to Lightning damage** (current just doesn't get a grip on you).
- **Stretch Strike (at will).** Your unarmed strikes have a **15-foot reach** and
  deal **1d8 + Wisdom** Bludgeoning damage, and count as magical.
- **Gum-Gum Barrage (Extra Attack).** When you take the Attack action with your
  unarmed strikes, you make **two attacks** instead of one. When you hit, you can
  pull yourself adjacent to the target or pull the target (Large or smaller) up to
  10 feet toward you.

You also gain a new way to use Wild Shape — the **Gears**:

- **Gears.** Rather than a beast, you can spend a use of Wild Shape to shift into a
  **Gear** for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert), using your own
  statistics. While in any Gear you gain **Resistance to Piercing and Slashing
  damage** (to go with your rubber body) and retain spellcasting; and while not
  wearing armor your Armor Class equals **10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom
  modifier** — a bouncing body is hard to hit clean, and you can use a shield and
  still gain this benefit. On transforming, choose **Gear Two** or **Gear Third**
  (you can swap which is active as a Bonus Action, for free):
  - **Gear Two (speed).** Your **Speed increases by 10 feet** and you have
    **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws.**
  - **Gear Third (size).** Your unarmed strikes deal an extra **1d8** Bludgeoning,
    count as one size larger for grapples and shoves, and you gain **temporary Hit
    Points equal to your druid level.**

## Gear Mastery (Level 6)

> You learned what your body can do if you cheat a little. Pump the blood. Inflate
> the bones. The first two Gears, perfected.

Your **Gears** come into their full power:

- **Perfected Gear Two.** Your **Speed doubles** (instead of +10 feet), your unarmed
  reach increases by **5 feet**, and you can take the **Dash** or **Disengage**
  action as a Bonus Action.
- **Perfected Gear Third.** Your Gear Third extra damage increases to **2d8**, a hit
  can knock a Huge-or-smaller creature **Prone** (Strength save negates), and the
  temporary Hit Points it grants increase to **your druid level + your Wisdom
  modifier.**

---

## Gear Fourth (Level 10)

> Coat yourself in your own bounce and the floor becomes a trampoline, the air a
> springboard, and every miss a setup for the next hit.

While in a Gear, you can spend a **Bonus Action** to enter **Gear Fourth** for 1
minute. You can do this **once per Short or Long Rest** (no extra Wild Shape use):

- **Bounce-Man.** You gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed (hover, by bouncing
  through the air)**, your unarmed strikes deal an extra **1d10** Force damage, and
  attacks against you have **Disadvantage** while you have at least 10 feet of open
  space to bounce in.
- **Rebound (Reaction).** When you're hit by a melee attack, you can use your
  **Reaction** to bounce the force back: the attacker takes Force damage equal to
  **half the damage you took**, and you can shove it 15 feet.
- **Kong / Snake.** Once per turn one of your attacks can become either a single
  massive blow (an extra **2d10** Bludgeoning) **or** a flurry that targets **up to
  three creatures** within reach (one attack roll against each).

---

## Awakening: Sun God Nika (Level 14)

> You stop stretching the rules and start ignoring them. The world goes rubbery,
> the laughter comes easy, and for a little while you are a god of freedom who
> happens to be throwing punches.

Once per **Long Rest**, as a **Bonus Action**, you Awaken for 1 minute. While
Awakened:

- **Liberation Drums.** Your size can be Large or Huge, you gain **temporary Hit
  Points equal to twice your druid level**, **Resistance to all damage** except
  Psychic, and **Immunity to being Frightened, Charmed, Paralyzed, Stunned,
  Restrained, or Grappled** (a free spirit can't be bound).
- **Cartoon Logic (active).** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature or
  surface, you can warp the environment for an instant — choose one: turn a **30-foot
  patch of ground rubbery** (Difficult Terrain; creatures starting there can be
  bounced 15 feet), stretch a wall or weapon to **block a line of effect**, or grab
  a Large-or-smaller creature and **fling it 30 feet** (Strength save negates the
  throw).
- **Bajrang Punch (capstone strike).** As a **Magic action**, inflate a fist to
  titanic scale and swing. One target within 60 feet (and each creature in a
  15-foot-radius around it) makes a **Dexterity saving throw**, taking **8d10**
  Bludgeoning damage and being knocked **Prone** and pushed 20 feet on a failure
  (half damage only on a success). Structures and objects in the area are flattened.
  You can use Bajrang Punch once on each of your turns while Awakened.

### Designer notes

- The escalation **Rubber Body → Gears → Gear Fourth → Awakening** is the point, and
  the low rungs stay free: **Stretch Strike is at-will with Extra Attack at level 3**,
  and the **Gears** are a Wild Shape form from level 3 (perfected at 6), gated only
  by Wild Shape uses, then at-will to swap between.
- The strong modes are now rationed: **Gear Fourth** is once per Short Rest, and
  **Awakening** is once per Long Rest — a true "main character moment" rather than a
  spammable button.
- **Awakening** (Resistance to all but Psychic + status immunities + reality-warping,
  for 1 minute) is the single biggest payoff in the document; trim its duration or
  drop the damage immunity to "B/P/S only" for a tamer table.
