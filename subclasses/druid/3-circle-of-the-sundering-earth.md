# Circle of the Sundering Earth

*Druid Circle — Blaster / control*

> The earth is not solid. You learned that the way you learn a secret — all at
> once, and it changes everything. To you the ground is a drumhead, the air is a
> pane of glass, and the sea is a sheet you can snap. You set your heels, you
> take hold of the world, and you *shake.* Mountains are just rubble that hasn't
> been told yet.

Inspired by the Gura-Gura no Mi — the power to vibrate anything, even the
intangible. This Circle reskins Wild Shape into an **avatar of the quake** and
leans hard into **Force damage**, **forced movement**, and **terrain control**.
It is the closest a Druid comes to being a walking earthquake.

## Circle Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Druid Level | Spells |
|-------------|--------|
| 3 | *Earth Tremor*, *Thunderwave* |
| 5 | *Shatter*, *Spike Growth* |

## Tremorsense & Quaking Touch (Level 3)

> You feel the world the way a spider feels a web. And when you press on it, it
> presses back — somewhere else, hard.

- **Tremorsense.** You gain **Tremorsense out to 30 feet.**
- **Resonance.** You can change the damage type of any Druid spell that deals
  **Thunder, Bludgeoning, or Lightning** damage to **Force.**
- **Quaking Touch (at will).** As a **Bonus Action**, you crack the air with a
  punch: one creature within 30 feet makes a **Strength saving throw**, taking
  **2d8 Force** damage and being knocked **Prone** on a failure (half and no Prone
  on a success). The die increases to **3d8** at level 7, **4d8** at level 13, and
  **5d8** at level 17. There is no limit on how often you can use it.

You also gain a new way to use Wild Shape — the **Avatar of the Tremor**:

- **Avatar of the Tremor.** Rather than a beast, you can spend a use of Wild Shape
  to become a living quake for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert), using
  your own statistics. Your size becomes **Large**, you gain **temporary Hit Points
  equal to your druid level**, **Resistance to Force and Thunder damage**, and
  unarmed strikes dealing **1d8 + Wisdom** Force damage with a 10-foot reach. You
  retain your spellcasting in this form.
- **Bedrock Hide.** While in this form and not wearing armor, your Armor Class
  equals **10 + your Constitution modifier + your Wisdom modifier** — an immovable
  wall of quaking stone that simply refuses to fall. You can use a shield and still
  gain this benefit.

## Quakewalker (Level 6)

> You stop turning into animals. There's no animal big enough for what you are now.

Your **Avatar of the Tremor** comes into its full power. While in that form, it
gains the following additional benefits:

- **Quake-Wreathed.** Your temporary Hit Points on transforming increase to **your
  druid level + your Wisdom modifier**, and your unarmed strikes deal **1d10 +
  Wisdom** Force damage.
- **Tremor Strikes (Extra Attack).** When you take the Attack action, you make
  **two attacks** instead of one.
- **Walking Fault.** The ground within **10 feet** of you is **Difficult Terrain**
  for your enemies, and once per turn when you hit a creature you can push it **10
  feet** and knock it **Prone.**
- **Crack the Air.** Your **Quaking Touch** becomes a **15-foot Cone** instead of a
  single target.

## Faultline (Level 10)

> You split the ground like cloth. Whatever was standing on it is now falling
> into it.

By **expending a use of Wild Shape**, as a **Magic action** you choose a
**60-foot-long, 10-foot-wide Line** or a **20-foot-radius area** within 120 feet.
Each creature there makes a **Strength saving throw**:

- **On a failure:** **8d8 Force** damage, knocked **Prone**, and **Restrained**
  (caught in the fissure). A Restrained creature repeats the save at the end of
  each of its turns, or can use an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check
  against your spell save DC to climb free.
- **On a success:** half damage and none of the other effects.

The area becomes **Difficult Terrain** for 1 minute, and any unattended object or
structure in the area takes **double damage.**

## Worldquake (Level 14)

> You take hold of the air itself and twist. Reality crinkles like a dropped pane
> of glass, and everything inside the crack is at your mercy.

- **Unstoppable Vibration (passive).** Your Force and Thunder damage treats
  **Resistance as nonexistent** and **Immunity as Resistance.**
- **Vibration Guard (Reaction).** When you or a creature within 60 feet is hit by
  an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to vibrate the strike apart, reducing
  the damage by **3d12.**
- **Worldquake (active).** Once per **Long Rest**, as a **Magic action** you seize
  the world and shake. Choose a **30-foot-radius Sphere** or **60-foot Cone** within
  120 feet. Each creature there makes a **Strength saving throw**:
  - **On a failure:** **10d8 Force** damage, hurled up to **20 feet** in a
    direction you choose, knocked **Prone**, and **Stunned** until the end of your
    next turn.
  - **On a success:** half damage and none of the other effects.

  Structures and unattended objects in the area are reduced to rubble. You may
  exempt any creatures you choose.

### Designer notes

- The Druid sibling of this repo's Seismic Sorcerer: **Quaking Touch is at-will**
  and the **Avatar of the Tremor** is a Wild Shape form from level 3 that gains
  Extra Attack and a difficult-terrain aura at 6 — strong, repeatable, but gated by
  a Wild Shape use.
- **Faultline** costs a Wild Shape use each cast, and **Worldquake** is the once-
  per-day finisher, so the big control swings have real cost.
- **Resonance** lets *Thunderwave*, *Call Lightning*, and similar staples read as
  Force, keeping the theme from your earliest picks. Both AoEs spare allies by
  choice — "devastating, not indiscriminate."
