# Circle of the Phoenix

*Druid Circle — Support / sustain (tank · healer · damage)*

> Everything that burns is also reborn. You learned to live in that hinge — the
> instant between ash and the new wing. Fire does not frighten you; it is the
> friend that puts you back together. You walk into the worst of it on purpose,
> and you come out the far side trailing embers and dragging your allies up by
> the collar, alight and unafraid.

Inspired by the immortal Phoenix: a Wild Shape Circle that is tank, healer, and
damage-dealer at once. Your special forms let you soak punishment, mend the
wounded with healing flame, and — when you finally fall — rise again from your
own ashes.

---

## Circle Spells (Level 3)

The listed spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared total.

| Druid Level | Spells |
|-------------|--------|
| 3 | *Cure Wounds*, *Burning Hands* |
| 5 | *Lesser Restoration*, *Flaming Sphere* |
| 7 | *Revivify*, *Aura of Vitality* |
| 9 | *Mass Cure Wounds*, *Wall of Fire* |

## Healing Flame (Level 3)

> Your fire chooses. It scours your enemies and warms your friends in the same
> breath.

- **Discerning Fire.** When you deal Fire damage with a spell, you can choose any
  number of creatures in the area to be unharmed; instead, each chosen creature
  **regains Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier** (minimum 1).
- **Ember Mend (at will).** As a **Bonus Action**, touch a creature (or yourself)
  to wreathe it in soft flame: it regains **1d8 + your Wisdom modifier** Hit Points
  and ends one of the **Burning**, **Poisoned**, or **Diseased** conditions on
  itself. The die increases to **2d8** at level 7 and **3d8** at level 13. You can
  use Ember Mend **once on each of your turns**, with no daily limit.

You also gain a new way to use Wild Shape — the **Phoenix Form**:

- **Phoenix Form.** Rather than a beast, you can spend a use of Wild Shape to take
  the form of a phoenix for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert), using your
  own statistics. You gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed (hover)**, **Resistance
  to Fire damage**, **temporary Hit Points equal to your druid level** when you
  transform, and talon attacks dealing **1d10 + Wisdom** Fire damage. You retain
  your spellcasting in this form.
- **Cinderscale.** While in Phoenix Form and not wearing armor, your Armor Class
  equals **13 + your Wisdom modifier** — shimmering heat and flame-feathers turn
  blows aside. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

## Rising Flame (Level 6)

> You spread wings of living fire. The fight looks very different from up here.

Your **Phoenix Form** comes into its full power. While in that form, it gains the
following additional benefits:

- **Searing Aura.** At the start of each of your turns, each enemy within **10
  feet** takes **Fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier**, and one ally within 10
  feet of your choice **regains** the same number of Hit Points.
- **Talon & Flame (Extra Attack).** When you take the Attack action, you make
  **two attacks** (fiery talons) instead of one.

---

## Rebirth (Level 10)

> The Phoenix has one trick worth a hundred others: it does not stay dead.

- **From the Ashes.** When you drop to 0 Hit Points (in any form), you can instead
  **fall to 1 Hit Point** and erupt in rebirth: you regain **Hit Points equal to
  half your maximum**, stand up if Prone, and each enemy within **15 feet** takes
  **5d8 Fire** damage (Dexterity save for half). You can use this once per **Long
  Rest.**
- **Renewing Pyre.** At the start of each of your turns, one ally within 15 feet who
  is below half their maximum Hit Points regains **Hit Points equal to your Wisdom
  modifier** — the embers never quite let your friends fall.

---

## Immortal Phoenix (Level 14)

> Ash, then wing, then ash again — the cycle has no end, and now neither do you.

Your Phoenix Form ascends:

- **Greater Phoenix.** Your temporary Hit Points on transforming increase to
  **twice your druid level**, your Searing Aura range becomes **15 feet**, and while
  in the form you gain **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing** in
  addition to Fire.
- **Cleansing Renewal (active).** As a **Magic action**, you flare brilliantly:
  each ally within 30 feet regains **5d8 Hit Points** and ends one condition among
  Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or Stunned. Once per
  **Short or Long Rest.**
- **Eternal Return.** Your **From the Ashes** feature now recharges on a **Short or
  Long Rest**, and when it triggers you may also bring **one ally who died within
  the last minute** back to life with Hit Points equal to your druid level.

---

### Designer notes

- "The party doesn't die while you're up." **Ember Mend is at-will (once per turn)**,
  **Searing Aura** makes every turn AoE damage + a topped-up ally, and **Discerning
  Fire** lets you drop fire spells *into* the melee without cooking friends.
- The genuinely strong pieces are rationed: **From the Ashes** is once per Long Rest
  (Short Rest at 14), and **Cleansing Renewal** is once per Short Rest. Phoenix Form
  is a Wild Shape form from level 3 (Searing Aura and Extra Attack come online at 6),
  gated by a Wild Shape use.
- If sustained healing is still too high, drop Renewing Pyre to "once per turn, only
  while in Phoenix Form."
