# Circle of the Monkey King

*Druid Circle — Melee striker / skirmisher*

> Somewhere a stone cracked open and a monkey stepped out already laughing. You
> are its heir. You don't commune with nature so much as *swagger* through it,
> a borrowed cudgel across your shoulders that can grow to scrape the clouds or
> shrink behind your ear. You wear another shape the way other people wear a
> coat, and you have seventy-two of them. Heaven filed a complaint. You filed it
> in the bin.

Inspired by Sun Wukong, the Monkey King: a trickster brawler whose two toys are
an absurd, ever-growing staff (**Shillelagh**, reforged into the Ruyi Jingu Bang)
and a Wild Shape that mimics the **72 Transformations**. At its peak you set
aside the menagerie and put on the Monkey King's own war-form.

## Ruyi Jingu Bang (Level 3)

> The Sea-Calming Pillar answers only to you. It is heavier than a mountain and
> lighter than a whisper, and it goes wherever you point.

Your **Shillelagh** is transformed into the *as-you-will golden-banded cudgel*:

- **Compliant Pillar.** While Shillelagh is active, the weapon's damage die
  becomes a **d10** and its damage is **Force**. At the start of each of your turns
  you can set its reach to **5, 10, or 15 feet** as it lengthens and shortens at
  your whim.
- **Twin Strikes (Extra Attack).** When you take the Attack action with the cudgel,
  you make **two attacks** instead of one.
- **Will-Made-Iron.** Once per turn when you hit with the cudgel, add **1d6 Force**
  damage, increasing to **2d6** at level 11.
- **Stow.** As an object interaction you can shrink the cudgel to the size of a
  needle (tuck it behind your ear) or restore it — it can't be disarmed or taken
  from you while you live.

You also gain a new way to use Wild Shape from the start — the **War-Monkey Shape**:

- **War-Monkey Shape.** Rather than a beast, you can spend a use of Wild Shape to
  take on a war-monkey form for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert), using
  your own statistics. Your size can be Medium or Large, you gain a **Climb Speed**
  equal to your Speed, and you gain **temporary Hit Points equal to your druid
  level** when you transform. You retain your spellcasting and wield the Ruyi Jingu
  Bang normally in this form (keeping Twin Strikes).
- **Body of Bronze.** While in your War-Monkey Shape and not wearing armor, your
  Armor Class equals **10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier** — the
  staff-master's deflecting, somersaulting defense. You can use a shield and still
  gain this benefit.

## Seventy-Two Transformations (Level 6)

> The first trick the Monkey King mastered was being *something else, right now,
> and convincingly.* Bird, beast, fish, a temple bell — it's all the same trick.

Your Wild Shape grows far beyond the beginner's bestiary:

- **Unbounded Forms.** When you Wild Shape into a beast, the **maximum Challenge
  Rating equals your druid level ÷ 3** (rounded down), and you ignore movement-type
  restrictions (you may take forms with a flying or swimming speed).
- **Quick Change.** Once per turn you can change directly from one form into another
  without first reverting (you still spend a use of Wild Shape for the new form).
- **Object Form.** As a Bonus Action you can assume the form of a Tiny-to-Large
  **inanimate object** for up to 1 hour, at will. Your Speed becomes 0 unless the
  object could move, and creatures must succeed on an Investigation check against
  your spell save DC to discern you aren't what you appear.

## The Monkey King's War-Form (Level 10)

> You stop borrowing shapes and put on your *own.* Phoenix-feather plumes rise
> from your brow like antennae, your fur turns to burnished gold, and the cudgel
> sings.

Your **War-Monkey Shape** blossoms into the Monkey King's true war-form. While
transformed, it gains the following additional benefits:

- **Greater War-Form.** Your temporary Hit Points on transforming increase to
  **twice your druid level**, you gain a **+10-foot bonus to Speed**, and a **Fly
  Speed (somersault cloud, hover)** equal to your Speed.
- **Plume Antennae.** You have **Advantage on Initiative** and on Wisdom
  (Perception) checks, and you can't be **Surprised.**
- **Wukong's Vigor.** You have **Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing
  damage.**

---

## Great Sage Equal to Heaven (Level 14)

> Heaven gave you a meaningless title to keep you quiet. You made it true.

Your War-Form ascends and your tricks turn legendary:

- **Three-Fold Cudgel.** While in your War-Form, your Twin Strikes improves to
  **three attacks** with the Attack action.
- **Hair Clones (active).** By expending a use of Wild Shape, pluck a handful of
  hairs and blow: as a **Bonus Action** you conjure **3 duplicates** of yourself
  that last 1 minute. Use the *Mirror Image* rule for protecting you, and once on
  each of your turns one clone can make a single cudgel attack (your Will-Made-Iron
  Force damage) as part of your Attack action.
- **Diamond Body.** While in your War-Form you have **Advantage on saving throws to
  avoid or end the Frightened, Charmed, Stunned, and Paralyzed conditions.**
- **Skull-Cracking Strike.** Once per turn when you hit with the cudgel, the target
  makes a **Constitution saving throw**; on a failure it takes an extra **4d10
  Force** damage and is **Stunned** until the end of your next turn (half damage and
  no Stun on a success).

---

### Designer notes

- A melee Druid that stays relevant 3→20: a **d10 Force reach weapon with Extra
  Attack at level 3** and a once-per-turn rider, scaling to three attacks at 14.
- **Seventy-Two Transformations** keeps the menagerie strong (CR ≈ level ÷ 3, the
  Moon-Druid ceiling), while the **War-Monkey Shape** is a Wild Shape form from
  level 3 that grows into the flying, resistant, full-casting **War-Form** at 10 —
  it costs a Wild Shape use, which is the limiter.
- The only rationed pieces are **Hair Clones** (a Wild Shape use) and **Skull-
  Cracking** (once per turn); everything at level 3–6 is at-will.
