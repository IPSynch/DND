# Circle of Titans

*Druid Circle — Bruiser / juggernaut*

> There is an older shape sleeping under your skin — vast, hot-blooded, and
> hungry to stand up. Other druids fold themselves down into wolves and hawks.
> You go the other way. You unfold. Steam boils off your shoulders, the ground
> remembers your weight, and for a few terrible minutes you are the largest thing
> on the battlefield and everyone knows it.

Inspired by the Titans of *Attack on Titan*: Wild Shape made colossal. Where a
classic druid shrinks into the wild, you erupt out of it — a towering humanoid
juggernaut of muscle and steam that regenerates, shrugs off blows, and hits like
a falling tower.

## Titan Shifting (Level 3)

> You bite down, the world goes white, and you stand back up *bigger.*

You gain a new use for Wild Shape: rather than a beast, you assume your **Titan
Form** for **1 hour** (or until you choose to revert). It uses your own statistics
with these changes:

- **Colossus.** Your size becomes **Large**. You gain **temporary Hit Points equal
  to your druid level + your Constitution modifier** each time you transform, and
  your **Speed increases by 10 feet.**
- **Steaming Fists.** Your unarmed strikes deal **1d10 + Strength** Bludgeoning
  damage (use Strength or Wisdom, your choice), have **10-foot reach**, and count
  as magical.
- **Twin Smash (Extra Attack).** When you take the Attack action in Titan Form, you
  make **two attacks** instead of one.
- **Earthshaker.** Once per turn when you hit a Large or smaller creature, you can
  knock it **Prone** or push it **10 feet.**
- **Titan's Hide.** While in Titan Form and not wearing armor, your Armor Class
  equals **14 + your Constitution modifier** — a colossus doesn't dodge, it endures.
  You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

## Hardening & Regeneration (Level 6)

> Crystal sheaths the knuckles; severed flesh hisses and knits. A Titan that
> won't fall down is most of the fight.

- **Hardening.** While in Titan Form, you can use a **Bonus Action** to crystallize
  your skin: you gain a **+2 bonus to AC** and **Resistance to Bludgeoning,
  Piercing, and Slashing damage** until the start of your next turn.
- **Regeneration.** At the start of each of your turns while in Titan Form and above
  0 HP, you regain **Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier + your
  proficiency bonus.** Severed limbs and similar maiming regrow on your next turn.
- **Steam Vent.** When you take damage, you can use your **Reaction** to vent
  scalding steam: each creature within 10 feet takes **2d8 Fire** damage (no save)
  and the area is lightly obscured until the start of your next turn.

## Titan Class (Level 10)

> Not all Titans are the same beast. Choose what *kind* of monster you become.

When you enter Titan Form, choose one of the following specializations (you can
choose a different one each transformation):

- **Attack Titan (mobility).** Your reach increases to **15 feet**, you have
  **Advantage on opportunity attacks**, and after you hit a creature you can move up
  to half your Speed without provoking, once per turn.
- **Armored Titan (defense).** Your Hardening AC bonus becomes **+4**, its
  Resistance lasts the whole fight without re-upping, and you can add **Resistance
  to one chosen damage type** (chosen each turn). Forced movement against you is
  halved.
- **Colossal Titan (devastation).** Your size becomes **Huge**, your reach **20
  feet**, your fists deal **+1d10** damage, and as a Bonus Action you can vent a
  massive blast: each creature within **15 feet** makes a **Constitution save**,
  taking **3d10 Fire** and being **Blinded** until the end of your next turn on a
  failure (half and no Blind on a success).

## The Founding Roar (Level 14)

> You open your mouth and the sound that comes out is older than language. Lesser
> things kneel.

Your Titan Form reaches its apex:

- **Towering Apex.** Your temporary Hit Points on transforming increase to **twice
  your druid level + your Constitution modifier**, and you gain a **third attack**
  with your Attack action.
- **The Roar (active).** As a **Magic action** you loose the Founding Titan's
  bellow in a **60-foot Cone**. Each enemy makes a **Wisdom saving throw**: on a
  failure it is **Frightened of you and Stunned** until the end of your next turn,
  then remains Frightened for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of its turns);
  on a success it is merely Frightened until the end of its next turn. You can use
  The Roar **once per Short or Long Rest.**
- **Last Stand.** While in Titan Form, the first time you would drop to 0 HP each
  day, you instead drop to **half your hit-point maximum** and the form endures (the
  nape seals shut). Once per **Long Rest.**

---

### Designer notes

- The bruiser of the set: **Extra Attack at level 3**, a Bonus-Action defensive
  shell plus per-turn Regeneration at 6, and a third attack at 14. The Titan Form
  itself is gated by Wild Shape uses, so its in-form perks stay at-will.
- **Regeneration** is intentionally Con + proficiency (≈6–11/turn) rather than
  flat-level so it heals steadily without making you unkillable.
- **The Roar** (Short Rest) and **Last Stand** (Long Rest) are the rationed pieces;
  Armored Titan's whole-fight Resistance is the strongest dial — trim it to "B/P/S
  only" if your table needs the air back.
