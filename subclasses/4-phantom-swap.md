# Warrior of the Phantom Swap

*Monk Subclass*

The battlefield, to you, is a board of pieces — and you have learned the secret that the
pieces can be *moved*. A clap, a gesture, a marked place, and suddenly the brute looming
over your ally stands where your ally stood, your ally stands safely behind you, and you
stand grinning where neither of them expected. You do not so much cross distance as
delete it. Allies adore fighting beside you; enemies never learn which threat is real
until the positions have already changed beneath their feet.

*Throughout these features, your **Monk DC** equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your
Wisdom modifier, and "Martial Arts die" refers to your current Martial Arts die. You
spend **Focus Points** (called Ki in some traditions). A Swap is a teleport: it doesn't
provoke Opportunity Attacks and ignores intervening obstacles, but each creature needs a
clear space to arrive in.*

## Level 3: Mark and Swap

Two points named, two points exchanged.

***Swap.*** As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to instantly swap positions
with one willing creature, or with one unwilling Large or smaller creature, that you can
see within 60 feet of you. An unwilling creature can resist by succeeding on a Charisma
saving throw. Each creature swapped this way, including you, has Advantage on its next
attack roll if the roll is made before the end of its next turn.

***Blink Step.*** When you take the Step of the Wind Bonus Action, you can teleport up to
the distance you would move instead of moving normally.

## Level 6: Field of Marks

A clap, and the board rearranges — best of all when you fight beside someone you trust.

***Field of Marks.*** On your turn (no action required), you can mark up to two points or
creatures you can see within 60 feet of you. A mark lasts for 1 minute or until you use
it. As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to swap the positions of any two of
your marked targets (which can include yourself), forcing a Charisma saving throw on each
unwilling creature. This counts as a single use of Swap regardless of how many targets
you reposition.

***Brotherhood.*** While you are within 30 feet of an ally, you and that ally both have
Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that is within 5 feet of both of you.

***Punishing Exchange.*** Immediately after you Swap, the first Unarmed Strike you hit
with this turn deals extra damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die.

## Level 11: Untouchable

You are never quite where the blow lands.

***Reactive Swap.*** When you or an ally you can see within 60 feet of you is hit by an
attack or fails a saving throw, you can take a Reaction and spend 1 Focus Point to swap
that target with another willing creature, or with the attacker — after you see the roll
but before damage is dealt. If you swap the target with the attacker, the attacker
suffers the effect in their place; otherwise the original target avoids it entirely.

***Phantom Mobility.*** The range of your Swap and other teleport features increases to
120 feet. In addition, the first time you Swap on each of your turns, you can make one
Unarmed Strike against a creature within 5 feet of your new position as part of the same
Bonus Action.

## Level 17: Black Flash

Position, severed and rejoined in a single instant — and where the two impacts overlap,
something far beyond an ordinary strike erupts.

***Instant Across.*** The first time you Swap on each of your turns, it costs no Focus
Point, and you can now swap with creatures of any size up to Gargantuan.

***Black Flash.*** Once on each of your turns, immediately after you Swap, your next
Unarmed Strike this turn becomes a Black Flash. It automatically scores a critical hit if
it hits, deals extra damage equal to six rolls of your Martial Arts die (Force), and the
target and each creature within 10 feet of it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw
or be knocked Prone and have the Stunned condition until the end of your next turn. After
a Black Flash, you can immediately Swap again without spending a Focus Point.

***Untouchable Duelist.*** When a creature within 5 feet of you misses you with an attack
roll, you can take a Reaction to swap with it and make one Unarmed Strike against it.

---

### Designer Notes

- The fantasy here is board control, not raw damage — though Black Flash makes sure the
  payoff exists. Most of the power budget is spent on *who stands where*, which is
  enormously strong in play: pulling a boss out of its own wall, feeding a minion into
  your wizard's *Fireball*, or yanking a downed ally to safety as a Reaction.
- If forcing only a Charisma save makes unwilling Swaps too reliable, let boss-tier
  creatures be immune to involuntary Swap while at full Hit Points.
