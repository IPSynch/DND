# Warrior of the Phantom Swap

*Monastic Order — Mobility / Duelist*

> The battlefield, to you, is a board of pieces — and you have learned the secret
> that the pieces can be *moved*. A clap, a gesture, a marked place, and suddenly
> the brute who was looming over your ally stands where your ally stood, and your
> ally stands safely behind you, and you stand grinning where neither of them
> expected. You do not so much cross distance as delete it. Allies adore fighting
> beside you; enemies never know which threat is real until the position has already
> changed beneath their feet.

A teleporting duelist inspired by the art of **swapping places** across a battle.
You mark points and creatures and trade positions at will, controlling space itself
— repositioning enemies into danger, yanking allies out of it, and chaining together
devastating partnered assaults.

> **Conventions.** "MA die" is your **Martial Arts die**; save DCs are your **Monk
> DC**. Swapping is a teleport: it doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks and ignores
> intervening obstacles, but you must have a clear destination space.

---

## Mark & Swap (Level 3)

*Two points named; two points exchanged.*

- **Swap.** As a **Bonus Action**, spend **1 Focus Point** to instantly **swap
  positions** with one willing creature, or with one unwilling Large or smaller
  creature, that you can see within **60 feet**. An unwilling creature can resist
  with a successful **Charisma save**. Each creature swapped (including you) gains
  **Advantage on its next attack roll** before the end of its next turn — momentum
  carried into a new place.
- **Blink Step (passive).** When you take the **Step of the Wind** Bonus Action, you
  can instead **teleport** the distance you would have moved.

---

## Mutual Love & Boogie (Level 6)

*A clap, and the board rearranges. Best, by far, when you fight beside someone you
trust.*

- **Field of Marks.** You can mark up to **two points or creatures** you can see
  within 60 feet (no action required, on your turn). As a **Bonus Action**, you can
  **swap the positions of any two marked targets** (creatures or yourself), forcing a
  **Charisma save** on each unwilling creature. This is a single use of Swap and
  costs **1 Focus Point** total regardless of how many you reposition.
- **Brotherhood (passive).** While you are within **30 feet** of an ally, both of you
  have **Advantage on attack rolls** against any creature within 5 feet of *both* of
  you. Two duelists, one cornered foe.
- **Punishing Exchange.** Immediately after you Swap, your **first Unarmed Strike**
  this turn deals an extra **MA die** of damage.

---

## Untouchable (Level 11)

*You are never quite where the blow lands.*

- **Reactive Swap.** When you or an ally you can see within 60 feet is hit by an
  attack or fails a save, you can use your **Reaction** and spend **1 Focus Point**
  to **swap that target with another willing creature, or with the attacker**, *after
  seeing the roll but before damage*. If you swap the target with the attacker, the
  attacker takes the consequences in their place; otherwise the original target avoids
  the effect entirely.
- **Phantom Mobility (passive).** Your teleport range for all features increases to
  **120 feet**, and the first time each turn you Swap, you can **make one Unarmed
  Strike** against a creature adjacent to your new position as a free attack.

---

## Black Flash (Level 17)

*Position, severed and rejoined in a single instant — and where the two impacts
overlap, something far beyond a normal strike erupts.*

- **Instant Across (passive).** Swap is **free** the first time you use it on each
  of your turns, and you can now swap with creatures up to **Gargantuan**.
- **Black Flash.** Once on each of your turns, immediately after you Swap, your next
  Unarmed Strike this turn becomes a **Black Flash**: it is **automatically a critical
  hit** on a hit, deals an extra **6 MA dice** of Force damage, and the target plus
  each creature within **10 feet** of it must succeed on a **Constitution save** or be
  knocked **Prone** and **Stunned** until the end of your next turn. After a Black
  Flash, you may immediately **Swap again for free**.
- **Untouchable Duelist (passive).** When a creature within 5 feet of you misses you
  with an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to swap with it and make one Unarmed
  Strike against it. Whoever swings at you ends up the one out of position.

---

### Designer notes

- The fantasy here is **board control**, not raw damage — though Black Flash makes
  sure the payoff exists. Most of the power budget is spent on *who stands where*,
  which is enormously strong in practice: pulling a boss out of its summoned wall,
  feeding a minion into your wizard's *Fireball*, or yanking the downed cleric to
  safety as a Reaction.
- **Reactive Swap** (level 11) is the keystone "feel-good" button: redirecting a
  killing blow onto the creature that threw it is exactly the source-material flex.
- Tuning lever: if forcing only a **Charisma save** makes unwilling swaps too reliable,
  let larger or boss-tier creatures add their Proficiency twice, or simply be immune
  to involuntary Swap while at full HP.
