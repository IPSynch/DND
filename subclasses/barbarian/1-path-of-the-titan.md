# Path of the Titan

*Primal Path — Bruiser (unarmed, transformation, scaling might)*

> There is a bigger version of you sleeping under your skin. Most people live
> their whole lives without ever waking it. You found the door early — a moment
> of terror, of fury, of something that mattered more than your own ribs — and
> you kicked it open. Now when you let go, you *grow*. Muscle stacks on muscle,
> your shadow doubles, and the thing that walks out of your rage is bigger,
> meaner, and stronger than the person who walked in. And every year you do it,
> the thing that comes out is bigger than the year before.

This path turns Rage into a **physical transformation** built around **unarmed
fighting**. Its defining gimmick is **escalation**: a Titan literally hits harder
at every tier, and a flat bonus that *climbs the longer a fight drags on*. Think
the Hulk getting angrier, or a Symbol of Peace going Plus Ultra.

---

## Titanic Form (Level 3)

The flavor: your Rage isn't a mood, it's a *metamorphosis*. Tendons creak, your
frame swells, and your fists become the only weapon you'll ever need.

While you are raging:

- **Monstrous Frame.** Your reach increases by **5 feet**, your carrying capacity
  doubles, and you have **Advantage on Strength checks and on attack rolls to
  Grapple or Shove.**
- **Crushing Fists.** Your **Unarmed Strikes** deal **1d8 + your Strength
  modifier** Bludgeoning damage (instead of the normal unarmed amount) and count
  as a weapon you're wielding for the purpose of your Rage Damage and any
  Barbarian feature. You can use Unarmed Strikes with Reckless Attack and Extra
  Attack as normal.
- **Awakened Vigor.** The moment you enter your Rage, you gain **Temporary Hit
  Points equal to your Barbarian level + your Constitution modifier**. These
  refresh each time you Rage.

---

## Escalating Wrath (Level 6)

The flavor: the longer the Titan is loose, the worse it gets for everyone in the
room. You don't tire — you *wind up*.

- **Rising Fury.** While raging, your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **1d8**
  Bludgeoning damage on a hit. This increases to **2d8** at level 10 and **3d8**
  at level 14.
- **Building Momentum.** Starting on your **second** turn of each Rage, you gain a
  stacking **+1 bonus to your Unarmed Strike damage rolls**, increasing by another
  +1 at the start of each of your turns, to a maximum of **+5**. The bonus resets
  when the Rage ends. (You get angrier; they get unluckier.)
- **Plus Ultra (Bonus Action).** Once per Rage, you can pour everything into one
  blow: make a single Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action. On a hit it deals an
  **two extra dice of its damage**, and the target must succeed on a Strength save
  or be knocked **Prone** and pushed **15 feet** away.

---

## Colossus (Level 10)

The flavor: there's no longer any hiding what you are. The Titan stands a head
taller than the doorways, and the ground remembers each footfall.

While raging:

- **Towering Size.** Your size becomes **Large** (your gear grows with you). Your
  reach is now **+5 feet** beyond Large's already-extended reach, and your
  Unarmed Strike die becomes **1d10**.
- **Seismic Landing.** Once on each of your turns, when you move at least 15 feet
  straight toward a creature and hit it with an Unarmed Strike, it takes an
  **extra 2d10** Bludgeoning damage and you can knock it Prone (no save), and each
  other creature within 10 feet of it takes Bludgeoning damage equal to your Rage
  Damage from the shockwave.
- **Unstoppable Bulk.** You have **Resistance to all damage** for the first **two
  rounds** of each Rage, you can't be **Grappled, Restrained, or knocked Prone** against
  your will while raging, and your jump distance triples.

---

## Avatar of Strength (Level 14)

The flavor: this is the form they tell stories about — the one that flips
vehicles, splits walls, and ends the fight with one wind-up. The Titan, fully
unchained.

While raging:

- **Apex Frame.** Your Unarmed Strike die becomes **1d12**, and your Strength
  score increases by **4** (to a maximum of **26**). Your size can become **Huge**
  at the start of your turn if there's room.
- **One For All (the wind-up, once per Rage).** When you take the **Attack
  action**, you can forgo all your attacks to deliver a single, earth-shaking
  blow at a point within your reach. Each creature of your choice within **20
  feet** of that point makes a **Strength saving throw** (DC = 8 + your
  Proficiency Bonus + your Strength modifier). On a **failure**, a creature takes
  **8d12 + your Strength modifier** Bludgeoning damage, is knocked **Prone**, and
  is pushed **15 feet** away; on a **success**, it takes **half damage** and none
  of the rest. Unattended
  objects and structures in the area take **double damage**.
- **Worldbreaker.** Your Unarmed Strikes ignore **Resistance** to Bludgeoning
  damage and treat **Immunity as Resistance**.

---

### Designer notes

- The "scaling" gimmick is delivered three ways at once: the **unarmed die**
  climbs 1d8 → 1d10 → 1d12 across tiers, **Rising Fury** adds 1d8 → 2d8 → 3d8,
  and **Building Momentum** rewards *staying* in melee with up to a +4 swing. A
  Titan that's been fighting for a while is genuinely dangerous — exactly the
  source-material fantasy, without runaway numbers.
- **One For All** is now a once-per-Rage **save-for-half AoE slam** — you still
  roll nothing to "hit," but enemies get their save and take half on a success.
  It's a big, climactic moment rather than a guaranteed delete button.
- Tuning lever: if the +4 Strength at 14 plus Huge size is too much for your
  table, cap Strength at 24 or keep the size at Large.
