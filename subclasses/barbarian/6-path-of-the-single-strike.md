# Path of the Single Strike

*Primal Path — Nuke (one overwhelming attack per turn)*

> Everyone else is counting hits. You only ever needed one. Somewhere past the
> point where training becomes obsession and obsession becomes something quieter,
> you stopped caring about combos, footwork, and follow-up. Why throw a flurry
> when a flurry is just an apology for the first blow not being enough? Yours is
> always enough. You wind up the way other people draw breath — without thinking,
> without effort — and then there is a sound like a door slamming on the world,
> and the fight is simply over. The hardest part of your day is finding anyone who
> makes you throw a second one.

This path **reflavors Extra Attack into a single, monstrous blow.** You give up
attacking more than once each turn; in exchange, every extra attack you'd have
made instead *pours into the one strike you do throw*. The fantasy is Saitama:
absurd, anticlimactic, overwhelming power in one punch.

---

## Single Strike (Level 3)

The flavor: you've thrown so many strikes that they've collapsed into one. All
your force, all at once, every time.

- **One Is Enough.** When you take the **Attack action** on your turn, you can
  make only **one** weapon or Unarmed Strike attack with it (you can still make a
  separate Bonus Action attack from another feature). When you do, that single
  attack deals an extra **2d12** damage of its type. This die increases as you
  gain levels: **3d12** at level 6, **4d12** at level 10, and **5d12** at level
  14.
- **Wind-Up.** Once Extra Attack would normally let you attack more than once
  (level 5+), each attack you *forgo* this way instead adds **+1d12** to your
  Single Strike (so the bonus naturally grows as your Extra Attacks would have).
- **Follow-Through.** If your Single Strike reduces a creature to 0 hit points,
  you can move up to your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks — there's
  nothing left to fight here, so you go find more.

---

## Serious Power (Level 6)

The flavor: you can put *more* in. You usually don't have to. When you do, things
break that aren't supposed to break.

- **Concentrated Force.** Your Single Strike ignores **Resistance** to its damage
  type and deals **double damage to objects and structures**.
- **Shockwave.** When your Single Strike hits, each other creature of your choice
  within **20 feet** of the target takes **Thunder damage equal to your Rage
  Damage + your Strength modifier** and must succeed on a Strength save or be
  pushed 10 feet and knocked Prone — the air can't get out of the way fast enough.
- **Read the Room.** Before you make your Single Strike, you can take a moment to
  measure the foe (no action): you gain a **+2 bonus to the attack roll**, and if
  it hits, you add your **Rage Damage a second time**.

---

## Serious Punch (Level 10)

The flavor: this is you actually trying — a little. The wind-up is bigger, the
sound is louder, and whatever you hit is generally not a problem anymore.

- **Overwhelming Blow.** When you hit with your Single Strike, you can choose one
  (you choose after seeing the hit land): knock the target **Prone** and push it
  up to 20 feet; force a Constitution save or be **Stunned until the end of your
  next turn**; or deal an additional **4d12** damage.
- **Can't Miss What Won't Dodge.** If you have Advantage on your Single Strike
  (such as from Reckless Attack) and it hits, it's a **critical hit** if either
  d20 is **18 or higher**.
- **Unflinching.** You can make your Single Strike even while Prone, Grappled, or
  Restrained without Disadvantage. Position never mattered to a punch like yours.

---

## Serious Series: Final Punch (Level 14)

The flavor: the one you've thrown maybe a handful of times in your life. The one
that doesn't have a counter, a guard, or a survivor. Normal day. Normal punch.

- **Final Punch (the button, once per Rage).** You make your Single Strike with
  **Advantage**; if it hits, it's a **critical hit**. On a hit, the force carries
  past the target: each other creature in a **60-foot Cone** behind it makes a
  Strength save, taking **8d12** Thunder damage and being hurled away and knocked
  Prone on a failure (half damage, no movement, on a success). The landscape
  behind your target is rearranged.
- **No Such Thing As Enough.** There is no upper limit to how many extra attacks
  feed your Single Strike — if any feature, Action Surge analog, or effect would
  grant you further attacks on your turn, each instead adds another **1d12** — and
  your Single Strike treats **Immunity** to its damage type as Resistance.

---

### Designer notes

- The whole subclass is a thought experiment: *what if Extra Attack made one hit,
  not many?* Every forgone attack becomes +1d12, so by level 14 your single swing
  carries roughly **4d12 from the feature plus the Wind-Up dice** on top of weapon
  dice, Rage Damage (potentially twice), and Brutal Strike — a big single number
  that rivals a caster's single-target turn without trivializing it.
- Because it's one attack, it pairs beautifully with **Reckless Attack** (one
  Advantage roll to land the haymaker) and with big weapon dice (greataxe, maul).
- **Final Punch** is the once-per-Rage payoff — you still **roll to hit** (with
  Advantage), it crits on a hit, and the cone behind allows a save for half. It's
  a devastating moment, not an auto-delete. Gate it to once per Long Rest if even
  that is too much.
- Tuning note: this path trades the *reliability* of multiple attacks for one huge
  roll. That swinginess is intended — when you whiff, you *whiff* — and Reckless
  Attack is the mitigation.
