# Path of the Tremor

*Primal Path — Melee / Ranged (quakes, shockwaves, area control)*

> Strike the air and the air strikes back. You learned, somewhere, that the world
> is not as solid as it pretends — that ground and stone and even the empty space
> in front of your fist are just *things waiting to be shaken*. When you swing,
> the blow doesn't stop at your reach. A crack runs out from your knuckles like a
> dropped pane of glass, and everything in its path feels the quake. You are not
> the strongest because you hit hard. You are the strongest because nothing is
> ever far enough away.

This path makes a Barbarian who **shakes the air itself** — landing melee blows at
a distance, converting force into **Thunder damage**, and toppling crowds with
shockwaves. The fantasy is Whitebeard's Gura-Gura no Mi: punch nothing, break
everything.

---

## Quaking Blows (Level 3)

The flavor: you stop hitting *objects* and start hitting the space they occupy.
The tremor does the rest.

While you are raging:

- **Shake the Air.** When you make a melee attack with a weapon or Unarmed
  Strike, you can instead target a creature or object within **30 feet** as the
  shockwave travels to it. You use your normal melee attack and damage rolls;
  this is still a melee attack for every rule that cares.
- **Tremor Damage.** Your weapon and Unarmed Strikes deal an extra **1d6
  Thunder** damage on a hit, and you can change the damage type of your weapon
  attacks to **Thunder**. This extra damage increases to **2d6** at level 10.
- **Cracked Footing.** The first time you hit a given creature on your turn, if it
  is Large or smaller and on a surface, it must succeed on a Strength save or be
  knocked **Prone**.

---

## Shockwave (Level 6)

The flavor: a single strike is never really single. The quake fans outward and
puts everyone nearby on the floor.

- **Concussive Strike.** Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with
  an attack while raging, you can send the tremor outward: each other creature of
  your choice within **15 feet of the target** takes **Thunder damage equal to
  your Rage Damage + your Strength modifier** and must succeed on a Strength save
  or be pushed **15 feet** and knocked Prone.
- **Quake Step (Bonus Action).** Once per Rage, you can stomp, cracking the ground
  in a **20-foot radius** around you. Each creature there makes a Strength save,
  taking **3d6 Thunder** (half on a success) and dropping Prone on a failure. You
  can leave any creatures you choose unaffected. The area becomes Difficult
  Terrain for your enemies until the start of your next turn.

---

## Seismic Reach (Level 10)

The flavor: distance stops meaning anything. Walls, cover, the gap across a
canyon — all of it is just air, and air is yours.

While raging:

- **Far Tremor.** The range of **Shake the Air** increases to **90 feet**, and
  your shockwaves pass through total cover and around corners as if the
  intervening barrier weren't there — you're shaking the medium, not throwing a
  projectile.
- **Air Crack (Reaction).** When you or an ally you can see within 30 feet is hit
  by an attack, you can use your Reaction to wrench the air apart between attacker
  and target, **reducing the damage by 3d10** and pushing the attacker 15 feet.
- **Ripple.** Your **Concussive Strike** radius becomes 15 feet, and whenever you
  score a critical hit it deals an extra **2d6** Thunder for that hit.

---

## Worldquake (Level 14)

The flavor: this is the one the survivors describe in whispers — the day the
ground stood up. You grab the world and *twist*.

- **The Big One (the detonation, once per Rage).** As a **Magic action** while
  raging, choose a point you can see within 120 feet. A quake erupts in a
  **40-foot radius** (or a 90-foot Cone originating from you). Each creature there
  makes a Strength save, taking **10d6 Thunder** damage and being hurled up to 20
  feet, knocked Prone, and **Stunned** until the end of your next turn on a
  failure (half damage and none of the rest on a success). Structures and
  unattended objects in the area are **shattered**.
- **Tremor Incarnate (passive).** Your Thunder and Force damage ignore
  **Resistance** and treat **Immunity as Resistance**, and creatures within 10
  feet of you have Disadvantage on saves against your Tremor features.

---

### Designer notes

- **Shake the Air** is the keystone — it makes you a melee Barbarian who never has
  to close distance, which is enormous tempo. It reaches 60 feet and routes around
  cover by level 10, so you're a real ranged threat on a class that usually has
  none.
- The forced-movement-and-Prone package (**Cracked Footing**, **Concussive
  Strike**, **Quake Step**) is reliable battlefield control without ever needing
  to land a save-or-die — it just keeps the enemy line on the floor.
- **Worldquake** is the signature "island-breaker": a once-per-Rage, save-for-half
  AoE that Stuns on a failure. Drop it to 6d6 and remove the Stun if no-save-area
  control is too strong for your group.
