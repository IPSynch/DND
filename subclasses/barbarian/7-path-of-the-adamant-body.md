# Path of the Adamant Body

*Primal Path — Defender (diamond-hard skin, immovable wall)*

> Hit you. Go ahead. Whatever you brought — fist, blade, falling masonry, the
> business end of something that kills tanks — it lands on a surface that simply
> does not care. When you let your rage settle into your bones instead of your
> temper, your skin runs hard and bright and faceted, and the world's best
> efforts skitter off you like rain off glass. You are the thing the party stands
> behind. You are the wall the enemy throws itself against until it has nothing
> left to throw. Others win fights by hitting harder. You win them by being the
> one who's still standing when everyone else is done.

This path is the **defensive juggernaut**: a Barbarian who turns their body to
**living diamond**, stacking AC, damage resistance, and a damage threshold until
they become almost impossible to meaningfully harm. The fantasy is Jozu's
Diamond Jozu — a glittering, immovable bulwark.

---

## Diamond Skin (Level 3)

The flavor: the rage crystallizes. Your flesh hardens into faceted, light-catching
adamant, and blows that should bite simply slide.

While you are raging:

- **Crystalline Hide.** You gain a **+2 bonus to Armor Class**, and your
  Unarmored Defense can be calculated as **10 + your Constitution modifier ×2**
  (use whichever AC is higher).
- **Glancing Blows.** When you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you
  reduce it by an amount equal to your **Barbarian level** (after your Rage
  Resistance is applied). A hit that's all edges and no give just *scratches* you.
- **Set Stance (Bonus Action).** You can plant yourself: until the start of your
  next turn you can't be moved against your will or knocked Prone, and the first
  time each turn a creature within reach hits you, it takes **Bludgeoning damage
  equal to your Rage Damage + your Constitution modifier** as the impact rebounds.

---

## Refraction (Level 6)

The flavor: a diamond doesn't just stop light — it bends it back. Force that hits
you has to go somewhere, and you decide where.

- **Adamant Frame.** You gain **Resistance to all damage** for the **first round**
  of each Rage, and you no longer have Disadvantage on Stealth or on ability
  checks from heavy armor.
- **Rebound (Reaction).** When a creature you can see within 30 feet hits you with
  an attack, you can use your Reaction to **reflect the blow**, dealing damage
  equal to **the full damage you took** (of the attack's type) back to the
  attacker.
- **Unyielding.** You have Advantage on saving throws against being **Stunned,
  Paralyzed, knocked Prone, or moved**, and you ignore the effects of Difficult
  Terrain while raging — the wall walks where it wants.

---

## Immovable (Level 10)

The flavor: there is a point past which more force simply doesn't matter. You are
past it. Cannon, ogre, avalanche — the math is the same.

While raging:

- **Damage Threshold.** You have a **Damage Threshold of 10**: whenever you take
  damage from a single source, if that damage doesn't equal or exceed your
  threshold, you take **none** of it. (Apply Resistance and Glancing Blows first;
  the threshold checks the result.)
- **Bulwark.** Allies within 10 feet of you gain **half cover** (+2 AC and Dex
  saves), and you can use your Reaction to impose **Disadvantage** on an attack
  roll against an ally within 10 feet of you — you simply step into the line.
- **Settling Weight.** Your AC bonus from **Crystalline Hide** increases to **+3**,
  and your **Adamant Frame** Resistance now covers the first **two** rounds of
  each Rage.

---

## Flawless Diamond (Level 14)

The flavor: the perfect stone. No flaw to crack, no seam to split. The strongest
thing in the room is just *you, standing there.*

- **Nigh-Indestructible.** While raging, you have **Resistance to all damage**
  (not just the opening rounds), your **Damage Threshold becomes 18**, and you are
  **immune** to the Stunned, Paralyzed, and Petrified conditions.
- **Diamond Dust (the button, once per Rage).** As a Reaction when you take
  damage, you can **shatter and reform**, taking **no damage from the triggering
  effect** and releasing a burst of razor shards: each creature within 20 feet
  takes **8d8** Slashing damage (Dexterity save for half) and is Blinded until the
  end of its next turn on a failure.
- **The Unbreakable Wall.** Once on each of your turns, when a creature you can see
  misses you with an attack, you can make one weapon or Unarmed Strike against it.
  They broke themselves on you; collect the debt.

---

### Designer notes

- The defensive stack is intentionally tough — by 14 you have **+3 AC, all-damage
  Resistance, a 15 Damage Threshold, and Glancing Blows** — so small hits do
  *nothing* and big hits are halved-then-reduced. That's how a martial survives
  the rocket-tag of high-level play without runaway "literally unkillable" math.
- **Damage Threshold** is the spiciest piece: against many-small-hits encounters
  it's near-immunity. Drop it to a flat 8 if that trivializes your monsters.
- **Rebound**, **Bulwark**, and **The Unbreakable Wall** keep the path from being
  purely passive — you punish attackers and actively shield the party, so being
  durable still *does* something on offense.
