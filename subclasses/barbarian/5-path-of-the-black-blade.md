# Path of the Black Blade

*Primal Path — Weapon master (peerless swordsmanship, flying slashes)*

> Anyone can swing a sword. You *ended the argument about who swings it best* a
> long time ago. Your rage doesn't make you wild — it makes you *certain*. Where
> a lesser fighter flails, you draw one clean line, and the line is always the
> right one. The blade is an extension of your will, and your will reaches
> farther than your arm: a flick sends a crescent of force across the field, a
> raised edge turns aside a blow that should have killed you. You don't seek
> fights. You don't need to. The strong come looking for you, and they leave
> humbled or they don't leave at all.

This path is the **swordmaster Barbarian**: precise rather than reckless, capable
of **ranged slashing waves**, parries, and a finishing cut that splits anything.
The fantasy is Dracule Mihawk, the World's Strongest Swordsman, whose every
motion is economical and absolute.

---

## Bladelord (Level 3)

The flavor: the weapon stops being a tool and becomes a limb. Distance is just
another thing for the edge to cross.

While you are raging:

- **Master's Grip.** You can use your choice of **Strength or Dexterity** for the
  attack and damage rolls of any weapon with the Slashing damage type, and such
  weapons gain the **Reach** property (or +5 feet if they already have it) in your
  hands.
- **Flying Slash.** When you make a melee attack with a Slashing weapon, you can
  instead loose a crescent of cutting force at a creature or object within
  **30 feet**, using your normal attack and damage rolls. It deals an extra
  **2d8** Force damage and is still a melee weapon attack. This extra damage
  becomes **3d8** at level 10.
- **No Wasted Motion.** You don't have Disadvantage on attacks from being far from
  your target with Flying Slash, and difficult terrain doesn't slow your first
  10 feet of movement each turn.

---

## Peerless Form (Level 6)

The flavor: you have nothing left to learn about defense. Every incoming strike
is just a line waiting to be redirected.

- **Parry (Reaction).** When you're hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to
  parry, **reducing the damage by 2d10 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier +
  your Barbarian level.** If this reduces the damage to 0 and the attacker is
  within your reach (including Flying Slash range), you may immediately make one
  weapon attack against it as part of the same Reaction (a **Riposte**).
- **Flawless Edge.** Once on each of your turns when you miss with a weapon attack
  while raging, you can reroll the attack once.
- **Reading the Blade.** You can't be Surprised while conscious, and you have
  Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see.

---

## World's Strongest (Level 10)

The flavor: the gulf between you and other swordsmen stops being a matter of
degree. Cover, armor, and distance are courtesies you choose to ignore.

While raging:

- **Cut Anything.** Your Slashing weapon attacks (including Flying Slash) ignore
  **Resistance** to their damage type, treat **Immunity as Resistance**, and deal
  **double damage to objects and structures**.
- **Crescent Range.** **Flying Slash** range increases to **90 feet**, and you can
  cut through total cover and around corners — the wave of force routes to your
  target.
- **Unmatched.** Your Slashing weapon attacks score a **critical hit on a roll of
  19 or 20**, and the first time you critically hit a creature each turn, it
  takes an extra **3d8** Force damage.

---

## One Slash (Level 14)

The flavor: this is the cut they build statues to. A single, perfect line that
parts whatever stands in it — flesh, steel, stone, or the air itself.

- **World-Splitter (the finisher, once per Rage).** As part of your Attack action,
  you can forgo all your attacks to deliver a single decisive slash along a
  **120-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line**. Each creature in the line makes a Dexterity
  save, taking **10d8** Slashing or Force damage (your choice) on a failure, half
  on a success. Creatures that fail are also knocked Prone. Walls, doors, and
  unattended structures in the line are simply **cut in two**.
- **Apex Swordsman.** Your **Parry** can now reduce damage from any source you can
  perceive (including spells and breath weapons), and when you Parry an attack to
  0, the Riposte automatically **critically hits** if it lands.

---

### Designer notes

- This is the rare Barbarian that's about **precision and control** rather than
  Reckless brawling — **Flawless Edge** (reroll a miss) and **Parry/Riposte** make
  you reliable and punishing without ever giving enemies Advantage.
- **Flying Slash** gives you a full ranged option a Barbarian normally lacks;
  routing it around cover at 10 makes you the duelist who *cannot be avoided*.
- **World-Splitter** is a once-per-Rage "one perfect cut" — a save-for-half line
  that also demolishes terrain. Bump it to 10d8 if your other capstones outshine
  it, or drop to 6d8 if it's too strong for your table.
