# Homebrew Barbarian Subclasses (D&D 2024 / 5.5e)

Ten original Primal Paths built on the 2024 *Player's Handbook* chassis. Each
grants features at **Barbarian levels 3, 6, 10, and 14**, matching the official
2024 subclass cadence.

Every introduction is written **setting-agnostic** — the flavor is framed so the
subclass drops cleanly into a medieval fantasy game, a modern superhero
campaign, a post-apocalypse, a wuxia table, or anything else. The mechanics
never assume a particular world.

These are tuned to be **very strong but respectable** — roughly a notch above
official subclasses, not broken. The design goal is to let a martial **keep pace
with high-level full casters**, where the game is otherwise dominated by magic.
To that end every path delivers what martials usually lack: **a nova turn**,
**some AoE**, **save-or-suck control**, **a ranged option**, **Resistance
bypass**, and **a little extra action economy**. But the signature "button"
features fire **once per Rage** and either roll to hit or allow a **saving throw
for half** — they're a strong, climactic moment, not an auto-win you spam.

If your group runs lean, dial the dice down a notch; tuning levers are called out
in each file's **Designer notes**.

## Design conventions used throughout

- **Rage** means the 2024 class feature: a **Bonus Action** to enter, lasts
  **10 minutes**, gives Advantage on Strength checks and saves, Resistance to
  Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing, and **Rage Damage** (+2 at levels 1–8, +3 at
  9–15, +4 at 16+). You can't cast or concentrate on spells while raging.
- **Rage Damage** always refers to that scaling bonus, so features that "add your
  Rage Damage" grow automatically as you level.
- **Unarmored Defense** is your level 1 AC = 10 + Dexterity modifier +
  Constitution modifier (no shield, no armor).
- **Reckless Attack** (level 2) and **Extra Attack** (level 5) are assumed
  available; several subclasses interact with them.
- **Brutal Strike** (level 9) is the 2024 feature you can stack with here unless a
  subclass says otherwise.
- A feature's **save DC**, where one is listed, is **8 + your Proficiency Bonus +
  your Strength modifier** unless stated otherwise.
- "While raging" effects switch off the instant your Rage ends.
