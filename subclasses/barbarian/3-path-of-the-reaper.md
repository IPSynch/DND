# Path of the Reaper

*Primal Path — Burst (critical hits, lowered crit threshold)*

> You don't fight angry. You fight *efficiently*. Somewhere along the way you
> stopped seeing enemies and started seeing seams — the gap in the armor, the
> stretched tendon, the half-second the guard drops. You wait for the seam, and
> when it comes you put everything through it at once. People call it luck when
> your blows land *just so*, again and again. It isn't luck. It's a professional
> who clocked in, found the weak point, and got the job done. The overtime pay is
> just blood.

This path is the **critical-hit Barbarian**: it lowers your crit threshold like a
Champion Fighter, then loads your critical hits with brutal, fight-ending riders.
The fantasy is Nanami Kento's cursed-energy ratio and his refusal to do anything
the inefficient way.

---

## Reaper's Eye (Level 3)

The flavor: you see the seam everyone else misses. Your weapon finds it whether
or not you meant it to.

- **Expanded Threat Range.** Your weapon and Unarmed Strike attacks score a
  **critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.**
- **The Ratio (7:3).** **Once on each of your turns**, when you hit a creature
  with an attack, you can call your shot to its weak point: the attack deals an
  extra **3d6** damage of the weapon's type (this becomes **4d6** while you're
  raging). You declare it after the hit is confirmed — it's a measured strike, not
  a free rider on every swing.
- **Clinical Precision.** When you have Advantage on an attack (such as from
  Reckless Attack) and **both** d20s would hit, the attack is treated as a
  **critical hit** even if neither die is in your crit range.

---

## Overtime (Level 6)

The flavor: clocking out isn't your style. When a job needs finishing, you stay
late — and the work gets uglier.

- **Brutal Criticals.** When you score a critical hit with a weapon or Unarmed
  Strike, you roll **two additional weapon damage dice** (on top of the doubled
  dice from the crit) and add your **Rage Damage a second time**.
- **Follow-Through.** Once on each of your turns, when you score a critical hit,
  you can immediately make **one additional attack** as part of the same action.
- **Pressure.** A creature you critically hit has **Disadvantage on its next
  attack roll** before the start of your next turn — your hits don't just hurt,
  they rattle.

---

## Lethal Tempo (Level 10)

The flavor: the seams open faster now. You've done this so many times the weak
point may as well be painted on.

- **Wider Threat Range.** Your attacks now score a **critical hit on a roll of 18,
  19, or 20.**
- **Execution.** When you score a critical hit against a creature, it must succeed
  on a Constitution save or be **Stunned until the end of your next turn**. A
  creature that is below half its hit point maximum makes this save with
  **Disadvantage**.
- **Sharpened Ratio.** Your **The Ratio** bonus increases to **5d6** while raging,
  and your critical hits ignore **Resistance** to the attack's damage type.

---

## Closing Time (Level 14)

The flavor: when the contract says the target doesn't walk away, the target
doesn't walk away. You stop hoping for the seam and simply *decide* it's there.

- **Guaranteed Crit (the button, once per Rage).** You can declare a single attack
  a **critical hit** before you roll — if it hits, it crits, automatically
  triggering every critical-hit feature you have, and it deals the **maximum** of
  its damage dice instead of rolling them.
- **Reaper's Toll.** Your critical hits ignore **Resistance** to the attack's
  damage type and treat **Immunity as Resistance**.
- **Relentless Professional.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a
  critical hit, you regain hit points equal to your Barbarian level and can
  immediately move up to your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. The job
  isn't done until the room is empty.

---

### Designer notes

- The crit range marches **19–20 → 18–20**, matching and slightly beating the
  Champion, and each crit *does more*: an extra die, double Rage Damage, a bonus
  attack, and at 10 a save-or-Stun. Reckless Attack's Advantage means you're
  fishing for those 18s constantly.
- **The Ratio** is now a **once-per-turn** measured strike (sneak-attack cadence),
  not free damage on every hit — strong and reliable, but no longer something you
  stack onto a full attack routine.
- **Clinical Precision** is the sleeper: with Advantage, *two natural hits* = a
  crit, so you crit more often than the raw range suggests — which is what makes
  the once-per-Rage **Guaranteed Crit** finisher land when you most need it.
