# Path of the Wyrm

*Primal Path — Bruiser / Flyer (draconic transformation)*

> The oldest, hungriest thing in the world is not a person, and somewhere in your
> blood, neither are you. It started small — a roughness to the skin, eyes that
> caught the light wrong, a temper that ran too hot to be human. Then you raged,
> truly raged, and the shape underneath came up to meet the air: scales like
> shields, a breath that melts the horizon, a silhouette that makes brave people
> remember they have homes to run to. Lesser kin sprout a wing or a claw. You
> have learned to come up all the way — to stop wearing the dragon and simply
> *be* the calamity with wings.

This path is the **draconic transformation Barbarian**: a half-dragon hybrid form
early, a true dragon form later, complete with **breath weapon, flight, natural
armor, and a presence that breaks morale.** The fantasy is Kaido — a beast in a
person's shape who becomes a literal monster.

---

## Draconic Aspect (Level 3)

The flavor: the dragon under your skin stops hiding. Scales, fangs, and a furnace
in your chest answer the call of your rage.

Choose a **damage type** when you take this subclass — Acid, Cold, Fire,
Lightning, or Poison — your *draconic element*. You can change it when you finish
a Long Rest.

While you are raging:

- **Scales & Claws.** You gain a **+1 bonus to AC** (natural armor) and your
  **Unarmed Strikes** deal **1d8 + your Strength modifier** Slashing damage and
  count as weapons for your Barbarian features. You have **Darkvision 60 feet**
  (or +60 feet if you already have it).
- **Breath Weapon (Bonus Action).** You exhale destruction in a **15-foot Cone**
  or a **30-foot Line**. Each creature there makes a Dexterity save (your Strength
  DC), taking **3d10** damage of your draconic element on a failure, half on a
  success. The dice increase to **4d10** at level 6, **6d10** at level 10, and
  **8d10** at level 14. You can use this **once per turn**, and a number of times
  equal to your Rage Damage bonus per Rage.
- **Elemental Bite.** Once on each of your turns when you hit with an Unarmed
  Strike, you can deal its damage as your draconic element instead, plus an extra
  **1d8** of that type.

---

## Hybrid Form (Level 6)

The flavor: half-and-half — the reach and wings of the wyrm on a frame that still
fits through doors. The most dangerous in-between thing alive.

While raging, you take on a **winged hybrid form**:

- **Wings.** You gain a **Fly Speed equal to your walking Speed**, and your reach
  increases by 5 feet from your lengthening limbs and tail.
- **Tail Sweep.** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with an
  Unarmed Strike, you can make a free **tail attack** against a different creature
  within reach: on a hit it takes **1d8 + your Strength modifier** Bludgeoning
  damage and must save or be knocked Prone.
- **Draconic Resilience.** You gain **Resistance to your draconic element**, and
  when a creature within 30 feet damages you, you can use your Reaction to exhale
  a quick one-target burst at it (using one of your Breath Weapon uses).

---

## Dragon Form (Level 10)

The flavor: no more half measures. The whole beast comes up, fills the room, and
the room is suddenly too small.

While raging, you can transform fully as part of entering your Rage:

- **True Dragon.** Your size becomes **Large** (gear grows with you), your
  Unarmed Strike die becomes **1d10**, your natural-armor bonus becomes **+2**,
  and your Fly Speed increases to **twice** your walking Speed.
- **Frightful Presence.** When you transform, and as a Bonus Action on later turns,
  each enemy of your choice within 30 feet must succeed on a Wisdom save or be
  **Frightened** of you until the end of its next turn. While Frightened by you, a
  creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls.
- **Wing Buffet.** When you fly at least 20 feet and land within 10 feet of a
  creature, or take off, each creature within 10 feet is knocked Prone (Strength
  save negates) and takes **3d8** Bludgeoning damage.

---

## Calamity Incarnate (Level 14)

The flavor: this is the shape that ends eras. When you come up *all the way*,
the sky darkens and the strongest people present quietly recalculate their
chances.

While raging, your Dragon Form becomes apex:

- **Apex Wyrm.** Your size can become **Huge**, your Unarmed Strike die becomes
  **1d12**, your natural-armor bonus becomes **+3**, and you gain **Immunity to
  your draconic element**.
- **Cataclysm Breath (the button, once per Rage).** You unleash an overwhelming
  breath in a **60-foot Cone** or **90-foot Line**. Each creature there makes a
  Dexterity save, taking **12d10** damage of your draconic element on a failure
  (half on a success), and on a failure is also subjected to your element's
  lingering effect (e.g., Fire: ongoing burning; Cold: Speed halved; Lightning:
  can't take Reactions) until the end of your next turn. Your breath ignores
  **Resistance** and treats **Immunity as Resistance**. Terrain and objects in the
  area are devastated.
- **Sky Tyrant.** Your Frightful Presence DC increases by 2, and creatures
  Frightened by you have **Disadvantage on attack rolls** against everyone, not
  just on rolls to approach — your shadow is its own battlefield.

---

### Designer notes

- The transformation arc is the whole point: **scales & a breath weapon at 3**,
  **wings + tail at 6**, **a true Large dragon with fear at 10**, **a Huge apex
  calamity at 14** — a clean power curve mirroring Kaido's hybrid-to-full
  progression.
- The **Breath Weapon** is a Bonus Action you can throw most turns (uses = Rage
  Damage per Rage), so you always have a ranged AoE option alongside your claws —
  unusual and strong for a Barbarian, but capped so it doesn't outshine the party
  caster every round.
- **Cataclysm Breath** is the once-per-Rage "level a city block" — save-for-half
  and Resistance-piercing. Bump to 12d10 if your other capstones overshadow it, or
  trim the cone to 30 feet if it's too strong.
