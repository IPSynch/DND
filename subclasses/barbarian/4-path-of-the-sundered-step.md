# Path of the Sundered Step

*Primal Path — Skirmisher (teleport-swapping, duelist)*

> The battlefield is a board, and you've learned to pick up the pieces and put
> them back down wherever you please — including yourself. A clap, a snap, a
> stamp of the foot, and *space folds*: you're where the enemy was, the enemy is
> where your ally was, and everyone is exactly one beat too slow to do anything
> about it. To you, distance is a suggestion and flanking is a guarantee. The
> best part isn't the chaos. The best part is the look on a real fighter's face
> when they realize they finally get to dance with someone who can keep up.

This path is the **position-swapping duelist**: a Barbarian who teleports across
the battlefield by trading places with creatures, sets up devastating flanks, and
shines one-on-one against a worthy rival. The fantasy is Aoi Todo's Boogie Woogie
and his joy in a real duel.

---

## Boogie Step (Level 3)

The flavor: a percussive *clap*, and two things in the world swap places. You
treat the whole battlefield like it's within arm's reach.

- **Swap (Bonus Action).** While raging, you can clap (or snap, or stomp) and
  instantly **teleport-swap positions** with one creature you can see within
  **30 feet**. If the creature is unwilling, it must succeed on a Strength or
  Charisma save (its choice) against your DC or be swapped and **knocked off
  balance** (Disadvantage on its next attack); on a success the swap fails. You
  can swap a **willing ally** with no save.
- **Disorienting Arrival.** Immediately after you swap, the **first attack you
  make this turn** against a creature within reach has Advantage and deals an
  extra **2d6** Force damage.

---

## Duelist's Joy (Level 6)

The flavor: nothing in the world is better than a real fight against a real
opponent. Your body knows it, and answers in kind.

- **Rival's Focus.** At the start of your turn you can mark one creature you can
  see as your **Rival** (no action). While your Rival is within 5 feet of you and
  no other enemy is, your attacks against it deal an extra **2d8 + your Rage
  Damage**, you have **Advantage on attack rolls** against it, and you have a
  **+2 bonus to AC** against it. You can move your Rival mark to a new creature on
  a later turn.
- **Counter-Step (Reaction).** When a creature you can see within 30 feet attacks
  you or an ally, you can use your Reaction to **Swap** a willing creature
  (including the target) before the attack resolves, potentially yanking it out of
  harm's way — or putting yourself in front of it.
- **Free Footwork.** The first **Swap** you make each turn no longer costs your
  Bonus Action.

---

## Fold Space (Level 10)

The flavor: you stop swapping one pair at a time. The battlefield rearranges
around you like furniture, and your strikes chase the chaos.

While raging:

- **Chain Swap.** When you use **Swap**, you can immediately swap a **second**
  pair of creatures you can see within range (any combination of allies and
  enemies). Unwilling creatures still save.
- **Black Strike.** Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature on the
  same turn you teleported (via Swap, or any other teleport), the hit deals an
  extra **4d6** Force damage, ignores **Resistance**, and the target can't take
  Reactions until the start of its next turn — a perfect, recoiling impact.
- **Blink Reach.** The range of all your Swap effects increases to **90 feet**.

---

## Everywhere At Once (Level 14)

The flavor: there is no "across the room" anymore. There is only "here," and you
decide where "here" is for everyone, all at once.

- **Battlefield Reset (the button, once per Rage).** As part of your turn (no
  action), you **rearrange the board**: choose up to **four creatures** you can
  see within 60 feet and place each into any other chosen creature's space (a
  four-way swap). Each unwilling creature makes a Strength or Charisma save; on a
  failure it's moved, **knocked Prone**, and takes **6d6** Force damage; on a
  success it's moved but suffers nothing else.
- **Perfect Duel.** While only your **Rival** is within reach of you, you score a
  critical hit against it on a roll of **19 or 20**, and you can't be flanked or
  surprised — your whole world narrows to the fight that matters.
- **Recoil Step (Reaction).** When you're hit by an attack, you can use your
  Reaction to **Swap** with the attacker and deal **Black Strike** damage to it as
  you trade places. Turnabout is fair play.

---

### Designer notes

- The core loop is **Swap → Disorienting Arrival → attack with Advantage**, free
  on the first Swap each turn from level 6 — so you're a pinball that always lands
  flanking while still attacking fully.
- **Counter-Step** and **Recoil Step** make you a strong protector *and* punisher:
  you can pull an ally out of a hit, or drag the attacker into your reach.
- **Battlefield Reset** is the showpiece — a once-per-Rage four-way board flip
  that now lets enemies save (moved either way, but Prone + damage only on a
  failure), so it's a powerful tempo swing rather than a guaranteed lockdown. If
  the four-way swap is too fiddly, cap it at a two-pair swap with the damage
  intact.
