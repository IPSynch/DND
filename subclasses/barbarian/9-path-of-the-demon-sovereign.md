# Path of the Demon Sovereign

*Primal Path — Bruiser / Control (demonic transformation, dread, life-drain)*

> There is a crown in you that no one gave you. It sits on something old and
> patient and not entirely your own — a bloodline, a bargain, a thing that found
> you in the dark and decided you'd do. Most days you keep it leashed and human.
> But when your rage runs deep enough, the leash slips, and what stands up
> afterward has horns, has wings, has eyes that make brave people forget their
> own names. You are not possessed. That's the frightening part. The demon
> doesn't take you over — it *is* you, the part you've been politely pretending
> isn't there. And it rules.

This path is the **demonic transformation Barbarian**: a Rage that births a
horned, dreadful form which **drains life, radiates fear, and warps the
battlefield with dark power.** The fantasy is Mirajane's Satan Soul and the
Gorosei's awakened devil forms — sovereign monsters wearing a person's face.

---

## Demonic Awakening (Level 3)

The flavor: the human mask comes off. Horns, claws, and a cold authority settle
over you, and the room gets a few degrees colder.

While you are raging, you take a **demonic form**:

- **Fiendish Claws.** Your **Unarmed Strikes** deal **1d8 + your Strength
  modifier** Necrotic damage (your choice of Necrotic or Slashing each hit) and
  count as weapons for your Barbarian features. You gain **Darkvision 60 feet**
  (or +60 feet) that pierces magical darkness.
- **Dread Aura.** Enemies that start their turn within **10 feet** of you take
  **Psychic damage equal to your Rage Damage + your Strength modifier**, and the
  first time each turn one
  of them attacks you, it does so with no benefit from being unseen — your
  presence drags them into the light.
- **Drain.** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature, reduce one to 0
  hit points, or score a critical hit, you regain **hit points equal to your
  Barbarian level.** The crown feeds.

---

## Sovereign's Dominion (Level 6)

The flavor: dread becomes command. The weak-willed feel the crown's weight and
their courage curdles.

- **Aura of Fear (Bonus Action).** Each enemy of your choice within 20 feet must
  succeed on a Wisdom save (your Strength DC) or be **Frightened** of you until
  the end of its next turn. While Frightened by you, a creature takes an extra
  **2d6** Psychic damage from your attacks.
- **Hellfire Strikes.** Your Unarmed Strikes and weapon attacks deal an extra
  **2d6** Fire or Necrotic damage (your choice), and once per turn when you hit a
  Frightened creature, that damage is **doubled**.
- **Dark Vitality.** The hit points you regain from **Drain** can exceed your
  maximum, becoming **Temporary Hit Points** (up to your Barbarian level).

---

## Greater Demon (Level 10)

The flavor: the form grows into itself — wings unfurl, the aura thickens, and the
sovereign stops walking the battlefield and starts *presiding* over it.

While raging:

- **Wings of the Pit.** You gain a **Fly Speed equal to your walking Speed**, and
  Resistance to **Necrotic, Fire, Poison, and Psychic** damage.
- **Withering Presence.** The radius of your **Dread Aura** increases to 20 feet,
  and creatures that fail a save against your **Aura of Fear** also have their
  Speed **halved** and can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn.
- **Soul Harvest.** When a creature dies within 30 feet of you, you gain
  **Temporary Hit Points equal to twice its Challenge Rating** (minimum 2) and a
  **+2 bonus to attack and damage rolls** until the end of your next turn. Death
  feeds the crown.

---

## Demon Sovereign (Level 14)

The flavor: the title becomes literal. You are no longer a person with a demon in
them — you are a demon *king*, and the battlefield is a court that has not yet
learned to kneel.

While raging:

- **True Fiend.** Your size can become **Large**, your Unarmed Strike die becomes
  **1d12**, you gain **Immunity to Necrotic and Psychic** damage and to the
  Frightened condition, and your **Dread Aura** damage increases to **3d6**.
- **Sovereign's Decree (the button, once per Rage).** As a Magic action, you exert
  your authority over a **40-foot radius**. Each enemy there makes a Wisdom save.
  On a failure, a creature is **Frightened and Incapacitated** until the end of
  your next turn and takes **10d8** Necrotic damage (you regain hit points equal to
  half the total dealt across all targets); on a success it takes half damage and
  is Frightened only. A creature already Frightened by you makes this save with
  **Disadvantage.**
- **Undying Crown.** The first time each Rage you would drop to 0 hit points, you
  instead drop to **hit points equal to twice your Barbarian level** and let out a
  pulse: each enemy within 15 feet takes **6d8** Necrotic damage (Constitution
  save for half). A king does not fall so easily.

---

### Designer notes

- The path's engine is **fear → bonus damage → life-drain**: Frighten a target,
  hit it for doubled rider damage, and heal off your hits and kills. It's a
  sustain bruiser that gets harder to wear down the longer the fight runs — the
  demon-lord fantasy, and a solid answer to attrition over a long adventuring day.
- **Soul Harvest** and **Undying Crown** make big fights tilt your way without
  snowballing out of control — modest temp HP and a single "don't die" pulse per
  Rage, rather than a runaway engine.
- **Sovereign's Decree** is the showpiece: a once-per-Rage AoE Frighten +
  Incapacitate + Necrotic with self-healing, save-for-half. If lockdown-plus-
  damage-plus-healing in one button is still too much, drop the Incapacitate (keep
  Frightened) at your table.
